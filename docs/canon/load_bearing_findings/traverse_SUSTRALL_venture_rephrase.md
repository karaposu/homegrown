## Finding Summary

- **The ladder, in one breath:** the system walks thinking space one traverse at a time; SUSTRALL is the walker-system that keeps the walking going — seeing, choosing, dispatching, remembering, assessing; **a venture is one purposeful, bounded walk of that system through a particular territory**; stacked ventures — walks composing walks — are the horizon.
- **Canon had already laid the bottom two rungs verbatim** — "one worker-loop run is one traversal" and "the whirl is the sustained loop of traverses" (`docs/canon/naming_change.md`) — and this rephrase extends that stated structure upward by exactly one rung. The full canon reread (all 36 files, ~5,700 lines, read in full per the user's directive) verified every anchor.
- **"Venture" names something canon gestures at four independent ways but never names**: explfine (canon's acceptance test) is venture-*shaped*; "bounded meaningful traversal" is the venture-*property*; an itinerary is the venture-*pattern*; and the Movement Frame speaks of "the walk" without naming the walk-unit. The mint is a **naming-of-the-latent, additive** — no existing term loses meaning; five gain a missing relatum.
- **The definition (kind first, per the project's own "names before kinds" pitfall):** a venture is a **RUN** — one episode of the system in motion — with three marks: a **specific purpose** (which makes its end judgeable), a **particular thinking space** (a bounded, readable territory), and a **beginning and an end** (ends by purpose — satisfied, deliberately abandoned, or handed off — never by timer; pauses are reversible).
- **Venture is tier-agnostic:** it is a run of the SUSTRALL-*shaped* system at whatever tier the project stands; **the tier grades WHO carries the five cargos** (seeing, selecting, dispatching, remembering, stop-judging) during it. The traversal sample is one venture, run at Level 0 with the human carrying all five — canon's own caption fits it exactly: *"The system exists. You are currently most of it."*
- **The sharpest payoff of the mint:** explfine is a *binary* acceptance test (the system carries a whole run, human contributing only seed and reviews — pass or fail); the venture-vocabulary makes the climb **gradable** — partial cargo-shifts become nameable milestones on the way. "Reach venture" is a cargo-shift statement, and three candidate bars are presented OPEN for the user.
- **The position, honestly:** the mover (`/traverse` + its in-loop equipment) is built and exercised — *at the artifact-contract level; whether the loop thinks better than skilled plain prompting is canon's still-open bet, never yet baseline-tested*. Every **between-loop** support is designed-or-dead: traversal memory has "zero instances ever" (canon's words), the routelog died unused, the mechanism catalog is user-gated and unwired. That asymmetry is exactly why the next breakthrough is to reach venture rather than to build more support first.
- **The suspicion-principle is canon-native, not new discipline:** it generalizes the project's own practiced-reality culture ("a one-day-old rule does not enter canon"; honest status labels; N=2 before protocols) to the between-loop class — the class where a support-design died on contact with evidence just this week. It **gates trust, never activity**: climbing continues; what stops is treating unexercised support-designs as settled ground.
- **The principle applies to itself:** this rephrase is Meaning-layer work, **provisional until the first venture-run exercises its terms** — fundamental in role, not in immunity.
- **Four delivery routes for canonization are sketched and ranked; the choice is the user's.** The gate's recommendation: the staged route — this finding plus a one-line pointer in `docs/canon/sustained_traversal_loop_of_loops.md` now, full canonization after the first venture-run (the exact pattern canon used for the Allocation Rule).

A venture is a run of the **system-sense**, never of the state-sense — that one commitment is what makes the rest of this finding coherent.

An optional relation-sentence, explicitly a relation and not a definition — refined post-compile with the name's stated root (the user, 2026-07-11): "SUStained" is meant as in a **sustained chain reaction**:

> *SUSTRALL is the system that runs ventures — "SUStained" as in a sustained chain reaction: the aim is venture-running that keeps itself going, each run's yield igniting the next. Until that criticality, the human is the whirl's energy source — which is why canon's quality bar exists ("the loop must be worth keeping in motion"): a sub-critical reaction must be worth its fuel.*

This gives "SUStained" both its object and its root. The object: what is sustained is venture-running. The root: self-sustaining — a sense canon already carries unlabeled, in the tinder-fire image ("self-sustaining enough that each cycle feeds the next," `docs/canon/minimum_viable_loop.md`) and in the SUSTRALL section's own energy clause ("the human is the whirl's energy source until autonomy is earned"). The two senses compose rather than compete: the chain-reaction sense is the aspiration; the worth-keeping-in-motion bar is the *sub-critical operating condition* (operator fatigue = fuel exhaustion). And the composition lands the dual precisely: the system-sense = the reactor; **the achievement-state = the criticality point** — each turn's yield triggering the next without external re-ignition. It also sharpens the horizon: stacked ventures are what the chain reaction looks like once it is self-sustaining — a venture's exhaust (routes, frontier, memory) igniting the next venture.

### 3. What a venture is

**Kind first** — the project's own methodology-pitfalls note warns, in its entirety: "KIND error: things got names before getting kinds." So: **a venture is a RUN — one episode of the system in motion.** It is not a system (that is SUSTRALL), not a capability (that is explfine's kind), not a pattern (an itinerary), not a capability-level (a tier), not a single cycle (a turn), and not a procedure or protocol (a venture is an occurrence, not a recipe).

**The three marks:**

1. **A specific purpose.** A venture is undertaken *for* something — a seeded aim the run answers to. This fills the first slot of canon's open termination question ("enough for WHAT?"): the purpose is what makes a venture's end *judgeable*. It fills that slot; it does not solve the termination question — the measure, detection, economics, risk, decidability, authority, and resumability sub-questions (the eight-way clarification in `devdocs/inquiries/`' termination finding, now canon at `docs/canon/clarifying_the_traversal-termination_question.md`) all remain open.
2. **A particular thinking space.** A venture traverses a bounded, readable territory — the same condition canon already places on explfine's input ("any bounded, readable territory"). Not the whole of thinking space; a region of it.
3. **A beginning and an end.** A venture begins when its purpose is seeded (canon's own input-form for the meta-loop: "seed plus context") and ends **by purpose**: satisfied, deliberately abandoned, or handed off — never by a timer. Abandonment includes judging that further movement is not worth its cost — that is canon's own stop-clause ("…or decide that more movement is not worth the cost," `docs/canon/worker_loop_logic.md` §6), a judgment, not a timeout. And bounded does not mean uninterrupted: pauses are reversible — the sample parked a branch for 32 days and recalled it.

**Tier-agnostic, with the tier grading the cargos.** A venture is a run of the SUSTRALL-*shaped* system — hands, eyes, will, memory — at whatever tier the project stands. What the tier grades is **who carries the five cargos** during the run: seeing, selecting, dispatching, remembering, stop-judging (canon's five transfer-cargos). At Level 0 the human carries all five. The climb to autonomy is the same venture-shape with cargos shifting onto the system — which is what makes "reach venture" a *cargo-shift* statement rather than a first-existence statement.

**Identity: thread-continuity.** One venture = one continuously-pursued thread. The purpose may sharpen and re-frame through findings — canon classifies this landscape as non-stationary ("the map is built by traversing it"), so purpose-sharpening is the expected dynamic, not an identity-break. Continuity is carried by the record chain (the continues-from / refines / corrects links between inquiry folders) and the navigator's returning intention. A deliberate pivot to an unrelated thread starts a new venture.

**The exemplar.** `devdocs/traversal_sample.md` records one venture: July 2 → July 10, 2026, "roughly 60 traverse-loop runs, steered by the user acting as navigator and traversal memory" (the sample's own words, verified on disk). One evolving-but-continuous thread — from "improve traversal memory" through the harvest machinery to "re-tool the generation pipeline, then return" — satisfied at the end, with the 32-day articulate2 park-and-recall as the reversible-pause case in the wild. In canon's frame: a Level-0 venture, all five cargos human-carried. Canon's feasibility verdict is the sample's caption: *"The system exists. You are currently most of it."*

**Two residuals, carried visibly rather than hidden:** (i) the *pivot-boundary* — exactly when a purpose-shift becomes a new venture — is answered here at the meaning layer (thread-continuity plus the record chain) but its determination *mechanism* is structural-layer work, deferred; (ii) the sample's end is read as purpose-satisfied (the re-tooling thread closed; harvesting resumed as the next pursuit) — the alternative reading (paused at a waypoint) is noted and low-stakes.

### 4. Why the mint coheres with canon (the reconciliation)

The full reread ran a collision-check against every canon document. No existing term names the bounded, purposeful run-unit — and canon gestures at it four independent ways:

1. **explfine is a venture-species — and the acceptance bar.** Canon: "point SUSTRALL at territory P (any bounded, readable territory) with goal G… until signals say stop." Tested against the three marks as worded: purpose ✓ (goal G — explore-and-define), particular space ✓ (territory P), beginning-and-end ✓ ("until signals say stop"). explfine keeps its identity entirely and gains a genus: explfine-runs are ventures whose purpose-type is explore-and-define.
2. **"Bounded meaningful traversal" is the property a venture bears.** Canon names the meta-loop's *target*: "bounded meaningful traversal: explore the region around a seed enough to answer the goal… or decide that more movement is not worth the cost." The phrase names a property; the venture is its countable bearer — *one* bounded meaningful traversal, undertaken on purpose.
3. **An itinerary is the pattern a venture may follow.** Canon's movement vocabulary: "itineraries — composed multi-loop journeys… many steps with a shape, not one step." Type versus token: the itinerary is the shape; the venture is a walked instance (which may also be improvised).
4. **"The walk" is the Movement Frame's presupposed but un-named object.** The frame's steering category is defined as "decisions ABOUT the walk," and its compliance rule — "types steps, never scores walks" — bars *scoring* walks, not *naming* them. The venture names the object the frame's own definitions presuppose. (Adding venture as a frame *category* would be a structural-layer edit; it is not proposed here.)

Plus two standing *needs* the mint answers: canon's own observation that "a continuous loop… has no built-in stop" (a venture ends by purpose — the missing stop-structure at the multi-loop scale), and the termination question's empty purpose-slot (mark 1).

**The additive verdict:** no existing term loses meaning; five gain a missing relatum. The compatible neighbors keep their places: an **inquiry** is one traverse's record-container (a venture's record is a *chain* of inquiry folders plus their links — what the sample-compile reconstructed by hand); an **episode** is the memory-kind vocabulary a venture's record instantiates; a **tier** is orthogonal (any tier's system runs ventures); a **branch** (child inquiry) is a within-traverse structure, below venture scale; **"seed plus context"** is canon's name for a venture's starting input.

**Why a NAME earns its place** (the project keeps a standing flag against vocabulary-proliferation, so this is argued, not assumed): "venture" is decision-bearing on two standing decisions — the breakthrough-criterion ("reach venture" needs a noun to be a criterion) and the suspicion-principle's release-condition (§6). A description cannot anchor either. **And the sharpest payoff:** explfine is *binary* — the whole capability passes or it doesn't. The venture-vocabulary makes the climb **gradable**: partial cargo-shifts become nameable milestones (§5's bars) that explfine's frame cannot express. The mint converts a pass/fail acceptance test into a graded climb.

Two notes that travel with the term, not inside the definition: the everyday connotation of "venture" (an undertaking with stakes, committed resources, an uncertain outcome) maps cleanly onto canon's own classification of this work (derivative-free search under expensive, noisy evaluation — with the operator's energy as the whirl's fuel); and the nearest computing-native analog, the reinforcement-learning *episode* (a bounded agent-environment run), is informative precisely where it breaks — an RL episode's terminal state is externally defined, while a venture's end is purpose-judged from inside, which is exactly the same-judge problem canon's termination finding names.

### 6. The suspicion-principle

**Scope, stated visibly as an interpretation the user can correct:** in the user's rule — "what we build other than traversal, things to support sustrall, they are all suspicious" — "traversal" is read as **the built mover** (`/traverse` and its in-loop equipment, exercised daily), and "things to support sustrall" as **the between-loop class**: mechanisms whose job is chaining traverses — memory designs, navigational-session attributes, selection-records, views and indexes, dispatch carriers. This is the reading on which the rule is non-trivial (under "traversal = the whole activity," nothing is other-than-traversal) and it matches the user's revealed practice (building and relying on in-loop equipment all week while repeatedly challenging between-loop designs).

**The rule** — four formulations were drafted; the gate ranked the composed form strongest, and the user may still prefer the simpler one:

> **The one-breath form:** no between-loop support mechanism is trusted, canonized, or built upon until the holistic venture-design shows where it fits.
>
> **The composed form (recommended):** every support item carries its grade — *built-exercised / built-unused / designed-unwired / discussed-only* — and only built-exercised items may be load-bearing in new work; a grade rises only when BOTH conditions hold: **design-fit** (the holistic venture-picture places the item) and **exercised-fit** (a real venture-run has used it).

**It gates trust, never activity.** Designing, drafting, and above all *climbing* continue — canon's climb-by-default (the andon rule) stands. The principle *complements* the Allocation Rule: climbing generates exactly the exercise-evidence that releases suspicion. The release is observable by construction (the two conditions), never a mood.

**It is canon-native.** The precedent chain, each item already canon: practiced-reality ("a one-day-old rule does not enter canon"; staged canonization) · "N=2 justifies a protocol; one is an observation" · committed-shape/placeholder-values · the honest status labels ("designed, not built"; "zero instances ever") · the instrument gap (support-quality is *unknowable* before the instruments exist) · the kind-error pitfall. And one live precedent from this very week: a between-loop support design (the central selections-ledger adoption inside the mechanism-extraction finding) was adopted pre-holistically and died on grep contact with canon — the correction is on record. The principle is that lesson made standing.

**It applies to itself.** Inverting the rule — "the holistic understanding is itself suspicious until support-builds exercise it" — holds, and strengthens it: this rephrase is Meaning-layer work, **provisional until the first venture-run exercises its terms**. No paradox hides here, and the resolution is worth stating precisely: the rule's *epistemic core* (staged trust, honest labels) is already canon-exercised norm — trusted now, inherited, not new; what is provisional is its *venture-specific scope-vocabulary*, which matures exactly as the rephrase does. A rule can be trusted as an instance of an exercised norm while its new vocabulary awaits exercise.

**Reflexive coverage, named:** the principle covers this week's own outputs — the mechanism catalog, the selections-ledger redesign, the sample's memory-requirements list — and this dive's own downstream consequences.
