# From Traverse to Venture — the settled vocabulary

This is a glossary of the terms the traversal-context work has settled. It records only the sure parts — what each term IS — not the design details, open decisions, or candidate mechanisms behind them. Sibling canon docs carry the fuller accounts (`load_bearing_findings/traverse_SUSTRALL_venture_rephrase.md`, `SUSTRALL_Reactor_Analogy.md`, `grasp_management.md`, `thinking_space_dynamics.md`).

## What a venture is

A **venture** is one bounded undertaking of the system: a run — an episode — over a particular thinking space, with a specific purpose. It is made of many turns chaining many traverses. It begins when a purpose is seeded, and it ends **by its purpose** — satisfied, abandoned, or handed off — never by a timer. Pauses are reversible; spanning days and sessions is normal.

A venture's identity is **thread-continuity**: the purpose may sharpen and re-frame as findings land, and it is still the same venture as long as the thread continues; a pivot to something unrelated is a new venture. A venture is **tier-agnostic**: what varies with the system's maturity is *who carries the work* — seeing, selecting, dispatching, remembering, stop-judging — not what a venture is. Today the human carries most of it.

## The vocabulary

**traverse** — the run-unit. One worker-loop run is one traversal; its record is one inquiry folder. A venture chains many of them.

**SUSTRALL** — the sustained system: the assembled loop-of-loops running venture after venture (and, as a state, the achievement of having it run sustained). "Sustained" means two things at once: **self-sufficiency** (the work's own yield feeds its continuation) and **non-self-destruction** (the running does not degrade the record it runs on).

**the traversal context (the context line)** — what the mover holds live while working: a finite span moving through the thinking space. It advances at the front and dissolves at the tail; what dissolves is not destroyed — it leaves artifacts (the record's files). The line is grasp set in motion: it cannot hold everything, and looking back costs span that could have held the front.

**the record** — the durable trail the traversals leave: inquiry folders, findings, indexes, concept maps. Cross-session memory lives here. Ventures chain through it.

**the standing project context** — the background context: the situation a venture keeps between runs, distinct from any one run's live line. It has **two halves** — backward (the combined gist of the runs so far) and forward (the open options plus the returning intention) — and **two operations**: update it at run-end, re-instate it at run-start. It is situation, not knowledge (the knowledge is merged into canon; nothing else holds *where the venture stands*). Today it lives mostly in the operator's head — the venture-grain carrier is named but not built.

**the returning intention** — the forward half's core: what the venture means to come back to. It is a held meta-goal — a standing condition waiting for its moment.

**meta-goal** — the kind of yield a run is undertaken for, one level above concrete results (an account, a repair, a validation, a limitation-map, and so on). A run's concrete result cannot be predicted in advance; the KIND being sought can always be stated in advance — and checked at run-end against what the run actually produced. Context-choice is never random: there is a meta-goal in mind each time, and naming its kind is what makes that recordable.

**retrieval's two initiators** — dissolved context comes back in exactly two ways. **Deliberate-initiated**: the mover decides to look back — a capped backward touch mid-run, an index scan between runs, a dedicated re-reading session across runs. **Cue-fired**: the past announces itself unbidden — "there was something related to this."

**resonance** — the cue-fired mechanism. The past doesn't watch; it is shaped so the present lights it up. At reminding time there is no scan over past items and no active watcher-process per item — each past thing carries its own firing conditions, and the current context itself is the query. The watching is structure, not computation.

**resonance-ready writing** — the practical consequence of resonance: unbidden return is a writing discipline. A past thing encoded without the cues its future contexts will present never fires. So records are written with links, distinctive phrasings, and conditions stated in the vocabulary tomorrow's work will actually carry.

**registered windows, and the observer gap** — the record already writes per-item firing conditions at scale: maturation-triggers on seeds, re-open conditions on findings — registered windows, each waiting for its event. What does not exist is a **standing observer**: a mechanism that holds the current context against those windows as a matter of course. Today the human is the observer — the windows fire when their events pass through the operator's own hands.

**the decision-slot** — the standing rule over all retrieval and notification: whatever comes back feeds a *choice*; nothing auto-runs. A fired reminder proposes; selection decides. This gap between yield and uptake is where steering lives, and it is preserved by design.

**the chain, and why the return channel matters** — ventures chain through the record: a turn's yield reaches later work only through memory. For the chain to feed itself — rather than needing a fresh human push for every turn — a yield must be able to *find its future consumer*. A system-side return channel is therefore a necessary condition for self-sufficiency (necessary, not sufficient: the yield's quality and the selection gates still decide everything else — and running human-fed is a legitimate mode; going further is a chosen goal, not a necessity). Over long time the cost shapes the mechanism: any scanning of the past costs more as the record grows, while resonance costs only per hit — so the longer the horizon, the more the unbidden channel must carry. That is why the reminding question matters more, not less, at venture and SUSTRALL scale.
