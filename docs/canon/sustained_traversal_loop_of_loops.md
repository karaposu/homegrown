# SUSTRALL — the SUStained TRAversal Loop of Loops

**Naming.** Coined *TraversalLovableLoop (TLL)* by the user; renamed **SUSTRALL — SUS(tained) TRA(versal) L(oop of) L(oops)** on 2026-06-10. The double L deliberately encodes the level-4 truth: every revolution of the outer loop contains a full worker-loop run. The original coinage's middle word is retired; its substance — the loop must be worth sustaining — is carried by the name's first word, **SUStained** (see "What 'Sustained' commits to" below).

SUSTRALL is the **era-goal** — the committed target of the current epoch, the vehicle toward the north star (`docs/canon/project_north_star.md`). It is the named, testable, assembled end-state of the meta-loop program (`docs/canon/worker_loop_logic.md` §6: "a stateful traversal engine… a controlled whirl").

---

## What SUSTRALL is (one sentence)

**SUSTRALL is the assembled, trust-graduated traversal system — worker loop-runners, a navigational individual session, and an orchestrator with traversal memory — revolving as a controlled whirl over thinking space, achieved when its per-axis autonomy gates are passed on recorded evidence and it can explore-and-define an arbitrary readable territory with the human contributing only seed and reviews.**

## One revolution (the unit of operation)

```
probe      a worker loop runs one inquiry to a finding
see        the navigational session enumerates the field of directions over the finished work
decide     the orchestrator makes a loop-control decision and selects the next route
dispatch   the next session starts
remember   traversal memory is updated (what was visited, selected, why, with what outcome)
assess     traversal signals checked → continue or stop
```

The loop already turns today — at autonomy Level 0, with human organs: the human reads the field, selects, dispatches, remembers, and stops. **SUSTRALL is not a thing to invent; it is the earned, per-axis transfer of those organs to the system** — exact through Level 3; at Level 4 one organ (the cross-head Evaluator) is genuinely new, grown rather than transferred.

## The components

**Loop runners — the hands.** The existing worker runners (`/MVL`, `/MVLw`, `/aMVLw`), each executing one inquiry at full depth. Control scope: per-cycle only (conclude-or-iterate within one inquiry). Unchanged under SUSTRALL.

**The navigational individual session — the eyes.** A fresh, context-isolated session, warmed on the terrain (project orientation → long-run trajectory → recent work → target artifacts), running the enumerator (routelister) over a finished cycle or territory. It produces the typed field of directions. Two load-bearing boundaries: it **never chooses** ("Navigation sees; it does not choose"), and it is a *session*, not a standing document — fresh perception each time. Its persistent product is the standing concept-map (the `_route.md` lineage), which is the cache of its perception, not a replacement for it.

**The orchestrator — the will.** The cross-inquiry controller; today 100% human. Its repertoire: the **closed seven-move loop-control vocabulary** (terminate / widen / merge / re-run-deeper / unblock / different-approach / revisit — *decided*, never enumerated), **route selection** among what the eyes enumerated, and **pattern invocation** from the open multi-loop pattern library (the MTTP class). Its organ: **traversal memory** — the cross-inquiry record of visits, selections, rationales, and outcomes; the one component with zero instances at naming time. Its body, at maturity: the **automation carrier** — the mechanism that spawns and sequences sessions without a human typing each command (named as a dependency; deliberately not designed at the meaning layer).

**The triple ↔ nine-axis correspondence.** The three-part decomposition and the autonomy ladder's nine-axis frame (`docs/future-seed/half-baked/autonomy_ladder.md`) are two resolutions of one architecture:

| SUSTRALL component | Ladder roles / axes |
|---|---|
| Loop runners | Worker |
| Navigational session | Navigator |
| Orchestrator | Selector + Runner (+ Evaluator at L4) + Memory (its organ) + Reflect-channel (it learns from its own recorded rationales and outcomes) |
| — its dispatch dimension | Multi-head (how many hands turn at once) |
| **Outside SUSTRALL** | Goal-formation — the human's retained seat |

The meta-loop is not one slot in the triple; it is what the assembled triple IS. At Level 4 the orchestrator internally differentiates into distinct Selector / Runner / Evaluator seats.

## What SUSTRALL is NOT

- **Not the end-goal** — the consciousness-gradient north star remains the asymptote; SUSTRALL is this era's vehicle.
- **Not a new thinking discipline** — it composes the existing ones.
- **Not a rename of the meta-loop concept** — every canon commitment survives re-test; SUSTRALL adds the assembly's name, its acceptance test, and its sustainability bar.
- **Not exhaustive discovery** — bounded meaningful traversal, per canon.
- **Not (in this era) a UI** — the navigate-by-intent interface is a later view layer over the artifacts.
- **Stopped below Goal-formation** — choosing what to care about stays human; transferring that seat belongs to Level 4+/5 territory.

## The bootstrap jump

**FROM** Level 0/1: automation lives *below* the inquiry boundary (six disciplines auto-chain within an inquiry); above it, every function is human — cross-inquiry traversal lives in the user's head.

**TO** Level 3 — the "certain degree" threshold: the system selects and dispatches sequential chains within familiar territory, holds memory, consumes its own reflections; the human seeds and supervises. Level 4 — the full whirl: parallel worker heads, cross-head evaluation, merge decisions.

**The five cargos that cross:**

| Cargo | From | To |
|---|---|---|
| Seeing | human reads the field | already artifact-borne (routelister maps); needs only the isolated-session habit |
| Selecting | human gut | system Selector, calibrated against recorded human rationales |
| Dispatching | human typing | the automation carrier |
| Remembering | the user's head | traversal-memory artifacts |
| Stop-judging | human fatigue | traversal signals (honest placeholders by canon design) |

**Three properties of the jump:**

- **A staircase wearing the name of a leap.** Each cargo graduates separately on the nine-axis dial, each step gated by evidence. Gate *shapes* are committed; gate *numbers* (≈10 recorded maps; ≈80% selector agreement) are placeholders by their source's own statement.
- **Self-provisioning.** The lower steps generate exactly the calibration data the upper steps require — recording selection rationales at Level 1 is what makes a trustworthy Level-2 Selector possible. Trust for traversal grows by climbing, not before it.
- **The turn-invariant.** *A revolution counts as a SUSTRALL turn when its selection-rationale is recorded into traversal memory.* By this definition the very next manually-orchestrated cycle, plus one written line of why, is already a SUSTRALL turn at Level 0/1. Pacing: gates constrain order, not calendar — deliberate honest data-collection may pass them quickly; manufactured records cannot (they defeat the records' only purpose).

## Explfine — the acceptance test

**Signature:** `explfine(P, G?)` — point SUSTRALL at territory `P` (any **bounded, readable** project territory; scale handled by staged resolution, not denied) with goal `G` (may be fuzzy; defaults to "understand P").

**Mechanism-shape:** the whirl alternates breadth (the eyes sweep concept-identities at increasing resolution — ~10 top concepts, then 50–100, then ~200) with depth (the hands define each concept: what it is, its boundaries, what it should integrate with), the orchestrator routing between them until signals say stop.

**Output — three artifact classes:**
1. The **concept-map**: components → sub-components → sub-concepts, with integration relations. **The detail-line (its hard boundary):** the map may name components, responsibilities, interfaces, contracts, and integration relations; it may NOT name file paths, languages, frameworks, or code constructs — like a survey map's declared scale, omissions are by contract, not by failure.
2. **Per-concept definition findings** — each visited concept gets a stabilized definition artifact.
3. The **frontier list** — what remains unexplored, declared honestly.

**Semantics:** *explored* = swept and visited at declared resolution (coverage auditable from the traversal record); *defined* = each visited concept has its definition artifact. **Doneness** is adjudicated by the traversal signals — placeholder-honest. **Differentiators** (durable even if generic repo-understanding commoditizes): the auditable reasoning trail, declared coverage, frontier honesty, resumability — the method, not just the map.

*Teaching analogies, flagged as analogies (no guarantee-import):* explfine is cognitive SLAM — exploring unknown territory while building the map you navigate by; the jump's staging is the autopilot certification ladder — each automation mode earned by demonstrated reliability, the pilot's role elevating to monitoring, never vanishing.

**The SUSTRALL-achieved checklist:** SUSTRALL is achieved when **(a)** the per-axis gates are passed at recorded-evidence level — system Selector (agreement-gated), system dispatch, system-held memory, system-consumed reflections for sequential chains [L3]; multihead + Evaluator where needed [L4] — **and (b)** an explfine demo completes on a fresh, bounded, readable territory with the human contributing only the seed and reviews, producing map + findings + frontier with declared coverage.

## What "Sustained" commits to

The name's first word is a quality bar, not an adjective: **the loop must be worth keeping in motion.** Concretely — turns are low-friction (one intent in → useful durable artifacts out), artifacts are trustworthy (checkable, resumable), interruption is graceful (stopping mid-whirl never loses or punishes), and outputs are wanted (maps one enjoys opening). This is load-bearing, not decorative: the documented Level-0 failure mode is operator fatigue, and until autonomy is earned the human is the whirl's energy source — a loop that isn't worth operating stops getting operated. After autonomy is earned, the same bar keeps review tolerable.

Optional observability for the bar (turn-cost, interruption-cost, artifact-trust incidents) is available instrumentation, not part of the definition.

## What achieving SUSTRALL means for self-improvement

Baldwin cycles ride SUSTRALL revolutions:

- **Trigger — split honestly in two.** *Process-level* improvement needs (spinning, stalls, coverage holes, untouched frontiers) become **system-detected** — the whirl watches its own work; the system-detected fraction of improvement triggers finally leaves ≈0%, *for the process class*. *Quality-level* needs (a discipline producing subtly worse output) still await the Predictive RC; SUSTRALL does not replace it.
- **Speed.** Detection-to-correction latency loses its dominant human-attention term — a detected process-issue can become the next probe in the same revolution.
- **Magnitude & Retention.** Cycle counting becomes mechanical (traversal memory records every encoded change's journey); revisit-moves make retention checkable (slow drift and cross-discipline transfer become collectible because something finally operates across inquiries).
- **Telemetry birth.** Nearly every starved measurement in the self-improvement-rate framework (`docs/future-seed/self_improvement_rate.md`) presupposes exactly the records traversal memory keeps. The measurement framework and SUSTRALL are two views of one artifact.
- **Reflexivity.** Self-analysis collapses into the capability itself: **explfine(self)** — the whirl pointed at this project's own harness.

## The path to achievement

**Tier 0 — decisions:** done (this document is the canonization step).
**Tier 1 — the climb starts (habits + one artifact; = L1):** create the first traversal-memory artifact ever; adopt the turn-invariant; run the eyes as designed (fresh warmed session per finding); accumulate ~10 recorded turns with rationales.
**Tier 2 — earned graduations:** refresh the dormant meta-loop skeleton against current names (gate: ≥3 recorded turns); Selector graduation — system proposes, human approves, agreement recorded (gate: agreement reliably high); selector-side memory schema (memory advances with its consumer).
**Tier 3 — the L3 threshold (two real builds):** the automation carrier (the one genuinely new infrastructure); orchestrator decision rules derived from recorded choices (gate: L2 data); stop-judgment v1 (signal placeholders + hard budget cap); the quality floor — per-spec manifests + advisory `tools/structural_check.sh` (already designed: `devdocs/inquiries/2026-06-09_21-47__discipline_specs_hidden_structural_abstraction/finding.md`).
**Tier 4 — the full whirl (L4):** multihead dispatch with per-head budgets; the Evaluator (the grown-new organ — needs its own design inquiry); pattern invocation in practice.
**Acceptance:** the explfine demo on fresh territory.

Minimum critical path: first memory artifact → turn-invariant → ~10 recorded turns → Selector graduation → carrier → stop-judgment v1 → explfine demo.

## Open edges

- **Stop-signals are placeholders** (`docs/canon/what_is_meaningful_traversal.md` — deliberately fuzzy); at L3+ they become load-bearing, so the substrate now has a consumer waiting.
- **The Evaluator** has no human-practice precedent to transfer; design needed when multihead becomes real.
- **The automation carrier** is named, not chosen — a process-layer decision gated on L2 calibration data.
