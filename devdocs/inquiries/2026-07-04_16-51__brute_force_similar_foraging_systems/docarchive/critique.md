## Phase 2-3 — Adversarial Evaluation + Verdicts

### Q4 (the grounding) — the sharpest — REFINE

**Prosecution (D3):** the convergent-arrival argument ("many independent systems arrive at the same architecture → it's forced by the problem") is undercut by its own list — ant-colony optimization, genetic algorithms, and simulated annealing were *deliberately copied* from ants, evolution, and annealing. Those are not independent re-derivations; counting them inflates the grounding.
**Defense + collision → REFINE R2:** the catch is correct and sharpens the claim. Scope the convergent-arrival grounding to the **genuinely independent** instances — biology (fungi, immune systems, vasculature, ants), physics (rivers, lightning, crystals), and society (markets, trails) that arose *without copying each other.* The **algorithmic** instances are explicitly *excluded from the convergence argument* — they are evidence the pattern is *copyable and useful* (engineers reach for it), which is a different, weaker point. With the algorithmic cases removed, the independent biological/physical/social re-derivations still carry the argument, and it is now honest. → fold into Q4.

### Q2 (the parsimony sort + sufficiency) — REFINE

**Prosecution (D2, D4):** "the constellation is sufficient, add nothing" conveniently protects the existing family; and it is internally inconsistent to call the immune system the "strongest bench item" (a genuinely distinct, project-faithful facet: abstract-space search) yet not add it.
**Defense:** sufficiency is argued via the parsimony test, not asserted — and non-sycophancy cuts the other way too (the inquiry credits the bench items with real facets rather than dismissing them). But the immune inconsistency is a real gap. **REFINE R1:** state *why* immune is benched despite being the strongest candidate — the existing scenes **already map their physical ground to the abstract idea-space** (the mycelium's soil *is* the thinking-space by the mapping), so the abstract-space facet is *covered-by-mapping, not absent.* Immune would make it *explicit* rather than *mapped* — a refinement the family could adopt, but not a missing facet — which is exactly what "strongest bench item, not yet needed" should mean. With that rationale stated, "strongest bench but not added" is consistent, not arbitrary. → fold into Q2.

### Q3 (the deep pattern + reframe) — REFINE (light)

**Prosecution (D6):** naming "decentralized reinforcement under uncertainty" is a truism — any competent observer states it; presenting it as a finding overclaims novelty.
**Defense:** the pattern's value is not novelty; it is *use* — it explains why most candidates duplicate and it sharpens the parsimony test. **REFINE R3 (light):** state explicitly that the pattern is a **known phenomenon** (self-organization, stigmergy — studied fields), not a discovery of this inquiry; the contribution is *naming the family's skeleton with it and deriving the membership test*, not finding the pattern. A one-clause honesty cut. → fold into Q3.

### The Emergent (design-space) — SURVIVE

**Defense:** the finding bounds it explicitly — candidate ideas, not recommendations; adoption is a separate inquiry; the examples are framed as questions ("should the harness have both?"). **SURVIVES.** The design-space reframe is the finding's most actionable contribution and is correctly scoped.

### Q1 + register (serves-the-user) — SURVIVE

**Defense:** the list *is* delivered (Q1); the honest sort is the value that keeps it from being a useless pile, and Q2 frames the sufficiency result as "a real service to the analogy hunt," not "your request was pointless." The brute-force ask produced the pattern, the grounding, and closure — it was productive. **SURVIVES.**
