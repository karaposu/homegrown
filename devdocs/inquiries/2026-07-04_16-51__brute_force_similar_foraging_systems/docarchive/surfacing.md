## Surfaced Items — the brute-force list (clustered)

### Cluster 1 — Biological collective foragers (MANY bodies, stigmergy)

- **1.1 Ant colonies** ★solid. Pheromone trails: ants reinforce productive paths by depositing attractant; unused trails evaporate. → **distinct-load candidate: a POSITIVE external reinforcement trail** (attract-toward-the-good-path), the sign-opposite of Physarum's avoidance trail. [HIGH pattern-fit]
- **1.2 Termites** ~partial. Stigmergic construction — build by responding to the structure so far. → distinct-load: stigmergy applied to *building*, not just pathing. [MED]
- **1.3 Honeybee nest-site selection** ★solid. Scouts advertise sites (waggle dance), recruitment builds, a quorum commits. → **distinct-load candidate: collective DECISION + COMMITMENT among discrete options** (the family's scenes forage but don't obviously *decide-and-commit*). [MED-HIGH]
- **1.4 Bird murmurations / fish schools** ★solid. Emergent coordination, no leader. → mostly DUPLICATE (no-hero + emergent), weak on forage/reinforce. [LOW — decentralization only]
- **1.5 Dictyostelium (cellular slime mold)** ~partial. Independent cells aggregate under starvation into a body. → distinct-load: many-independent-cells-becoming-one-body (a transition the family lacks), but niche. [LOW-MED]

### Cluster 2 — Biological growth/foraging networks (ONE body, reinforce-and-branch)

- **2.1 Mycelium** — already dived (the accumulator / persistent-record scene). [reference]
- **2.2 Physarum** — already dived (the re-decider / external-memory scene). [reference]
- **2.3 Plant root systems** ★solid. Forage water/nutrients; branch and thicken toward good patches. → DUPLICATE of the mycelium (a slower, rooted forager). [LOW-MED — duplicate]
- **2.4 Vascular growth / angiogenesis** ★solid. Vessels grow toward demanding (hypoxic) tissue, guided by chemical signal; prune low-flow vessels. → distinct-load: growth PULLED BY DEMAND (the resource *signals* for supply), vs the forager searching blind. [MED]
- **2.5 Neurons — axon guidance + synaptic pruning + Hebbian** ★solid. Growth cones navigate to targets; synapses used-are-strengthened, unused-are-pruned; "fire together, wire together." → **distinct-load candidate: the record becomes CAPABILITY** (a learned function/weights), not just a map or trail. [HIGH]
- **2.6 Coral / colonial growth** ~partial. Accretive branching. → DUPLICATE of the mycelium's build-and-keep. [LOW]

### Cluster 3 — Biological abstract-space searchers (search a NON-physical space)

- **3.1 Immune system — clonal selection + affinity maturation** ★solid. The repertoire searches *antigen space*; high-affinity clones are reinforced and hypermutate toward better fit. → **distinct-load candidate: the search space is ABSTRACT** (a space of possible antibodies), not physical ground — the closest instance to searching a space of *ideas*. [HIGH]
- **3.2 Evolution / natural selection** ★solid. A population searches fitness landscape; selection reinforces the fit, variation explores. → **distinct-load candidate: POPULATION-parallel search + no individual persists** (variation+selection over generations). [HIGH]

### Cluster 4 — Physical / non-living (same reinforce-the-flow dynamic, NO life)

- **4.1 River / drainage networks** ★solid. Water carves channels; deeper channels carry more water (positive feedback) → dendritic network. → **distinct-load candidate: SUBSTRATE-INDEPENDENCE** — the reinforce-the-flow pattern with no organism at all. [HIGH for grounding]
- **4.2 Lightning leaders** ★solid. Branching stepped search for the conductive path; the struck path carries the discharge. → distinct-load: explore-many-branches-then-one-commits (fast). [MED]
- **4.3 Diffusion-limited aggregation / dendritic crystal growth (frost, snowflakes)** ★solid. Branching growth by where particles happen to stick. → DUPLICATE of the branching-network shape (physical). [LOW-MED]
- **4.4 Crack / fracture propagation** ~partial. Follows the path of least resistance. → DUPLICATE (physical branching). [LOW]

### Cluster 5 — Algorithmic (the engineered / mathematical cousins)

- **5.1 Reinforcement learning (explore/exploit)** ★solid. The direct algorithmic naming of the whole dynamic. → distinct-load: the *formal* statement of explore-exploit; not a scene but the mechanism named. [reference-frame]
- **5.2 Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS)** ★solid. Explore/exploit over a tree, expand promising nodes, back up values, prune. → **distinct-load candidate: the ALGORITHMIC MIRROR of the project's OWN move-layer** (explore/exploit/backtrack + value backup) — arguably a *description* of the traverse, not an analogy to it. [HIGH — but a mirror, not a sibling]
- **5.3 Ant colony optimization / genetic algorithms / simulated annealing / particle swarm** ★solid. Engineered instances of stigmergy / population-search / explore-then-cool. → DUPLICATE (the algorithmic re-implementations of clusters 1–3). [LOW-MED as a group]
- **5.4 Preferential attachment (Barabási–Albert)** ★solid. "Rich get richer" network growth — nodes with more links attract more. → distinct-load: the pure MATH of reinforce-the-already-strong (the reinforcement dynamic in the abstract). [MED]
- **5.5 Gradient descent / hill climbing** ★solid. Local reinforce-toward-the-gradient. → DUPLICATE (local exploit, no global search). [LOW]

### Cluster 6 — Socioeconomic / human (the dynamic at social scale)

- **6.1 Market economies / capital allocation** ★solid. Capital flows to returns; funding rounds reinforce winners, starve losers. → **distinct-load candidate: a FUNGIBLE, transferable shared budget** reallocated to winners — the closest instance to the allocation dimension's *budget*. [MED-HIGH]
- **6.2 Desire paths / trail formation** ★solid. Pedestrians reinforce shortcuts by walking them — human stigmergy. → DUPLICATE of ant pheromone (positive external trail). [LOW-MED]
- **6.3 Science / citation dynamics** ★solid. Citations preferentially attach; research effort flows to hot areas. → OVERLAPS humanity (the citation graph) — already in the family. [LOW — already covered]
- **6.4 Internet packet routing / transport-network growth** ~partial. Adaptive routing reinforces good routes. → DUPLICATE (engineered stigmergy). [LOW]

## The deep pattern (surfaced as the candidate payoff)

Across all six clusters, one mechanism recurs: **decentralized reinforcement under uncertainty** — search blindly, reinforce what pays, prune what doesn't, and let a network/trace accumulate. It has several established names depending on the medium: **stigmergy** (when coordination is via an external trace — ants, termites, desire paths), **preferential attachment** (network growth — citations, the web), **adaptive/reinforcement networks** (rivers, vasculature, mycelium), and **explore-exploit** (the algorithmic framing). → **candidate emergent for downstream: the "analogy" was never about fungi — the family has been collecting INSTANCES of one substrate-independent pattern, and the thinking-space traversal architecture is itself another instance.**
