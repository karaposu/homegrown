## SV2 — The parsimony test applied: most duplicate; no clear new scene is needed now

The inherited parsimony discipline (16-25): a scene enters the family only if it makes vivid a *distinct, nameable facet* no existing scene does. Run the ~9 candidates through it, non-sycophantically.

- **Ant colony (positive external trail)** — many independent bodies coordinating via an external attractant trace. This is *humanity's* model (independent bodies + external shared trace), exactly as 16-25 assigned it. Its one fresh note — a *positive* "go here" trail vs Physarum's *negative* "avoid here" trail — is a marginal facet, not a scene. **DUPLICATE (humanity-side), marginal note.**
- **Honeybee (decision + commitment)** — the explore → quorum → *commit* arc. A real facet the foraging scenes underplay (they search; they don't dramatize converging on one answer and acting). **BENCH — a genuine facet, but not currently needed (CONCLUDE already models the commit).**
- **Angiogenesis (demand-pulled growth)** — the resource *signals* for supply. This actually *breaks* the membership test (it is guided, not blind-foraging-under-uncertainty). **Near-EXCLUDED — a different pattern (demand-pull).**
- **Neurons (record-becomes-capability)** — the record is a learned function, not a map. A genuinely distinct facet — but one the *project may not have* (the harness reads records; it does not learn weights). **BENCH — distinct but of uncertain applicability.**
- **Immune system (abstract-space search)** — searches a non-physical possibility space (antibody space) by reinforce-the-high-affinity. **BENCH — the strongest bench item (see SV4/SV5); but the existing scenes already *map* their physical ground to the abstract idea-space, so it is not needed as a new scene now.**
- **Evolution (population-parallel + no-persistence)** — many candidates in parallel, selection culls, only the lineage persists. Overlaps humanity (many heads) + the supersedes-graph. **BENCH — a real facet (population/selection), partly covered.**
- **Rivers (substrate-independence)** — the pattern with no organism at all. Not a scene (a mindless, weak forager) but a **grounding argument** (SV5). **CONTRIBUTES TO GROUNDING, not a scene.**
- **MCTS (algorithmic mirror)** — explore/exploit/backtrack + value-backup: a near-*description* of the project's own move-layer, not an analogy to it. **MIRROR, not a sibling** (a precision-frame, useful, but not a family member).
- **Markets (fungible budget)** — a transferable, priced shared budget reallocated to winners. Sharpens the allocation dimension's pool with *fungibility*. **BENCH — a real facet (fungible budget), modestly distinct.**

**Result (non-sycophantic):** the parsimony test yields **no candidate that must be added now.** Most duplicate; a handful contribute a distinct facet but sit on a **bench** (add only if the family later needs that facet); one (rivers) feeds grounding; one (MCTS) is a mirror not a sibling. **The constellation (mycelium + Physarum + humanity, over the fungus move-layer) is confirmed sufficient.** The brute-force list's value is *not* a pile of new analogies — it is the confirmation that the existing family already covers the needed facets, plus the two payoffs below.

## SV3 — The deep pattern committed (the family's skeleton)

Across all six clusters — fungi, ants, immune systems, rivers, markets, algorithms — one mechanism recurs: **decentralized reinforcement under uncertainty.** Search blindly (no controller, no map); reinforce what pays and prune what doesn't; let a network or trace accumulate as you go. It wears different names by medium — *stigmergy* (coordination via an external trace), *preferential attachment* (network growth), *adaptive/reinforcement networks* (rivers, vasculature, mycelium), *explore–exploit* (the algorithmic framing) — but it is one pattern.

This is the family's **skeleton**: the thing every scene in the constellation shares. The mycelium, Physarum, and humanity are not three separate ideas that happen to rhyme — they are three *instances* of this one pattern, each dressing it in a different body.

## SV4 — The reframe adjudicated: the pattern is the skeleton, not a replacement for the scenes

The brute-force list tempts a reframe: *"the analogy was never about fungi — it is this abstract pattern, and the organisms are disposable instances; so state the pattern and drop the scenes."*

Steelman it, then hold the Clean-Resolution-Trap guard. The true part is real and committed (SV3): the pattern is substrate-independent and the scenes are its instances. But the conclusion "drop the scenes" **fails**, on the ground the whole prior chain established: an analogy's value is a *vivid, structural-fit scene*, not an abstraction. The abstract pattern is correct but inert — it does not teach, and it does not surface the specific sub-mechanics the scenes do (the mycelium's persistent body, Physarum's external memory, humanity's records-bridging-death). The skeleton needs flesh to be graspable.

So the reframe is **additive, not dissolving.** It gives the family two things it lacked: a **name for the skeleton** (what all the scenes share, and why so many candidates fit — they are all instances), and a **precise membership test** for the parsimony discipline. The parsimony test, restated with the pattern in hand: *a candidate earns a place only if it makes vivid a facet of the pattern no existing scene does.* That is exactly why most of the brute-force list duplicates (same skeleton, different skin) and why the bench items are held, not added.

Steelman the opposite over-correction — *"add all nine distinct-load candidates."* Rejected by the same test and by anti-proliferation: a facet being *nameable* is not a facet being *needed*. Eight scenes would bloat the family past use; the discipline is to add a scene only when the family actually needs that facet made vivid.

## SV5 — The grounding, strengthened (and scoped)

The pattern's ubiquity is itself a **grounding** — and a broader one than the family had. The humanity analogy grounded the *architecture* (a civilization actually produces understanding); Physarum grounded the *mechanism* at lab scale (a brainless network optimizes). The brute-force list grounds the **pattern**: decentralized-reinforcement-under-uncertainty appears independently in biology (fungi, ants, immune systems, vasculature), in physics with no life at all (rivers, lightning, crystal growth), in computation (RL, MCTS, evolutionary algorithms), and in society (markets, desire paths, citations). A pattern that re-derives itself that many times, across that many substrates, is not a biological accident — it is something like a law of systems that search under uncertainty with feedback.

Scope it honestly (non-sycophancy): this grounds the *pattern's reality and robustness*, not the *project's guarantee* — that the project instantiates the pattern well enough to produce meaningful traversal is still the open question the monitoring/spec work addresses. And the two most useful "abstract-space" instances — the immune system (searching antibody space) and evolution (searching fitness landscape) — matter here because they show the pattern is not merely *physical-spatial*: it runs in genuinely abstract possibility-spaces, which is where thinking-space traversal lives. That is the strongest reason the immune system leads the bench (SV2): if the family ever wants a scene that searches an abstract space *explicitly*, rather than mapping physical ground to it, the immune system is the first to promote.
