## Phase 3 — Ambiguity Collapse

#### Ambiguity 1: Does "venture = a SUSTRALL run" require the ACHIEVED SUSTRALL, or a run of the SUSTRALL-shaped system at any tier?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** ventures strictly presuppose SUSTRALL-achieved; the sample is only a pre-venture demonstration, and calling it "one venture example" is loose speech to be corrected in the rephrase.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** (1) It renders the user's stated aim self-contradictory: "our next breakthrough is to reach venture" would then require SUSTRALL's gates already passed — but those gates are the LATER breakthroughs; nothing could ever be the first venture. (2) Canon's own feasibility verdict describes the Level-0 system as the SAME system with human organs ("the whirl turns with you as its selector, dispatcher, memory, and refiner") — the system-sense exists at every tier; only autonomy grades. (3) The SUSTRALL definition's "achieved when" clause conditions the STATE-sense, not the system-sense; runs are runs of systems. The counter conflates the dual's two senses — the exact equivocation R1 warns against.

**Confidence:** HIGH.

**Resolution:** **Venture is tier-agnostic** — one bounded, specific-purpose run of the SUSTRALL-shaped system (hands + eyes + will + memory), at whatever tier; **the tier grades WHO carries the five cargos during it** (Level 0: the human carries all five — the sample; the climb = moving cargos onto the system across a whole venture).

**What is now fixed:** the sample IS a venture-token (Level-0, human-powered); "reach venture" means a cargo-shift statement, not a first-existence statement — precisely why gate-terms fit it.
**What is no longer allowed:** venture-as-post-achievement-only; equivocating system/state in the ladder.
**What now depends on this choice:** the position statement's shape; the exemplar claim; the three reach-venture bars' very intelligibility.
**What changed in the model:** "reach venture" sharpened from "attain a thing" to "run one whole venture with named cargos on the system's side."
