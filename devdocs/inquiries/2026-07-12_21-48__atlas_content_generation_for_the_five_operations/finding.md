## Question

The Venture Atlas (the project's 3D map of the inquiry record, at `docs/visualisation/app/`) now has the Time-Road home view and the route layer — but the user's felt problem persists at a deeper layer: *"I don't know what I will use this for… I only am seeing the nodes… I don't have any information regarding the holisticity of the project."* His analogy: a sculptor working a huge statue who can scroll photos of individual parts but can never see the whole figure — nor judge which parts are finished and which were left rough. He named five things he actually does with this project — **make judgments, put seeds, give guidance, make decisions, and hold holistic + individual understanding** — and asked: taking these five as the base, **what can the inquiry folders GENERATE** (by aggregation — explicitly *not* by inventing a concept taxonomy, which he distrusts) **to feed a visualization that serves them?** He also floated one interaction idea (buttons on a note's available routes: "this route will be expanded, or built, or another dive") to be examined.

## Finding

The project's record is ~230 timestamped inquiry folders (the atlas's domain; another ~256 findings sit in deliberately excluded archive folders). Two builds shipped earlier today — the Time-Road and the route layer — and this inquiry is the layer beneath them: not *how to draw*, but **what the record can honestly feed the drawing**, derived from what its operator actually does. It should be read as the prior foreseer finding's **data-layer completion**: that finding mapped the seat's jobs to instrument surfaces; this one maps the seat's acts to data feeds.

### 1. The base — jobs, acts, tests

Three layers, each answering a different question. **Cargos** (canon's five: *"seeing, selecting, dispatching, remembering, stop-judging"*) are the seat's JOBS — why instruments exist. **The user's five operations are the seat's ACTS** — what the foreseer does in the gap canon says all between-inquiry work lives in. **Tasks** are the acceptance tests — how a build proves it serves them.

| Operation | Rides on cargos | The act, concretely |
|---|---|---|
| make judgments | stop-judging + trust-grading | read a region's evidence; judge solid / needs-work / done |
| make decisions | selecting + dispatching | choose what's next; commit |
| give guidance | — (the source face) | shape HOW the next run goes — constraints, corrections, watch-fors |
| put seeds | — (the source face; canon's L3 verb) | write a purpose into the next run |
| holistic + individual understanding | seeing + remembering | the reads that feed the other four |

Every cargo is ridden; the two operations with no cargo row — seeds and guidance — land exactly on the **source face** the prior finding's operator-side table was known to under-carry (the seat is also the project's source). That asymmetry is why this is an extension, not a rename. One split matters: "put seeds" here means **venture-seeding** (canon's verb — writing the next run's purpose), which the map can stage; harvest-seed germination records keep their own home (the seed index), per the standing honesty table.

### 3. The sculptor's answer — the region ledger

The statue's *intended* shape is canon's (the gated knowledge lens's job, unchanged). The statue's *built* shape is derivable now: **the REGION LEDGER** — per venture/chain: members, age-span, last-worked, chain-depth, standing mix, promotion marks, DEFERRED load, open-route ratio, seeds count. Two homes ship: the venture tile (each settlement's card gains its ledger row) and a corpus-wide sortable flyout — sort by open-ratio and the "done at surface level" regions surface themselves; sort by promotion and the hardened ones do. Map-level badges at mid zoom are deferred (glyph clutter — pennants shipped today; revival: living with them shows region cues are missed). Honesty by construction: counts and marks only, "—" where data is absent (never interpolated), **no composite scores** — the ledger is exactly canon's "countable record" over which the foreseer's judged estimate operates.

### 4. The buttons — the composing line

The user's future — *"instead of prompting… click buttons regarding available routes"* — was adjudicated against canon's decision-gap text, which supersedes the prior boundary's UX-flavored ground. The line: **composing serves the gap; firing deletes it.**

- **B1 — compose-from-route (SURVIVES):** every route row gets a control; the clipboard receives a composed `/traverse` command carrying the route's own direction and verb. The human pastes and fires.
- **B2 — compose-preview (SURVIVES):** an editable preview before copy — the foreseer shapes the seed text, which is "give guidance" made concrete.
- **B3 — batch composer (DEFERRED):** multi-route plans graze the system's future sequencing job; revives only if the foreseer demonstrably queues several dives a day by hand.
- **B4 — a launch button (KILLED):** *"auto-fired next turns would delete the steering."*

And the formal correction: the prior finding justified tee-up-only by "zero gain over copy-paste." B1/B2 show real gain — the justification was wrong even though the boundary was right. The corrected ground: **the line is where the act lives, not how much typing it saves.**

### 5. Concepts without the machinery — and the rule, clarified

Free-floating concept extraction stays exactly as gated (fabrication surface; unsolved cross-folder identity resolution). But the want behind it — *which concepts exist, where effort went, what's untouched* — is servable twice over without it:

- **The canon-area table (probe-confirmed rich):** findings already cite `docs/canon/` 308 times across 73 findings, touching 31 of the 38 canon docs. Parse those references → per-canon-doc counts → the work-vs-intention read, using the project's OWN concept list, whose identity problem the project already solved by writing canon.
- **The identities index (appetite-gated, last):** the 581 hand-written concept names in the route indexes as a browsable list (click = fly to the inquiry) — authored names only, never map nodes.

This stressed the standing atlas-domain rule, which came out sharper: **the atlas may RENDER what lives in the inquiry folders; it may READ other homes only to count and link references (names and counts, never content); rendering another home's CONTENT requires that home's own lens and gate.** Every prior verdict was re-checked under this wording; none changed.

After ① and ②, every one of the five operations has at least one folder-fed instrument — and because the feeds are data (render-independent), the same layer is the future Level-3 supervision surface's data, pre-built.
