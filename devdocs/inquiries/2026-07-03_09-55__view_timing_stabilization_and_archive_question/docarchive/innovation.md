## Phase 2 — Generate (principal content + mandated inversions)

### (a) The archive answer (build-up style, finding-ready)

> A view needs the past to stay where it is — every excerpt points at a finding's path. So the question "after the view, do we archive the past inquiries?" is really two questions fused. **First: does archiving lose information here?** Measured answer: no — this house's archive (`devdocs/inquiries/_archive/`, 265 inquiries strong) sits INSIDE the searched tree; archived findings still come back from every grep, every digest, every discovery run. Archiving here is shelving within the library, not removal from it. Your worry was the right instinct pointed at the wrong risk: content survives a move — **links don't.** **Second: should VIEW-GENERATION do the moving?** No, on three independent grounds: (i) the 265 precedents were moved era-bulk when a whole period turned — the practiced model is a deliberate storage act, never a per-topic side effect; (ii) a move breaks paths — the stabilization view's own excerpt-pointers would be wrong at birth, and the supersession-tagging would silently stop matching moved findings; (iii) an inquiry may only move when ALL its concepts are done — and inquiries entangle ~8–10 concepts each (measured), so deciding "all done" per inquiry is exactly the per-thing bookkeeping you refused two days ago. **The resolution: decoupled.** Views never move, rename, or archive anything — generation is read-only. Era-bulk archiving stays available as its own deliberate act, when a period turns.

### (b) The ceremony (final form) + two inversions

**Inversion (mandated: "the two-step ceremony is heavy — most topics deserve a lightweight close"):** correct — the full traverse is earned, not default. **Resolution: two tiers.**

- **Light close (the default):** regenerate the concept-view; its header carries the declaration — `**Stabilized:** by user declaration, 2026-07-03`. One step, minutes. The topic is closed-as-of-now; reopening costs nothing (new findings simply arrive; a newer view supersedes).
- **Full consolidation (for topics that earned it):** assembly → **traverse over the assembly** → the **consolidation finding** (history; carrying `refines:`/`supersedes:` over what it consolidates) → regenerate the view, now headed by the consolidation and auto-tagging the superseded. Earned by: contested history (many superseded findings), a live decision resting on the topic, or the user wanting the synthesis itself.
- **The dividend (full tier):** the consolidation's frontmatter makes every FUTURE view of the concept honest automatically — the ceremony feeds the machinery.

**Inversion (mandated: "'consolidate' collides with routelister's CONSOLIDATE verb"):** correct — the engagement vocabulary owns that word. **Resolution: the invocation uses the user's own word:**
> **"stabilize \<concept\>"** → the light close. **"stabilize \<concept\> with consolidation"** → the full tier.
No vocabulary collision (stabilization is not an engagement verb); the word already means exactly this in the user's usage.

### (c) The doctrine lines (verbatim-ready for the protocol's build) + inversion

**Inversion (mandated: "five additions is scope-creep on a one-page protocol"):** sized honestly — the five collapse into ~15 lines across two small sections plus two clauses:

1. *(the never-moves clause — constitution)* **"Generation is read-only over `devdocs/inquiries/`: no output of this protocol moves, renames, or archives any inquiry. Archiving, if ever, is a separate deliberate storage act (era-bulk, as practiced) — never a side effect of views."**
2. *(the stabilization section — ~8 lines)* **"The canonical occasion: when the user declares a concept stabilized. Light close (default): regenerate the view; header carries `Stabilized: by user declaration, <date>`. Full consolidation (earned): assembly → traverse over it → the consolidation finding (with supersession frontmatter) → regenerate. Invocations: `stabilize <concept>` / `stabilize <concept> with consolidation`. On-demand generation is unchanged — stabilization is an occasion, not a gate."**
3. *(the one-concept clause — inputs)* **"One concept per view. Entangled inquiries appear in many concepts' views — views point, they never own."**
4. *(the robustness clause — the walk's step)* **"Match supersession targets by path OR basename (folders keep their names when moved; the walk must survive era-bulk archiving)."**

All four load-bearing; the canonical-occasion sentence rides inside line 2.

### (d) The growth answer (the honest reframe, with the felt sentence) + inversion

**Inversion (mandated: "the honest answer will read as dismissing the tidiness feeling"):** the reframe must make views-as-entry FELT. **The sentence:**

Around it, the honest facts: nothing bounds the live folder continuously and nothing needs to (grep, the digest, and listing are count-insensitive — measured); the practiced bound (era-bulk when a period turns) stays available for the day the current era closes.
