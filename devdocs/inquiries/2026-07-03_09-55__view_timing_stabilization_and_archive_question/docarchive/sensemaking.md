The kernel that survives: the unbounded-growth concern — answered honestly at A3, not by coupling.

#### Ambiguity A2 (guard b): At stabilization — a finding, a view, or a new artifact kind?

No new artifact kind; each artifact in its home; the ceremony strengthens every FUTURE view of the concept (the strategic dividend).
