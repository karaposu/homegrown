### 3. The strongest counter, tested and rejected

The most pointed objection to the FULL OVERTURN verdict is: *"specific overrides general — routeman's spec is more specific than canon; specific wins."*

This counter fails on two structural grounds.

First, the authority chain analysis: discipline specs are IMPLEMENTATIONS of canon principles. Canon describes what each discipline IS as a thinking-discipline (the project-wide commitment); specs describe how each discipline's identity is operationalized (the discipline-specific implementation). The authority flows canon → spec, not spec → canon. Reading the spec as overriding canon inverts the authority chain without justification.

Second, the specific-overrides-general principle applies when (a) the specific document EXPLICITLY amends the general AND (b) the specific is at higher authority. Neither holds here. The routeman spec does NOT explicitly say "routeman is uniquely exempt from the standalone + domain-agnostic principle." Inferring such an exemption from spec text requires the loop-compatibility-biased reading itself — circular.

Furthermore, the spec is INTERNALLY CONSISTENT with canon when read correctly. §1.1 verb-meaning is generic; §1.2 + §1.5 are loop-context (parallel to critique line 41); §1.4 "Includes" is inclusive; §2.2 taxonomy is substrate-generic; §3.5 supports progressive refinement. When a reading produces conflict with canon and an alternative reading produces no conflict, the no-conflict reading is structurally preferred.
