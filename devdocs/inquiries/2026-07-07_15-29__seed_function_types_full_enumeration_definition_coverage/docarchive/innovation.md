## Q2 [★ CENTER, core-3×] — Firming the count + the coverage proof

### Mechanism 1 — Combination (Generator): the derivation

Combine two dimensions of "how a seed is news about a load-bearing thing X":
- **Presence:** is X absent or present?
- **Confidence-direction** (for a present X): does the seed move your confidence in a load-bearing attribute of X up or down?

The product yields exactly the populated regions:

| Region | = | Type | Action verb |
|---|---|---|---|
| X absent → you gain it | improvement / novelty-door | **Inspiration** | **ADD** (build the absent) |
| X present, confidence UP | regression-clears / absence-of-failure | **Affirmation** | **CONFIRM** (close/keep) |
| X present, confidence DOWN | regression-fires / "things that are broken" | **Provocation** | **CHALLENGE** (open/doubt) |

The triad in plain question-terms: inspiration **answers a new question**; affirmation **closes an open question**; provocation **re-opens a closed question**. (The absent-branch does not split by confidence — an absent thing has no soundness-confidence yet — so the space is three populated cells, not four.)

### Mechanism 2 — Inversion (Framer): the count, REQUIRED piece-level

**Inversion (a) — invert "THREE" → "TWO (provocation folds into inspiration)":** claim — "learning X is flawed" is just learning something new (the flaw), so it's inspiration.
- *Re-defeat:* the provocation-seed's own content is confidence-DOWN about a PRESENT thing (X you have may be flawed) — not the gaining of an absent fix. The fix is a later, separate object the provocation *triggers* (the lifecycle-link — collapsing the two is the seed↔trigger stage-error the 14-26 finding warned against). And the action is opposite: provocation OPENS (re-opens a settled question); affirmation CLOSES; inspiration BUILDS. Defeated.

**Inversion (b) — invert "THREE" → "FOUR+ (another type exists)":** handled by Absence Recognition below.

**Depth-check (invert again → system-level):** first inversion is component-level ("provocation is/isn't its own cell"). Invert to the system: *what individuates a function-type at all?* → **a type is an action-class** (a seed's function-type IS the action it routes). Then the count is not a free parameter — it is exactly **the number of distinct actions a seed can route**. Three distinct actions (ADD / CONFIRM / CHALLENGE) ⇒ three types. This is the system-level statement: the taxonomy is **action-individuated**, so coverage = every distinct action has a type and every type has a distinct action.
- *Multi-axis check:* **existence-axis** (could the count be ZERO?) — no; the gate guarantees every seed is news, so ≥1 action always fires. **Identity-axis** (what IS a type?) — an action-class (confirmed above). Both axes agree: three.

### Mechanism 3 — Absence Recognition (Generator): the honest fourth-type probe (coverage-both-ways)

*Patch-level — is there a gate-passing seed that fits none of the three?* *Redesign-level — if the set were built from scratch, would it have a fourth?* Test five candidates; each must either FOLD (into one of the three by its action) or DECOMPOSE (into a combination):

| Candidate fourth | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Re-prioritization** ("X matters more/less than you thought") | FOLDS | confidence up/down on X's *importance*-attribute → affirmation (CONFIRM the raised weight) / provocation (CHALLENGE the assumed weight). Once "confidence" ranges over any load-bearing attribute, not just soundness, this is not new. |
| **Connection-revealing** ("X connects to Y") | FOLDS | you GAIN an absent thing (the connection) → inspiration (ADD). |
| **Provocation-of-a-PLAN** ("a paper kills a thing you were about to build") | FOLDS | the plan/intention is a *present* thing you hold → confidence-DOWN on it → provocation (CHALLENGE). |
| **Redirection** ("here's a better approach than your current one") | DECOMPOSES | = provocation (your current approach may be worse — CHALLENGE) + inspiration (the better approach — ADD). A compound of two types, not a new atom. |
| **Confirmation-of-absence** ("good thing you didn't build X") | FOLDS | confidence-UP on a decision you hold (the choice not-to-build) → affirmation (CONFIRM). |

**Result: no fourth ATOMIC type survives.** Every candidate folds into ADD/CONFIRM/CHALLENGE or decomposes into a combination. This active-probe-that-finds-nothing is the coverage-both-ways rigor the user's "be extremely careful" asked for — it *strengthens* the joint-exhaustiveness claim (the three aren't just sufficient for the cases we thought of; a deliberate search for a fourth came back empty).

### Mechanism 4 — Constraint Manipulation (Framer): both directions

- **ADD** the constraint "every type must route a distinct action" (the decision-vs-label test as a hard rule) → the count collapses to exactly the distinct-action count = **three**; the fourth candidates die because none adds a fourth distinct action.
- **REMOVE** the constraint "confidence is about soundness only" → generalize to "confidence about any load-bearing attribute" → re-prioritization (importance-attribute) distributes into affirmation/provocation rather than demanding a fourth type. Removing the over-narrow constraint *prevents* proliferation.

### Q2 self-check (both-ways, per the invocation)

Three is **not caving-to-2**: it rests on the 14-26 finding's OWN internal gap (it grounded the confidence-door in regression = fire-centric canon, but typed only affirmation = the clear outcome) — structural, file-grounded, independent of the user's ask. Three is **not proliferation**: the decision-vs-label test *forces* exactly three distinct actions, and a deliberate fourth-probe came back empty. Both directions defeated; coverage holds by construction.

## Q3 [the THIRD TYPE] — Developing provocation

### Mechanism 5 — Domain Transfer (Generator): what "OPEN" is, from two source domains

- **Native (software testing):** a test that FIRES (fails) is an **alarm that opens an investigation** — it is not the same event as a passing test (green = affirmation) nor as writing new code (build = inspiration). The failing test says "look here," not "here's the fix." The harness's own Primitive RC works exactly this way: it catches "things that are broken" (`evolving_quality_assetment_component.md:28`) — a *detection*, separate from the repair.
- **Deliberately-different (medical screening):** a positive screening result **opens a workup**. It is not the diagnosis, not the treatment, and not the all-clear. It routes you to investigate.

Both domains confirm: **OPEN is a distinct action** — an alarm/flag that routes to investigation, sitting *upstream* of any fix.

**Concretely in the harness,** a provocation-seed produces a **"re-examine X" item** — a doubt-driven watch-entry (structurally like a NASCENT seed's maturation-trigger, but triggered by *lowered confidence* rather than by *unrealized potential*).

### Mechanism 6 — Inversion (Framer): provocation's action, REQUIRED piece-level (the load-bearing sub-claim, prosecuted hardest)

**Invert "provocation's OPEN is a distinct action" → "OPEN folds into inspiration's REFINE":** claim — investigating a flaw is just refining X.
- *Re-defeat:* **refine acts on a KNOWN improvement** — you already know the change to make. **OPEN is upstream** — it does not yet know there is a problem, let alone the fix; it can resolve *three* ways after investigation: to a **refine** (flaw confirmed + fix found), to an **affirmation** (investigated → X is fine after all), or to a **build** (X must be replaced). An action that precedes and can terminate in refine/affirm/build is not identical to refine. Canon anchor: the RC *firing* is an alarm that opens investigation, not a fix — the harness's own architecture separates detect-the-break from repair-it.
- *Honest flag:* this OPEN-vs-refine boundary is the closest of the three action-pairs; it survives here, but Critique should prosecute it once more (carried forward).

### Mechanism 7 — Lens Shifting (Framer): the un-exemplified caveat

- Under the **current-harvest lens** (what's actually been harvested): provocation looks empty — 0 provocation-seeds vs 2 inspiration (17/19) + 1 affirmation (20) → it looks weak/speculative.
- Under the **coverage lens** (the space the definition admits, not the current sample): provocation is structurally real regardless of population. **Affirmation was ALSO empty until paper 20** — and the cost of the *old* gate lacking the affirmation-cell was that paper 20's mirror was un-scorable. An un-typed provocation-seed would be un-scorable the same way.

The lens-shift resolves the caveat's status: **un-exemplified is a population-note, not an existence-defeater.** Keep it PROMINENT and honest (0 harvested; monitor-for-population), but it does not weaken the structural claim.

### Provocation — candidate names (left open, trade-offs noted)

| Name | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| **provocation** | vivid, memorable | connotes annoyance / rhetorical provocation |
| **destabilization** | accurate (lowers confidence in a settled thing) | heavy, clinical |
| **regression-seed** | maps directly to the canon (regression-fires) | "regression" is loaded (software-specific; also the harness's own term for something else) |
| **challenge-seed** | plain, matches the CHALLENGE action verb | slightly generic |
| **doubt-seed** | plain, captures confidence-down | may undersell (doubt ≠ a decision-relevant finding) |

Recommendation deferred to the finding/user — the *structure* is fixed regardless of the label (H4/H9). Working name: **provocation**.
