### Region B — the current TWO poles and their gate-mapping · tag: core · confidence: HIGH

- **B1** — inspiration = novelty-door = **news-about-the-absent** = (by the finding's analogy) improvement-detection. `core`
- **B2** — affirmation = confidence-door = **news-about-the-present** = (by the finding's analogy) regression-detection. `core`

### Region C — THE CANDIDATE GAP: the confidence-door's DIRECTIONALITY · tag: core · confidence: HIGH

- **C1** — affirmation is **confidence-UP**: you were unsure X is sound; now you're surer it is ("the typed design is sound — don't rebuild it"). `core`
- **C2** — But a seed can drive **confidence-DOWN**: you trusted X; a source makes you see X may be **flawed** ("systems like yours fail at Z"). Is it covered by the current two? `core`
- **C3** — **The asymmetry that makes this pressing:** if confidence-UP is its own type (affirmation) but confidence-DOWN has no home, the taxonomy is lop-sided. The clean cuts look like either **1** (everything is just "news," no sub-types) or **3** (inspiration / affirmation / provocation) — which would make the finding's **2** a possibly-unstable middle. Surface, do NOT decide. `core`
- **C4** — Two live dispositions for provocation, both kept open for Sensemaking:
  - **(a) it's a THIRD type** — confidence-down is a distinct epistemic-effect with a distinct action (open-a-problem).
  - **(b) it FOLDS into inspiration** — "learning X is flawed" = learning a new absent thing (the awareness-of-the-problem / the needed fix), so it's novelty wearing a negative sign. `core`

### Region D — the REGRESSION-ASYMMETRY (file-grounded crux) · tag: core · confidence: HIGH

- **D1** — Canon read fresh: regression-detection catches **"Things that are *broken*"** (`evolving_quality_assetment_component.md:28` — "Format violations, missing sections, removed safeguards, internal contradictions"). Regression-detection's **characteristic output is a FIRE** — it *finds a break*. `core`
- **D2** — So the finding's map "affirmation = regression-detection" is **imprecise**: affirmation is the **CLEAR** outcome (regression did NOT fire → "absence-of-failure signals are first-class," `north_star:61`). The **FIRE** outcome (regression *detected* → something IS broken) maps to **provocation**, not affirmation. Regression-detection is thus a **two-outcome door**: fires → provocation (confidence-down), clears → affirmation (confidence-up). `core`
- **D3** — **The striking consequence:** the harness makes regression-detection **PRIMARY**, and a detector's primary job is to **fire** (catch the break). So the provocation-type may be the *more central* half of the regression-door — the finding centered affirmation (the clear/quiet outcome) and **missed the fire outcome entirely**. This is the strongest file-grounded evidence FOR a third type. `core`
- **D4** — **Counter, kept live (do not let D3 run away):** the quality architecture is about the system detecting regressions in **its own output**; the harvest is about what a **paper** delivers. The mapping is an *analogy* the finding used to ground the gate — it grounds the *structure*, but "a paper breaks my design" may be rarer than "a paper reveals a new problem" (which is novelty → inspiration). Whether provocation is *populated* in the harvest (are there real provocation-seeds?) is a separate question from whether it's *structurally* forced. Both surface; Sensemaking adjudicates. `sub`
