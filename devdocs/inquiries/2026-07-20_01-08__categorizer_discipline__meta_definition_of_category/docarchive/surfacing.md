### Region C — the candidate structure (possibility tail; provenance = A-instances + B-theory + his words)

- **C1 (core) — the CATEGORY meta-definition candidate:** a category = **a named region in a chosen axis-space, purpose-relative, with a membership judgment attached.** Three constitutive parts: the AXIS chosen · the BOUNDARY drawn (test or prototype) · the NAME that makes it usable. **IS NOT:** a SCORE (types-never-scores) · a NATURAL FACT found lying around (purpose-relative by theorem [B6] and by owned practice [A1]) · a BARE LABEL (a name without a membership judgment decorates, it doesn't categorize — A8 the live instance) · a PILE (a category that excludes nothing does no work — the practiced Excluded-section is half the act) · the thing's IDENTITY (individuation precedes filing).
- **C2 (core) ★— the CATEGORIZER verb-meaning candidate:** given an ENTRY, a PURPOSE, and a CATEGORY-SYSTEM, judge which named region the entry belongs to — via **TWO STACKED JUDGMENTS**: (a) ★the AXIS judgment — which axis governs THIS entry's filing, chosen by **the handling-difference test** (which dimension of difference changes what the consumer will DO with the entry; a category system earns its keep only if different categories license different handling); mis-categorization is usually an AXIS error (filed by topic when the consumer needed urgency), not a bin error — then (b) the REGION judgment — the membership test at the boundary; prototype-distance when graded; the uncertainty rule set by the system's failure ASYMMETRY (which mis-filing is unrecoverable). **NOT-list:** not individuation (upstream) · not ranking/scoring · not naming/minting (uses names; coining is another act) · not clustering (discovering regions vs assigning to given ones) · not system-DESIGN (choosing the system's axes is the designer's act) — with the open-system nuance: on misfit, a CLOSED system gets a signal ("the generator may be wrong"), an OPEN system permits an instance-grounded extension (A4/A6's owned precedents) — the system-kind gates the move.
- **C3 (core) ★— the axis-concepts a categorizer chooses BY (his literal ask):** (i) the consumer's HANDLING (the test above — the primary criterion); (ii) the purpose's GRAIN (the basic-level point: the axis-level where sameness-within and difference-between are both high FOR THE PURPOSE); (iii) the system's KIND (closed → the generator's axes are fixed; open → the practice's attested axes); (iv) STABILITY over the entry's expected life (a fast-changing attribute makes a bad filing axis for a durable record); (v) the failure ASYMMETRY (file so the unrecoverable miss is avoided — A1's lean-to-split, transferred).
- **C4 (core) — the system-kind note:** CLOSED/GENERATED vs OPEN/PRACTICE systems; a categorizer must know which kind it serves — treating open as closed forces bad fits; treating closed as open corrupts the generator.
- **C5 (sub) — the honesty test staged for sensemaking:** is axis-primacy (the two-stacked-judgments structure) TRUE or over-fitted to his phrasing? The support to weigh: most working systems FIX the axis at design time (the categorizer inherits it — the bin judgment is the daily act); the axis judgment RE-ENTERS per-entry when systems are faceted (A3), when multiple systems compete for the entry, or when the fit fails — so axis-primacy may be honest as "the deep judgment, mostly pre-made, re-opened exactly at the hard cases."
