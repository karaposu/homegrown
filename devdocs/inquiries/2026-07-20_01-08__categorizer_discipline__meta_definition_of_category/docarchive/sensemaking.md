## Phase 5 — Conceptual Stabilization

**The stabilized model.** A category is a named region in a chosen axis-space — purpose-relative (by the project's own practice and, formally, by theorem), carrying a membership judgment. It is not a score, not a natural fact, not a bare label, not a pile, not the thing's identity. The categorizer is the discipline that, given an entry, a purpose, and a category-system, judges the entry's home region(s) under the system's membership regime — two stacked judgments: the axis judgment (which dimension governs — chosen by the handling-difference test, plus grain, system-kind, stability, and failure-asymmetry) and the region judgment (membership at the boundary, with the uncertainty rule set by the system's failure asymmetry). The axis judgment is mostly pre-made at design time and re-opens exactly at the hard cases — faceted systems, competing systems, misfits — which is where the discipline earns its name. And the categorizer must know its system's kind: in a closed system a misfit indicts the generator; in an open system it is a legal, instance-grounded extension moment.
