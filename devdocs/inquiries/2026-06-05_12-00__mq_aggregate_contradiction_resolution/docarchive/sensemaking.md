### Ambiguity A1: Is "merge" one operation or several (reconcile vs synthesize vs arbitrate vs meta-perceive)?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** All four are different intensities/forms of the same underlying operation (e.g., arbitrate is a degenerate reconcile; synthesize is reconcile-with-positive-content). One unified essence is sufficient.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** Each is structurally distinct in OUTPUT TYPE: reconcile produces VERDICTS (binary or graded); synthesize produces UNIFIED CONTENT (positive); arbitrate produces SELECTIONS (one option chosen); meta-perceive produces OBSERVATIONS (about the MQ-set). These output types are not reducible to one another. A single-output essence would lose either verdict-shape OR content-shape.

**Confidence:** HIGH

**Resolution:** Merge has TWO sub-aspects: reconciliation (verdict-shape) AND synthesis (content-shape). Hybrid essence ESS9 (reconcile-OR-surface) captures the real-world need: reconcile when confidence allows; surface tension as content when not. Meta-perception is the COGNITIVE OPERATION TYPE (perceiving the MQ-set); reconcile/synthesize are its OUTPUTS.

**What is now fixed?** Merge's essence has two output dimensions (verdict + content).

**What is no longer allowed?** Pure single-output essence (verdict-only OR content-only) is rejected.

**What now depends on this?** Output shape (OUT region) collapses to hybrid (OUT2/OUT4 family); pure-verdict (OUT3) and pure-content (OUT1) eliminated.

**What changed in the conceptual model?** Merge is a meta-cognitive operation producing a hybrid output (verdict + content), not a single-output reconciler or synthesizer.
