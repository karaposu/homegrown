## Phase 3 — Ambiguity Collapse

### Ambiguity 1 — Is a "pattern" a member of the class, or is the class itself a "pattern of patterns"?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** The class IS a pattern (meta-pattern), and members are instances within that meta-pattern. This framing fits "pattern language" framing where the "language" is itself a meta-pattern. Conflating class with members.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** The class has a different identity from any single member. The class is the LIBRARY; members are entries in the library. Confusing them produces structural incoherence — you can't add members to a single pattern (it's a pattern, not a library), and you can't INVOKE a class (it's a category, not a procedure). The meta-loop INVOKES members; it CONSULTS the class.

**Confidence:** HIGH.

**Resolution:** The class is a CATEGORY / VOCABULARY (library of named patterns). It is NOT itself a pattern. Members are individual patterns. The meta-loop USES the class as a vocabulary; it INVOKES specific members.

**Fixed:** Class vs. member relationship.
**No longer allowed:** Treating class as itself a pattern; treating individual members as fully describing the class.
**Depends:** Subsequent work distinguishes class-level concerns (membership criteria; vocabulary structure) from member-level concerns (per-pattern definition).

### Ambiguity 2 — Does the class include or exclude the 7 loop-control moves?

Loop-control moves are DECIDED from a CLOSED vocabulary (7 moves, period). Patterns are INVOKED from an OPEN vocabulary (compositional shapes the meta-loop applies). The operational logic differs (decide-vs-enumerate). The SCOPE differs (control moves at BOUNDARIES between loops; patterns BEFORE the multi-loop effort starts, shaping the whole effort). Two distinct cognitive operations.

**Confidence:** HIGH.

**Resolution:** Class does NOT include the 7 loop-control moves. Different cognitive operations at different scopes.

**Fixed:** Class is OPEN library of compositional shapes; 7 moves are CLOSED decision vocabulary.
**No longer allowed:** Treating 7 moves as patterns; conflating decisions with compositions.
