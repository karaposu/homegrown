## Phase 2 — Generate (full coverage: 4 Generators + 3 Framers)

### Framer — Lens Shifting
- **Generic:** stop seeing vitality as a *score you compute*; see it as a *triage decision you already make*. low/mid/high = triage tiers.
- **Focused (consumer's lens):** view it through the **meta-loop's** eyes — vitality is *advice to the dispatcher* (a recommendation strength for develop-vs-deepen), not a measurement. Under that lens the only questions that matter are "does the build lean on this?" (impact) and "is it pinned down?" (likelihood).
- **Contrarian (lazy-rater lens):** view it as something a *rushed, non-expert rater* must answer in 3 seconds. Under that lens anything past impact×likelihood is dead weight → validates the lightness bound.

### Generator — Combination
- **Risk matrix:** meaning-gap + the impact×likelihood **risk grid** (risk management) = a gap is a hazard plotted on a 3×3 grid; low/mid/high are the diagonal bands.
- **Project's own severity:** gap + critique's purpose-fitness × articulate's ambiguity = *the same grid the project already uses for defects*, with the gap as the hazard → name candidate **"the gap's risk grade."**
- **Stub/placeholder:** deferability + the engineering "stub it and fix later" = the project's existing REFINE-able/fixability notion, re-used as the cheapness escape.

### Framer — Inversion (MANDATORY; depth-iterated; multi-axis)
Central assumption: *"a two-axis-plus-cap risk rubric decided by three glance booleans is the right lightweight way to set low/mid/high."*
- **L1 (component) — "one axis (impact) suffices":** steelman — just ask "does the build depend on it?"; likelihood only refines within. **Result:** doesn't kill the second axis — it **sharpens the mapping**: impact is the *primary* axis (the gate), likelihood is *secondary* (the escalator). A high-impact gap with one obvious reading is "deepen quickly," not "resolve first" — only likelihood separates those. → impact-gates / likelihood-escalates is *earned*, not decorative.
- **L2 (system) — "no rubric: read low/mid/high straight off the gap by gestalt":** steelman — the 16-10 field is first-pass; the rater just *feels* it. **Result (crystallizing):** the booleans don't *compute* the rating — **they audit the gut.** They NAME what the gestalt already does (impactful? ambiguous?) so the rating is *inspectable* and two raters converge. The rubric is the gut-call made inspectable, not a replacement for it — that is *why* a rubric without it being heavy.
- **Existence-axis — "drive the boolean count toward zero / one":** one conjoined boolean ("load-bearing AND ambiguous?") is maximally light **but collapses MID** (you can't distinguish impact-yes/likelihood-no from impact-no) → you get only high-vs-not. **Result:** producing *three* tiers structurally *requires* the impact bit and the likelihood bit kept separate; deferability supplies the low-floor. This is the structural reason it is exactly **2 axes + 1 cap** — no fewer.
- **Identity-axis — "vitality = opportunity/unlock, not risk":** steelman — a gap is vital because deepening it *unlocks* a lot (the meaning-unlocking origin), not because skipping it hurts. **Result:** unlock-a-lot = build-depends-on-it-a-lot = the *same impact axis* viewed from its upside; risk and opportunity are **duals**. Risk is the more domain-agnostic surface and matches the project's existing severity vocabulary → keep risk-framing primary; record the opportunity-dual as a framing note.

### Framer — Constraint Manipulation (both directions)
- **ADD "rater has 3 seconds, no domain expertise":** forces glance-tier booleans; kills any sub-signal checklist → validates the lightness bound.
- **ADD "two different raters must agree on the tier":** forces yes/no-shaped questions (objective-ish: depends? / ambiguous? / stub-able?) over a 1–10 gut score → **structural justification for BOOLEAN form over continuous scoring** (answers the user's "boolean not weighted" preference with a *reason*).
- **REMOVE "must reuse existing vocabulary":** if free to invent fresh, you'd re-derive impact×likelihood anyway (it's the domain-agnostic minimum — see Domain Transfer convergence) → reuse is *free lightness*, not a compromise.
- **REMOVE "lightweight":** a heavy vitality = a full risk-register per gap, which defeats the first-pass field (16-10) → the lightweight constraint is **constitutive**, not optional.

### Generator — Absence Recognition (both levels; bidirectional)
- **Patch-level (missing):** the rubric doesn't gloss *what each tier tells the meta-loop to do.* Adding a one-line tier-meaning (low = build now / mid = deepen quickly / high = resolve before building) makes the attributive rating actionable without adding weight.
- **Patch-level (already-present-in-different-form):** "confidence in the rating itself" looks absent — but 16-10 already sets default confidence low; it's present elsewhere, not a gap.
- **Redesign-level (already-present):** if designed from scratch, would this be a *new* capability? **No** — the project *already* makes this judgment every time td-critique assigns **burden-of-proof by stake** (reversible/small → low; hard-to-reverse/large → high). Vitality is **that same judgment surfaced as a field**, not a new faculty → the cleanest identity-clean argument.

### Generator — Domain Transfer (native + deliberately-different)
- **Native (risk management):** the **risk matrix** (impact × likelihood, banded) — the seed's exact structure; ALARP's "accept the risk" = the deferability cell.
- **Native (software):** **bug triage** — severity × frequency → P0/P1/P2; "won't-fix / defer" = deferability; low/mid/high = P-levels.
- **Different (medicine):** **triage** — acuity × certainty → immediate/delayed/minor; "watchful waiting" = deferability.
- **Different (translation — the comprehenslate corpus):** a translator at an ambiguous term decides "resolve now vs footnote-and-move-on" = impact × ambiguity × deferability → confirms the rubric fits *prose*, i.e. is genuinely domain-agnostic.
- **Convergence:** four independent domains all instantiate **impact × likelihood + a defer/accept escape** → strong evidence it is the domain-agnostic *minimum*, not an arbitrary pick.

### Generator — Extrapolation
- **Accumulation (future, resist):** ratings across many inquiries could become calibration data ("which gap-types run high") — heavy, multi-phase → RESEARCH FRONTIER, not now (lightness).
- **Anti-hedge (now):** if raters hedge everything to "mid," the field dies. The impact **gate** (forces low when the build doesn't depend) and the deferability **cap** (forces low when stub-able) are precisely what *prevent mid-inflation* → the gates aren't decoration, they're the anti-hedge mechanism. (Folds into the mapping justification.)

## Assembly Check — the emergent crystallization

Combining survivors A+B+C+D produces an architecture none has alone:

> **Vitality is triage for meaning-gaps.** It is the project's *own* severity judgment (purpose-fitness × ambiguity = impact × likelihood — the same risk grade it already assigns to defects) **re-pointed at a gap and read in 3 seconds**, with a "can-wait" escape (deferability = watchful waiting). The three booleans don't *compute* the tier — **they audit the gut**, naming what a careful rater already senses so the rating is inspectable and portable to the meta-loop. The mapping is **forced, not chosen**: impact **gates** (no dependency → low), likelihood **escalates** (depends but clear → mid; depends and ambiguous → high), deferability **caps** (stub-able → low) — three tiers need exactly these two bits plus the cap, which is *why* it's two axes + one cap and no heavier.

**Name candidates (ranked):** ① **gap triage** / "the gap's triage tier" (strongest — carries acuity×certainty + watchful-waiting + fast-non-expert all at once); ② **the gap's risk grade** (ties it to the project's existing severity grid); ③ keep "vitality" as the field's word, and use *triage* as the *explanatory metaphor* for how it's decided. **Recommendation:** keep the field-word **vitality** (16-10 owns it); adopt **triage** as the crystallizing metaphor + **"the booleans audit the gut"** as the one-line defense + the **tier-glosses** (low = build now / mid = deepen quickly / high = resolve before building) as the actionable surface.
