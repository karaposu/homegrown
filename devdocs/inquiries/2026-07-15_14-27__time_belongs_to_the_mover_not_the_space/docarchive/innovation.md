## P1 — THE VERDICT (finding-ready)

**Yes — with one precision. Time is relevant to the traversal entity only; and "thinking space is a space" is right if "space" means *clockless*, not *frozen*.**

The terrain — thinking space in the territory sense, whose points are questions — has **no clock of its own**. It is not frozen: canon classifies the landscape as *non-stationary* (`algorithm_family:11`), and "findings rewrite the territory" (`:22`). But every change to it is **authored by traversal** — the terrain changes only when it is walked; it never moves itself. So time is never a property the terrain *carries*. **The traversal entity is the sole source of time in the system.**

The core is a **coordinate-vs-cause** distinction: time is never a *coordinate of* the terrain (the space view's X≠time); it is the traversal entity's *action on* it. A map of the terrain at any instant is pure adjacency-geometry with no time axis; between instants it is rewritten *by* the mover. The terrain is what time acts upon, never a carrier of time.

**Two riders keep it honest:**
- **"Clockless" is a working model, not ontology.** (Gloss: *clockless* = has no clock of its own; changes only when traversed.) Canon's own hedge stands — thinking space is "a working model, not an ontological commitment" (`Traversal_Thesis:33`); no metaphysical claim about the terrain's nature is intended.
- **"Traversal entity" spans levels.** It is the object-level walker (`/traverse` + equipment) *and* the meta-level self-redesigner (Baldwin edits on the self-space) — "one machine, two spaces" (`kernel_bet:119`). The claim only stays clean at this reading: object-only, the self-space's drift would look like autonomous terrain-change and the claim would leak.

## P2 — THE RECONCILIATION (finding-ready)

**The three-place note has three *guises* but only two *homes*.**

| Guise (from the note) | Home | Horizon |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — "not an axis of the territory" | **the terrain** | — (clockless; no time) |
| 2 — "time belongs to the mover" (the walk) | **the traversal entity** | within-run (the walk-order) |
| 3 — "the map's own history" (growth) | **the traversal entity** | across-run (cumulative) |

Time has **two homes**: the terrain (none) and the traversal entity (all), and the traversal entity's time has **two horizons**:
- **within-run** — the walk-order of one traversal (grain-1 token-path, grain-2 revolution-path; "the inquiry folders ARE the path record" `Thesis:34`; the road's position = time);
- **across-run** — findings rewriting the terrain + the known region growing (append-only accretion; "the self-space's walked path" `kernel_bet:121`).

**Place 3 is not a third home.** The map's growth is the traversal entity's cumulative product: the map *is* the mover's trail ("the folders ARE the path record"), and its growth mechanism is "findings rewrite the territory" — findings are traversal outputs. There is no time-source in place 3 but the mover; so place 3 is the across-run horizon of the *same* home, not a new one.

### Mandated inversion — the place-3 collapse
- **Invert:** *"Place 3 is a genuine third home — the map is a real third object with its own history, distinct from terrain and mover."* → **test:** the map is indeed a **real distinct object** (the record layer — worth its own line in the note), BUT its *time* has no independent source; it is authored by the mover's cumulative walking. **True-half kept:** the map is a distinct object that *shares the mover's time-source* — a third *object*, not a third *time-home*. (This nuance is why the re-word keeps three bullets but re-frames bullet 3 as the mover's longer horizon, rather than deleting it.)

**The model-boundary (stated, not hidden).** Canon models *all* terrain-change as traversal-authored (findings; Baldwin edits) and does **not** separately clock exogenous world-drift (the world changing what's askable, independent of the project's traversal). Under the project's own model a question enters the terrain only when traversal reaches it — so the claim holds **by the model's boundary**. That is a modeling commitment, not a proof; a model that separately clocked exogenous drift would put a (small) clock outside the mover. Canon does not, so within canon the claim is clean.
