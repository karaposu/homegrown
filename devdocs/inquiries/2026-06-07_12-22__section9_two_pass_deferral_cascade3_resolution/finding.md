### Context the reader needs

This finding sits inside an ongoing chain of refinements to the **articulate_simple** discipline at `devdocs/how_articulate_simple_should_be.md`. The discipline takes a compact task statement and runs five operations on it (Itemize / Meta-question / Deconstruct / MultiDepth / Rephrase) to produce a per-item bundle the rest of the project's cognitive loop can work with. The discipline is committed to **substrate-bounded operation** per §1 of the doc — it does not fetch project files; the substrate is the task statement + the LLM's general knowledge + any session context already loaded.

Three prior cascade resolutions established a structural pattern. The **21-52 finding** (`devdocs/inquiries/2026-06-06_21-52__no_commitments_pre_surfacing_safety/finding.md`) established the **substrate-bounded chain**: any commitment the LLM makes at the articulate_simple stage about properties of the task is a guess (no project context to ground it); downstream consumers act on the guess as actionable framing; downstream is polluted with pre-context guesses. The 21-52 finding narrowed Meta-question's permissive-answer range from four shapes to two shapes (identified-ambiguity / explicit-empty). The **23-18 finding** (`devdocs/inquiries/2026-06-06_23-18__multidepth_purpose_wrapped_cascade_resolution/finding.md`) extended the chain to MultiDepth's purpose-wrapped output, narrowing it similarly. The **11-40 finding** (`devdocs/inquiries/2026-06-07_11-40__rephrase_constraint_source_cascade2_resolution/finding.md`) refined Rephrase's constraint source — since Meta-question commitments and MultiDepth purpose-wrapped commitments had both been removed, Rephrase's "MQ-constrains-Rephrase relation" no longer had its load-bearing input; the verdict refined Rephrase to ambiguity-spanning variants within a multi-source composition.

Each of those three findings, in its Next Actions section, named **Cascade C** (or "Cascade 3" — the 21-52 finding's original naming): §9 of the doc commits articulate_simple as "complete on its own"; the cumulative cascade pressure structurally pressures that commitment. ### The "complete" ambiguity is the pressure point

§9's "complete on its own" claim is ambiguous between two senses:

- **Scope-completeness**: articulate_simple does what it claims within its pre-context scope. The discipline produces a useful framing under its substrate-bounded scope — it does not overreach, but it does what it sets out to do.
- **Identity-completeness**: articulate_simple IS the whole discipline of articulate. There is no "rest of articulate" outside the articulate_simple form; the two-pass form is an optional elaboration, not a structurally-necessary part of the identity.

A defense of PRESERVE could survive if the §9 claim were interpreted as scope-completeness only. But the doc's consistent usage rules this out:
- Doc opening (line 5): "This document describes articulate_simple — the single-pass form of the cognitive operation... The two-pass form... is a separate design and is explicitly out of scope here." — identity-completeness sense.
- Line 26: "the foundational form; the two-pass form... is built on top of it." — identity-completeness sense.
- §9 itself: "Articulate_simple is **complete on its own** — it produces a useful, defended framing in one pass. The two-pass form adds context-informed refinement; the simple form is the foundation that refinement builds on." — identity-completeness sense.

The doc's consistent usage is identity-completeness across line 5, line 26, and §9. PRESERVE cannot survive at this layer without ignoring the doc's own framing.
