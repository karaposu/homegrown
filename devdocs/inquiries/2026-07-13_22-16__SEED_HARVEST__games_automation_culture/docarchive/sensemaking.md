## THE SOURCE'S CLAIM-STRUCTURE (A1 — stabilized for provenance; each claim names its exhibiting systems)

1. **Controlled comparison by fixed terrain:** seeded runs and dailies (Spelunky, Hades, Slay the Spire) give different movers IDENTICAL terrain — isolating skill from luck is solved and mass-practiced; dailies ban replays to keep the comparison honest.
2. **In-run self-measurement over repeatable segments:** speedrun splits time named segments against one's own personal best (gold splits) — "ahead/behind, and where" is legible DURING the run.
3. **Explicit information-purchase:** RTS scouting (StarCraft) spends units/time/risk to reveal terrain and enemy state before committing; cadences are learned discipline; over- and under-scouting both have named costs.
4. **Cheap offline models before expensive commitment:** theorycrafting simulators (SimulationCraft, Path of Building) are community-built, versioned models used to optimize before spending in-game; known drift-vs-reality costs.
5. **Past decisions as study objects:** replay review (fighting games, chess.com engine-annotated review, CS demos) — the run is recorded automatically and re-watched deliberately.
6. **Negative-feedback challenge modulation, with a trust price on hiddenness:** DDA (RE4's hidden rank, L4D's intensity curve, racing rubber-banding) holds engagement in a band — and players who DETECT hidden adjustment feel cheated.
7. **Return points with priced abuse:** quicksaves/checkpoints enable recoverable exploration; save-scumming deletes consequence-weight; permadeath is the deliberate absence of return — checkpoint spacing is a designed judgment about instructive loss.
8. **Shipped automated-choice architectures:** behavior trees, utility AI, GOAP (F.E.A.R.'s backward-chaining planner), HTN — the internals of the family's bot-member.
9. **Many-mover coordination:** raid practice = differentiated roles (trinity) + a rhythm-caller + wipe-and-retry + shared external memory (guides, boss-timer addons) — with an addon-dependence cost (kin to quest-marker deskilling).
10. **Instrumented consequence-free practice:** fighting-game labs expose measurement live play lacks (frame data, hitboxes, recording dummies) for isolated drills — with a transfer caveat.
