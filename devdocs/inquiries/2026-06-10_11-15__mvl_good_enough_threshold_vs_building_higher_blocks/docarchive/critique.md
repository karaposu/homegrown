### The andon-cord allocation (the recommendation; carries the RE-TEST trigger — taken first)

**Prosecution (strongest):** (a) **The joy-exception swallows the rule.** The operator's stated pattern IS enjoying polish ("i still improving it through individual disciplines and optimizing it various ways") — an exception keyed to excitement reopens polish-as-default for exactly this operator; the pause becomes decorative. (b) The closing-line habit adds a NEW obligation — the same ceremony-risk the throughput inversion warned about. (c) "The two debts travel with the switch" double-books the pre-registration file, which is already a MUST in the Traversal Thesis finding.

**Defense:** (a) The exception carries three structural guards: it is **costed** (a session, taken knowingly), **logged** (the closing line records it — every joy-session feeds the very data stream that ends guessing-based polish), and **capped by consultation** (the dashboard is re-read every ~5 inquiries; a polish streak is visible in the live row as turns-not-taken). The DEFAULT is what changed; joy-sessions are visible deviations — and this corpus's own standing principle is that *declared deviation is design*. (b) The closing line REPLACES an existing prompt (the observation prompt already prints at every run's end and has been ignored 109 times); net-new friction ≈ one sentence. (c) Conceded — fix the wording: the pre-registration file rides as *the standing MUST, referenced*; the checker is the second debt.

**Collision:** (a) falls — with the guards stated EXPLICITLY in the finding (cost + log + cap), the intent (no more unmeasured polish as the silent default) survives the exception; what the prosecution correctly killed is any UNLOGGED or UNCAPPED version. (b) falls. (c) lands as a wording fix.

**Verdict: SURVIVE-REFINED.** The guards become part of the rule's text; the debt clause becomes a reference. **The RE-TEST trigger is DISCHARGED:** the joy-exception preserves sensemaking's anti-data-starved intent — deviation visible, default intact.
