## SV4 — primary anchors, stabilized

**A-ORDERING (the causal spine) — STABLE.**
The miss is a **structural ordering problem**, not a discipline malfunction: a good anchor-framing needs the canon model, but the framing step (articulate_simple) runs **first and cold**, spec-barred from consulting canon (input = task statement + session context only). The canon-need therefore has **no legal entry point at framing time**; articulate_simple frames the anchor from whatever session context holds — and in a harvest run, session context is saturated with the running harness, so the anchor comes out "machinery." This is the project's own already-named **"pre-context phase limitation."**

**A-LOCATION (origin / executor / cause) — STABLE.**
- **Origin = articulate_simple.** Its MQ2 named the anchor as "the traversal **machinery** (surfacing/traverse/routelister/navigate)" (thin a_s:35-36) — zero canon concepts.
- **Executor = surfacing.** It received a machinery-bounded territory, built anchor-axis A1–A9 (all machinery), and transparently logged "no anchor outside the traversal machinery was surfaced (territory-bound)" (thin surfacing:99). It did not originate the miss; it faithfully surfaced what it was handed.
- **Cause = operator-salience, laundered through the session-context-only limit.** The machinery is what's warm because the operator is running the harness; articulate_simple can use only warm context; so the bias enters the loop precisely at the framing step's spec-mandated intake.

**A-HYPOTHESIS-MAP — STABLE (each re-sized honestly, both directions).**
- **HA (surfacing should have fetched canon) — REFUTED**, on spec grounds: surfacing draws from a **given, explicit-bounded** territory; its boundary-discovery sub-phase fires only for **unbounded** territories, so it is not mandated to override a bounding it was handed. *One nuance held open (the only place HA survives):* should surfacing carry a **purpose-adequacy flag** — "does this territory even contain what the purpose needs?" — and raise it when the territory looks mis-bounded? That is a live surfacing-spec question, not a thin-dive execution failure.
- **HB (the failure is upstream in articulate_simple) — CONFIRMED, but REFRAMED.** The machinery-framing did originate at articulate_simple — but articulate_simple did **not fail**. It hit its **designed** pre-context limit. "articulate_simple failed" is the wrong verb; "the canon-need had no legal entry at framing time" is the right one.
- **HC (enrich the source before articulate_simple) — REFUTED as placed.** The claim-axis was **already enriched** — the thin dive summoned 12 spider features from established knowledge (thin surfacing:19-30). - **HD (the articulate2 loop-restructure) — CONFIRMED and ELEVATED.** articulate2 is not a vague recollection: it is the project's **own documented, endorsed, deferred** design — "articulate_simple is the pre-context phase … the post-context phase is provided by articulate_simple → /surfacing → articulate2 … the structural resolution of overreach for the pre-context phase's limitations" (how_articulate_simple_should_be.md:50, 7), originating in an inquiry where the user's "eventually we'll need articulate2" was endorsed (2026-06-06_11-16). It is the **exact** resolution the ordering problem calls for.

**A-FIX-STRUCTURE (the composing two-part fix) — STABLE.**
The fix has two halves that **compose, not compete**:
1. **Get canon into context** — bound surfacing's territory to the canon model (the 19-14 fix, at seed_harvester §6). Without this, there is no canon for a re-framing to use.
2. **Use that context to re-frame** — a second articulation after surfacing (articulate2), so the anchor axis is re-derived from canon rather than inherited from the cold machinery-framing.
Neither half alone suffices: (1) alone gets canon into surfacing but leaves the *framing* still machinery-anchored (unless the harvest protocol also re-specifies the anchor at surfacing-time, which is what the 19-14 fix does for harvests); (2) alone has no canon to re-frame with. There is a **deadlock** the composition breaks: the warm re-frame needs canon, but canon enters via surfacing, whose territory is set by the cold frame.

**A-FIX-GRAIN (two grains + their relationship) — STABLE.**
- The **19-14 seed_harvester §6 fix** is **harvest-specific / local / shippable now** — it hard-codes "use the canon traversal model as the anchor axis" into the one protocol that knows its anchor should be canon.
- **articulate2** is **loop-general / root / already-designed-deferred** — it fixes the ordering problem for *any* traverse whose good framing needs unsurfaced context, harvest or not.
- **Relationship = subsumption.** Ship the local fix now; articulate2 is the eventual general resolution, and once it re-frames the anchor from canon, the harvest hard-code may become **redundant** (subsumed). Local-now, global-later.

**A-19-14-RETEST — STABLE (confirmed-but-incomplete).**
The 19-14 "add an active canon-surfacing step" fix is **confirmed but incomplete (frame-revised)**: it correctly locates the **first half** (canon-into-context) but did not name the **second half** (the cold framing needs a warm re-do). Not wrong; incomplete and now mechanically pinned.
