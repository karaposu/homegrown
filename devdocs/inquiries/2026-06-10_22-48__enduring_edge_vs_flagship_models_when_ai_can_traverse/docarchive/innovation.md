## Phase 2 — Generate (7 mechanisms × 3)

### 1. Lens Shifting (Framer)

- **1G — the infrastructure lens.** Frame the project as *the rails, not the train* — providers build faster trains (models); the durable business is the rails, signals, and dispatch (governance/orchestration). One vivid framing for the reframe (P2).
- **1F — the regulated-industry lens (focused).** Every industry that got powerful + autonomous grew a *governance layer that became mandatory and valuable*: aviation → black boxes + the FAA; finance → audit trails + SOX; medicine → trial protocols + the FDA; nuclear → containment + the NRC. **The pattern: capability arrives first, then the governing layer is forced into existence and becomes non-optional.** AI is pre-its-FAA-moment; the project is building the black box + the protocol before the mandate. Adopted as the verdict's strongest evidence (P1) — the "rises with capability" claim has four historical precedents.
- **1C — the "providers will eat this" adversary (contrarian).** "OpenAI/Anthropic will ship audit logs, memory, and agent controls — your edges evaporate." Answer (sharpening A5): a provider auditing its own opaque, non-deterministic model is **the fox guarding the henhouse** — structurally not neutral; their memory is **single-vendor lock-in** (cross-session, never cross-PROVIDER); their controls serve **their** product roadmap, not the operator's sovereignty. The durable edge is the **independent, model-agnostic, operator-owned** stance — exactly what a first-party provider cannot occupy. **Adopted: each Tier-1 edge restated as "third-party-neutral by construction," not "feature providers lack."**

### 2. Combination (Generator)

- **2G — provenance × portfolio orchestration = "the accountable router."** Combine the top edge with the economic edge: a system that routes each turn to the cheapest adequate model AND records why-each-route-was-chosen-and-what-came-of-it produces *accountable multi-model cognition* — something no single provider offers (they can't audit a competitor's model in your pipeline). A combined flagship-grade edge.
- **2F — owned-theory × the corpus = "a proprietary science of YOUR thinking space."** The metacognition theory + the accumulated findings = a domain-specific *theory + evidence base* of how good thinking proceeds in your field — an asset that compounds and that a general model has no access to. Strengthens E7 + the dataset edge (E-corpus).
- **2C — sovereignty × continuity = "cognitive escrow."** A new framing: your accumulated traversal is held independent of any provider — if a model is deprecated, lobotomized by a safety update, or priced out, your thinking SURVIVES and re-attaches to a new engine. "You own the cognition, you rent the engine" becomes **cognitive escrow / insurance against provider risk** — a CFO-legible value proposition. Adopted (sharpens E11/sovereignty).

### 3. Inversion (Framer; depth-checked)

- **3G — "what kills the project" inversion.** Invert the relevance question: what future OBSOLETES it? Only one — if a provider ships a model that is *simultaneously* superintelligent AND natively transparent, persistent-across-providers, operator-steerable, and neutrally self-auditing. That bundle is *commercially self-contradictory* (a provider has no incentive to make its model auditable BY a competitor's tooling or portable TO a competitor's engine). So the obsolescence path is structurally blocked — which is the strongest form of the relevance verdict. Adopted into P1.
- **3F — the top-edge inversion (focused, adopted).** Challenge "provenance is the top edge" → maybe SOVEREIGNTY is, because no level of model intelligence can give you something it structurally withholds (portability/ownership). Resolution: they're co-#1 but provenance is *more universal* (every domain needs it; sovereignty matters most to regulated/sensitive ones), so **provenance leads, sovereignty is the close second — and they fuse in "the accountable, portable cognitive record."** Sharpens the top-edge claim.
- **3C — the seed-(c) inversion (contrarian, adopted).** Invert "weak-model-amplification is transitional" → maybe it's PERMANENT because there will ALWAYS be a frontier gap (today's weak model is last year's flagship; the scaffold always lifts the current-cheap tier). Resolution: TRUE — there's always a cheap tier and always a gap to lift, so the *economic* form is permanent (portfolio arbitrage); what's transitional is only the *"makes it as smart as a flagship"* capability claim. This confirms the migration (capability→economic) and even strengthens it: **the amplification edge is permanent in its economic form, transitional only in its capability form.** Adopted.

### 4. Constraint Manipulation (Framer; both directions)

- **4-ADD (generic).** Each edge ships with a one-line **"the pitch"** (how you'd say it to a skeptical buyer) — forces the edge to be concretely valuable, not abstract.
- **4-ADD (focused).** Each Tier-1 edge names the **buyer who needs it most** (provenance → regulated/scientific/legal orgs; sovereignty → defense/medical/frontier-labs; orchestration → cost-sensitive scale users) — grounds the imagination in a customer.
- **4-REMOVE (generic).** Drop the tiers (flat edge list)? → loses the strategic payload (where to invest); the tiers ARE the decision-relevant content. Fails.
- **4-REMOVE (contrarian).** Drop the speculative corpus-dataset edge (keep only the 6 solid)? → it's the most imaginative one and the user asked for imagination; keep it, clearly flagged speculative/gated. Keep.

### 5. Absence Recognition (Generator; both levels, bidirectional)

- **Patch-level — hunting an 8th/9th edge:** (i) **Interoperability / the cognition lingua-franca** — if the harness's record format becomes a standard, OTHER tools/teams can read and build on your traversals (a network-effect edge, distinct from memory: memory = you reuse it; interop = others can). (ii) **Adversarial robustness via externalized critique** — the harness's separate critique pass catches a model's confident wrong answers that the model itself won't flag (the self-reference trap externalized) — distinct from "verification" as a *safety/reliability* edge specifically. (iii) **Pacing / human-in-the-loop legibility** — the harness produces thinking at a CADENCE a human can supervise (checkpoints, turns) vs a flagship's opaque one-shot — a *collaboration* edge. Three more candidates surfaced.
- **Redesign-level:** none — the 3-family / 3-tier structure holds; the new candidates slot into existing families (interop → substrate; robustness → structure; pacing → structure).
- **Bidirectional (already-present):** the project ALREADY embodies the top edge in this very session — every inquiry IS an auditable provenance record (the findings + Inherited-Commitments Re-test + corrigenda). The edge isn't aspirational; it's the artifact you're already producing. Strong grounding line.

### 6. Domain Transfer (Generator; native source included)

- **6-native (software).** **The compiler/IR analogy:** models are like CPUs (raw compute getting faster); the harness is the compiler + intermediate representation + debugger + version control around them. Nobody stopped needing version control because CPUs got faster — the tooling layer's value rose with the compute. One precise framing for "value rises with capability."
- **6-different (the 1G/1F regulated-industry + infrastructure framings)** — already seated; the aviation black box is the single best image (provenance = the flight recorder that's mandatory precisely BECAUSE planes got more autonomous).
- **6-different (biology — the genome vs the cell).** REJECTED — analogy budget; the compiler + black-box images are sharper and less strained.

### 7. Extrapolation (Generator)

- **7G — the end-state picture.** Extrapolate the reframe: at the limit, the project is **"the operating system for autonomous cognition"** — it doesn't think, it makes thinking accountable, persistent, portable, and steerable across whatever engines exist. The OS outlives any CPU generation. One vivid end-state line.
- **7F — the value-curve.** Plot it: as model capability ↑, the per-call value of the harness's *cognition* → 0, but the value of its *governance* → ∞ (more autonomy = more to govern). The two curves cross; past the crossover the project is ALL governance — which is why aiming the build at Tier-1 now is positioning for the post-crossover world. Adopted as the payload's sharpest form.
- **7C — the failure path.** If the project chases capability (Tier-3) instead of governance (Tier-1), it gets caught on the wrong side of the crossover — out-competed on cognition, having under-built the governance moat. The strategic risk named precisely. Adopted.
