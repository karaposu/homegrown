**Strongest counter (everything folds):** "population search, exploration/exploitation, diversification tactics, even randomness — all family-level content the selection frame + innovate's coverage + the territory-weighting lesson absorb; zero live corners."
**Why it fails (structural):** scope-check each absorber: the selection frame owns *selection-over-variants*, not *stochastic proposal*; innovate's coverage owns *within-run mechanism diversity*, not *cross-run selection policy*; the territory-weighting lesson is a *deliberate judgment heuristic*, not a randomization lever. No owned mechanism randomizes anything, anywhere — while CSA's escape-from-local-optima is *load-bearing on randomness at three named points*. The absorption-streak shows our judgment-driven selection CAN lock. One corner survives all absorbers.
**Confidence:** HIGH. **Resolution:** the boundary lands: ABSORBED — population/selection family, divergence-then-convergence, parsimony, fl↔extrapolate rhyme, AP-tuning↔territory-weighting; LIVE — **one germ-region: deliberate stochastic selection as an anti-lock design option**, with two-three application sites (source/territory choice; which-seed-to-develop-next; possibly the crossing's move choice); CONFIRMING-RICH — the two-track structure (see Ambiguity 2).

### Ambiguity 2 — two-track: gap or confirmation?
**Strongest counter (it's a gap):** "we gate before recording; CSA moves-then-remembers — the separation of a free wander-track from a selective keep-track is a design we lack."
**Why it fails:** the 17-51 crossing account IS that separation, stated as a rule: GENERATE ungated and one-to-many (the wander), GATE after (the keep), with "the gate filters after generation, never during" as a named position rule. CSA's non-greedy position + greedy memory is the same architecture in numeric form. What we genuinely do NOT have is *continuing the search from rejected/worse positions* (CSA's position track carries on from bad spots; our gated-out candidates are dropped, not walked-from) — but no current decision turns on that residue.
**Confidence:** HIGH. **Resolution:** the two-track structure is a **rich confirmation** of the generate-then-gate architecture from an independent domain (an engineered optimizer converging on the same separation the crossing account committed) — confirming-PLUS material, not a seed; the continue-from-worse residue is noted, un-promoted (no decision changes).
**Fixed:** corner 2 → the confirming column. **Depends:** the ladder position (Ambiguity 4).
