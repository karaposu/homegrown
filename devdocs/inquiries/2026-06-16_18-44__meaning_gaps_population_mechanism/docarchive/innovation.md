## Phase 2 — Generate (4 Generators + 3 Framers)

### Framer — Lens Shifting
- **Generic:** stop seeing it as "generate a gap list" (a *production* task); see it as "**read out** what framing already perceived" (a *reporting* task). The gaps aren't produced — they're reported.
- **Focused (cost lens):** by marginal cost — framing already pays the perception; the gaps are **free** (zero marginal perception). A dedicated pass pays the cost twice.
- **Contrarian (quality lens):** a purist says by-product perception is unsystematic → misses gaps (the inversion steelman; addressed below).

### Generator — Combination
- **by-product + "exhaust":** the gaps are the **exhaust** of the framing perception — what it produces as a side-output. *"Perception exhaust."*
- **+ the depth-signal:** the gaps = the depth-signal, pluralized + rated → *"a multi-item depth-signal."*
- **+ compilers:** a compiler emits **warnings** as a by-product of the parse it already runs — not a second pass. The meaning-gaps are routelister's **warnings**, emitted during framing.

### Framer — Inversion (MANDATORY; depth- + multi-axis-iterated)
Central assumption: *"extend framing (reuse) is the right mechanism — not a dedicated pass."*
- **L1 (component) — "framing's perception is too shallow; a dedicated mini-decompose finds better gaps":** steelman — a methodical pass catches important-but-unobvious gaps incidental framing misses. **Result:** the field is **first-pass by design** (a prompt, not a contract) — a "more systematic" list claims a confidence the field *disowns*; the systematic version is exactly what `/decompose` is for, and `16-10` makes the field "refinable by a full `/decompose`." So by-product is right *for the field*; systematic is right *downstream*. **Complementary, not competing** — refines, doesn't kill.
- **L2 (system) — "by-product gaps are biased toward what framing happened to notice":** steelman — incidental perception has blind spots. **Result (crystallizing):** TRUE per-run — and exactly what the field's low-confidence + lean-to-list + the **re-run loop** handle. The field doesn't claim completeness; the meta-loop deepens a listed gap, the map re-runs, and new gaps surface on the next framing (perception governs, §3.5). So the gap list is an **incremental, iteratively-refined signal — completeness accrues across re-runs, not in one shot.** The bias is real per-run but washes out over the loop.
- **Existence-axis — "ZERO authoring: gaps are implied, never written":** steelman — the meta-loop could infer gaps from the route's WHY/Guidance. **Result:** the whole point (`16-10`) is an *explicit, rated* list giving the vitality signal; implicit gaps give no vitality. Not viable — clarifies why an explicit emit is required.
- **Identity-axis — "what is the mechanism?":** invert "a generation step" → "a **transcription** step." routelister already perceives the gaps (in framing); the mechanism **transcribes** that perception into the field, adding the vitality tag. Identity = transcription-plus-rating, not generation.

### Framer — Constraint Manipulation (both directions)
- **ADD "framing already happened (the perception is sunk)":** the gaps are free; *not* emitting them **wastes** a perception already paid for. Reframes "should we add gap-generation?" → "should we **discard** a perception we already have?" — and discarding is the waste.
- **ADD "the field must stay first-pass":** forbids the systematic pass; confirms by-product.
- **REMOVE "must reuse framing":** even if a dedicated pass were free, it produces a *wrong-confidence* (too-systematic) artifact for a first-pass field → still no. Reuse isn't just cheaper; it's the **right confidence level**.
- **REMOVE "first-pass":** if the field could be high-confidence, a `/decompose` would fit — but `16-10` fixed it first-pass. So **first-pass is the constraint that selects by-product over systematic.**

### Generator — Absence Recognition (both levels; bidirectional)
- **Patch-level (missing):** the fallback's "too opaque" threshold — supply it: *"if framing can't confidently name even one facet, emit the bare meaning-unready flag."*
- **Patch-level (already-present):** routelister already emits **Confidence** per route (§5.2) → the fallback **reuses** it (low framing-confidence → bare flag), not a new measure.
- **Redesign-level (already-present-in-different-form):** the depth-signal IS the bare-flag form already → the degradation ladder is **fewer rungs of the same depth-signal**: multi-item rated → singular (bare flag) → none. Identity-clean.
- **Redesign-level (missing):** designed from scratch, would gap-authoring be a separate discipline? No — it's a perception routelister already does; a separate discipline would re-perceive. Confirms reuse.

### Generator — Domain Transfer (native + deliberately-different)
- **Native (compilers):** **warnings** emitted as a by-product of the parse/typecheck already run — not a second pass. The strongest, native analogy.
- **Native (code review):** a reviewer reading code to understand it also notices "this part is unclear" — a by-product of the read, not a separate audit.
- **Different (reading):** **margin notes** ("unclear here") made *while* reading — not a re-read.
- **Different (medicine):** a doctor noting "needs follow-up on X," severity-tagged (urgent/routine), as a by-product of the exam — not a separate workup.
- **Convergence:** compiler-warnings, review-notes, margin-notes, exam-follow-ups are all **by-products of a perception already happening, severity-tagged inline** → strong convergence on "by-product, not a second pass" + "tagged in the same glance" (which also defends the one-glance generate-and-rate).

### Generator — Extrapolation
- As routes re-run, the gaps refine (perception governs) → over many runs the gap list for a target **converges** as the target is deepened. The mechanism is self-correcting across time (the per-run bias washes out) — reinforces the incremental-signal reframe.
- If routelister accumulates many by-product signals (gaps, depth-signal, Frontier, Excluded), it grows a coherent **"perception-exhaust" layer** — a tiny frontier; **resist** (scope), note only.

## Assembly Check — the emergent crystallization

Combining A+B+C+D+E:

> **The population mechanism is routelister emitting its "perception exhaust" — like a compiler emitting warnings as a by-product of the parse it already runs.** When framing a DEVELOP/CONSOLIDATE route, routelister already perceives the target (it must, to write the route's WHY/Movement/Guidance); it **transcribes** the under-understood facets it notices into the `Meaning-gaps:` block, **severity-tagged (vitality) in the same glance**. It is **transcription, not generation** (the perception is already paid for — *not* emitting the gaps would discard it). It is **first-pass by design**: the systematic version is the downstream `/decompose`, which the field explicitly defers to — *complementary, not competing*. It is **incremental**: completeness accrues across re-runs as perception governs, not in one shot — which is what answers the "by-product is biased" objection. And it **degrades gracefully** by emitting **fewer rungs of the same depth-signal** (multi-item rated → bare meaning-unready flag → none), keyed to routelister's **existing per-route Confidence** (low framing-confidence → bare flag).

**Name recommendation (ranked):** ① **"perception exhaust"** / a **by-product emit** (crispest — the gaps are the exhaust of a perception already happening); ② the **compiler-warnings** analogy (most memorable for *when* it fires); ③ **"transcribe, don't generate"** (the operative one-liner); ④ **"a multi-item depth-signal"** (the identity-clean structural name). Use "perception exhaust"/by-product as the lead, the compiler-warning analogy as the intuition, and "transcribe-not-generate" as the operative instruction.
