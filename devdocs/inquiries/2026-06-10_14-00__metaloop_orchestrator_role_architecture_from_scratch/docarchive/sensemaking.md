#### Ambiguity A2: Does "eyes never choose" survive re-derivation?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** it survives in FULL — enumeration and choice must live in different sessions, or motivated perception corrupts the field; the articulation-first design proves the corpus believes this.

The defense that works is **ordering + artifact** (the field produced and WRITTEN before the choice), not session count. Separate sessions add a second, real benefit — a stranger's read of the artifacts — which matters more as stakes and autonomy rise; that makes it a dial, not a validity condition. (And the heavy version fails the retained energy bar at L0–L1: two spin-ups per turn for a solo operator.)

**Confidence:** HIGH. **Resolution:** the seam survives WEAKENED as **the field-before-choice rule** (a turn is valid only if the enumerated field is written before the pick), with **fresh-seat enumeration as the quality dial** — recommended when the operator's session is long/invested, default at L4. **No longer allowed:** choose-while-enumerating; treating the session split as what makes a turn legitimate.
