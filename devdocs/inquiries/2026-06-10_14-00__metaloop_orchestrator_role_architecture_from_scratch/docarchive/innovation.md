## Phase 2 — Generate (7 mechanisms × 3)

### 1. Lens Shifting (Framer)

- **1G — cockpit/logbook lens.** The turn in aviation terms: scan the instruments (see), decide (choose), execute (start), **log it** (record) — and the logbook is what flight-hours, licenses, and incident analysis all run on. Consistent with the corpus's existing flight-hours framing for recorded turns. Candidate TEACHING IMAGE to replace the demoted hands/eyes/will triple.
- **1F — job-title lens (focused on naming).** Names should be JOB TITLES (what one does), not department names (where one sits). "The chooser/Selector" is a job title; "orchestrator" is a department name — it smuggles a seat image (a conductor standing somewhere). This is the naming criterion the prior discussions lacked.
- **1C — the "you just renamed the triple" adversary.** Skeptic: eyes→dial'd discipline, will→chooser-role, hands→runners — cosmetics! Answer with a checkable difference: the old map's MODAL force demanded building seats (a navigational session as prerequisite; an orchestrator session to design); the new map's force is a validity RULE plus optional dial — under it, **Turn 1 can legally happen today, in this very session**, with zero new sessions built. The build list shrinks; that is not cosmetics. Adopted into the answers and repairs.

### 2. Combination (Generator)

- **2G — turn validity × the turn-invariant.** The allocation finding's habit (record selection + rationale) and this inquiry's field-before-choice rule UNIFY into one statement: **a turn commits only if the field is written before the pick, the rationale is recorded, and an outcome slot is opened.** One rule, two findings aligned, no duplicate doctrine.
- **2F — the kind-table × the SUSTRALL worklist.** Re-walking `devdocs/next_steps_for_sustrall.md` under the new architecture: Turn 1 — unchanged but CLARIFIED (performable in the current session; fresh-seat optional); "Selector graduation" — already the chooser-role's L2 half under its better name; "orchestrator decision rules" (Tier 3) — renames to "Selector scope rules"; "run the eyes as designed" — softens to the dial. A small worklist consistency pass becomes a named repair item.
- **2C — the L4 compare function × td-critique.** Compare (merge/promote/stop across parallel heads' findings) may be a **critique VARIANT** rather than a new discipline — same adversarial machinery, different candidate type (findings, not ideas). An honest design note for the deferred Evaluator inquiry; reuse before invention.

### 3. Inversion (Framer; depth-checked)

- **3G — unit inversion.** "The turn is the unit" → invert: the FINDING is the unit; turns are glue. Resolution (absorbed): two layers, two units — the inquiry (producing a finding) is the within-layer unit; the turn is the BETWEEN-layer unit. One clarifying sentence; no conflict.
- **3F — dial inversion (focused).** "Fresh-seat enumeration is the quality option" → invert: a cold session MISSES context a warm one holds — freshness can hurt. Resolution — ADOPTED into the dial's statement: the dial trades **stranger's-objectivity against context-richness**, and warming is the price paid to restore context after buying objectivity. The dial is a real trade, not a free upgrade.
- **3C — naming inversion (contrarian).** "Names should match the user's vocabulary" → invert: the user's vocabulary CAUSED the tangle (three names used interchangeably is the complaint). The kindest naming is the one that **cannot regrow the confusion**: each name must map to exactly one KIND, and fail the seat-reading test. Applied: "orchestrator" FAILS the anti-regrowth test (the conductor image re-implies a seat); "Selector" PASSES (unambiguously a role); "meta-loop" PASSES for the cycle (it is grammatically a loop). ADOPTED as the naming criterion.

### 4. Constraint Manipulation (Framer; both directions)

- **4-ADD (generic).** Every architecture element in the finding carries an inline **kind-tag** — `[cycle]` `[role]` `[rule]` `[file]` `[dial]` `[plumbing]` `[L4-judgment]` — the calibrated-claims format applied to architecture; a grep finds any future kind-slippage.
- **4-ADD (focused).** Each of the seven answers: ≤2 sentences, zero reliance on demoted text.
- **4-REMOVE (generic).** Drop the turn record from L1 ("just take turns, record later")? → kills the gates (no data ever), breaks the pre-registration ordering, reduces the climb to vibes. Removal fails; the file is load-bearing.
- **4-REMOVE (contrarian).** Drop the quality dial (always enumerate inline)? → loses the L4 default and the long-invested-session case where self-reading demonstrably degrades. Removal fails; the dial stays — as a dial.

### 5. Absence Recognition (Generator; both levels, bidirectional)

- **Patch-level:** (i) the package lacked a **turn-performer statement** — who may perform a turn: ANY agent that can satisfy the validity condition (the human today, with or without assistant execution; automated sessions later) — the turn is performer-agnostic by design. (ii) The **worklist consistency pass** (from 2F) named as a repair item.
- **Redesign-level:** the canon edit's natural shape is **REPLACE, not patch** — one new section ("The Between-Inquiry Layer": the turn, the kinds, the role, the rule, the levels) replacing the SUSTRALL canon's component descriptions, written self-contained with status marks. Patching the triple sentence-by-sentence would leave the seat-frame's skeleton in place.
- **Bidirectional (already-present):** the corpus already PRACTICES the new model — today's sessions performed complete turns with phases in one session (see-ish → choose → start; record absent), and the worker runners established the phases-in-one-session-with-artifact-handoffs pattern this architecture reuses one level up. Cited as live evidence, not authority.

### 6. Domain Transfer (Generator; native source included)

- **6-native (software).** **The transaction:** a turn is a unit of work with a COMMIT condition — the validity condition is the commit criteria; an unrecorded turn is an uncommitted transaction (it happened, but it doesn't COUNT — exactly the turn-invariant's semantics). One-line precision metaphor.
- **6-different (aviation).** The cockpit/logbook image (1G) — promoted to the candidate replacement teaching image: *the pilot (the Selector) scans instruments (the written field), decides, executes, and logs the flight (the turn record); licenses (autonomy levels) are earned from the logbook, never from simulator polish.* One image, several existing framings unified (flight-hours, gates, the record).
- **6-different (governance).** Separation-of-powers as a second image — REJECTED (analogy-inflation guard; the cockpit image suffices).

### 7. Extrapolation (Generator)

- **7G — level-invariance (the architecture's strongest property).** The turn's definition does not change across autonomy levels — the same cycle runs at L0 and L4; ONLY THE PERFORMERS of phases change (human chooses → system proposes → system chooses scoped; hand-typed start → carrier; human-written record → auto-appended record). The ladder becomes a pure role-transfer story over an invariant cycle — which preserves, at the goal layer, the organ-transfer framing the user has affirmed, now stated without seat-dogma.
- **7F — the record at scale.** The turn record's accumulation becomes the standing report the future Selector reads — consistent with prior findings; no new commitment.
- **7C — graceful degradation.** If the recording habit fails, the architecture degrades to today's status quo — working but unclimbing. Its failure mode is **stagnation, not breakage**: no component can crash because almost nothing is a component. Honest line for the finding.
