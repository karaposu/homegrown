## Finding

### 1. The two angles — what a meta-goal is

A route — and above it a venture — carries two typed angles, not one.

The first angle is owned. The routelister discipline (the project's route-enumeration practice, spec at `/Users/ns/.claude/skills/routelister/references/routelister.md`) types every route with one of nine engagement-verbs — DEEPEN, DEVELOP, PURSUE-SEED, INVESTIGATE-FRONTIER, REFINE, REFRAME, DIAGNOSE, TEST, CONSOLIDATE — under its own membership test: *"a verb for how to engage a concept."* The verb types **how** the run engages.

The second angle is his. The **meta-goal** — his word, kept, glossed as *the kind of yield a run is undertaken for, one level above concrete results* — types **what the run is undertaken for**. This angle occupies a genuinely open cell, verified by three reads this dive:

- The route schema's `Lands` field holds a free-form landing *instance* per route ("a built code-state for a DEVELOP, a settled decision for a PURSUE-SEED…"), and its `WHY` field a free-form gain — no type-vocabulary over either.
- `docs/canon/what_is_meaningful_traversal.md`, read in full, holds five quality signals (Coverage, Convergence, Productivity, Directedness, Depth) — **all judged after the walk**; its only goal-anchor, "the original question," is untyped. The doc has no before-the-walk content at all.
- The venture rephrase (`docs/canon/load_bearing_findings/traverse_SUSTRALL_venture_rephrase.md`) states that purpose sharpens through findings without breaking venture identity — and gives no mechanism for why sharpening isn't a break.

The two angles are non-redundant — many-to-many in the record, both directions. Same verb, different kinds: the 13-08 context-line dive's DEVELOP route landed a *build* (the visualizations), while the 10-10 label-fix dive's development landed a *design* (the semantic-identity objects, on paper); the 15-13 finding's first onward route is a single route whose two candidate landings are two different kinds (a design-instance vs a build-plus-first-inheritance instance), pending his pick. Different verbs, same kind: *adjudications* arrived via a refine-type dive (the 14-27 time dive — "refine this further") and via a consolidate-type dive (the 13-08 context-line dive's three-layer verdict). A one-to-one shadow of the verbs could not produce either pattern.

One vocabulary serves every level. His own example spans two rungs in a single sentence: "stabilizing a method [the gain rung — venture-facing] via uncovering db schema requirements [the route rung — run-facing]." The WHY-climb's rungs are all filled from the same kind-vocabulary, up to venture purpose — which is why one list can serve both the run-grain wiring and the venture-grain wiring below.

**The honest form, with both guards.** This is not a new field: the ingredients (the verbs, Lands, WHY, the prediction schema) are owned. And it is not already owned: no shared yield-kind vocabulary exists anywhere. The precise state — caught by this dive's own adversarial pass, against its own earlier wording — is that the practice ALREADY writes kind-words every dive, in free-form per-dive coinages: every dive's Goal statement carries a literal `Kinds:` line (the 11-24 space-first dive: *"Kinds: knowledge-consolidation + design-enumeration + honesty/feasibility adjudication + roadmap-framing"*; the 10-10 label-fix dive: *"Kinds: defect-fixes + design + adjudication + grading"*). Each dive coins its own words; no vocabulary is shared across dives. **The enumeration standardizes what each dive already states in its own words — it does not type for the first time.** That correction shrinks the novelty claim and strengthens the case: the vocabulary is evidently natural to the practice.

### 2. Claim one — why meta-level predictions are both accurate and feasible

Canon itself classifies the search this project runs as one where concrete outcomes cannot be predicted: noisy, expensive evaluation on a non-stationary landscape, with *"true evaluation arrives only after execution"* (`docs/canon/algorithm_family.md`). Pre-run direction already works through estimates — *"a prior proposes and an estimate guesses."* So a prediction pitched at the concrete-result level is infeasible by classification. That is his "we don't know the concrete end result."

A prediction pitched at the yield-kind level escapes this on two counts:

- **Feasible:** you can always state, before the run, what kind you are going for. It costs one word.
- **Accurate — in a checkable sense:** *accuracy means the run ends with a yield that actually is of the stated kind*, checked against the finding's deliverable at run end. It does NOT mean "the walker didn't change his mind" — a run's yield must honestly match the stated kind, and a run whose honest yield-description differs in kind from its stated undertaking is a **miss-in-kind** (a stabilize-run that instead uncovers the method is wrong yielded an adjudication or a limitation-map — a miss, and useful). Guarding this distinction requires the kind to be stated ex-ante in writing — which is itself an argument for the enumeration: recordability is what makes accuracy testable at all.

Why does the yield-kind stay stable when concrete results don't? Two stabilities, not one. The verb constrains the coarse landing family (the schema's own per-verb landing examples show this — a DEVELOP lands some built state). And the undertaking's kind is chosen at a stratum coarse enough that the walk's concrete surprises rarely force a kind-change — *what* you uncover varies; *that* you were uncovering-requirements usually survives. The second stability is not the verb's shadow (the many-to-many evidence above), and "rarely" is precisely the honest limit: **the hit-rate is unmeasured.** The mechanism is argued on canon; no rate has ever been counted. The instrument that would count it exists as a germ (rx-S3 in `devdocs/seeds/_seed.md` — an explicit prediction before each selection, measured after), and misses-in-kind are exactly the prediction-vs-outcome delta canon's Popperian schema (`docs/canon/thinking_space_dynamics.md` §7: prediction / prediction_window / observable_outcome) treats as calibration data.

One more precision: the altitude is a **working band**, not a free lunch. Below it — concrete results — predictions are infeasible. Above it — "make progress" — they are uninformative. A meta-goal that could never miss would be too vague to inform; the possibility of a detectable miss is what makes the prediction worth stating.

### 3. Claim two — never random

Structurally true of the practice: every dive carries a Goal; the routelister's Excluded section exists precisely for candidates whose engagement does nothing toward the goal; the WHY field is mandatory even when the line of sight is weak. Choice runs goal-first by construction.

Two riders keep it honest. First, canon deliberately germinated a bounded exception: the p21-S1 seed (a small, capped random-jump lever against judgment-lock). It is a designed corrective, not a refutation — a capped perturbation *presupposes* the non-random baseline it perturbs. Second, being meta-goal-driven is not the same as the meta-goal being explicit: today the goals live in per-dive prose and in his head. The enumeration is what would make never-random **recordable** — a stated kind per choice, checkable later.

### 4. Claim three — enumerable, and produced

Four warrant legs: the cell is open (the three verified absences); the instances exist (the record's Goals, kinds-lines, WHY statements); a consumer exists (the rx-S3 prediction germ needs a vocabulary to state predictions in); a derivation precedent exists (the seed function-typology was derived the same way — from real instances).

Usefulness, graded honestly at two speeds. **Paper-now:** typed statements cost one word each; typed forward-half options and WHY-rungs become comparable across dives; and misses-in-kind become *detectable* — you can only observe a miss against a stated kind. **Calibration-deferred:** the payoff where typed predictions feed Selector calibration waits on a reader that is germ-grade, not built; until then the rate stays unmeasured.

### 5. The v1 meta-goal vocabulary — thirteen yield-kinds

Derived bottom-up from real recorded undertakings — NOT top-down from the nine verbs (deriving from the verbs would produce one kind per verb by construction and make the two-angles claim untestable; the bottom-up direction is what lets kinds visibly cross verbs). Rules: every kind cites at least one real undertaking; the list is **provisional, raw-material grade** (bound by the meaningful-traversal doc's own warning against premature formalization); kinds are **types, never scores**; one run may stack kinds (composites are the norm); the list is **open-ended**; and it is **sample-limited** — largely one session's findings plus his two examples, so wider sweeps will reshape it.

Three membership decisions were forced open and resolved explicitly rather than papered over:

- **STABILIZATION was added, with a macro note — and an admission line so the door doesn't swing open.** His first example named it; the candidate list had missed it. It earns the slot on three grounds: the record's own ex-ante language attests it (the commit message "towards stabilization"); completed instances exist (the seed-harvester fix package, proven by its live test); and it passes a distinct-action test — calling a run "a stabilization" commits you to *iterate until the wobble stops* (a loop with an exit condition), which "a validation" (one check) does not. The macro note: a stabilization typically spans runs and decomposes at run grain into validations, repairs, and consolidations — composite-legality makes that a feature. The admission line for future macro-kinds: usage-attested in the record's own ex-ante language + at least one completed instance + a distinct follow-on action. Hypothetical compounds ("improve X," "mature X") fail that line today.
- **BUILD and DESIGN were split, not merged.** The structural proof is the gate between them: this project's standing law makes builds user-gated, so the design→build crossing is a governed decision point (the animation storyboard sat *designed* until his "later" arrived and it became a *build*). A vocabulary that merges the two erases the location of a gate the practice actually operates.
- **HARVEST was added by the adversarial pass itself.** The instance-audit found the record holds a whole genre of runs undertaken to gather seeds — and none of the twelve candidate kinds covered it. Its addition is the sample-limited mark in action.

Composites are the norm: the 11-24 space-first dive's single Goal stacks a CONSOLIDATION + an OPTION-FIELD + a VALIDATION — its own kinds-line says so in its own words. And the WHY-climb composes the same vocabulary across rungs: his example, typed, reads *"a STABILIZATION via a REQUIREMENTS-UNCOVERING."*

Honest residue: some live WHY statements name gains as *effects*, not kinds ("unstick-the-climb," "capture-before-escape") — they type imperfectly. That is evidence the vocabulary is open, not complete, and it stays raw material.
