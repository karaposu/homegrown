## P2 — The three verdicts (finding-ready wording; shapes committed upstream)

**(a) The altitude claim — CONFIRMED-WITH-MECHANISM.** Canon itself classifies the search's concrete outcomes unpredictable: noisy, expensive evaluation on a non-stationary landscape, "true evaluation arrives only after execution" (algorithm_family :11/:20). Yet predictions pitched at yield-kind altitude are **feasible** — you can always state ex-ante what kind you are going for — and **accurate**, by a **two-legged stability**: the verb-floor constrains the coarse landing family (the schema's own per-verb landing examples), and the walker-held kind persists through concrete surprises because it is *held*, not verb-implied — what you uncover varies; that you were uncovering-requirements doesn't. The accuracy is not the verb's shadow (m:n). Precision riders: the altitude is a **working band** — below it (concrete results) predictions are infeasible; above it (vague "make progress") they are uninformative; a meta-goal that never missed would be too vague to inform. **Misses-in-kind are the mechanism's honest edge and its food**: they are exactly the Popperian delta (thinking_space_dynamics §7's prediction/observable_outcome schema) the calibration loop wants. And the hit-**rate** is UNMEASURED — the mechanism is argued, not counted; rx-S3's per-selection predictions are the instrument that would measure it.

**(b) Never-random — CONFIRMED-WITH-TWO-RIDERS.** Structurally true of the practice: every dive carries a Goal; the routelister's Excluded section exists precisely for goal-irrelevant candidates; the WHY-rung is mandatory even when weak. Rider (i): canon's own p21-S1 germ — a deliberate, capped random-jump lever against judgment-lock — is a designed exception that *presupposes* the non-random baseline it would perturb; it bounds the claim, it does not break it. Rider (ii): meta-goal-DRIVEN is not meta-goal-EXPLICIT — today the goals live untyped in per-dive prose and in the operator's head; the enumeration is what would make never-random RECORDABLE.

**(c) Enumerability — CONFIRMED-AND-PRODUCED.** Four warrant legs: the cell is open (the three absences); the instances exist (the record's Goals and WHY-lists); a consumer exists (rx-S3's prediction-per-selection germ); a derivation precedent exists (the seed function-typology). Usefulness graded **two-speed**: *paper-now* — typed forward-half options and typed WHY-rungs cost one word each, and misses-in-kind become DETECTABLE (you can only observe a miss against a stated kind); *calibration-deferred* — the Selector-calibration payoff waits on a reader that is germ-grade, not built.
