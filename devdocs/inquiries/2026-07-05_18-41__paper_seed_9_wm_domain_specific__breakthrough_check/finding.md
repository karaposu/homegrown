# Finding: Does Paper 9 (Working Memory Storage Is Intrinsically Domain Specific) Generate a Breakthrough for Us? — No; the harvest's third consecutive "confirming-plus," a similarity-sharpener.

## Question

Eighth dive in the parked-paper harvest (`devdocs/paper_seed/`), where each paper is read in full and adjudicated — honestly, both ways — for whether it yields a genuine import for our project (a "cognitive harness": markdown thinking-disciplines run through a `/traverse` pipeline that builds a cross-session memory). Every dive is gated by the **import test**: *an insight is a real import only if it NAMES something we already do but hadn't named, or RESOLVES a confusion we already had — otherwise it is merely confirming or decorative.* Prior verdicts: paper 1 → **consolidation** (YES, generative); paper 2 → **reach≠grasp / the availability illusion** (YES, regulative — a session mistakes what it can *reach*, i.e. look up, for what it actually *holds*, i.e. has loaded); papers 3, 4, 6 → NO (bounded-residue / cleanest / bounded-residue); paper 7 → controlling access to WM (NO, confirming-plus / residue-sharpener; the first scored forecast); paper 8 → WM as an emergent property (NO, confirming-plus / ontology-clarifier; the second scored forecast). Papers 5 and 10 remain un-dived.

The user pointed at `devdocs/paper_seed/9.md` — **Fougnie, Zughni, Godwin & Marois (2015), "Working Memory Storage Is Intrinsically Domain Specific"** (*J. Exp. Psych: General*; 2045 lines, 8 experiments) — and asked, as before: **"dive deep [into] if this would generate breakthrough for us or not."** The paper's thesis: when confounds are stripped (task coordination, shared representational format, verbalization/chunking strategies), a visuospatial WM array and an auditory WM array show **no interference even at high load** — they store in parallel. Interference appears *only* when the two share a representational format (Experiment 8: both spatial). The refined conclusion, in the General Discussion, moves past the domain-specific-vs-general dichotomy: **the amount of interference between two sets of items scales with the amount of representational *overlap* between them** — distinct representations store in parallel with no cost; overlapping ones compete.

This dive is the **third** scored forecast of the harvest method, and the **first test of a refinement** adopted at the end of the paper-8 dive: after two consecutive magnitude-overcalls (papers 7, 8 both predicted LEAN-YES and landed NO), the forecast was split into two stages — a *region* prediction committed **before** grounding, and a *magnitude* prediction committed only **after** the grounding pass. Both stages are scored here, and the result is itself a finding about the method.

## Finding Summary

- **The answer is NO — paper 9 is not a breakthrough. It is the harvest's *third consecutive* "confirming-plus"** — and, like papers 7 and 8, it sharpens/tidies an existing frame rather than opening a new one. Its specific flavor is a **similarity-sharpener**: it is the *third visit* to a single residue — the context-pollution residue that paper 3 first opened and paper 7 sharpened with an encode-time location. Paper 9 adds a *similarity* axis.

- **The delta is real — arguably the richest of the three confirming-plus results.** Our canon already has a rich account of representational overlap (the `Intuition-similarity` primitive, with its surface-vs-structural distinction) — but *only in a retrieval role*: similarity is how you find matching prior work (current item vs the corpus). Paper 9 supplies a genuinely different **configuration**: two items *co-resident in the buffer* interfere in proportion to their overlap (item↔item, within-buffer) — a *within-buffer interference cost*, not a retrieval signal. Paper 7's capacity-tax graded context pollution by *quantity* ("effective grasp = raw − irrelevant load"); paper 9 adds a *quality* axis on top: near-duplicates blur super-linearly, so **diversity of held content is protective**, and — the counterintuitive edge — *loading more distinct context can be cheaper than loading less overlapping context*. "Less-overlapping is better," not merely "less is better."

- **But NO, on four defeaters that survive the delta's strongest hearing.** (1) The overlap *concept* is already ours (Intuition-similarity); paper 9 re-applies it, doesn't supply it. (2) The genuine novelty — the co-residence-interference configuration — decomposes into two pieces we *already own*: the similarity relation (Intuition-similarity) + finite-buffer competition (reach≠grasp / the capacity-tax). A new configuration built entirely from owned pieces is a combination/sharpening, not a new frame. (3) It *refines, doesn't resolve* — it names a plausible-but-*undocumented* dynamic (we have no recorded "similar items blur" pain), where reach≠grasp named the mechanism beneath the *documented* cold-navigation failure. (4) The consistency bar: papers 7 (added a *time*) and 8 (changed a *status*) corrected existing accounts and were confirming-plus; paper 9 (adds an *axis*) is the same grade.

- **What was quarantined — and it's most of the paper.** The headline "domain-specific *stores*" is a fact about separate *sensory modalities* (visual, auditory) that we lack: our context window is one homogeneous text buffer, with no modality separation. So the parallel-storage result doesn't transfer; only the functional residue (interference scales with overlap) maps. The 8-experiment apparatus is quarantined too.

- **The two-stage forecast, scored — and it worked.** The **region** call (committed before grounding: "paper 9 will engage the interference-structure of the buffer — whether pollution is similarity-graded") landed exactly; region-finding is now right 3-for-3 (papers 7, 8, 9). The **magnitude** call — committed *after* grounding, at the end of Surfacing, as LEAN-NO/confirming-plus, and pre-registered *before* the independent Sensemaking adjudication — was **accurate** (the verdict is NO/confirming-plus). This is the **first evidence that deferring the magnitude call to post-grounding removes the systematic overcall** that hit papers 7 and 8 (both pre-grounding LEAN-YES). It is one datapoint, not a validation — but it is the datapoint the refinement predicted.

## Finding

The harvest's job is to find the few ideas that genuinely sharpen our design while refusing to manufacture importance — and, three NOs deep on the working-memory cluster, refusing the opposite error too: reflexively grading down to look calibrated. This dive stressed that second guard hardest, so the delta got a genuine hearing before the verdict.

### Why the answer is No

Paper 9's whole transferable content maps onto a residue we have already visited twice. Paper 3 first flagged that our context management lacks a selective keep-out and named context *pollution* (irrelevant content derailing a traverse). Paper 7 sharpened it: pollution is a *capacity tax*, graded by *quantity* — the more irrelevant load, the less effective grasp. Paper 9 sharpens the same residue again, this time along a *quality* axis: the interference between held items scales with how much they *overlap*.

The anchor for the No is the consistency bar the harvest has held since paper 3: a breakthrough means a *new frame* (the generative frame of consolidation, or the regulative frame of reach≠grasp), not the sharpening of an existing one. Papers 7 and 8 were both real, useful corrections of existing accounts — and both were scored confirming-plus for exactly this reason. Paper 9 is structurally identical: a real, useful correction of the pollution account, not a new frame.

### The delta, credited fairly — the co-residence configuration

It would be too easy to dismiss paper 9 as "we already have similarity." The careful version, which credits the delta properly, is this. Our `Intuition-similarity` primitive is a *retrieval* operation: it matches the current item against the corpus to *find* relevant prior work (surface or structural match). It describes a relation between one active item and an external store. Paper 9's interference is a *different configuration entirely*: two items both **co-resident in the active buffer**, competing and blurring in proportion to their overlap. That within-buffer, item-against-item interference is genuinely not what our similarity primitive describes — so paper 9's contribution is real and un-named, not a mere restatement.

And it carries a concrete, counterintuitive consequence. The naive view — which reach≠grasp and the capacity-tax roughly encode — is that held items compete for one finite pool, so *less is better* (a quantity view). Paper 9 inverts it: distinct items barely compete; it is *overlap* that competes. So loading *more distinct* context can be cheaper than loading *less overlapping* context; diversity of held content is protective; and when the keep-out evicts, it should drop the items that **overlap the signal** first, because those interfere most. That is a real design principle, and it makes paper 9 arguably the richest of the three confirming-plus results.

### Why the delta still doesn't clear the bar

The richest confirming-plus is still confirming-plus. The decisive point is that the new configuration is built from two pieces we already own: the *similarity relation* (Intuition-similarity: surface/structural closeness) and *finite-buffer competition* (reach≠grasp / the capacity-tax: co-resident items compete for the buffer). Paper 9 combines them — "co-resident items compete *more* when similar." A new arrangement of owned pieces sharpens an account; it does not re-found one.

Two further defeaters hold. The dynamic paper 9 names is *plausible but undocumented* for us — we have no recorded failure of "the system confused two similar held items," the way paper 2's reach≠grasp had the documented cold-navigation failure to resolve; so paper 9 *refines* the residue rather than *resolving* a standing confusion. And the consistency bar is decisive: to grade paper 9 a YES would be to grade papers 7 and 8's equally-real corrections inconsistently.

I checked this against the mirror hazard directly, because three consecutive NOs on one cluster is exactly when reflexive grading-down creeps in. The test: am I holding paper 9 to a higher bar than reach≠grasp got? No — paper 9 is objectively thinner. Reach≠grasp named the mechanism beneath a *documented* failure, generalized to all traverses, and unified three mechanisms. Paper 9 names an *undocumented* dynamic, refines *one* residue, and unifies nothing. The asymmetry is real; the No is earned, not streak-driven. And I did not overcompensate: the delta is credited as the richest confirming-plus, not zeroed, and not ranked above paper 2's actual YES.

### The two-stage forecast — the method result

Because this dive split the forecast, the finding scores both halves, and the split is itself the point.

The **region** call was committed before any grounding: paper 9 would engage the *internal interference structure* of the context buffer — specifically whether pollution is *similarity-graded*. It landed exactly (the paper's refined thesis is "interference scales with representational overlap"). The topic-novelty heuristic's region-finding is now right three times running (papers 7, 8, 9) — it reliably points at the facet a paper will engage.

The **magnitude** call was the one that had overcalled twice. This time it was *deferred* until after the grounding pass, and committed at the end of Surfacing — as LEAN-NO/confirming-plus — *before* the independent Sensemaking adjudication ran. Sensemaking then landed NO/confirming-plus. So the magnitude was pre-registered post-grounding and independently confirmed: **accurate**, where the two pre-grounding magnitude calls (papers 7, 8) had overshot. The mechanism is exactly what the refinement predicted: magnitude depends on how much the priors already cover, and only grounding reveals that (here, grounding surfaced that Intuition-similarity already owns the overlap concept — the fact that made the magnitude LEAN-NO). This is one datapoint, not a validation; but it is the datapoint the two-stage split was designed to produce.

### A harvest-level observation (provisional)

Stepping back: after paper 2's reach≠grasp (a regulative YES), *every* subsequent working-memory-cluster paper — 3, 7, 8, and now 9 — has yielded only confirming-plus. The plausible mechanism is **territory saturation**: once a territory's load-bearing frame is imported, same-territory papers predominantly *sharpen* it rather than opening new frames. If so, the remaining harvest (papers 5, 10) should be weighted toward *different* territories if novelty is the goal. This is a meta observation about the harvest method, provisional at four-in-one-territory — not a design import.

## Reasoning

**Why No, tested three ways.** The dive carried a triple hazard: a committed magnitude forecast (pull toward confirming it), a two-NO track record on this cluster (pull toward reflexively grading down), and — once both were guarded — the pull to over-credit the delta to look balanced.

- **Against forecast-confirmation.** The magnitude was LEAN-NO, but the delta's YES-case (the counterintuitive inversion; the un-named co-residence configuration) was run at genuine force before the No was confirmed — not rubber-stamped.
