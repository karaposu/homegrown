## A. FACTs from the paper (quoted verbatim where load-bearing)

- **A-1 [core] — The headline: WM storage is intrinsically domain-specific; distinct-domain arrays store in PARALLEL with no interference.** "performance of these 2 WM tasks are independent from each another, even at high WM load." Across seven experiments, a visuospatial array and an auditory-object array showed no dual-task cost when confounds were stripped. FACT.
- **A-2 [core] — Interference appears ONLY with shared representational format.** "Only when we introduced a common representational format (spatial information) to both WM tasks did dual-task performance begin to suffer" (Exp 8). FACT.
- **A-3 [core — the refined thesis] — Beyond the dichotomy: interference scales with representational OVERLAP.** "it may no longer be fruitful to conceive of working memory capacity as controlled by either a set of independent memory stores or a single domain-general store… the amount of interference between two sets of objects may scale with the amount of representational overlap between the two sets" (functional distance, Kinsbourne & Hicks 1978); "multiple levels of representation interact in determining the amount of overlap." FACT. **This is the load-bearing functional claim.**
- **A-4 [supporting] — The method innovation: strip the confounds and cross-domain interference vanishes.** Prior studies found cross-modal costs; those were confounds — "task preparation and coordination, overlap in representational format (e.g., space-based), and use of cognitive strategies (e.g., verbalization or higher order chunking)." Control them → no interference. FACT.
- **A-5 [supporting] — No single domain-general pool (or it's negligibly small).** "the present findings challenge the scope and importance of a domain-general store and strongly constrain its potential role in working memory capacity"; a domain-general store, if it exists, "would either be so small as to go undetected… or… not accessible." FACT.
- **A-6 [quarantined] — The domain-specific STORES + neural/modality substrate.** Separate auditory/visual/spatial stores, distinct neural correlates, parietal cortex, the 8 dual-task experiments, K/Cowan capacity formulas, Bayes factors, dots/bird-calls/consonants. FACT-in-paper but OUT of scope (MQ4): no brain, no sensory modalities.
- **A-7 [supporting] — Perfect cross-domain independence is the EXCEPTION in everyday life.** "the absence of interference… may only occur under fairly specific experimental conditions, and may not usually occur for the type of objects we interact with in our everyday lives. Perfect sharing… may be the exception, rather than the rule." FACT. (Because everyday objects overlap in many representational dimensions at once.)

## B. FACTs from our canon / prior findings (read verbatim this dive)

- **B-1 [core — the KEY grounding] — We ALREADY have a rich "representational overlap" concept: the Intuition-similarity primitive, with TWO kinds of similarity.** `thinking_space_dynamics.md`: "**Intuition-similarity** | Pattern-matches current item against corpus (similarity only…) | Finds prior work that matches in surface OR structure." And §"Two kinds of similarity, not one": **Surface similarity** (content, vocabulary, same domain) vs **Structural similarity** (relational pattern, regardless of surface). FACT. **BUT note the ROLE: our similarity is a RETRIEVAL/MATCHING signal (find relevant prior work) — NOT a within-buffer interference cost.**

FACT. **This names pollution's QUANTITY (how much junk), not its QUALITY/similarity (how much the junk overlaps the signal).**

## Mapping (generated — three directions + reverse)

### HAVE — paper 9 names something we ALREADY have/do (→ confirming risk)
- **M-H1 [core] — "representational overlap" (A-3) ≈ our Intuition-similarity (surface + structural) (B-1).** We ALREADY have a rich account of representational overlap between items — including the surface-vs-structural distinction paper 9 gestures at ("multiple levels of representation interact"). The *overlap concept itself* is not new to us. [confirming candidate — but see M-L1 for the role-shift]
- **M-H2 [supporting] — "finite capacity / competition" ≈ reach≠grasp + paper 7's capacity-tax (B-2/B-4).** The general fact that held items compete for a finite buffer is ours. [confirming]

### LACK — paper 9 names something we plausibly need but haven't named (→ import candidate)
- **M-L1 [core — THE CENTRAL CANDIDATE] — Interference in the buffer is SIMILARITY-GRADED, not just QUANTITY-graded — and this is a NEW ROLE for similarity (a within-buffer interference COST, not a retrieval SIGNAL).** Paper 7's capacity-tax graded pollution by QUANTITY (raw − irrelevant load). Paper 9 adds a QUALITY axis: two held items interfere in proportion to their representational OVERLAP — near-duplicates blur/compete far more than distinct items, so overlapping junk is super-linearly costly and *diversity of held content is protective*. Critically, this is a DIFFERENT role for similarity than our Intuition-similarity primitive (which uses similarity to RETRIEVE matching prior work); here similarity is a COST *among held items* (they confuse each other). Candidate design consequence: the keep-out (paper 3/7) should evict items that OVERLAP the signal first (they interfere most), and context management should DIVERSIFY rather than pile up near-duplicates. THE question: is this a real import (a similarity/quality axis on interference that paper 7's quantity-axis didn't name) — or covered by Intuition-similarity + capacity-tax → confirming-plus? [OPEN — the central adjudication]

### DOESN'T-APPLY — paper 9 content that does not transfer
- **M-D1** — the domain-specific STORES + neural/modality substrate (A-6): separate visual/auditory/spatial brain stores are a fact about *sensory modalities we lack*. Our context window is ONE homogeneous text buffer (B-5) — no modalities. Quarantined.
- **M-D2** — the experimental apparatus (8 dual-task experiments, K formulas, dots/tones): quarantined.

### REVERSE — what WE have that the paper illuminates from outside
- **M-R1 [supporting] — The one-modality quarantine is itself informative: our context window may be closer to a single DOMAIN-GENERAL pool than a brain's modality-separated stores.** A brain gets free parallel non-interfering storage across modalities; we have only ONE format (text), so by paper 9's own logic our held items overlap maximally (all text) and interference is the RULE, not the exception (cf. A-7 — everyday overlapping objects interfere; ours are all "everyday"). This *reframes why context pollution is a real problem for us*: we lack the domain-separation that protects a brain. [a genuine reverse-lens — feeds M-L1]
- **M-R2 [peripheral] — Paper 9 vs paper 8 tension is mostly moot for us.** Paper 9 (domain-specific stores) vs paper 8 (no domain-specific stores) — both are claims about *brain architecture* we quarantine. The functional residues (interference-by-overlap; WM=activation-of-the-others) don't actually conflict for us. [note, not load-bearing]
