> ### 10. Label-Tested, Substance-Untested
>

> **Sub-recognitions (five distinct sub-mechanisms with one shared symptom):**

> - **(i) Name-vs-meaning conflation** — surface name tested as user-verbatim; the name's operational meaning (often LLM-auto-completed) not interrogated.
> - **(ii) Worked-example-as-illustration** — worked examples are load-bearing structural commitments but treated as illustration; the candidate's mechanism is not applied literally to the example.
> - **(iii) Category-bounded test coverage** — a category bounds the test scope in one of two ways: (a) within-category-undifferentiation (single category tested as uniform; distinct sub-cases not surfaced) or (b) candidate-set-restriction-by-taxonomy (inherited taxonomy restricts what's a candidate).
> - **(iv) Unit-presupposition-not-tested** — top-down reading of the operation preserved; bottom-up reading of the primary UNIT's presupposition (verb-first vs noun-first part-of-speech) not surfaced.
> - **(v) Artifact-type-ambiguity** — artifact's TYPE (conceptual entity vs authored deliverable) determines what testing applies; type-ambiguity itself not tested.
>
> Shared symptom: dimension's STATED scope ≠ IMPLEMENTED scope. Shared tendency: evaluation operationally restricted to surface even when stated scope includes substance.
>
> **How to recognize.** Post-hoc, a later inquiry produces a `corrects:` or `refines:` finding showing the prior critique tested labels but missed substance. Harm evidence is often in critique's own artifact (the dimension's check produced surface-verifiable verdict; substance was visible to a literal or bottom-up reading the practitioner didn't apply).
