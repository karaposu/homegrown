If P2 hands P3 owner-labels without the existence determination, P3's gap/phantom calls would be ungrounded → hidden coupling. **Mitigation: P2's last verification criterion makes the determination mechanism explicit, and the P2→P3 interface carries the determination, not just the label.** P4↔P3 share the assumption that "fix" means a *layer-located* fix (which layer owns it), not just a description — the guard operates on the fix's *location*; both pieces must treat fixes as layer-located.
