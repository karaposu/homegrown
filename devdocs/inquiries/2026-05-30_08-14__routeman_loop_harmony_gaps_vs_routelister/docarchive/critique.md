And the fix is the parsimonious one (no new component; ADD-CONTENT to the meta-loop spec) and serves the endgoal (the chooser needs the full menu).
- **Collision:** the phantom objection (F2) dies — the gap is real. BUT the failure-case objection (MERGE needs perception) *lands*: the composer must read **both** its loop-state **and routelister's index** (the concept-map) to compose perception-dependent loop-control moves (MERGE = "these two enumerated concepts are one"; UNBLOCK = "this concept's prerequisite concept is now done"). **Position: viable core, one boundary refinement.** → **SURVIVE + REFINE.**
