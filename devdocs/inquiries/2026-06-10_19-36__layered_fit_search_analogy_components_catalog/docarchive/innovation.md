## Phase 2 — Generate (7 mechanisms × 3, compact)

### 1. Lens Shifting (Framer)

- **1G — the exam-rubric lens.** The scorecard as a grading rubric: each must-have is a pass/fail question worth "disqualifying" weight; high-value items are partial credit. Frames the deliverable as an instrument, not prose.
- **1F — the cartographer's-confession lens (focused).** Every map lies (projection distorts); a HONEST map states its distortion. The must-have "no global visibility" is the analogy confessing it cannot show the whole space — one teaching line for must-have #4.
- **1C — the missing-must-have adversary (contrarian).** "Five must-haves — is one missing?" Sweep: is there a property whose absence makes an analogy actively misleading that isn't listed? Candidate: **irreversibility / history-dependence** — the path you took CHANGES what's reachable next (the corpus's path-bias; watersheds erode the channel they flow in). A static-landscape analogy already fails must-have #2 (non-stationary), but #2 says the shape changes — it doesn't say YOUR TRAVERSAL changes it. **Adopted: must-have #2 sharpens to "the landscape changes, AND your traversal is part of what changes it" (path-dependence)** — folding the new property into #2 rather than adding a sixth (keeping the must-list minimal). The watershed exemplar already carries it (water cuts the channel it then follows).

### 2. Combination (Generator)

- **2G — the probe column × the bird's-eye.** Each must-have's yes/no probe is validated by running it against the bird's-eye (it must FAIL the ones the diagnosis says it fails) — the probes are calibrated on the known-bad case. Adopted into P1+P2 as a cross-check.
- **2F — merge/seam × routelister individuation (the quotable anchor).** The merge-vs-seam high-value entry cites the discipline's own rule verbatim ("same identity's manifestations merge; distinct identities hold a seam") — the analogy requirement is grounded in committed mechanics, not aesthetics.
- **2C — the vocabulary pool × composite analogies.** Since no single phenomenon passes all must-haves, the pool is explicitly framed as COMPONENTS FOR A COMPOSITE (watershed carries non-stationarity+path-dependence; slime mold carries fit-search+memory+pruning; crown-shyness carries individuation) — the next inquiry may assemble rather than choose. Adopted into P4+P5.

### 3. Inversion (Framer; depth-checked)

- **3G — the "good analogy" inversion.** Instead of "what must it include," ask "what must it NOT do" — the disqualifiers: imply a fixed target (optimization), imply global visibility (the bird's-eye), imply sequential layer-completion (the relay), imply a static map, imply a central planner. The must-haves are exactly the negations of these disqualifiers — adopted as a dual presentation (each must-have paired with the sin it forbids), which makes the scorecard sharper (a reviewer spots sins faster than confirms virtues).
- **3F — the dimensionality inversion (focused).** "Find a low-dim analogy honestly" → invert: a TIME-based analogy has unbounded dimensionality for free (a conversation, a piece of music, an embryo developing) — nothing forces it into a fixed-D picture. Adopted as the dimensionality sub-question's leading hint for P5 (dimension-agnostic SOURCES dodge the global-visibility sin natively).
- **3C — the no-analogy-at-all inversion (contrarian).** "Maybe the honest move is NO master analogy — just the scorecard + the named phenomena, used piecewise." Legitimate: the catalog has standalone value (it grades, it enriches canon) even if the hunt never crowns a winner. Adopted as P4's composite note's stronger form: the deliverable may be terminal — the scorecard IS the enrichment the user asked for; the analogy is optional downstream.

### 4. Constraint Manipulation (Framer; both directions)

- **4-ADD (generic).** Each must-have gets a ONE-LINE probe phrased as a question answerable yes/no about any candidate ("Does the territory reshape as the searcher moves through it?").
- **4-ADD (focused).** The scorecard fits ONE table (must-haves) + one short list (high-value) + one line (pool).
- **4-REMOVE (generic).** Drop the high-value tier (must-haves + pool only)? → loses the merge/seam individuation insight and the meaningful-limits (DNA) component — the richest exemplar contributions. Fails.
- **4-REMOVE (contrarian).** Collapse must-haves #2 and #4 (non-stationary + no-global-visibility) into "the space is not a static viewable map"? → they fail differently (a film of a changing blob still implies global visibility; a local-horizon walk on a static terrain still misses non-stationarity) — two distinct disqualifiers. Keep separate. Fails.

### 5. Absence Recognition (Generator; both levels, bidirectional)

- **Patch-level:** (i) **the searcher's identity** — what IS the thing traversing? (the loop; the Selector; the meaning-region itself growing?) — the analogy must name its protagonist; added as a high-value entry (*the analogy must have a clear traverser, and it should map to the loop/Selector, not a disembodied "we"*). (ii) **fit AGAINST what?** — fit-search implies something-to-fit; the must-have should name that the fit is against the GOAL/constraints (the DNA), not a pre-known answer. Folded into must-have #1's probe.
- **Redesign-level:** none — the scorecard form holds.
- **Bidirectional (already-present):** the corpus ALREADY has a partial analogy in active use — "thinking space as territory the loops traverse" (`thinking_space_dynamics.md`) and the cartographic vocabulary (map, frontier, dead zones). The scorecard's first job is to grade THAT incumbent: it passes structured-space and (weakly) fit-search, but a static "territory" fails non-stationarity + path-dependence. Noted in P4 — the incumbent is itself a candidate the scorecard improves.

### 6. Domain Transfer (Generator; native source included)

- **6-native (the project's own).** The incumbent territory/cartography metaphor (5-bidirectional) — graded, not extended.
- **6-different (developmental biology).** Morphogenesis already seeded; reaction-diffusion + morphogen gradients stay in the pool.
- **6-different (jazz improvisation).** A soloist fits the next phrase to the evolving harmony — non-stationary (the harmony moves), path-dependent (what you played constrains what fits next), local-horizon (you can't hear the whole solo at once), no central score. Scores HIGH on the must-haves — added to the pool as a dimension-agnostic (time-based) candidate, supporting 3F.

### 7. Extrapolation (Generator)

- **7G — the scorecard as a standing instrument.** Beyond this analogy hunt, the rubric grades any future thinking-space diagram, teaching image, or onboarding metaphor — a durable artifact. One line.
- **7F — the catalog enriches canon regardless.** Even with no winning analogy, the must-haves (non-stationarity+path-dependence; interleaved back-pressure; local horizon; individuation seams) are concrete dynamics that sharpen `thinking_space_dynamics.md`. Adopted as the finding's standing value.
- **7C — the over-fit failure.** A future inquiry could tune an analogy to pass all must-haves yet still mislead on something unlisted — so the scorecard ships with "necessary, not sufficient; add must-haves as new misleadings are found." One honest line.
