## Phase 3 — Ambiguity Collapse

#### Ambiguity 1: Is "sustained" ONE sense with two components, or two senses stapled?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** in strict physics NOMENCLATURE, "self-sustaining chain reaction" means k≥1 only — a bomb IS a self-sustaining chain reaction in physics texts. Component 2 (non-self-destruction) is not in the term; welding it on is retrofitting, and canon's quality bar is a separate sense that happens to be nearby.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds) — partially, and the partial win is kept:** the counter is RIGHT about bare physics nomenclature — and this becomes a register-note the finding carries. But the user's stated source is the REACTOR register ("sustained for sustrall comes from sustained atomic reactions" — reactors, not weapons; their question pairs "reactor and sustrall logics"). In reactor-ENGINEERING usage, "sustained" is what operators DO to a reaction — sustain it: keep it going at controlled power over time. A reaction that self-terminates in microseconds by blowing up its assembly is not "sustained" in any operational sense. In that register the word unites both conditions necessarily: to sustain = the reaction feeds itself (else it dies) AND does not destroy its own means of continuing (else it ends). The two components are jointly necessary conditions of one operational sense, not stapled senses.

**Confidence:** HIGH for the reactor-engineering register, WITH the register-note carried honestly (bare physics nomenclature covers component 1 only).

**Resolution:** "Sustained," in the register the name SUSTRALL draws from, = **self-sufficiency + non-self-destruction, jointly**. Canon's quality bar ("worth keeping in motion") is component 2 — doing double duty: sub-critically it means worth the human's fuel; post-critically it means the continuation must not consume the assembly (operator trust, record trustworthiness). The user's chain-reaction etymology is component 1. One sense, two necessary conditions.

**What is now fixed:** the shared-term account's shape; the bar and the etymology compose, neither replaces the other. **No longer allowed:** presenting the quality bar as a rival sense; citing bare physics nomenclature as if it carried component 2. **Depends on this:** the finding's center; the D3 reconciliation. **Model change:** the account gains a register-note that makes it more defensible, not less.
