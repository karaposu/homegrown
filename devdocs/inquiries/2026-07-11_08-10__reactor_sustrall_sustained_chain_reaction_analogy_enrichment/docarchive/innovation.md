## P1 — THE SHARED-TERM ACCOUNT (why "sustained" is the load-bearing word in both)

### The account (principal text)

In the register SUSTRALL's name draws from — reactors, not weapons — **"sustained" names one operational sense with two jointly necessary conditions:**

1. **Self-sufficiency at the threshold.** A chain reaction is *sustained* when the process's own products suffice to continue it: each fission's neutrons cause, on average, at least one further fission (k reaching 1). Below that, the reaction is real but borrowed — it runs only while an external source feeds it, and dies the moment the source stops.
2. **Non-self-destruction under control.** A bomb also satisfies condition 1 — briefly. It is a self-sufficient chain reaction that ends by *destroying its own critical assembly*: the explosion disassembles the geometry that made the chain possible, and the chain terminates with its medium. Nobody calls that sustained operation. A reactor is the same physics **held at the threshold indefinitely** — k kept at 1 by control and feedback, the assembly preserved by the very way the reaction is run.

**Register-note (carried honestly):** bare physics *nomenclature* uses "self-sustaining chain reaction" for condition 1 alone — a bomb qualifies in a textbook. The two-condition sense is the reactor-*engineering* usage — what operators mean by *sustaining* a reaction: keep it going, which requires both that it feeds itself and that it not consume its own means of continuing. The user's stated source ("sustained atomic reactions" — reactor logic) is this register.

**The composition (what this resolves in the project's vocabulary):** the user's chain-reaction etymology IS condition 1 — the aspiration that each turn's yield ignites the next without re-ignition. Canon's quality bar — *"the loop must be worth keeping in motion"* — IS condition 2, doing double duty: **sub-critically** it means worth the human's fuel (the human is the source; operator fatigue is source-exhaustion); **post-critically** it means the continuation must not consume the assembly (the record's trustworthiness, the operator's trust). The bar and the etymology are not two senses of "Sustained" — they are the two conditions of the one reactor-sense.

**The CP-1 fact and what it teaches:** the first human-made self-sustaining chain reaction (Fermi's pile, December 1942) ran for roughly half an hour at roughly half a watt. **The achievement was the threshold, not the power.** Nothing about "sustained" requires big, long, or productive-at-scale — it requires the loop honestly closed. Mapped: "reach venture" / the first system-side continuation does not need to be impressive; it needs the yield-feeds-next-turn loop genuinely closed, at whatever wattage. (This is the physics-side twin of the position statement's smallest-honest-bar reading.)

**The gained reading:** canon's "controlled whirl" — the adjective canon chose unprompted — reads as **controlled criticality**: the whole engineering, in both domains, is *sitting at the threshold under control* — not above it (bomb / junk-cascade), not below it (dies / stalls), but exactly at it, held there by feedback.

### Core-paragraph wording variants (for the gate)

- **V-contrast (bomb-first):** "A bomb is a self-sufficient chain reaction; nobody calls it sustained. It ends in microseconds by blowing apart the very assembly that made it possible. 'Sustained' — in the reactor register SUSTRALL's name comes from — means BOTH things at once: the reaction feeds itself, AND it does not destroy its own means of continuing. That second condition is canon's quality bar; the first is the chain-reaction aspiration. One word, two conditions, and the whole discipline is holding the threshold under control."
- **V-threshold (CP-1-first):** "The first sustained chain reaction ran for twenty-eight minutes at half a watt — the achievement was the threshold, not the power: the process's own products sufficed, for the first time, to continue it. But the threshold alone is not the word's whole content: a bomb crosses the same threshold and ends by destroying its own assembly. 'Sustained' means self-feeding AND self-preserving — the chain-reaction aspiration plus the worth-keeping-in-motion bar, composed."

### Piece-level Inversion (meta-decision; assumption reversed: "one sense, two conditions")

**Inversion-candidate:** drop condition 2 — SUSTRALL's "Sustained" = pure self-sufficiency; the quality bar is a separate norm that merely coexists.
**What follows:** the bar dangles unexplained beside the name (why would "SUStained" carry a worth-bar at all?); the bomb-contrast loses its teaching role; and the account contradicts the user's own register (they named reactors, whose usage carries both). **5-test:** fails scrutiny (the register) + fertility (loses the composition). **REJECTED** — with the register-note preserving the honest kernel the inversion pointed at (bare nomenclature does read condition-1-only).

## P4 — THE BREAK-LIST (first-class; each break with its rider)

1. **E1 — Quality (the hardest break).** Neutrons are identical; findings are not. A chain of junk traverses is numerically self-sustaining (k≥1) and worthless. Physics' k *counts*; SUSTRALL's continuation must *count and judge*. **THE QUALITY-RIDER:** every k-statement in project use carries the qualifier *"…of gate-passing quality"* (or equivalent) — a continuation only counts if the triggering yield survived its gates. Nothing in the reactor models a fission that "shouldn't have counted"; this rider is the analogy's permanent tax.
2. **E2 — Measured vs judged.** k is a gauge reading; the SUSTRALL-analog ("did this turn's yield trigger the next without human injection?") is a **judged estimate over a countable record** — countable because recorded turns name which routes fired; judged because attribution has edges (did the human's seed relay a route, or re-frame it?). Usable as vocabulary and rough instrumentation; never decimals, never imported constants.
3. **E5 — The breeding asymmetry.** In a reactor, excess neutron *population* is the danger and is throttled. In SUSTRALL, questions BREED promiscuously and *should* — a good finding opening many frontiers is health, not excursion. **The control point differs: reactors throttle the population; SUSTRALL throttles DISPATCH (selection), never question-birth.** Any control-vocabulary import must be worded at the dispatch point.
4. **E6 — The stakes-rider.** The failure *structures* transfer (positive feedback, pit-fighting, prompt-firing); the nuclear *stakes* do not. Degradation here is weeks-scale and recoverable; no urgency rhetoric rides in.
5. **E7 — Theorems-never.** Vocabulary and heuristics transfer; formulas, thresholds, and physics quantities do not (canon's own transfer rule for this problem-class).
6. **★E3 — The product-role question, developed BOTH ways (the gate grades):**
   - **Reading 1 (MATCH):** both domains sustain the chain FOR the aggregate of per-event yield — a power reactor's product is the sum of per-fission energy; SUSTRALL's product is the sum of per-traverse understanding. In both, the chain is instrumental; the yield is the point. The structural parallel is exact at this altitude.
   - **Reading 2 (BREAK):** the aggregation differs in kind — energy is fungible and measurable (watts add), understanding is non-fungible and judged (findings don't "add"; they compose, supersede, correct). A reactor's output can be summed by a meter; a venture's output must be *read*. So "the chain is instrumental to its yield" matches, while "the yield aggregates" does not.
   - **Delivered material:** the two readings are not exclusive — Reading 1 holds at the instrumental-role altitude; Reading 2 marks where the parallel stops (the aggregation arithmetic). Proposed landing for the gate: MATCH-at-role, BREAK-at-aggregation, stated as one graded row.

## P3 — THE DESIGN-YIELD (two ordered claims + the guard)

**Claim 1 — MEDIUM-NECESSITY.** In a reactor the chain's medium is physical proximity: neutrons fly from fission to fission; the reaction needs no memory because the universe transmits its triggers. **SUSTRALL's chain has no proximity.** A turn's yield reaches the next turn only through **record + recall** — something must carry "what this turn produced and opened" to "what the next turn takes up." Traversal memory is therefore not a support organ standing BESIDE the chain; **it is the chain's MEDIUM** — no medium, no chain, regardless of how good the yield is.

**Claim 1's tier-index (from the inversion below — kept):** the chain always runs through a memory-medium, but the medium is not necessarily the *built* one. **Today the medium is substantially the human's memory** (the sample's own cast table: traversal memory = "mostly the user's head"; the load-bearing 32-day recall was a human act, record-assisted). The traversal-memory build is thus **the medium's transfer, not an accessory's addition** — which is exactly why "remembering" is one of canon's five transfer-cargos: the cargo IS the chain's transmission.

**Claim 2 — DELAY-BENEFIT (the design-rider).** The medium's read-later delay is SUSTRALL's **delayed-neutron fraction**. In a reactor, the small fraction of neutrons that arrive seconds-to-minutes late is the entire reason humans can steer the chain; a reactor critical on prompt neutrons alone is uncontrollable. In SUSTRALL, yield lands as artifacts read later — and that gap between yield and uptake is where ALL between-turn steering lives (selection, gating, redirection). **A memory design that auto-fired next turns the instant yield landed would be prompt-critical: faster, and unsteerable.** The delay is a feature to preserve in any future memory design, not a latency to engineer away.

**The suspicion-guard (verbatim-honest).** This re-framing is meaning-layer: it informs the release-condition's *design-fit* half (when the venture structural layer is designed, memory sits at its center, not its periphery). It does **not** bypass *exercise-fit*; memory DESIGNS remain suspicious and user-gated; **design-centrality ≠ build-now** — the standing order (design against a real venture-run) is untouched.

**The one-line compression (the P3→P2 export; no new predicates):**
> *the record ↔ the chain's medium (yield reaches the next turn only through record + recall; today substantially the human's memory) — whose read-later delay is the delayed-neutron fraction: the property that makes the whirl steerable.*

### Piece-level Inversion (meta-decision; assumption reversed: "the record is the medium")

**Inversion-candidate:** the record is NOT the medium — **the HUMAN is**: at Level 0 the chain demonstrably runs through the user's head (the sample's decisive recall was human memory; the record only assisted).
**What follows:** the medium-claim as first stated over-credits the built record TODAY. **Test:** the inversion is half-right and **improves the claim** — the necessity holds for *a* memory-medium (no transmission, no chain — true at every tier); WHICH medium carries it is tier-indexed (human now → built later). **KEPT as the tier-index folded into Claim 1** (above). This also strengthens coherence with canon: it explains why "remembering" is a cargo at all.

### The brief-mandated center-Inversion (adversarial companion to the map)
