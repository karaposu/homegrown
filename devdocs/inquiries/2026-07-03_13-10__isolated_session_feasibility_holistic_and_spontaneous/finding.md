## Finding

### 1. Where memory-responsibility actually lives

Start from what canon already commits. The north star's delegation map is explicit: the system owns the SUBCONSCIOUS work — structured thinking (the disciplines), pattern-following composition (the runners), durable artifacts (the inquiry folders) — while the human is the CONSCIOUSNESS layer: spontaneous attention, valuation, real-time steering, cross-inquiry memory, goal-formation. That is not an aspiration; it is the Level-0 architecture, and the between-inquiries gap is named in the same document as SUSTRALL's target. So the question "can the session's context carry it all?" has a design answer before any mechanism is examined: it was never asked to. Around the context sit six other organs — scent-bearing summaries, pointers and supersession chains, the route-maps, `_state.md`, the read-surfaces, the protocols — and the seventh is you.

The steelman deserves its full run here: *only in-context tokens influence the next token; organs on disk are potential energy; a lazy session reads nothing and the system is inert.* All true — and it is exactly why the design's whole effort goes into **staged loading**: the warm-up doctrine stages the first load, and the programs force every later one (surfacing's territory sweep is a mandatory step of /traverse, not a suggestion; the Synthesis Trigger derives mechanically from the articulation; CONCLUDE's template refuses a finding without the inheritance re-test). Nothing works from disk; everything works by getting loaded on schedule. What survives of the steelman is one honest residue: **all of this is one-invocation-deep.** A session nobody pointed at a runner or a warm-up gets none of it. That is the design's shape seen from its exposed side — a human-energized system SHOULD concentrate its required discipline into one typed line — and the line is genuinely load-bearing.

### 2. Holistic understanding — the gap this inquiry found and fixed

Holistic decomposes into where-it-WAS and where-it-GOING, and checking the shipped tiers at the letter found a real hole: the full-read read views + frontier — the settled story and the live edge — while the DIRECTION layer sat unread in every tier. It existed all along: the canon documents state the objective; the route-maps hold the open field (26 live maps, 2,370 lines of typed onward routes). The fix, sized honestly:

> **Step 0 (the direction layer):** read the direction documents — the canon that states the objective and the loop-of-loops direction (today: `project_north_star.md` + `sustained_traversal_loop_of_loops.md`; if canon reorganizes, the direction role is whatever states objective + direction). Then scan the digest-window maps' open routes (format-contract: open = an At-a-glance row whose ✓ column — the last — is empty; the exact grep ships tested at build).

~600 lines. The full-read becomes: **direction → views → frontier**, with the order line earning its place against a known long-context effect: *direction first, views middle, frontier last — the live edge sits where attention is freshest; the settled past, the most redundant layer (tagged, pointed, regenerable), absorbs the middle.* Numbers, un-laundered: today ≈ 3.2k (zero views exist; direction + pure frontier); at ~5-view maturity ≈ 3.6–3.9k. And one line for the light tier: the digest header — already carrying `frontier: N` — gains `direction: docs/canon/project_north_star.md`, so even a digest-only session knows where steering lives.

### 3. Spontaneous attention — the split, then the shares

The phrase names two capabilities. The STANDING kind — a watcher that notices, unprompted, across sessions, that today's work touches an old constraint — is the consciousness-layer capability, and canon assigns it to the human at Level 0. What an isolated session has is weaker and real: **within-window salience.** Everything the warm-up loaded is attended at every forward pass — that is how transformers work — so a loaded frontier summary can light up mid-task without being asked. Honest bounds: the effect degrades with context length and position (a known long-context effect, not measured locally), and it covers only the loaded surface. This is the same mechanism the closed trail rides — in-window presence keeps evidence attendable — one fact serving two consumers: there, the critique step's ability to kill revived dead claims; here, the worker's chance of noticing an old detail.

Beyond the window, recall is deliberate, and the design does not leave "deliberate" to mood: every inquiry's surfacing step re-greps the territory fresh (recall re-derived per question); the Synthesis Trigger forces the named priors into context; the supersession walk re-tags what moved. The session never REMEMBERS the project; it re-derives the relevant slice, on schedule, from artifacts built to make re-derivation cheap — which is what the summaries, pointers, and chains are for.

### 4. The verdict

**Feasible — for the session's share.** With step 0 in place, a cold full-read session holds the objective and open field, the settled story, and the raw live edge in ~3.2k lines; everything deeper is one pointer away, and the programs force the reads at the right moments. The two limits, plainly: standing cross-session noticing remains the human's — by canon's assignment, not by this design's failure — **and the verdict is the current rung, not the ceiling: growing the system's share of the watching is what the SUSTRALL program (traversal memory; the navigation-session role, already canon-defined with its question-list) is for.** And protection is one-invocation-deep: free-form sessions get none of it. The system is one right invocation away from working — and one lazy invocation away from inert.
