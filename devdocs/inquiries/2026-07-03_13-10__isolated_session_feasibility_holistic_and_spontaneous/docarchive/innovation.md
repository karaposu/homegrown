## Phase 2 — Generate (principal content + mandated inversions)

### (a) The responsibility paragraph

> Your premise — "all responsibility will be with the context of the isolated AI session" — contradicts the architecture's own design, and in a reassuring direction. The north star assigns the roles explicitly: the SYSTEM handles the subconscious work (structured thinking in the disciplines, pattern-following in the runners, durable artifacts in the inquiry folders), and **the HUMAN is the consciousness layer: spontaneous attention, valuation, real-time steering, cross-inquiry memory, goal-formation** — that is canon, today, at Level 0. Around the session's context sit six other memory-organs: scent-bearing summaries, pointers and supersession chains, route-maps, `_state.md` resume, the read-surfaces (digest and views), and the protocols. And the protocols don't wait for the session to feel like using them — inside a runner, activation is spec-forced: surfacing's territory sweep is a mandatory step of /traverse, the Synthesis Trigger derives mechanically from the articulation, CONCLUDE refuses to close without the inheritance accounting. **One concession the steelman earned:** all of this is one-invocation-deep — a session started with no runner and no warm-up gets none of it. Responsibility doesn't spread across the session's whole behavior; it concentrates into the first line typed.

### (b) The holistic paragraph (the gap found and fixed)

> Holistic understanding decomposes into where-it-WAS and where-it-GOING — and checking the shipped tiers at the letter found a real hole: the full-read reads views + frontier, which is where-it-was ONLY. The direction layer existed all along (the canon docs state the objective; the route-maps hold the open field — 26 live maps, 2,370 lines) but no tier read it. The fix is step 0: read the direction documents (~500–700 lines) and scan the recent maps' open routes, THEN the views, THEN the frontier. Full-read ≈ 3.1–3.4k lines today — still a tenth of the raw read, and now actually holistic: the objective, the settled story, the live edge, the open field.

### (c) The spontaneous paragraph (the split, then the shares)

> "Spontaneous attention" names two different things, and the answer must not blur them. The STANDING kind — a watcher that notices, unprompted, across sessions, that new work touches an old constraint — is the consciousness-layer capability, and canon assigns it to YOU at Level 0 ("nothing watches between inquiries yet — precisely what SUSTRALL's traversal memory changes"). What the session has is weaker and real: **within-window salience.** Every token loaded at warm-up is attended at every forward pass — a frontier summary can light up mid-work, unasked, because attention runs over the whole window continuously. That is genuine spontaneity, honestly bounded: it degrades with length and position (long-context retrieval weakens toward the middle), and it covers only what the warm-up loaded. Beyond the window, recall is DELIBERATE and the protocols force it at the right moments: every inquiry's surfacing step re-greps the territory fresh; the Synthesis Trigger names the priors; the supersession walk re-tags the moved. The session doesn't remember the project — it re-derives the relevant slice per question, from artifacts built to make re-derivation cheap.

### (d) The verdict paragraph

> Feasible — **for the session's share, which is the share the architecture actually assigns.** With step 0 in place, a cold full-read session holds the objective, the open field, the settled story, and the raw live edge in ~3.1–3.4k lines, and every deeper need is one pointer away with the programs forcing the reads. Two limits, stated without flinching: (1) standing cross-session noticing is the human's today — by canon's own assignment — and growing the system's share of it is what SUSTRALL's traversal memory is FOR (the roadmap's named frontier, not this design's surprise failure); (2) protection is one-invocation-deep — a free-form session with no runner and no warm-up is exactly as blind as your worry fears. The system is one right invocation away from working, and one lazy invocation away from inert.
