### Considered Articulations

1. **Feasible-with-mechanism reading:** attention over loaded summaries IS spontaneity; drilling is depth; verdict feasible — show the mechanisms.

3. **Honest-ceiling reading:** feasible for X (awareness, scent-triggered recall, forced re-checks), infeasible for Y (unasked cross-links whose scent isn't loaded) — name both sides.
4. **Needs-decomposition reading:** holistic and spontaneous are DIFFERENT problems with different mechanisms — answer each separately, then compose the verdict.
