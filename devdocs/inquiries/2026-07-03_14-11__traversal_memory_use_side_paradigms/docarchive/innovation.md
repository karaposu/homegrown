### The mandated inversion (the AMBIENT cluster)

**Steelman:** "AMBIENT conflates two passivities — a deliberately LOADED surface (a warm-up act happened) vs marks encountered incidentally IN the territory (no act at all); grab-bag?"
**Engagement:** the cluster's unifying fact is real — during work, no per-need act occurs; the material is already present and reaches attention by itself. What differs is WHERE it sits: in-window vs in-territory. That is a within-cluster distinction the rows themselves carry, not a cluster split (splitting would make two one-row clusters and lose the shared operation).
**Resolution:** the cluster keeps its unity, renamed to make the passivity honest — **SITS WITH YOU** — and each row's claim names its WHERE. *(Kept; the kernel became the rows' wording.)*

### (b) The composite section

> The current design is not one paradigm — it is a COMPOSITE occupying six-plus positions: U2 (the digest + views — the protocol chain), U4 (forced per-question greps — surfacing's spec), U5 (route-maps and pointer-drilling — the routelister layer), U6 (the warm-up's standing surface — the feasibility finding), U10-human (the consciousness layer — the north star), U11-as-doctrine (warming — committed, unpracticed), plus one U3 rule (the order line) and weak U7 (marks read in passing). Judging "the current paradigm" against alternatives is a category error — the real questions are per-seat: which OCCUPIED positions underperform, and which EMPTY seats would pay.

### (c) The empty seats (the use-side's open field)

1. **U8 push-driven** — the seat canon itself names empty: a watcher that interrupts sessions (or the user) when new work touches old constraints. SUSTRALL's traversal memory is its designed occupant.
2. **U9 prefetch** — warm-ups chosen by PREDICTED task instead of fixed tier: "today you're consolidating X — here's X's view, its open routes, and the two contested chains."
3. **U10-session** — the navigation-session oracle: a dedicated isolated session that HOLDS the cross-run picture and answers workers' questions (canon-defined role, unbuilt).
4. **U3-authored** — real onboarding tours: an ordered reading path FOR COMPREHENSION of the whole project (the order-rule is one sentence of this family).

- **Spaced re-exposure / rehearsal** — excluded, failed conjunct named: the practice has no retaining subject between exposures (sessions are stateless); its time-trigger kernel lives inside U8's rhythm half.
