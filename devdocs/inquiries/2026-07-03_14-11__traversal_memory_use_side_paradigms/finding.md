### 2. The composite (where the design actually sits)

The current design spans **U2 + U4 + U5 + U6 fully, U10's human half, U11 as doctrine, one U3 rule, and weak U7** — eight touches across six clusters. This is why "should we switch paradigms?" is the wrong question shape: there is no single incumbent. The workable questions are per-seat: *which occupied seats underperform* (e.g., U7's structure-teaches-little), and *which empty seats would pay* (§3).

### 3. The empty seats (the use-side's open field)

1. **U8 push-driven** — the seat canon itself names empty: a watcher interrupting sessions (or you) when new work touches old constraints. SUSTRALL's traversal memory is its designed occupant.
2. **U9 prefetch** — warm-ups chosen by predicted task instead of fixed tier: "today you're consolidating X — here is X's view, its open routes, its two contested chains."
3. **U10-session** — the navigation-session oracle: an isolated session that HOLDS the cross-run picture and answers workers' questions (canon-defined, unbuilt; its first probe is the routed cold run).
4. **U3-authored** — a real onboarding tour: an ordered reading path for comprehension of the whole project.

### 4. The exclusion

**Spaced re-exposure / rehearsal** — excluded, failed conjunct named: no retaining subject between exposures in a stateless-session practice; its time-trigger kernel lives inside U8's rhythm half.
