### Collapse 2 — protocol's fault or execution's?
**Resolution: SPLIT, precisely.** Proximate cause = execution habits (worklist pre-selection, mode minimization, the merge). The answer to "is it protocols fault?" is: **the protocol's text says the right thing and lacks the enforcement surfaces to make execution do it.** **HIGH.**

### Collapse 3 — what's the honest yield-expectation? (the contrarian, run alongside)
**Resolution: BOUNDED, not inflated.** Strong absorbers are real; the fix must not promise 5+/paper. The counterfactual supports **1–4 records per fresh, relevant source** as the natural range (variance restored — 0 stays possible; 1 stays common; 2–3 become reachable; the current invariant "exactly 1, always" is the anomaly being fixed, not the floor being raised). ### Collapse 4 — does fixing this require loosening the gate?
**Resolution: NO.** Every fix candidate operates before the gate (coverage), around it (granularity, second-harvest), or after it (telemetry visibility); the gate's conditions stay exactly as they are. The user's stake ("otherwise this whole process doesnt mean much") is answered by breadth + visibility, not by lowering the bar — a lowered bar would destroy the credibility that makes a seed worth recording at all. **HIGH.**
