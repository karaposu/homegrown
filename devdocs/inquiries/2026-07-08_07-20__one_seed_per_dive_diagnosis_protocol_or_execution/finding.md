# Finding: why every harvest run yields exactly one seed — and the fix

## Question

The user's method-critique: all four harvest runs (papers 21 → 24) produced exactly one seed each; highly relevant academic sources should yield multiple seeds at least; find the reason — is it the protocol's fault? The stake: without the capability of listing multiple seeds, useful seeds are being dumped and the process loses its point.

**Background for a fresh reader.** The paper-harvest extracts gated "seeds" (anchored maybe-hypotheses) from studied sources via the seed_harvester protocol riding a /traverse dive: a crossing step GENERATES candidate germs (source claims × project anchors), a gate at Critique KILLS or passes them, survivors are RECORDED in the finding and a global index. Four protocol runs exist; every one recorded exactly one seed.

- **THE FIX PACKAGE is drafted ready-to-apply** (7 protocol edits, ~13 lines total when batched with the 6 already-pending touch-ups, + 2 execution rules + 1 index convention). Its success criterion: **restored VARIANCE at unchanged gate-quality** — 1–4 records per fresh relevant source, 0–1 per re-pass; never a quota.
- **One recovery is recommended** (user-approved): un-merge p21's C2 into its own record (p21-S2) — the one candidate that passed the gate and still didn't get an index entry.

## Finding

### 1. The numbers (file-verified, from the four dives' archived artifacts)

| Run | Source | Innovation mode | Generated | Gate result | Records |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| n=1 | paper 21 (fresh) | Generator-weighted (full breadth, 7/7 mechanisms) | 7 candidates + 4 probes | 1 germ passed; 3 killed; **the assembly had MERGED 3 gate-worthy site-candidates into that 1 germ** | 1 |
| n=2 | paper 21 re-read | minimum-mechanism — *"a 7-mechanism sweep over a single evidence-grade item would manufacture"* | 1 | 1 killed (fold, sound) | 0 + 1 enrichment |
| n=3 | paper 23 (fresh) | minimum-mechanism-plus — *"one hard candidate + probes; a full sweep would manufacture"* | 1 hard + 3 probes | 1 passed; 1 folded | 1 |
| n=4 | paper 24 = 16 (re-pass) | minimal — only changed anchors generate | 1 + 1 probe | 1 passed; 1 killed | 1 |

The decisive comparison is n=3: paper 23's surfacing produced **nine core claims and seven live anchors**; the protocol's own §6 mandates the crossing *"per major source claim, the three moves, one-to-many, ungated"* — executed honestly that is on the order of **27 crossing-attempts. Four pre-selected worklist entries ran.** The narrowing happened at sensemaking ("the crossing worklist… NOTHING ELSE"), before generation began.

### 2. The chain (each link with its evidence)

1. **Worklist pre-selection** (the largest and least visible drop): sensemaking closed the crossing field before innovation ran — n=3's and n=4's "NOTHING ELSE" clauses. The protocol never instructs this; its §2 rule 2 names it as the failure it is: *"the gate applied inside the generative step strangles the divergence."* The anti-manufacturing guard was applied at the wrong stage, disguised as proportionality.
2. **Mode minimization ratified it:** after n=1, every dive chose a reduced generation mode with the same justification; "do not stop at the first candidate" (§2 rule 1, verbatim) inverted in practice into *stop once THE candidate is identified* — the singularization is visible in the artifacts ("THE candidate", pre-gate).
3. **Record-granularity compression:** n=1's one broad run generated three gate-worthy site-candidates (source-selection; seed-selection; the adaptive form) and merged them into one record ("one lever… one record, plural sites") — defensible, but no rule governed it, and it halved-to-thirded the one run that had breadth.
4. **The satisficing seal:** once one candidate survives, no step asks *"what else does this source offer?"* A one-seed finding looks complete; the pattern had no internal error signal and ran four times. **Your complaint is the error signal, arriving from outside — exactly the no-feedback structure p23-S1 transferred from the Einstellung paper, now observed at the harvest's own yield layer.**
5. **Band-anchoring** (subordinate, MED evidence): every dive's forecast band was never exceeded and in-band was always credited; nothing ever said overshoot-with-gate-quality is a win.

### 3. The fault attribution (the answer to "is it protocols fault?")

- **The protocol's generation text: exonerated.** It says the right things, verbatim: one-to-many is intrinsic; don't stop at the first candidate; never gate during generation.
- **The protocol's completeness: guilty of four gaps.** No anchor-side breadth rule ("per major source claim" — but never *per hot anchor*); no coverage accounting (nothing shows attempted-vs-possible); no record-granularity rule (the merge answered to nothing); no §9 failure mode for pre-gate narrowing (the table catches "no crossings" and "manufactured seeds" — not "crossings ran on a pre-selected field"). The text said the right thing and lacked the enforcement surfaces to make execution do it.
- **The execution habits: the proximate cause.** The worklists, the modes, the merge are all in my run-artifacts — each locally reasoned, jointly producing the invariant.
- **The gate: exonerated kill-by-kill.** C4 (decorative speculation — the move-sweep already exists), C5 (no decision at either bar), C6 (the practice literally exists), n=2's ablation candidate (the pattern is specified in the innovate spec — quoted), n=4's allocator (owned territory + premature twice). None was a dumped seed.

### 4. THE FIX PACKAGE (drafted, ready to apply — your call; nothing applied yet)

**Protocol edits (7; batchable with the 6 already-pending one-liners → one ~13-line edit session):**

1. **§2 rule 1, append:** "Run the crossing over an explicit coverage table — every major claim × every hot anchor: each cell either attempted (a stated hypothesis-sentence, however weak) or skipped with a stated reason. The interesting candidates are often in cells the first pass would have skipped."
2. **§6 Sensemaking row, append:** "Sensemaking stabilizes both crossing-inputs and may flag likely-hot cells — it never closes the field; the crossing worklist is the coverage table, owned by Innovation."
3. **§6 Innovation row, append:** "…run over the §2 coverage table; skips carry reasons."
4. **§7, after the schema:** "Granularity: distinct anchor ⇒ distinct record (cross-reference kin records); merging sites into one record requires explicit in-record justification."
5. **§9, new row #8 — Pre-gate narrowing:** recognition: "the crossing field was selected before the crossing ran (pre-picked worklists; 'THE candidate' singularized upstream; modes sized to one candidate on a fresh source); candidate-count ≈ survivor-count on a rich source" · corrective: "re-open generation with the coverage table; the anti-manufacturing guard's home is the GATE and the RECORD, never generation (§2 rule 2)."
6. **§9 #6, append:** "…and an above-band yield where every record is gate-clean is equally valid — never trim records to stay in-band."
7. **§10, extend telemetry:** "coverage: cells attempted/possible (+ skip-reason count); after the first survivor, the field-level second-harvest question was asked (answer may be 'nothing')."

**Execution rules (no protocol text needed):** fresh sources default to Generator-weighted generation — anything less must be justified cell-by-cell in the coverage table; "THE candidate" singularization is banned from pre-gate artifacts.

**Index convention (F9):** at each append, note the trailing per-source yield distribution; **zero variance across ≥4 fresh sources = a standing flag** — the pattern that ran four dives unnoticed becomes self-announcing.

**What was deliberately NOT included:** any quota (killed as manufacturing-by-mandate); any gate change (the gate is the product's credibility); the "accept 1/dive" do-nothing option (killed against your consumer-report and two facts it can't explain).

### 5. The honest expectation (so the fix isn't oversold)

The absorbers are real — the harness is mature, and many crossings genuinely fold. The counterfactual (n=3 under the coverage rule) supports **1–4 records per fresh relevant source, 0–1 per re-pass** — not 5+. The package's success signal is **variance**: if the next fresh harvests produce 0, 2, 3, 1 with a full coverage table and unchanged gate rigor, the fix worked; if they produce exactly 1 forever with full tables, that too is data (and F9 will flag it).

### 6. Recovery (routed, user-decides)

- **RECOMMENDED — p21-S2 via a small user-approved step:** C2 (the seed-selection site: occasionally develop a *random* nascent seed from the index instead of the top-priority one) passed the gate inside the merged germ and lost its index entry to the merge. Un-merging it is a one-step re-record with provenance, not a re-litigation.
- **Optional — the F8 retro-recovery dive:** one bounded dive re-running crossing+gate over papers 21+23's uncrossed fields under the coverage table (hindsight-guard: gate unchanged; most cells expected to fold). Also serves as the package's calibration test on known material. Alternative: forward-only.
- **Not recovered:** the p23 kill-reopen texture (re-checked under the new granularity rule — still fails the news test; stays folded, now with a named rule behind it).
