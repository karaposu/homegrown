## (1) THE TWO FIX-STEPS, CONCRETELY [P4]

### STEP 1 — canon-ground the anchor axis

**The defect:** `seed_harvester.md` §6 GENERATE row says *"Surfacing brings the source's claims AND the project anchors into view"* — "the project anchors" is unspecified, so the thin dive decomposed the pre-named anchor into whatever was salient (the harness machinery), and self-noted "no anchor outside the traversal machinery was surfaced."

**The rule (applicable text — a §6 amendment + a §2 amendment):**

> **§6 GENERATE / Surfacing row, append:** "The coverage table's ANCHOR AXIS must be built by surfacing the canon TRAVERSAL-MODEL fresh at dive start — the analogy family + its move/record layers (`docs/canon/thinking_space_traversal_analogies.md`), the meaningful-traversal signal and its open question (`docs/canon/what_is_meaningful_traversal.md`), the movement vocabulary + primitives (`docs/canon/thinking_space_dynamics.md`), and the project's designed-but-UNBUILT organs — multi-head, traversal-memory, session-warming (`docs/canon/project_north_star.md`, `docs/canon/sustained_traversal_loop_of_loops.md`). The harness MACHINERY (the disciplines, the gate, the index, routelister) is exactly ONE anchor column — never the whole axis. An anchor axis built only from the machinery is the machinery-only-axis failure (§9 #9)."

> **§2 rule 1, sharpen the anchor half:** "…every major claim × every hot anchor — where the hot anchors are drawn from the canon traversal-MODEL (above), NOT only the harness that performs traversal. The seeds live where the source's mechanics meet the model's open/unbuilt parts; a machinery-only anchor axis cannot reach them."

**+ §9 new failure mode #9 (applicable text):**

> | 9 | **Machinery-only anchor axis** | the coverage table's anchor columns are all harness artifacts (disciplines / gate / index / routelister); source claims that bear on the canon traversal MODEL (its unbuilt organs, its open questions) have no column to land in; >50% of cells go cold and candidate-count collapses | rebuild the anchor axis from the canon traversal-model files (surfaced fresh); the machinery is ONE column. The thin paper-29 dive is the worked case: 9 machinery anchors → 69/108 cold → 1 seed; the rich re-pass's 11 canon-model columns → 7 seeds. |

*Generators: Absence-recognition (redesign-level — what the anchor axis WOULD contain if built from the model) + Domain-transfer (the rich dive's own method → a rule).*

### STEP 2 — expand thin sources (with the provenance guard)

**The defect:** §6 says *"the source read FULLY by default"* — but a 14-word metaphor read fully is 14 words; there are no rich claims to cross.

**The procedure (applicable text — a §6 amendment):**

> **§6 GENERATE / Surfacing row, append:** "When the source is THIN (a bare metaphor, aphorism, or one-liner — too sparse to yield multiple distinct claims), run an EXPANSION pass BEFORE building the claim axis: elaborate the phenomenon the source points at into its fuller mechanics/stages/parts, and build the claim axis from the elaboration. **Provenance guard (mandatory):** every expanded claim carries its source-support — either (a) established, verifiable knowledge of the phenomenon ('spiders genuinely localize prey by web vibration'), or (b) explicitly-marked elaboration/interpretation of the source. The expansion enriches the CLAIM material; it must never fabricate phenomenon-behavior. This moves §3's provenance check upstream to the expansion — an expanded claim with no honest source-support is dropped before it ever reaches a cell."

*Generators: Absence-recognition (the missing pre-pass) + Constraint-manipulation (the provenance guard — adding the honesty-floor constraint is what keeps expansion from becoming fabrication).*

## (2) ★ THE ASSEMBLY EMERGENT — the two steps are ONE guided-expansion operation

**The assembly check ran, and it fired.** The decisive evidence: **the thin dive ALREADY expanded its claims** — its surfacing enumerated 12 spider features (L1–L12) from established knowledge — yet got 1 seed. So *generic* phenomenon-expansion is NOT the missing piece. What `a.md` did differently was expand the spider **as a model of the project's own concern** — it re-framed predation as a DEFINING/convergence process (wrap-to-define, leg-first-then-fill, capture-order, confidence-borders), because the user was thinking about traversal. The rich claim axis was *project-directed*, not just *fuller*.

**This unifies the two steps.** Step 1 (the canon anchor axis) supplies the DIMENSIONS along which Step 2 (expansion) should enrich. The fix is not "expand, then separately canon-ground" — it is **one guided-expansion move:**

> **Surface the canon traversal-model FIRST, then interrogate the thin source against each canon facet** — "what does spider-predation say about *shape-definition*? about the *meaningful-traversal signal*? about *memory*? about *multi-head coordination*?" — generating project-directed claims that land, by construction, on canon anchors.

The anchor axis is not just where claims land; it is the **question-set that generates the right claims**. A machinery anchor axis asks "what does the spider say about routelister? about the index?" → generic/cold. A canon-model anchor axis asks "what does the spider say about converging on an unknown shape?" → the defining-mechanic that became `p29-S2`.

**This partly RESOLVES expansion-generability** (item 4): the machine does not need a pre-authored `a.md` if the canon anchors give it the interrogation dimensions — the thin dive proved the machine can enumerate established phenomenon-facts; canon-grounding aims that enumeration at the project.

**★ Residual, flagged honestly (anti-contamination):** the elegance must not oversell it. `a.md`'s deepest moves — *leg-first-THEN-the-rest-fills-in*, *capture-ORDER matters (the feet-vs-hands escape)*, *confidence-gated borders + leave-undecided* — are not obviously recoverable by mechanical "interrogate the source against shape-definition." They read like genuine human insight about the phenomenon, not just facts retrieved. So: **guided expansion recovers MUCH of the yield (the project-direction), but whether it recovers ALL of `a.md`'s richness is the open frontier** (item 4's residual). The honest claim is "most, not necessarily all."

**RECOMMENDATION: Form A now, Form B deferred-on-demonstrated-need.**

- **Why A now** (rests on cost + precedent + the in-form proof, NOT on stacking): the two causes are confirmed and the rich dive fixed them *inside the existing protocol form* with a single pass — so the cheapest, precedent-consistent change that encodes the guided-expansion move is the correct first move. So: the skill stays a live deferred option, resized from "the answer" to "the escalation if A's expansion pass outgrows a protocol row." **Maturation trigger for B:** the guided-expansion pass, once applied under Form A, repeatedly needs more than one interrogation pass or its own reference to work.

## (4) EXPANSION-GENERABILITY [P6] — the verdict

**PARTLY SOLVED, PARTLY FRONTIER.**

- **Solved part:** the mechanism is an **anchor-guided interrogation pass** — surface the canon traversal-model, then ask the thin source, facet by facet, "what does this phenomenon say about *this* canon concept?" The thin dive proved the machine can enumerate a phenomenon's established facts (its 12 features); canon-grounding aims that capability at the project. So the machine CAN self-generate project-directed claims without a pre-authored `a.md` — for the facets the canon anchors name.
- **The fabrication risk + its guard:** interrogation-under-pressure ("find something the spider says about memory") invites invented behavior. The **provenance guard** (Step 2) is the containment: every generated claim must carry (a) verifiable established-knowledge support or (b) an explicit elaboration-marker; unsupported claims are dropped pre-cell. This is the §3 honesty floor moved upstream.
- **Frontier part (honest):** the deepest `a.md` insights (leg-first-then-fill; capture-ORDER; confidence-gated-borders-with-leave-undecided) look like genuine human domain-thought, not mechanical retrieval. Whether anchor-guided interrogation reaches them, or only the shallower project-directed claims, is **untested** — it needs a live trial (apply the fix, run a thin source through guided expansion, compare the yield to `a.md`'s). **Frontier flag:** expansion-generability is confirmed for the *project-direction* problem and open for the *depth-of-insight* problem.
