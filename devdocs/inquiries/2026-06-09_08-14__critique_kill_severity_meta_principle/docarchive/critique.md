### Frame-premise prosecutions (D8 operationalization)

Three what-if-wrong prosecutions, run independently of testing the candidates:

**Premise (a): "Every candidate has a clearly definable purpose."**
- *What-if-wrong:* candidates with intentionally fuzzy purposes exist (exploratory ideation, art critique, ethical deliberation).
- *Evidence:* P-FAP frontier piece names this exact category; outcome was defer-out-of-scope, which honestly bounds the principle's scope. The premise is not claimed universally — it's claimed for /td-critique's terminal-candidate scope.
- *Result:* SURVIVES. The principle doesn't claim universal purpose-clarity; it scopes itself.

**Premise (b): "Purpose-fitness is binary (defect blocks purpose or it doesn't)."**
- *What-if-wrong:* defects that PARTIALLY block purpose (a software bug causing occasional slowdowns; an ambiguous contract clause interpretable in context; a research methodology with minor bias that doesn't invalidate conclusions).
- *Evidence:* The spec's existing SURVIVE-with-caveats language (line 234) handles degradation cases. But this means purpose-fitness has an IMPLICIT THRESHOLD ("fatal" vs "degraded") that isn't fully articulated.
- *Result:* PARTIAL SURVIVES. The binary framing is approximately right but elides the "fatal threshold" judgment. The principle should be refined: a defect is kill-worthy iff it CRITICALLY (not just partially) blocks purpose-fulfillment.

**Premise (c): "Stakes (composite calibration) is per-task; meta is single-axis."**
- *What-if-wrong:* a task surfaces a structural axis that DOESN'T fit the composite framing (ethics: deontological violations are severe regardless of likelihood; security: exploitability composite includes discoverability).
- *Evidence:* For ethics, deontological violations CAN be cast as a calibration axis ("deontological-violation-presence"), preserving purpose-fitness ("be ethically acceptable"). For security, exploitability composite is already in the framework's spirit. The per-task composite is rich enough.
- *Result:* SURVIVES.

**Aggregate frame-premise robustness:** 2/3 premises survive cleanly; (b) survives with REFINE (threshold articulation needed). Frame-premise prosecution PASSES with one refinement target.
