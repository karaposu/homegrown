# Finding: Does Paper 7 (Controlling Access to Working Memory) Generate a Breakthrough for Us? — No, but it is the closest-to-yes No of the harvest.

## Question

Sixth dive in the parked-paper harvest (`devdocs/paper_seed/`), where each paper is read in full and adjudicated — honestly, both ways — for whether it yields a genuine import for our project (a "cognitive harness": markdown thinking-disciplines run through a `/traverse` pipeline that builds a cross-session memory). Every dive is gated by the **import test**: *an insight is a real import only if it NAMES something we already do but hadn't named, or RESOLVES a confusion we already had — otherwise it is merely confirming or decorative.* Prior verdicts: paper 1 → **consolidation** (YES, generative); paper 2 → **reach≠grasp / the availability illusion** (YES, regulative — a session mistakes what it can *reach*, i.e. look up, for what it actually *holds*, i.e. has loaded); paper 3 → top-down attention (NO, with a bounded residue); paper 4 → dual process theory (NO, cleanest); paper 6 → theory of mind (NO, with a bounded residue). (Paper 5 remains un-dived.)

The user pointed at `devdocs/paper_seed/7.md` — **Vogel, McCollough & Machizawa (2005), "Neural measures reveal individual differences in controlling access to working memory"** (*Nature*) — and asked, as before: **"dive deep [into] if this would generate breakthrough for us or not."** The paper's thesis: working-memory capacity is severely limited (3–4 items), and the limit forces a **filtering** mechanism that keeps irrelevant items out. Its striking finding is that individual capacity differences are partly *filtering* differences, not just *storage* differences — high-capacity people efficiently exclude irrelevant items; low-capacity people waste capacity storing distractors (and so may actually hold *more*, of the wrong things). A third experiment dissociates two controls: **appending** items to memory (intact in everyone) versus **excluding** irrelevant items at the door (the deficit).

This dive carried one extra discipline the earlier dives did not. At the end of the paper-6 dive we committed to **forecasting before diving** — stating a prediction up front so the topic-novelty heuristic (from the paper-4 dive) could be tested as a real forecast rather than fit in hindsight. The committed forecast for paper 7, recorded in this inquiry's framing before any analysis: **LEAN YES — a bounded-real import, likely regulative, medium confidence.** That prediction is scored, honestly, as part of this finding.

## Finding Summary

- **The answer is NO — paper 7 is not a breakthrough — but it is the closest-to-yes No of the whole harvest.** Its honest category is **"confirming-plus"**: more than paper 4's confirming-nothing (its content is real), but on the No side of the breakthrough line.

- **Everything paper 7 says maps onto a residue paper 3 already flagged — but paper 7 sharpens it.** The paper-3 dive left an *open, un-operationalized* residue: our context management is lopsided — richly built on the bring-in side (warming loads relevant terrain) but thin on the keep-out side (only a blanket "compaction" that flushes everything). Paper 3 named the missing keep-out ("selective suppression") and a failure mode ("context pollution"), but left them undeveloped and deferred. Paper 7 is the first paper in the harvest that **closes/sharpens a prior paper's residue rather than opening its own** — a distinct role we're calling a **residue-sharpener**.

- **It sharpens paper 3's residue in three ways — and they are not equal.** Ranked by how much each genuinely adds:
  1. **Most additive — the keep-out has a *time* (encode-time).** Paper 7 frames filtering as "controlling *access* to working memory" — gatekeeping *at the door*, at the moment items would enter. That gives our missing keep-out a specific location, distinct from two keep-out operations we already have: blanket compaction (a bulk flush) and consolidation's pruning (offline, between traverses). Encode-time selective exclusion is a genuinely empty cell.
  2. **Real but near-corollary — pollution is a *capacity tax*.** Paper 3 framed context pollution as *derailment* (irrelevant content biases or anchors the traverse). Paper 7 reframes it as capacity *consumption*: irrelevant items eat the scarce buffer, so **effective grasp = raw capacity − irrelevant load** — grasp is *wasted*, not merely underused. This sharpens reach≠grasp, but it is close to a corollary of it (if grasp is finite, junk in grasp obviously costs signal).
  3. **Re-evidence — loading and gatekeeping are *dissociable*.** The paper's third experiment shows appending items and excluding items are separable competencies (you can be good at one, bad at the other). This is cleaner evidence for paper 3's "lopsided management" observation, not a new concept.

- **Why No and not Yes — the honest boundary.** Papers 3 and 6 each also *sharpened* our understanding and left bounded residues, and both were scored No; in this harvest a *breakthrough* means a **new frame** (consolidation; reach≠grasp), not the sharpening of an existing one. Decisively: paper 3's residue had a *meaning* face (what is the keep-out?) and a *structural* face (its deferred question — should we lift an existing primitive to this level, or build a new selective-eviction concept?). Paper 7 resolves part of the *meaning* face but leaves the *structural* face entirely open. That is a **partial resolution** — real (hence confirming-plus), but not the full import that would clear the bar.

- **The forecast, scored honestly: direction right, magnitude overcalled.** The committed prediction (LEAN YES) got the *direction* right — the heuristic correctly forecast that paper 7 would engage exactly paper 3's un-operationalized residue, and it did. But the *magnitude* was too high: engaging-and-sharpening a residue is not the same as clearing the breakthrough bar, so LEAN-YES overshot; the honest verdict is No-with-real-sharpening. This is the forecast-first exercise working as designed — a committed prediction that the adversarial verdict *corrected* rather than one the verdict was bent to confirm.

## Finding

To set the stage: the harvest's purpose is to find the few ideas that genuinely sharpen our design while refusing to manufacture importance where there is none. Paper 7 is the hardest call so far, for a specific reason — this time the pull toward a Yes came from *inside* the process (our own committed forecast leaned Yes), so the primary discipline was resisting confirmation of our own prediction without over-correcting into an unfair No.

### Why the answer is No (the verdict and its anchor)

Paper 7's entire functional content maps onto a residue the paper-3 dive already flagged. That dive found our context management **lopsided**: we have a rich bring-in half (warming — loading the relevant terrain into a fresh session) and only a crude keep-out half (compaction — a blanket flush of everything). Paper 3 named the missing precision (selective suppression — keep the specifically-irrelevant out while keeping the relevant loaded) and a distinct failure mode (context pollution — a traverse derailed or bloated by irrelevant content), but explicitly left them as an *open, deferred, evidence-gated residue*.

The anchor for the No is a **consistency bar**: papers 3 and 6 each sharpened our understanding and left bounded residues, and each was scored No because a breakthrough in this harvest is a *new frame* — the generative frame of consolidation, or the regulative frame of reach≠grasp — not the sharpening of an existing frame. Paper 7 sharpens paper 3's existing residue. By the harvest's own standard, that is No-side. What makes it *confirming-plus* rather than plain confirming is that the sharpenings are real content, not empty restatement — which the next section sizes honestly.

### The three sharpenings, ranked by what they actually add

Not all three sharpenings carry equal weight; presenting them as three equal imports would overstate the paper.

**Most additive — the keep-out has a time (encode-time).** Paper 7's framing is "controlling *access* to working memory": the filtering happens *at the door*, at the moment an item would enter the buffer. That locates our missing keep-out precisely, and distinguishes it from the two keep-out operations we already have. We have blanket compaction (drop everything and start fresh) and consolidation's pruning (offline, between traverses, when episodic detail is distilled to gist). We do *not* have **encode-time selective exclusion** — keeping the specifically-irrelevant from entering in the first place. Paper 3 named "selective suppression" without pinning its timing; paper 7 gives it a time. This is the one genuinely additive piece.

**Real but near-corollary — pollution is a capacity tax.** Paper 3 framed context pollution as *derailment* — irrelevant content anchors or biases the traverse. Paper 7 reframes it as capacity *consumption*: irrelevant items literally take up the scarce buffer, so a system's *effective* grasp is its raw capacity minus its irrelevant load. Its vivid form is the counterintuitive result that a *lower*-capacity system may hold *more* total information — because it is holding junk that crowds out signal. Mapped onto us: a failing traverse may be *over*-loaded with irrelevant context, not under-loaded. This genuinely sharpens reach≠grasp (grasp is *wasted*, not just underused). But it sits close to a corollary of reach≠grasp: once you accept grasp is finite (paper 2) and that irrelevant content can fill it (paper 3's pollution), "junk costs signal" nearly follows. Real, but the weakest-standing of the three as a new import.

**Re-evidence — loading and gatekeeping are dissociable.** The paper's third experiment shows appending items to memory and excluding items are *separable* competencies — low-capacity subjects append fine but fail to exclude. This is a clean proof of what paper 3's "lopsided management" already asserted (we built the bring-in half and not the keep-out half — which only makes sense if the two are separable). Cleaner evidence, not a new concept.

### Why not Yes — the meaning-resolved / structure-still-open boundary

This is the closest-to-Yes No of the harvest, so the boundary deserves to be drawn precisely rather than waved past. The strongest case for a Yes is that paper 3 *explicitly left its residue open and deferred* — and the import test admits an insight that RESOLVES a pre-existing confusion, so *operationalizing an open residue* could count as a real import.

That case is real but does not quite clear the bar, and seeing why is the useful part. Paper 3's residue had two faces. Its **meaning** face: what *is* the keep-out, and what is the failure it prevents? Its **structural** face — the question paper 3 explicitly deferred: should the keep-out be built by *lifting* an existing within-act primitive (our canon's "Inhibition") up to the cross-traverse level, or by *building a distinct* selective-eviction concept? Paper 7 resolves part of the meaning face (pollution is a capacity tax; the keep-out lives at encode-time) but says *nothing* about the structural face. So it is a **partial resolution** — enough to make the residue sharper and more operationalizable, not enough to be the import that closes it. Confirming-plus, not Yes.

### The forecast, scored

Because this dive committed a prediction before analysis, the finding scores it — both because honesty requires it and because the score is itself a data point for evaluating the harvest method.

- **Direction: right.** The refined topic-novelty heuristic (from the paper-4 dive) says an import appears where a paper touches a facet our canon has not yet *operationalized*. The forecast reasoned that paper 7 would engage paper 3's un-operationalized keep-out residue. It did — precisely there. The heuristic's *predictive* (not merely hindsight) use is validated in direction.
- **Magnitude: overcalled.** The forecast leaned Yes (a bounded-real import). The honest verdict is No-with-real-sharpening. Engaging-and-sharpening a residue is not the same as clearing the breakthrough bar; LEAN-YES was too high.
- **Why this is the exercise working, not failing.** The value of forecasting before diving is exactly this: a committed prediction that the adversarial pipeline can *correct*. Here the correction fired — the analysis nearly credited "sharpens the residue" as a Yes to make the forecast land, and the consistency bar caught it. A prediction the verdict is bent to confirm would be worthless; a prediction the verdict corrects is a real test.

### The residue-sharpener role (a note about the harvest method, not our design)

Paper 7 occupies a verdict-role the harvest had not seen. Papers 1–2 opened breakthroughs; papers 3 and 6 opened bounded residues; paper 4 confirmed nothing. Paper 7 *closes/sharpens a prior paper's open residue*. Naming this role ("residue-sharpener") completes the harvest's outcome-taxonomy a little further. This is a **meta-observation about the harvest method**, not an import for our traversal-memory design — it changes nothing about the design; it just gives the harvest a more accurate vocabulary for what a dive can produce.

### The bound

Meaning-layer only. Whether and how to *build* an encode-time selective-gatekeeping mechanism is a structural question — and it inherits paper 3's own deferred, evidence-gated route (build the keep-out only if context pollution proves load-bearing at the involuntary inflows: tool-dumps, context accumulation, anchoring). Paper 7's contribution to that eventual build is only the encode-time framing, not a mandate to build. Everything neural and visual in the paper — the contralateral-delay-activity brainwave measure, the prefrontal "bias signal," the capacity formula, the coloured-rectangle arrays — is quarantined: we have no brain and no visual buffer.
