## Phase 2-3 — Adversarial Evaluation + Verdicts

### Q4 (satisfaction + allocation) — the sharpest — REFINE ×2

**Prosecution A (D1, D4):** "the mycelium is the family's first whole-architecture scene" is a post-hoc rationalization — the user *already said* mycelium is most satisfying, and the finding reverse-engineered a structural reason to agree. That is confirmation-closure, not analysis.

**Defense:** the claim is independently falsifiable and was tested by inversion — "it's just the move-layer relabelled" fails because the network-as-persistent-path and fund-across-fronts are demonstrably absent from a single tip's momentary search. The three concerns are named concretely in the scene (tips = move; network = record; translocation = allocation), not asserted. So the structural reason stands on its own, independent of the user's preference. **Collision → SURVIVES the post-hoc charge**, but two precision-cuts are forced:

- **REFINE R1 (D3 — internal consistency, the real catch).** Q4 says "the network *is* the record"; Q5 says the mycelium "has a record but performs no re-reading-for-proportion." Left as-is these read as a contradiction. The fix is a precision the finding must carry: **the mycelium holds the record as *physical path-memory* — the substrate, the body that is the trace of where it searched — but not the record as *proportion-kept, re-read memory*.** So the whole-architecture reading is precisely: move + record-*as-substrate* + allocation, with the *proportion-correction* (the deliberate re-read) as the named gap. This makes Q4 and Q5 consistent and makes "whole-architecture" mean something exact rather than expansive.

- **REFINE R2 (D1 — connect the gift to the caution).** The very thing that makes the mycelium satisfying (it binds all the concerns into one structure) is also a slight *over-integration*: in the mycelium the network is at once memory and resource-conduit — one structure — whereas the project keeps the shared corpus (memory) and the shared budget (attention/compute) separable. The finding must state that **the unification is both the gift and a mild over-claim** — the mycelium is a touch *more* unified than the target it models. (This connects Q4's satisfaction to Q5's corpus≠budget caution instead of leaving them in different pieces.)

**Prosecution B (D6, layer-status):** "a third concern, not a rival layer" is a hedge. **Defense:** falsifiable — a wholly-independent layer would need its own mechanics (it has none; built from move + pool), and mere enrichment would add nothing beyond single-search reinforce (it adds cross-front funding). Both failing conditions are named; D3-reading (multi-front scaling) is what remains. **SURVIVES** — falsifiability-shaped, no refine.

### Q3 (two multihead models, "the project is both") — SURVIVE

**Prosecution (D4):** "the project is both models" is a diplomatic dodge that spares the user a real decision. **Defense:** it is not indecision but a *scale-distinction* — the two models are views at different scales (mycelium = intra-system live; humanity = cross-session mortal-heads), and the finding names what each *uniquely* holds (mycelium = shared living substrate; humanity = independent mortal heads bridged by records). The inversion "it's only one model" fails because both properties are demonstrably real in the project (sessions die; corpus+budget are shared-live). **SURVIVES.** The scale-distinction is what makes "both" load-bearing rather than wishy-washy — it is already explicit in Q3; no refine, but this is the piece's crux and must not be softened in the finding.

### Q2 (counterpart map + backtrack-resource insight) — SURVIVE

**Prosecution (D1):** "backtrack reclaims the budget" is a cute embellishment, not a real mapping. **Defense:** in a finite context/attention budget, effort that would have gone to an abandoned branch *is* freed for live ones — the reclaim is real, and it is genuinely additive to the prior finding's re-aim-only backtrack. **SURVIVES.** Framed as enriching-not-overturning the move-layer; correct.

### Q1 + biology-honesty — SURVIVE (D2 strong)

**Prosecution (D2):** relaxing structure-not-biology smuggles contested science into load-bearing positions. **Defense:** the load-bearing biological claims (one connected genetic individual; whole-network translocation) are textbook-solid; the contested claim (mycorrhizal wood-wide web) is explicitly bounded out; the "no brain / no decision" caveat is present; the refined rule is stated once, early. No contested science carries any load. **SURVIVES** — this is the dimension the relaxation most endangered, and it holds.

### Q5 (pairing preserved + losses) — SURVIVE

**Prosecution (D1):** the fuller mycelium (whole-network reallocation) *does* now supply proportion, dissolving the pairing rule. **Defense:** reallocation follows gradients (fund where it pays); it never re-reads the whole to catch over-weighting, and the comet-hunter's rich seam *still pays*, so gradient-reallocation would fund it, not correct it. Dead-end/gradient correction ≠ proportion re-read. **SURVIVES** — the distinction is crisp; the pairing rule holds. Losses (independent-heads clarity weaker; biology not overclaimed) stated plainly, not softened.

### Q6 (inherited-commitments re-test + guards) — SURVIVE

**Prosecution (D5):** the priors' commitments are reinvented to fit, not faithfully re-tested. **Defense:** the move-layer, pairing rule, and structure-not-biology (from 15-11), and the independent-heads model, hero-constraint, and memory's-reach ladder (from 14-23), match what the Synthesis Trigger recorded; each is either preserved (move-layer, ladder), preserved-and-extended (two-layer account gains the allocation concern at its intersection), or re-tested (pairing, against the fuller mycelium). The hero-constraint is checked faithfully in both models (independent-but-partial minds; one body with no controlling centre). **SURVIVES.** Observatory-pick confirmed orthogonal/untouched; homegrown honored lightly.

### The Emergent (two complementary scenes) — REFINE ×1

**Prosecution (D1):** the emergent just restates Q3. **Defense:** Q3 is about the two multihead *models*; the emergent is about the *analogy-family's scene-set* converging on two complementary whole-ish scenes — and it answers the user's "are we close?"
