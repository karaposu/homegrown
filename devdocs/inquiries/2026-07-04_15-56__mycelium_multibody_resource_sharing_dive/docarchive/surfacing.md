## Surfaced Items (relevance-tagged)

### Region A — the known mycelial resource-sharing mechanism (honesty-bounded)

- **A1 — one organism, mostly [LOAD-BEARING]** ★solid. A single mycelium is typically ONE genetic individual — an interconnected hyphal network grown from one spore/genet. The "humongous fungus" (*Armillaria*, Oregon's Malheur forest) is ~one individual spanning kilometres, among the largest organisms known. → **Answers the user's "one organism or a collaboration?": mostly ONE organism** (one connected body), not a collaboration of separate individuals.
- **A2 — the collaboration-nuance [SUPPORTING]** ★solid (anastomosis) / ~partial (individual-boundary). Same-species (and same-mycelium) hyphae ANASTOMOSE — fuse into a continuous network; vegetative-compatibility rules govern whether two encounters fuse or reject. So the "one organism" boundary is real but fuzzy at the edges: a network can merge with kin. → the user's "collaboration of same species?" has a grain of truth at the fusion-boundary, but the core case is one connected body.
- **A3 — translocation: whole-network materials [LOAD-BEARING]** ★solid (that it happens) / ~partial (exact mechanism/proportion). Water, carbon, nutrients TRANSLOCATE across the network (cytoplasmic streaming, mass flow, diffusion) — moving from resource-rich regions and toward growing tips. → **THE core answer to "whole-network or local-path?": growth at X/Y CAN draw on WHOLE-network materials, translocated to where growth is happening — not only the local path-A portion.** (Don't overclaim the precise routing; that it pools and redistributes is solid.)
- **A4 — active reallocation [LOAD-BEARING]** ~partial (well-documented in cord-forming fungi). Foraging mycelium THICKENS cords/rhizomorphs toward good patches and ABANDONS unproductive regions, recycling their material back into the network. → the whole network FUNDS THE PROMISING FRONTS and defunds the dead ones — a live reallocation, not just static sharing.
- **A5 — no brain [LOAD-BEARING]** ★solid (no nervous system) / ~partial (how "decisions" emerge). Allocation is local rules + physical source-sink gradients, not central control; the apparent optimization is emergent. → keeps the NO-HERO property in a new form: a single body with no controlling centre.
- **A6 — the wood-wide web [ANTI-RELEVANT / boundary]** ⚠contested. Mycorrhizal networks (fungus–plant symbiosis, inter-plant resource shuttling) are a DIFFERENT structure, and recent literature disputes the strength of the popular claims. → **bound OUT of the single-mycelium seed**; a place to NOT overclaim.

- **B6 — one-connected-network = the project is ONE system [LOAD-BEARING].** The harness is one system running many fronts (traverses) on one shared substrate (corpus + budget) — not a set of wholly separate organisms.

### Region C — the two multihead models (the central structural distinction)

- **C1 — the HUMANITY model [LOAD-BEARING].** Many INDEPENDENT organisms (minds), each mortal, bridged by EXTERNAL shared records (writing). Coordination is asynchronous, across deaths. No shared live body; no shared live resource pool. The bridge is the archive.
- **C2 — the MYCELIUM model [LOAD-BEARING].** ONE organism, many growing tips (fronts), sharing ONE LIVE resource pool via translocation across a connected body. Coordination is synchronous, within one continuous life. No per-head death — a dead tip's material is reclaimed by the network.
- **C3 — which is the project? (candidate) [LOAD-BEARING, the central finding].** BOTH illuminate different views of the same system. The MYCELIUM model illuminates the INTRA-system live view: one harness, many traverses, one shared corpus + one budget, reallocated live. The HUMANITY model illuminates the CROSS-session independent-mortal-heads view: sessions die, records bridge them. → **the mycelium ADDS the SHARED-SUBSTRATE / LIVE-RESOURCE dimension the humanity model (independent heads) lacked.**
- **C4 — they are complementary, not rival [LOAD-BEARING, tension held].** The project genuinely has BOTH properties: sessions ARE mortal (humanity-like) AND the corpus+budget ARE shared-and-live (mycelium-like). So the two multihead models are two VIEWS of one architecture — this bears on the memory's-reach ladder (14-23) and the hero-constraint (both models keep no-total-sight, by different means).
