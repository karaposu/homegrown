# Concept-map — articulate_warm operation-set + warm-only mechanisms (within-concept index)

Iteration 1 (fresh). Nodes + links of the design and its onward field. (No process/control-flow state — that lives in _state.md.)

## Nodes

- **context-symmetry** — cold's 3 limits (identification-only · no-commitment · never-asks) = no-context consequences; warm inverts each (commit[done] · re-run[A] · identify-conflicts-flag[B]). Organizing frame; A⊥B.
- **item-A-trigger-gated-re-run** — re-run the context-sensitive inherited ops; carry the context-invariant.
- **one-scan-trigger** — "re-run iff a single context-delta scan shows the op moved" (mirrors the :122 self-check); the §9 cost-guard.
- **doc-§4-correction** — promote MQ4 out of "optional" + replace flat "optional" with the trigger-gated principle. Item A's realization.
- **MQ4-promotion** — MQ4 (boundary/exclusions) is a STRONG warm re-run (context reveals exclusions), not "optional".
- **item-B-conflict-detection** — a warm-only identify-move: request-premise vs surfaced-reality incompatibility, 2-shape, don't-adjudicate. Unowned.
- **escalation-ladder** — none→PROCEED · resolvable→re-anchor+MED-FLAG · severe-premise→HIGH-FLAG+Q-payload.
- **emit-flag/runner-asks** — discipline emits (identify+flag+payload); runner surfaces/asks/blocks. Canon-safe.
- **flag-type-discriminator** — a `content-conflict` flag-type (or new verdict) so the runner tells content-conflict from operational-flag (:245).
- **grade≠adjudicate** — warm grades the conflict's severity (like its verdict) but never decides request-vs-reality.
- **warm-formulates/runner-poses** — warm writes the clarifying question (as payload); the runner poses it.
- **object-ownership** — conflict-detection is warm's because the premise is warm's OWN output (checking its product against reality); sensemaking consumes-the-frame + is biased-to-reconcile.
- **autonomy-degrade** — block-and-ask needs an operator → under autonomy degrade to flag-and-best-effort.
- **runner-block-policy** — traverse step 8 gains a selective block-on-severe-content-conflict (operator-present-only).
- **additive-two-faced-Meaning** — warm = re-anchor loop-controller + first request-vs-reality conflict-gate. Additive.
- **build-priority-A≫B** — A improves every warm pass (do first); B is a rare cheap safety-net.
- **context-symmetry-lens** — "sort operations by context-sensitivity; add operations context enables"; reusable for any cold/warm pass; modest, derived.
- **the-09-05-structure** — the thin-wrapper warm-behavior section that houses both.
- **canon-doc-§4** — docs/how_articulate_warm_should_be.md §4 (the re-run split; the correction target).
- **the-verdict-system** — articulate_simple's 5 compound verdicts (the FLAG action Item B rides + the discriminator extends).
