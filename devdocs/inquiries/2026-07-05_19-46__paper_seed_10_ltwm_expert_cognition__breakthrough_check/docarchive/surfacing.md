## PART A — Paper 10's insights as FACTs (the external territory)

- **A-1 [core] — Long-term working memory (LTWM): experts use LTM *as* working memory.** FACT (quoted): classic WM has "rapid fade… information held in working memory fades very rapidly, and is lost in a matter of seconds"; but for experts in their domain this "is not true" — a chess master, shown a position then distracted, "can return it to mind with virtually no loss of information." "Here, then, is a form of memory that is fast, reliable, and does not fade, but is very narrowly focused." LTWM lets experts "access and also encode information very rapidly, but only in the narrow domain of their expertise."

- **A-2 [core] — The mechanism: retrieval structures + retrieval cues.** FACT (quoted): "The model is based on retrieval cues and retrieval structures. A cue is an item of knowledge (a 'chunk'…) that is linked by association to a longer encoding [the cue 'reinstates the conditions of encoding']. A retrieval structure is a set of retrieval cues that the expert creates over time and which is relatively stable." Example: the cue "Sicilian defense" "allows immediate encoding of the relative position of thirty two pieces" — the master "does not need to encode the specific board location of each piece." Acquired over ~10 years of practice.

- **A-3 [core] — LTWM is powerful and flexible WITHIN its domain, but NOT innovative.** FACT (quoted): the retrieval structure "can assess a huge range of situations very rapidly… a powerful and flexible kind of thinking. But it is limited to a narrow domain" (a chess structure "is useless in playing Go"). "L-TWM is not inherently innovative… not adept at creating truly novel responses because the retrieval structure and cues are stable, linked elements of long-term memory itself."

- **A-4 [core] — Enhanced working memory (EWM) enables innovation via FREED-UP attention capacity.** FACT (quoted): "A constellation deployed by L-TWM does not exhaust the attention capacity of working memory, especially during routine tasks that have become recipes. The free attention capacity can be used to innovate, plan ahead, or… daydream." And: "The reason Keller's blacksmithing is not identical to Neandertal knapping is that EWM provides freed-up attention capacity. Especially when L-TWM has converted umbrella plans to recipes, the smith has processing capacity available to make unique comparisons, think further ahead… One result of this is innovation."

- **A-5 [supporting] — connaissance / savoir faire (declarative / procedural).** FACT (quoted): LTWM "consists of skills (which we have labeled *savoir faire*)… such as stone tool knapping. It also includes the declarative knowledge of those skills (which we labeled *connaissance*)." Both are "components of the knapper's stock of knowledge," both "acquired through extended periods of practice."

- **A-6 [supporting] — stock of knowledge / umbrella plan / constellation (Keller's technical cognition).** FACT (quoted): stock of knowledge = "the pool of information acquired… during years of practice"; umbrella plan = "the smith's mental representation of an ultimate goal… and his associated general overview of the step-by-step procedures"; constellation = "configurations of ideas, implements, and materials" that complete one step, with "active feedback between the actions… and his or her constellation." "Umbrella plans would appear to be constructed using retrieval structures."

- **A-7 [supporting] — WM capacity = maintaining attention despite interference; low capacity → captured by old habits.** FACT (quoted, Kane & Engle): "maintenance of attention in the presence of interference is the critical control function of working memory capacity"; low-span participants are "more susceptible to long-term memory interference, including retroactive and proactive interference," and have "greater difficulty in solving a new task because of prior habits of thought." Low capacity → "vulnerability to interference… when there is competition between the new task goal and old habitual responses."

- **A-8 [peripheral] — WM enables counterfactual simulation / "thought experiments."** FACT (quoted): WM "would allow for the reflection of multiple past experiences… actively choose a future action or create an alternative action"; enables "mental models of future behavior," "accurate mental simulation of varying actions, allowing the evaluation of the success or failure of these actions without taking a physical risk"; "thought experiments… increased the probability of the success of the real experiment."

- **A-9 [DOESN'T-APPLY — quarantined] — the archaeology + personality + genetic substrate.** FACT: Levallois reduction, chaîne opératoire, cores/debitage/refitting, Chatelperronian emulation, Mousterian/Aurignacian, OIS dating; Neandertal personality (stoicism, harm-avoidance, hunting-trauma, rodeo-athlete injuries); the ~50–100 Ka genetic mutation, phonological-store lengthening, prefrontal cortex, fMRI, replacement scenarios. Quarantined biology/archaeology per _branch.md.

## PART B — Our canon + prior commitments as FACTs (the mapping ground)

- **B-1 [core] — reach≠grasp / the availability illusion (paper 2).** FACT (prior finding): a session mistakes what it can *reach* (look up — the whole repo/canon) for what it *holds* (the loaded fraction — tiny); "the context window is a cache over a backing store, holding the loaded fraction." Grasp is finite and small. **KEY GROUNDING: reach≠grasp treats grasp as a roughly FIXED small capacity. Paper 10's LTWM says experts BYPASS the fixed limit via retrieval structures — hold vastly more in effective active use, within a domain. This is the load-bearing tension to adjudicate.**

- **B-2 [core] — paper 1's consolidation + the memory-KINDS taxonomy.** FACT (prior finding): memory is a lifecycle (encode→consolidate→retrieve→reconsolidate) across kinds (working / episodic / semantic / option). Consolidation (episodic→semantic, the offline between-traverse pass) was the load-bearing WRITE breakthrough. **KEY GROUNDING: consolidation named the WRITE into semantic memory (canon). Paper 10's retrieval structures are about the READ-BACK from semantic memory — the retrieve stage the taxonomy NAMED but never operationalized. Paper 10 may be the missing read-back complement.**

- **B-3 [supporting] — paper 3/7's context-pollution / keep-out residue.** FACT (prior findings): irrelevant loaded content is a capacity TAX (effective grasp = raw − irrelevant load); the keep-out is encode-time gatekeeping. **GROUNDING: capacity is framed as a scarce resource to PROTECT. Paper 10 (A-4) reframes the freed capacity as what ENABLES innovation — a WHY for the protection, not just economy.**

- **B-4 [core] — the Intuition-similarity primitive.** FACT (`thinking_space_dynamics.md`): "Pattern-matches current item against corpus (similarity only). Finds prior work that matches in surface OR structure." A RETRIEVAL operation: current-item → corpus. **KEY GROUNDING: this is our closest existing concept to "retrieval cues." Question for Sensemaking: does Intuition-similarity already cover paper 10's retrieval structures, or is a *retrieval structure* (a STABLE, PRE-BUILT, domain-specific set of cues that reinstates a large encoding) something MORE than one-shot similarity matching?**

- **B-5 [supporting] — canon (semantic) vs disciplines/skills (procedural).** FACT: our canon docs are declarative knowledge (what things ARE); our traverse disciplines/skills are procedural know-how (how to run the thinking). **GROUNDING: this is exactly A-5's connaissance/savoir-faire split — we HAVE both, unnamed as a pair.**

- **B-6 [supporting] — the innovate discipline + option-memory.** FACT: our `/innovate` discipline generates novelty (combination, domain-transfer, inversion, etc.); option-memory is the prospective kind (paths not taken). **GROUNDING: A-4/A-8's "freed capacity → innovation / thought experiments" bears on WHY innovation needs free capacity, and A-8's counterfactual simulation bears on option-memory.**

- **B-7 [core] — the harvest verdict-ladder + the territory-saturation signal.** FACT (prior findings): generative (paper 1) / regulative (paper 2) / bounded-residue (3,6) / confirming-nothing (4) / confirming-plus ×3 flavors (7,8,9). The saturation signal: after paper 2's YES, the WM-capacity cluster (3,7,8,9) all yielded confirming-plus; **paper 10 is the FRESH territory (expertise/retrieval) the signal said might yield more. This is the test of that prediction.**

## PART C — The three-direction mapping (HAVE / LACK / DOESN'T-APPLY) + REVERSE

### HAVE (paper 10 names something we already do)
- **M-H1 [core] — connaissance/savoir-faire ↔ canon/disciplines (A-5 ↔ B-5).** We already HAVE the declarative/procedural split (canon vs skills). Paper 10 names it as a *pair within one stock of knowledge* — a mild naming we lacked, but both halves are already ours. Likely HAVE (a labeling), not a new frame.
- **M-H2 [supporting] — retrieval cues (one-shot) ↔ Intuition-similarity (A-2 ↔ B-4).** We already HAVE similarity-based retrieval (current-item → corpus). Paper 10's *cue* resembles it. BUT the *retrieval STRUCTURE* (stable, pre-built, domain-specific) may exceed it — see M-L1.

### LACK (paper 10 names something we plausibly need but have no concept for)
- **M-L1 [CENTRAL CANDIDATE] — the RETRIEVAL STRUCTURE: a stable, pre-built index that expands EFFECTIVE grasp.** Paper 10's core mechanism (A-1/A-2): experts hold far more in active use than the raw capacity limit by having a *stable retrieval structure* that lets a small cue reinstate a large encoding. For us: reach≠grasp (B-1) treats grasp as fixed-small; paper 10 says a good retrieval structure BYPASSES the limit. **Candidate import: effective grasp is NOT fixed — a well-indexed store (canon + memory organized as retrieval structures) lets a session pull far more into effective use per unit of loaded context. This COMPLICATES reach≠grasp: the gap between reach and grasp is not a fixed capacity wall but a function of how well the store is indexed for cue-based recall.** The design consequence (deferred): organize canon/memory as retrieval structures (stable cue→large-encoding links), not flat docs. **This is the YES-candidate — run at genuine force in Sensemaking.**
- **M-L2 [supporting] — free-capacity-enables-innovation as a WHY for offloading (A-4 ↔ B-3/B-6).** We keep-out pollution and consolidate to SAVE capacity (economy). Paper 10 reframes: the freed capacity is what makes INNOVATION possible (the combinatorial/experimental work). Candidate: a *purpose* for our capacity-management we hadn't named — offload the routine (to canon/retrieval structures) precisely to free capacity for the hard novel thinking. Possibly a real reframe; possibly covered by the keep-out's existing "protect grasp" logic. Adjudicate.
- **M-L3 [supporting] — the expertise-vs-innovation AXIS (A-3/A-4).** A genuinely different distinction: LTWM (fast expert retrieval, non-innovative, stable) vs EWM (capacity for novel combination). For us: a warm session using canon/memory to handle a familiar inquiry FAST (expert retrieval) vs the capacity to produce a genuinely novel finding (innovation) — two different modes. Candidate: we may conflate "handled it well via prior work" (expertise) with "produced something new" (innovation). Adjudicate whether this axis is load-bearing or decorative.

### DOESN'T-APPLY (quarantined)
- **M-D1 — the archaeology + personality + genetic substrate (A-9).** Levallois/Chatelperronian, Neandertal personality, the ~50–100 Ka mutation, phonological store, prefrontal cortex. Quarantined — the FUNCTIONAL residue (A-1..A-8) is what maps.
- **M-D2 — the human 10-years-to-mastery timescale + procedural/motor imagery (A-2/A-5).** "Muscle memories," fencing parries, ten-year apprenticeships — biological/embodied specifics; the functional point (retrieval structures take accumulated practice to build) may transfer, the embodiment does not.

### REVERSE (what WE have that paper 10 doesn't cover)
- **M-R1 — our store is EXPLICIT, EXTERNAL, and INSTANTLY writable/readable.** A human expert's retrieval structure is built over 10 years of practice and lives implicitly in LTM. Our "retrieval structure" (canon + memory index) is explicit text we can author, edit, and re-index in ONE consolidation pass — no decade, no muscle memory. This ASYMMETRY matters: paper 10's "retrieval structures take years / are stable and hard to change" is the OPPOSITE of our situation (we can rewrite the index cheaply). So the *design consequence* transfers but the *constraint* (slow, stable, hard-won) does NOT — arguably we should exploit that we can build retrieval structures deliberately and fast.
- **M-R2 — option-memory (prospective) has no clear paper-10 analog.** A-8's counterfactual simulation is close but paper 10 has no prospective/paths-not-taken store; ours (from paper 1) does.
