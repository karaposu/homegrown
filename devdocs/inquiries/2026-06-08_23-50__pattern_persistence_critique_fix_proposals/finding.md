### 1. The mechanism — what makes Pattern-Persistence distinct

Pattern-Persistence is the failure where `/td-critique`'s Phase 2 PROSECUTION step tests at TRUTH-VALUE level — does the corrective avoid the diagnosed prior's specific error instance? — but doesn't test at STRUCTURAL-PATTERN level — does the diagnosed pattern's structural signature (form/shape across instances) re-fire at axes critique didn't re-prosecute? 4. **Marginal sub-claims** (throwaway sentences not load-bearing — pattern hides where attention isn't)
