### MultiDepth

**literal-statement:** "What can we add to innovation as a prompt to make sure it always runs at full scale?"

### Considered Articulations

1. **Template-enforcement reading:** make the 7×3 grid a REQUIRED output section (like CONCLUDE's re-test) — structure forces content; the checker fails its absence.
2. **Hard-floor reading:** replace "aim for full coverage" with "full coverage is the DEFAULT; running below requires a named justification recorded in the artifact" — floors bind.
3. **Runner-binding reading:** the leak was substantially the invocation layer — bind the runner (traverse's step 2) to never downgrade the mode; the spec alone can't stop a narrower brief.
4. **Layered reading (1+2+3):** all three, because each closes a different leak (content, language, invocation).
