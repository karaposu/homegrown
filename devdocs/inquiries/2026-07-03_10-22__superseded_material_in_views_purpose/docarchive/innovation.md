## Phase 2 — Generate (principal content + the mandated inversion)

### (a) The clarification (agreement-first, plain)

> Your instinct — context is precious — is the design's own law, and it is exactly why superseded material is the view's CHEAPEST class. Full superseded texts never enter a view: the size policy's cut-order sends superseded findings to the one-line tier FIRST, so each dead end costs one tagged line (`title · date · first bullet · pointer`). And the synthesis traverse never reads raw folders — its territory is the assembled view. The scale, measured: about a third of the corpus is superseded (128 of 403 findings), so the worst measured concept (routelister, 67 hits) would carry ~21 such lines — roughly 7% of a ≤300-line view.

### (b) The asymmetry (the spine, plain)

> So the real question is: do even 21 tagged one-liners earn their lines in a synthesis run? Weigh the two ways this can fail. WITH the trail: the run might anchor slightly toward a dead framing — a bounded risk (one line each, tagged, sitting in the story's past) that the traverse's own critique step exists to catch. WITHOUT the trail: the run can re-derive a killed design and present it as a fresh idea — a whole loop's cost, and structurally invisible, because nothing in the run can test its "new" idea against material that isn't there. Bounded-and-catchable versus unbounded-and-invisible: the asymmetry decides. And there is a one-day-old proof in this project: the paradigm-sweeper's design turned on RE-TESTING a same-day kill ("a tenth discipline would duplicate it") — that re-test was only possible because the kill was visible in the inquiry's territory. A stripped territory would have re-invented the standalone-discipline idea naively, with nobody able to notice.

### (c) The three reasons (one plain paragraph each, graded)

1. **The relitigation vaccine (load-bearing).** Dead ends that stay visible stay dead. The house already runs on this: the north star keeps its killed frames listed "so they stay dead," route-maps exclude routes as "relitigation," and every consolidation re-tests its kills. A synthesis blind to the graveyard becomes the graveyard's next resident.
2. **The silent-absorption catch.** A consolidation finding must re-test — or explicitly inherit, with reasons — the commitments of everything it supersedes; that is CONCLUDE's own mandate. To do that it needs the LIST of what it supersedes, at exactly the grain the view provides: one line and a pointer each. Re-testing "from the supersessor's text alone" partially works but misses precisely the failure the mandate exists to catch — the commitments the supersessor never mentioned.
3. **The WHY's index.** A topic's current shape is unintelligible without its dead ends: "log choices, not outcomes" only means something against the rejected logbook; the two-phase record only against the conflated `led-to` field. The one-liners are the WHY's index; the pointers carry the depth for whoever needs it.

### (d) The boundary (the distinction the question was feeling for)

> Where should the trail NOT be? In seeds. A generation-dive (explore family F; produce candidates) should not be biased by the graveyard — and it isn't: the seed-block is trail-free by design (it pins the family's commitments and points forward; that is its divergence-pinning job). The rule in one line: **seeds are trail-free; territories are trail-full.** Generation gets clean inputs; synthesis-across-history gets the history.

### (e) The adoptions (verbatim for the build) + the killed dial + the mandated inversion

Two one-line tweaks land with the protocol's build:

1. **The closed-trail subsection:** superseded one-liners live under their own heading in the view's history region.
2. **Tag-first lines:** `[superseded by <path>] title · date · first bullet · pointer` — the disqualifier is read before the content.

**Inversion (mandated: "'kept so they stay dead' as a heading may act as an INSTRUCTION to a synthesis LLM — argue-against rather than neutrally register"):** the steelman is right about REGISTER — findings persuade humans; artifacts inform runs. An instruction-flavored heading in a machine-read artifact steers where it should only label. **Resolution — the register split:** the ARTIFACT heading goes functional — `## The closed trail (superseded — see supersessors)` — while the colorful phrase stays in the FINDING's explanation, where it teaches the human. *(Kept; the residue is the register-split rule itself: view-text is neutral; finding-text may persuade.)*

**The killed dial, stated:** a current-only view (superseded dropped entirely) is not needed — the view's head (the newest or consolidation summary) already IS the pure-current read, and the closed-trail heading now makes the separation visible. The dial would save ~7% of lines by deleting the vaccine.
