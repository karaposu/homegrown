## Phase 5 — SV6 (Stabilized Model)

**No-fit is the assignment's third verdict, and it is detectable exactly where placement is checkable.** Assignment as commonly practiced asks only the relative question (which bin fits best) — a question that always has an answer, which is why cramming beats signaling [general: the argmax point; owned: the forced fit hides like an over-merge]. Reliable no-fit detection adds no new machinery: the finding's own unit already demands a stated ground per placement (:46); no-fit is that same demand read for failure — when no ground can be truthfully stated, the entry does not fit, and the verdict types by WHERE the ground fails along the entry's relation to the axis-space: the axis does not apply to the entry at all [the crispest form; live at routelister:124, with the rehome]; the axis applies but no named region covers the value [the coverage gap — generator evidence in closed systems, the extension moment in open ones]; or the region is reachable only by stretching past the bin's own stated bar [graded systems; checkable only if the bar is written]. Three neighbors must never be converted into no-fit: between-two-regions uncertainty (ruled by the asymmetry question), fits-several (a regime question), and undecidable-yet (an epistemic gap, not a verdict). Reliability is two-sided — the same ground-stating that stops forced fits stops lazy rejections (the house's own gates prove both guards livable) — with the asymmetry lean deciding only residual ties, toward the visible error. And the definitional fork lands sized: define no-fit as an OUTCOME (small — the verdict + these conditions), never as a CATEGORY (a no-fit bin is the pile arriving in slow motion and destroys the very evidence the signal exists to carry); an "unclassified" queue is legitimate only as workflow with an exit rule. Mostly, this names what the house already does — which is the honest answer to "do we need an extra definition?": yes, a small one, and it was already half-written in practice.

**Differs from SV1:** the fence generalized (undecidable-yet), the feasibility leg landed (no new per-entry cost — the failure branch of an existing test), the protection leg landed (types-never-scores keeps the house conjunctive and hence detectable), the evidence-channel stake landed (false signals pollute system evolution), and the verb tension was resolved as an unfolding, not a revision.
