## WORKSPACE — Part 1: Paper FACTs (territory = paper 13, verbatim load-bearing phrases)

- **A-1** [core] Distributed cognition (Hutchins) = "the shared awareness of goals, plans, and details that **no single individual grasps**"; spans "internal resources (memory, attention, and executive function) and external resources (the objects, artifacts, and materials)." (FACT)
- **A-2** [core] Cognition is distributed **through time** — "distributed through time so that **the products of earlier events can transform later events**." (FACT)
- **A-3** [supporting] "A cognitive process is **bounded by the relationships among the elements** that are part of it, instead of how close the elements are in physical space." (FACT)
- **A-4** [core] Cognitive artifacts = "objects such as schedules, display boards, lists, and worksheets that are **part of a distributed cognition**"; "**highly encoded representations of what matters in this domain**"; encoding is "compact, efficient" and "**inherently connected to what is meaningful in the domain**." (FACT)
- **A-5** [core] **THE QUALITY CRITERIA (6 base)** — "any cognitive artifact... must be accurate, efficient, reliable, informative, clear, and malleable." Verbatim: ACCURATE = "Current and valid in its representation of the system state"; EFFICIENT = "Impose the least burden on users to create and obtain information"; RELIABLE = "Available for use when needed"; INFORMATIVE = "Contain information that pertains to circumstances of interest to the team"; CLEAR = "Unambiguous and free from confusion"; MALLEABLE = "Able to be manipulated by those who use them." (FACT)
- **A-6** [core] **THE VALUE-ADD LAYER (4)** — PROMPTING = "survey information in the distributed cognition for **gaps and inconsistencies that go unnoticed. Nominating such item(s)** for the schedule writer to consider"; SPECULATION = "speculate about possible courses of action and then choose among them"; CONSEQUENCES = "display the consequences of choices"; VALUE-BASED DECISIONS = "templates of schedule-planning strategies... **capture scheduling expertise and make it available for use by others**." (FACT)
- **A-7** [core] **PLAN→LOG transformation** — artifacts "reveal organizational change by **evolving from a plan of procedures that may be performed at day's start into a log of procedures that have been performed by day's end**"; "many artifacts **evolve as they pass through many different hands**." (FACT)
- **A-8** [supporting] **THE COORDINATOR** — makes allocation decisions "on the spot"; "The path that the AC takes in making assignments **strives to reduce the degree of uncertainty to a manageable set**"; balances demand (volume/acuity/composition) vs resources (availability/flexibility/resilience). (FACT)
- **A-9** [supporting] **TWO-PATH METHOD** — "the first path shows how the artifact is created, the second uses the artifact to reveal the basis for its creation." (FACT)
- **A-10** [peripheral] **VARIETY = RESILIENCE** — three coordinators wrote the schedule three different ways; "This variety among coordinators is **evidence of resilience in the face of constraints**." (FACT)
- **A-11** [supporting] **UNCERTAINTY-REDUCTION** — the process "seek[s] to **reduce uncertainty to the smallest possible amount before it begins**." (FACT)
- **A-12** [supporting] **PAPER-vs-COMPUTER / understand-work-first** — systems that "simply mimic physical artifacts" are "necessarily **blind to the sophisticated and refined interactions** that practitioners have developed"; "Understanding the origin and nature of the physical artifacts relies on deep and sustained observation." (FACT)
- **A-13** [peripheral] **THE HEALTHCARE / PATIENT-SAFETY thesis** (the paper's actual purpose) — improve patient safety / resource management via better computer-supported cognitive artifacts in a high-hazard, time-pressured OR suite. (FACT)
