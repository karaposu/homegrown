**Crispest statement (target 1):** **Consolidation is the offline, between-traverse pass that turns accumulated episodic records into gist-based canon — stripping detail, extracting the reusable claim, binding it into existing canon, and pruning what's spent.** Its *absence as a named concept* is the reason our memory work fragmented into five separately-invented fixes. The paper's anchor: consolidation into neocortex involves *"loss of specific finer details, such as temporal and spatial information… an evolution from episodic memory toward semantic memory, which consists mainly of gist-based facts."*

## Piece-Level Inversions (REQUIRED — the two load-bearing pieces)

### Q3 Inversion (intervention-shape/claim axis) — "consolidation is a real, new-to-us stage"

**Reverse:** *"It's just distillation with a fancier borrowed name. We already knew we distill findings into canon. Renaming that 'consolidation' adds nothing — it's vocabulary theater."*

**What follows if the reversal held:** naming it would change nothing; we'd keep the same five fixes, just with a group label.

**Re-defeat (the TRIPLE — a rename cannot do any of these, let alone all three):**
1. **It unifies five previously-scattered fixes into one process.** Before, distillation / staleness-pruning / mark-done / canon-authoring / index-summarization were invented separately, in different inquiries, as unrelated chores. A mere synonym for "distillation" wouldn't reach the other four. Consolidation names the *category* they were all instances of.
2. **It resolves a confusion we already had.** 00-47 worried the index "goes stale" and had no account of *why*. The lifecycle answers it: stale = we built encoding and retrieval but *no consolidation stage*, so records accumulate raw and never mature. A rename explains nothing; this *dissolves* a standing puzzle.
3. **It lands exactly on the between-traverse navigation-session layer.** Consolidation is necessarily offline (see the emergent below) — it can't run inside a worker. That places it precisely on the layer the 10-57 finding was independently trying to unload. A rename has no location; this one has a forced address.

A relabel does zero of these. Doing all three is the signature of a real import (K1: it both NAMES-what-we-do and RESOLVES-a-confusion). **Inversion tested, rejected; principal claim survives, strengthened.**

### Q4 Inversion (claim axis) — "canon-authoring IS memory-consolidation, literally"

**Reverse:** *"Surface coincidence. Everything compresses — summaries compress, abstracts compress, tweets compress. Calling canon-authoring 'consolidation' is a cosmetic match on the single boring property that all compression shares."*

**What follows if the reversal held:** the identity claim collapses to a weak analogy; canon-authoring is just "a kind of summarizing," memory-talk optional.

**Re-defeat — the match holds on THREE genuinely independent axes (target 2), not one:**
1. **Size axis — lossy context-stripping.** Canon drops the dated blow-by-blow, keeps the timeless claim. (Paper: *"loss of specific finer details… temporal and spatial information."*) This is the axis the "everything compresses" objection targets — and it's only *one* of three.
2. **Direction axis — episodic→semantic KIND-change (independent of size).** Compression could stay *within* a kind: a shorter dated log entry ("on 07-05, briefly: X because Y") compresses on axis 1 while remaining an *event-memory*. Canon does something else — it strips the event-nature itself: "on 07-05 we found X because Y" → "X". It converts event-memory into fact-memory. A summary that stayed episodic would match axis 1 and *fail* axis 2 — proving they're independent.
3. **Timing+integration axis — offline schema-binding (independent of both).** Canon is authored *between* inquiries (not during the worker's live encoding) and is *integrated into the existing canon web* (a new doc reconciles with and links to prior canon; the index updates). (Paper: consolidation *"integrates them into the network of pre-existing long-term memories."*) You could imagine an inline, standalone gist-write that matched axes 1+2 but happened mid-traverse and stood alone — it would fail axis 3. So this axis is independent too.

Three independent structural matches (size / kind-direction / offline-integration) is **identity, not coincidence** — a cosmetic match lands on one axis; matching all three is what "the same operation" means. **Inversion tested, rejected; the strong claim ("canon-authoring IS consolidation") survives.**

### Assembly check → the EMERGENT (target 4)

**E1 — Consolidation independently re-derives the navigation-session's memory-organ role (a second road to SUSTRALL).** Reasoning: consolidation must be **offline** — a system busy *encoding* a new episode cannot simultaneously *reorganize* accumulated past episodes into gist (different modes; the paper puts consolidation in sleep, "selective reactivation… during sleep"). In our terms: a worker mid-traverse is fully occupied encoding its own inquiry record; it *cannot* also be maturing the accumulated past into canon. Therefore consolidation must run **between traverses, by something that is not the worker** — i.e., a between-traverse layer whose job includes tending the matured memory. That is *exactly* the orchestrator / navigation-session that SUSTRALL derived from the loop-of-loops architecture. **Two independent roads reach the same conclusion:** SUSTRALL got there from control-flow (worker/eyes/orchestrator); the memory lifecycle gets there from the offline-necessity of consolidation. Per the shared-input detection check, these grounds are *genuinely different* (loop-architecture vs memory-stage-necessity) — so the convergence is **independent, not spurious**. This is strong corroboration that the between-traverse layer is real and that memory is its organ. **ACTIONABLE emergent — the strongest assembly result.**

**E2 — The import test (Q1) + the bound (Q6) form a reusable seed-harvest method.** Together they are a general protocol for harvesting any parked `paper_seed/` paper without over-borrowing: transfer only what NAMES-what-we-do or RESOLVES-a-confusion; quarantine the rest. But specifying it as a *method* is process design. **RESEARCH FRONTIER / DEFERRED** (revival trigger: when a second `paper_seed/` paper is harvested) — flagged out of this meaning-layer run to honor Q6.

**E3 — The four kinds cover our existing content-memory artifacts with no orphan.** working=in-traverse context; episodic=inquiry/`_route.md` records; semantic=canon; option=ODI. Every content-memory artifact we have is one of the four. Noted as *supporting* (a completeness claim edges structural — not pressed here). **DEFERRED to the structural run.**
