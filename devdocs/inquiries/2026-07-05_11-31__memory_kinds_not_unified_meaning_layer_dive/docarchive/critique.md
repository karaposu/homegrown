### Q3 — Consolidation [the center]

- **Defense:** consolidation is strictly *broader* than distillation. Distillation (00-21/00-47) meant **shrinking** the index (volume control). Consolidation additionally covers mark-done (state-transition, not shrinking), canon-authoring (a **kind-change**, not a size-change), and schema-binding (integration). It is a superset that absorbs distillation as one facet — a generalization, not a synonym. And the paper earns its keep not via the label but via the **shape** it contributes (maintenance is a KIND-CHANGE that is OFFLINE) — a shape the domestic "memory-maintenance" framing lacked, and the shape that yields E1 downstream.
- **Collision — is the TRIPLE independent?** The three claims do different *kinds* of work: unifies-5-fixes = **scope** (a category); resolves-staleness = **explanatory power** (answers a why); lands-on-the-layer = **location** (a forced address). Each is realizable without the others (a taxonomy bucket unifies without explaining; a one-off explanation explains without unifying; a located concept need do neither). Genuinely independent — and consolidation passes all three.
- **Verdict: SURVIVE (strong).** The center holds under its strongest prosecution. The finding should explicitly state consolidation ⊋ distillation (superset) to pre-empt the rename charge.
