**Structural points:** the three horizons are the ONE seat's three faces (now-carrier / later-supervisor / always-reader); the account's skeleton = cargo × horizon × (map-surface | other-instrument | uncovered).

**Foundational principles:** verbatim canon over paraphrase (all role-lines quoted); era-honesty; instrument-honesty (map ≠ toolkit).

**Meaning-nodes:** "the foreseer" (the cross-era name for the human seat — grounding, not minting; operator/navigator/supervisor are its per-register names) · "the open-work field" (the 389-route + DEFERRED + LIVE-seed surface) · "tee-up" (the map's relation to dispatch).

## Phase 3 — Ambiguity Collapse

#### C1: Does the cargo-account replace the task-list method?
**Counter-interpretation:** two usefulness frameworks is one too many — the new grounds the old obsolete.
**Why the counter fails (structural):** they answer different questions — the cargo-inventory says WHOSE jobs and WHY (the seat), the task list says WHAT must demonstrably work (operational acceptance); mapping the 11 tasks onto the cargos shows full coverage with no orphan task (find-X/recent → selecting+remembering; continues-what/story → seeing; stale/active/anomalies → stop-judging; read-well/jump → the reading instrument's tee-up; feels-alive → the seat's own delight) AND one orphan CARGO-face: selecting's open-work half has NO task — which is exactly the discovered gap, now visible from the foundation side too.
**Confidence:** HIGH. **Resolution:** the cargo-inventory FOUNDS, the task list OPERATES; a twelfth task ("see my open field") becomes the selecting-gap's operational form IF an instrument is chosen. **Depends on this:** the account's structure; the road re-rank.

#### C2: Is the route-lens THE answer to the selecting-gap?
**Counter-interpretation:** the gap is a map gap — put the 389 routes on the road.
**Why the counter fails AS STATED:** 406 route-rows as nodes re-creates the hairball the project just escaped, and route-rows are LIST-shaped data (typed verbs, priorities, ✓-marks) whose native reading is a queue, not a territory. But the counter's core is right that the map has a role: the honest split is **map = the WHERE and HOW-MUCH (an open-count cue per node/settlement; fly-to the inquiry, read its routes in detail) · a list-instrument = the WHAT (the queue itself, sortable)** — both behind one new gate (the route-layer parse is one adapter addition feeding both).
**Confidence:** HIGH on the split's shape; the tiering is the gate's. **Resolution:** instrument-split stabilized; Innovation develops both halves + the gate.

#### C3: Is dispatching in the map's scope?
**Counter-interpretation:** a "launch /traverse" button — the map as cockpit.
**Why the counter fails (structural):** dispatch is the foreseer's terminal act (canon keeps even L3's dispatch systemward-of-the-human only for CHAINS, with the human seeding); a web map launching shell commands crosses an instrument boundary for zero gain over copy-paste. **Resolution:** the map TEES UP dispatch — orientation → selection → a copy-resume-command button (`/traverse <path>` onto the clipboard) — and stops there. The tee-up is one button; the act stays human.
**Confidence:** HIGH.

#### C4 (load-bearing test on insight 1): Is "the foreseer = the seat, era-graded" the canon's structure or this dive's imposition?
**Counter-interpretation:** canon has operator/navigator/supervisor as SEPARATE roles; unifying them under one name flattens real distinctions.
**Why the counter fails (structural):** canon itself grades ONE human position across eras — the cargo-table's "the tier grades WHO carries" is a statement about one seat changing hands cargo-by-cargo, and the kernel-bet's "you are most of it" names the same seat at Level 0; operator/navigator/supervisor appear in different REGISTERS (reactor analogy / the sample / north-star levels), never as co-present distinct persons. The unification is canon's own; the coinage only names it.
**Confidence:** HIGH — with the guard honored (verbatim lines checked; canonization stays the user's).
