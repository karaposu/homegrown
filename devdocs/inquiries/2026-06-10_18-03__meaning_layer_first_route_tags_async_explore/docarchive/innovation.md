### 1. Lens Shifting (Framer)

- **1G — construction lens.** "Don't pour concrete on an unsurveyed site" — the materialization gate in one image. Candidate canon teaching line (light).
- **1F — option-pricing lens (focused).** Deferring an artifact keeps an option open cheaply; building early exercises it before the uncertainty resolves; the trigger is the exercise condition. Accurate but the analogy budget is committed (lazy evaluation) — held to zero lines.
- **1C — the speed adversary (contrarian; the round's most important pass).** "Meaning-first slows everything — sometimes building the artifact IS how meaning stabilizes (prototyping; probe-by-building; the corpus's own run-the-probe-then-critique)." The attack lands on a naive reading and produces the package's sharpest refinement: **the Layer tag marks the WORK, not the output's file-type.** A quick prototype built to TEST whether a concept's meaning holds is **meaning-layer work** (an epistemic probe) even though it emits code — it passes the gate by definition. What the gate actually blocks is *commitment-grade* artifact-building on unstable meaning (the real waste), not probing. Adopted into the principle and the field text, with the probe-build as the worked counter-example.

### 2. Combination (Generator)

- **2G — layer-distribution × the Expedition checkpoint.** The existing move-distribution telemetry line gains layer counts (`meaning×6 structural×2 process×1`) — monoculture detection extends to layer-skew for one extra token. Adopted as a consequence note.
- **2F — the viability gate × the Selector's option-vocabulary.** "The dive precedes descent" is expressible in EXISTING vocabulary: prefer the TEST/DIAGNOSE-typed route on the parent over go-deeper routes into its children. No new move; the gate is a preference over existing types. Adopted into the one-line policy forms.
- **2C — deferred-not-lost × the see-phase ledger-read (closes the "who fires the trigger" gap).** Parked materialization routes carry the trigger "meaning stabilizes"; the spine RE-READS the ledger at every see-phase (the Expedition already commits this), and integrate-runs re-confirm identities with fresh Confidence — so trigger-checking is the EXISTING ledger-read, no new mechanism. When a concept's meaning-Confidence flips HIGH, its parked artifacts re-surface at the next see-phase.

### 3. Inversion (Framer; depth-checked)

- **3G — artifact-first inversion.** When is building-first right? (a) probe-builds — handled by 1C (they're meaning-work); (b) meaning already HIGH-Confidence — the gate simply doesn't apply (pass-through); (c) external demand/deadline — the override right. The inversion confirms the gate's edges; absorbed.
- **3F — the dive-never inversion (focused; adopted).** "Always explore children directly — sometimes touching ONE child is the cheapest way to settle the parent's viability." TRUE — and it upgrades the gate rather than breaking it: the viability gate's honest form is **"spend the CHEAPEST DECISIVE PROBE first"** — usually a parent-level dive, but child-sampling IS a legitimate probe when it is the cheapest decisive one (the sampled child is then itself meaning-work). The gate stops being dogma ("always dive before descending") and becomes economics ("buy the cheapest viability information first"). Adopted as the gate's final form.
- **3C — identity-level tagging inversion.** "Tag concepts, not routes — concepts have layers." Fails: one identity legitimately hosts routes at MULTIPLE layers (DEEPEN its meaning vs DEVELOP its artifact); the layer belongs to the ENGAGEMENT'S WORK, which is route-level — confirming 1C's work-not-output rule from the other side. Absorbed with the clarification.
