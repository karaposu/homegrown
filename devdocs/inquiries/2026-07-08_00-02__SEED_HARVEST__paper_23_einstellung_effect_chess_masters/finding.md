## Finding

### 1. What the paper shows

Chess players of widely ranging strength solved problems with two solutions — a familiar, non-optimal one (the smothered mate) and a shorter, less familiar one — plus matched "extinction" versions where moving one piece disabled the familiar solution. Everyone solved the extinction problems; the failures on the two-solution problems were therefore pure blocking, not missing knowledge. The blocking was enormous (ordinary experts performed like players three standard deviations weaker), invisible to its victims (all searched for a better solution, for as long as the successful players did, and declared the familiar one best), invisible to expert *predictors* (the 86/74/59-vs-50/18/0 miss), and graded by expertise (grandmasters escaped; the paper's resolution: "inflexibility of experts is both reality and myth — but the greater the level of expertise, the more of a myth it becomes"; the escape mechanism is finer-differentiated memory indexing). A third experiment added a subtler shape: a plan *correctly rejected* in one position could not be re-considered in the next, where its blocking feature persisted but other changes had neutralized it — only the best players re-examined it. And the effect's danger is structural: a good-enough solution gives **no feedback** that a better one exists.

### 2. What the harness already owns (the boundary)

Most of this lands on owned ground, checked absorber by absorber: the lock phenomenon itself (the harvest's papers-5–14 one-frame streak is a recorded instance; p21-S1 targets it at the selection level); distrust of self-assessment *where mechanized* (the verify-canon discipline exists because felt-complete assessments failed eleven recorded times — the paper is that phenomenon, measured); counter-generation *at bounded loci* (the innovate discipline's Inherited Frame Audit, piece-level inversions, and survival-bias variant force challengers at meta-decision pieces and seed-level assumptions); finding-level re-open conditions (the Refinement-Triggers template); and the mental-extinction move (the status-quo-bias corrective — "would I reach the same conclusion if it were undocumented?" — is literally the paper's 1-solution manipulation run in the head).

One reliance is *not* owned: **the ordinary, free-form prosecution steps** — td-critique's "construct the strongest possible case against," sensemaking's "state the strongest counter-argument" — where most prosecuting actually happens. Their reliability is assumed, verdict-consuming, and named as a risk in no prior artifact. The bounded machinery's own trigger conditions are the evidence: the designers mechanized where they saw risk and left the rest free-form — which *expresses* the standing confidence this paper gives reason to doubt.

### 3. The gate's adjudication (the three prosecutions)

**The LLM-disanalogy (the hardest test, faced first):** the paper's mechanism is human long-term-memory chunk-indexing; its effect sizes and gradient do not transfer. The candidate survives on what does: the structural triad — retrieval weighted by familiarity (for an LLM: higher-probability continuations, further raised by a warm session frame), a satisficing stop (a strong-seeming counter ends the paragraph), and no error signal (a plausible counter "works") — plus corroboration that is entirely native (our own misses, not chess data). The disanalogy *narrowed* the claim rather than killing it, and the unknown — our actual miss-rate — became the seed's exact action.

**The ownership steelman:** "the machinery shows we already distrust unaided search" fails on scope — mitigations elsewhere are not doubt of *this* reliance; the confirmation of the mechanization *strategy* is real and lands in the ladder.

**The class-population check (shown):** all four grounds of the pass — the target's presence/reliance/prior-undoubtedness, the evidence's bearing, the news test, the grade — are type-independent. The verdict is class-blind; the milestone decided nothing.

**The grade — LIVE, with its honest cap.** Unlike p21-S1 (an optional lever for a human-steered selection process — nascent), the doubted mechanism here runs inside every dive today, and the routed act is cheap and concrete: run the audit. The confidence field stays *med*: the doubt is multi-source-evidenced; its project-side magnitude is unmeasured — measuring it is the item's job, and the paper's numbers are never to be quoted as ours.
