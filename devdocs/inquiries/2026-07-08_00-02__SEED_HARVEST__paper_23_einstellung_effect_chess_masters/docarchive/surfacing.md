## Region A — the source's claim-inventory (verbatim-anchored) [core]

- **A1. The paradigm** (core, HIGH — :198-216, :368-381): two-solution problems (a familiar-but-non-optimal solution + a better less-familiar one) vs 1-solution "extinction" versions where the familiar solution is disabled by moving one piece. The extinction control shows failures aren't knowledge-gaps: **every expert who failed the 2-solution problem solved the 1-solution version** (:454-457, :631-633).
- **A2. The headline quantification** (core, HIGH — :483-484, :666-668, :874-876): *"The presence of a familiar solution reduced the problem solving abilities of the experts to about that of players 3 SDs lower in skill."* (GM 100% / IM 50% / M 18% / CM 0% on the 2-solution problem, all 100% on extinction — Table 2 :509-521.)
- **A3. The expertise gradient** (core, HIGH — :527-531, :877-878, :1034-1035): *"the more knowledgeable the experts were, the less likely they were to be trapped"*; *"Greater expertise led to greater flexibility, not less"*; *"The way to avoid inflexibility is not less expertise but more."* Mechanism (:889-906): better-indexed, more differentiated memory (chunks/templates) — the optimal solution is retrievable even in the short verification window.
- **A4. THE SEARCH-WITHOUT-RETRIEVAL FINDING** (core, HIGH — the sharpest item; :485-492, :540-550, :715-720, :902-903): ALL players, having found the familiar solution, **genuinely looked for a better one** (verbal protocols; the habitual double-check) — and the failers spent THE SAME time searching as the finders (8-9s vs 7-10s median; Exp 2: 9-22s vs 9-20s). *"the source of expert inflexibility is not motivational. They spent as long looking."* The block is at **retrieval**, not effort: a genuine-feeling, time-consuming search still surfaces only what the familiar solution lets through. (NOTE: this paper has NO eye-tracking — the gaze looking-without-seeing study is a separate Bilalić et al. paper; what THIS paper shows is search-effort-without-retrieval, protocol-verified.)
- **A5. THE METACOGNITIVE MISS** (core, HIGH — :493-500, :536-539, :694-697, :1023-1024): 12 experts **shown both solutions** predicted 86%/74%/59% success (IM/M/CM); actual: 50%/18%/0%. One: *"No Master would miss [the shorter solution] in this position."* *"The hidden power of the Einstellung effect was demonstrated by the inaccurate metacognition of another group of experts who seriously underestimated the effect."* Experts cannot feel the blocking power even when both options are visible.
- **A6. The no-feedback danger** (core, HIGH — :546-548, :1016-1026, :1040-1045): *"there is no feedback available on the inappropriateness of the well-known solution"* — the good-enough solution works, so nothing signals a better one exists. Real-world: Reason's nuclear operators; *"the majority of errors in expert text editor behaviour"*; Exp 2's Problem 3, where the familiar solution leads to a FORCED LOSS (not always benign).
- **A7. Experiment 3 — re-considering a correctly-rejected plan** (core, HIGH — :775-831): players who had CORRECTLY rejected a plan in problem 2 (because of a critical feature) could not re-consider it in problem 3 where the feature persisted but OTHER changes neutralized it. *"Most of the players mentioned the right plan as a possibility but discarded it because they evaluated it negatively. The best players... proceeded to examine the right plan in more detail"* — only they broke the fixation. A correct rejection, once made, blocks re-examination while its trigger-feature remains visible.
- **A8. The two-stage model + difficulty interaction** (sub, HIGH — :957-963, :669-676, :866-867): retrieve-first, then a quick verification-search (seconds); flexibility = better performance at BOTH stages; the effect interacts with difficulty (easy problems → ordinary experts flexible too; hard → even supers waver).
- **A9. Theory adjudication** (side, HIGH — :916-951): the super-experts' "switch" (seeing the optimal FIRST sometimes) favors indexing theories (chunking/template — fine discrimination) over pure strengthening (ACT-R) or instance-race (Logan) accounts. *"rare but optimal solutions are so well indexed that they can be retrieved in spite of the presence of typical and well-reinforced solutions."* Plus: *"Creativity is a consequence of expertise"* (:1038).
