## Phase 2 — Generate (7 mechanisms × 3)

### 1. Lens Shifting (Framer)

- **1G — instrument lens.** The system as observatory: providers grind the lenses (weights); the project builds the mount, tracking, and observation log (the loop + artifacts) and points the instrument. Understanding advances by instrument-building — a respected epistemic tradition (microscopes, telescopes). Feeds the outsider quotable form: *"we don't grind new lenses; we build the observatory around existing ones."*
- **1F — evolution lens (focused on path bias).** Pretraining = phylogeny (species-level priors); post-training = domestication (breeder preference); prompting = immediate stimulus; **the fifth layer = culture** — transmitted, persistent, self-modifying practice that reshapes what the same organism does. Culture is humanity's own fifth layer; the analogy enriches the dictionary and gives the S1/S2 story its developmental frame.
- **1C — product lens (contrarian).** Read the importance statement as a skeptical investor: consciousness vocabulary without scoping reads as woo. Adopted as a CONSTRAINT on the outsider form: lead with capability + auditability; the research window is the upside, never the headline.

### 2. Combination (Generator)

- **2G — white-box offer × the measurement framework.** The research window needs no new instrumentation: indicator traces + the 15 self-improvement-rate questions ARE the observation protocol of the model organism. The window's feasibility claim becomes concrete: the instruments are already designed; only the traversal memory they read from is pending.
- **2F — S1/S2 analogy × the failure-mode catalogs → the Bias Atlas.** A new artifact candidate: map every cataloged discipline failure mode to its human-cognitive-bias analog (premature stabilization ↔ jumping-to-conclusions/anchoring; surface fluency ↔ availability; rubber-stamping ↔ confirmation bias; survival bias ↔ survivorship; false convergence ↔ premature closure…). Value: (i) it is the S1/S2 analogy made testable (each mapping predicts where a discipline should help); (ii) it is a publishable bridge artifact between the project and cognitive science; (iii) it took one combination step because both halves already exist in the corpus.
- **2C — path-bias stack × representation-level steering.** Adversarial edge: if per-call activation steering can replicate the fifth layer's effects, distinctness weakens. Converted into a named test (the steering-comparison arm in the test-set) rather than left as a worry.

### 3. Inversion (Framer; depth-checked)

- **3G — the central inversion (on claim 3).** Belief: "skills change how the AI traverses." L1 invert: the loop never changes the model's per-call traversal — every call still follows path bias. L2 (system-level): **the fifth layer is selection, not steering** — it changes which traversals are *generated again, tested, kept, and built upon*. Resolution at two grains, both true: **within-call**, context shapes a single biased sample; **across-calls**, the loop runs generate–test–keep selection over many samples (critique's verdicts ARE the selection pressure; Baldwin encoding makes selections heritable). This sharpens claim 3 decisively against "just prompting": prompting shapes one sample; the loop is an evolutionary architecture over samples. Multi-axis check — existence-axis: is anything changed per-call at all? Yes, context/skill text does bias the call (steering exists at grain 1); identity-axis: what IS the fifth layer? a selection-and-memory system wearing a prompting interface.
- **3F — focused (test-set).** "Test the loop against prompting" → invert: test by REMOVAL — ablation arms (the loop minus critique; minus surfacing; minus memory) for per-component attribution, instead of all-or-nothing comparison. Adopted into the test-set.
- **3C — contrarian (dictionary).** "Path bias is the provider's" → invert: **the project also embeds bias** — the disciplines encode their author's cognitive culture; the fifth layer is OUR path bias. Adopted as an honesty clause in the definition: the fifth layer is not bias-removal but **bias-choice** — declared, inspectable, editable bias replacing opaque, inherited, uneditable bias. The distinctiveness claim is auditability + editability, never neutrality.

### 4. Constraint Manipulation (Framer; both directions)

- **4-ADD (generic).** Every canon-bound sentence of the claim-set carries an inline status tag — `[canon]`, `[repo-fact]`, `[test: <name>]`, or `[flag: speculation]`. The calibration rule become format, checkable at a glance.
- **4-ADD (focused).** Pre-registration constraint for indicator-emergence: criteria count only if written BEFORE the first traversal-memory traces exist (the mirage caution operationalized as a timing rule).
- **4-REMOVE (generic).** Remove the "phenomenal out of scope" boundary — what would be gained? Nothing testable; only credibility lost and the substrate-honest canon broken. The removal experiment CONFIRMS the scope is load-bearing, not timid. Recorded.
- **4-REMOVE (contrarian).** Remove "this project as the instance" — write the thesis project-agnostically (any scaffolded system), with homegrown as the case study. Gains external/publishable value; risks diluting the WHY-stratum function. Survives as a sub-option inside the attachment piece, not as the primary form.

### 5. Absence Recognition (Generator; both levels, bidirectional)

- **Patch-level absences:** the package lacked a definition of **"traversal" itself at its two grains** (grain 1: the token-path within one call; grain 2: the revolution-path across calls — the loop operates at grain 2 over grain-1 episodes); lacked an explicit **falsifier per claim** (added to the test-set's design); lacked the negative-result statement for the window (recovered from sensemaking A5).
- **Redesign-level.** Designed from scratch, the WHY-stratum is ONE canon page linking Wager → gradient → window — which is exactly the "thin both" attachment option (new short thesis doc + one-paragraph Wager pointer).
- **Bidirectional (already-present).** The corpus already contains the hedged thesis: `docs/unevaluable/consciousness.md` ("adjacent ingredients"), the `random_notes` "Controversial" note ("an ARCHITECTURE from which meta-cognition could emerge"), and the skeptic summary's hypothesis statement. The package should **quote its ancestors**, not merely cite them — this is promotion-with-lineage, and it proves the thesis is native, not imported enthusiasm.

### 6. Domain Transfer (Generator; native source included)

- **6-native (AI/search).** The **generator–evaluator** decomposition, and specifically the AlphaGo precedent: a policy network alone (the biased prior) plays far weaker than the same network inside tree search (selection over its proposals). *Search on top of priors beats priors alone* — a famous, rigorous precedent for claims 3 and 6(a), in exactly the two-grain form the central inversion produced: unchanged generator, transformed outcomes via structured selection.
- **6-different (biology).** **Model-organism practice:** genetics chose C. elegans and Drosophila for *inspectability and manipulability* (small, fully mapped — the worm's complete connectome), not for human-likeness. The white-box offer is the same move: the project's loop is a candidate **C. elegans of functional cognition** — not because it is brain-like, but because every step is mapped and editable.
- **6-different (philosophy of science).** The **instrumentation tradition:** understanding has repeatedly advanced by building instruments that render phenomena observable and manipulable before theory matured (microscope → cell theory). The research window claims this lineage: the loop as an instrument for cognition-phenomena. Counters "engineering isn't science" with history.

### 7. Extrapolation (Generator)

- **7G — convergence pressure.** If fifth-layer structure demonstrably helps, providers will absorb it into post-training (training on multi-step traces; built-in deliberation) — the fourth layer eats the fifth's lessons over time. Consequence for the importance statement: the durable assets are the **auditable method, the declared-bias practice, and the window** — not any specific loop trick. Honesty about defensibility, written in.
- **7F — measurement timeline.** Indicator statistics become meaningful only after on-the-order-of-100 recorded revolutions; the window opens slowly. Expectation-setting belongs in the attachment proposal: this is a decade-shaped bet, matching the asymptotic north star.
- **7C — the negative branch.** If baseline tests show NO outcome gain over skilled prompting: what survives? The white-box window still functions (auditability is independent of superiority), the Bias Atlas still stands, and the bias-choice honesty still differentiates. Consequence adopted: the importance statement must be written **negative-branch-resilient** — importance = the capability bet AND the window, such that the window alone sustains the project's research identity if the bet underperforms.
