**Strongest counter-interpretation:** "[∥ 11-30] and (prerequisite) are dependency edges in disguise; blessing any of it dissolves the no-edge rule and routelister starts sequencing."

**Confidence:** HIGH.
**Resolution:** the line is drawn RIGHT (inter-concept dependency edges stay banned — they are the seed of sequencing graphs); [∥]-style cross-map identity pointers are LEGAL and name a real unmet need; (prerequisite) is legal; the WHY-phrasing case earns a clarifying example in the spec (how to state gate-ness as an area-property), not a rule change.
