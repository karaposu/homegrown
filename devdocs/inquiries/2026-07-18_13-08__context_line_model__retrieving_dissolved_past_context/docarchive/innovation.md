## P1 — THE MODEL (finding-ready)

**The context line** — *the finite span of live context, laid along the mover's path through thinking space; it advances by dissolving at the tail.* In one formula: **grasp set in motion.**

The picture: thinking space is the terrain (points = questions; clockless — it changes only when traversed). The mover walks it. What the mover *holds while walking* — canon's **grasp**, the live context — is not a point but a **span**: it stretches back over the last stretch of path, covering the nodes still held live. The span's length is fixed (reach is vast; grasp is small). So **advancing is paid for at the tail**: gain five centimeters at the head, lose five at the tail. The lost stretch does not vanish — it crosses the **encode→consolidate boundary**: it persists as **artifacts** (the md record — "the inquiry folders ARE the path record") and **digests** (summaries, indexes), but it is no longer held — *reachable, not grasped* (the availability illusion: "the ability to look something up is not the same as holding it"). Looking back — **retrieval** — is possible and *costs grasp*: every re-load spends span that could have held the front. And the picture recurses: **every digest layer is itself a finite line** (the live proof: MEMORY.md is over its load limit right now).
