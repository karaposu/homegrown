## Phase 3 — Ambiguity Collapse

### Ambiguity 1 — Candidate A: real lack, or decorative reframe (the INFLATION test)?
**Strongest counter-interpretation (that A is a REAL import):** the false-photograph structure (K1) is genuinely structural — physical-vs-mental representation is a real distinction, and cold-navigation's terrain-only warming (K2) might be a *limitation* of cold-navigation, not evidence against A. On this reading, A names a real lack: the navigation session SHOULD model the worker-as-agent, and our warming misses it.
**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** our architecture **deliberately designs the agent-modeling away** (K3). Findings are authored self-contained; consolidation distills the worker's episodic detail into semantic gist *inside the artifact*. The mind is **externalized into the record on purpose** — so a reader recovers meaning by reading the distilled artifact + the terrain, not by reconstructing the author's mind. That is *why* warming is terrain-based, not an oversight: the design goal is to make cross-session theory-of-mind UNNECESSARY. So A's "agent-modeling gap" is not a gap — it is a thing our memory design specifically eliminates. (This is structural, not precedent: the mechanism — self-contained-gist-in-artifact — is what removes the need.) And A-2 (false-belief ≈ superseded findings) is already carried by `SUPERSEDED_BY` + reconsolidation (B-5).
**Confidence:** HIGH.
**Resolution:** **Candidate A is DECORATIVE — killed.** The false-photograph parallel is elegant but names nothing we lack and resolves no confusion we had. The INFLATION trap the branch flagged is real, and this is it.
**What is now fixed:** cross-session work in our system does not require, and is designed not to require, theory of mind; the artifact replaces the mind.
**What is no longer allowed:** crediting "cross-session ToM" as an import; laundering the ToM label into the steering canon.
**What now depends on this:** the verdict headline (NO); the honest note that A's death CONFIRMS (does not import) the self-contained-findings principle.

### Ambiguity 2 — Candidate B: bounded-real import, or generic "control for confounds"?
**Strongest counter-interpretation (that B is DECORATIVE):** "an apparent high-level failure may be a lower-level failure in disguise — control for the confound" is just good experimental hygiene, generic and domain-neutral. We didn't need a ToM paper to know to control confounds.
**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** the *generic* form is decorative, granted — but the *specific* form is not. The specific confound the paper dissociates (a **competence/reasoning** failure vs a **working-memory** failure) is exactly the confound our domain is MOST prone to, because reach≠grasp (S1) establishes that grasp is tiny — so grasp-failures are COMMON and easily misread as competence-failures. The import is the **specific pairing** (apparent-reasoning-failure ⇄ grasp-failure) applied on the **evaluation/observer side** (S1) — a side reach≠grasp had only addressed from self-perception — **plus** a concrete dissociation procedure (put the info "in front of" the session / warm it and re-test; if the failure vanishes it was grasp, not competence). That NAMES an evaluation practice we hadn't named.
**Confidence:** MED-HIGH (real, but small and an extension — see below).
**Resolution:** **Candidate B SURVIVES as a bounded-real import** — but explicitly as an **extension of reach≠grasp to the evaluation layer**, not a free-standing breakthrough. Smallest honest form: *when a traverse's output looks like bad reasoning, first control for grasp (was the relevant context loaded?) before concluding a competence deficit; the dissociation test is to re-run with the material in front.*
**What is now fixed:** B is a diagnostic that belongs to the evaluation layer (regression-detection / quality-assessment / critique), scoped as a reach≠grasp corollary.
**What is no longer allowed:** inflating B into a standalone "breakthrough"; also, zeroing it to keep the NO clean (the mirror error).
**What now depends on this:** the residue in the finding; a route to the evaluation layer.
