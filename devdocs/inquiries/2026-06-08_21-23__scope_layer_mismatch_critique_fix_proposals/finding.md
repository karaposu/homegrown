### 1. The mechanism — what makes Scope-Mismatch distinct

Scope-Mismatch is the failure where `/td-critique`'s Phase 2 DEFENSE step invokes a defense at scope X to neutralize a concern at scope Y. Defense-phase reasoning often pulls a project memory (e.g., "PERMISSION-not-CONSTRAINT" defined at LLM-emission scope, "anti-FETCHING" defined at input-reach scope, "Layer Commitment" defined at meaning-layer scope, "structurally testable" defined at intra-spec scope) and uses it to neutralize a concern at a different scope (consumer scope, emission-content scope, structural-layer scope, persistent-across-artifact scope). The defense passes Phase 2 Collision without the scope gap being traversed.

**The structural insight: defense quality has TWO orthogonal axes — STRENGTH and SCOPE-MATCH.** Existing /td-critique catches strength deficit via #3 Nitpicking ("defense absent or too weak"); no existing failure mode catches scope-match deficit. The fix adds scope-match as a defense-quality dimension: a TABLE construct (Defense Scope | Concern Scope | Match?) per defense invocation at Phase 2 DEFENSE step.

### 6. Defense-Quality Two-Axes Substrate (NEW substrate piece)

The substrate piece this inquiry establishes — no prior sibling had this:

Defense quality has TWO orthogonal axes:
- **STRENGTH:** is the defense well-formed / non-trivial / robust? (#3 Nitpicking catches deficit)
- **SCOPE-MATCH:** does the defense's scope match the concern's scope? (#5 catches deficit)

A defense can be:
- **Strong + scope-matched** → VALID (defense survives collision; standard SURVIVE)
- **Weak + scope-matched** → #3 Nitpicking fires (defense absent or too weak)
- **Strong + mis-scoped** → #5 fires (defense present but wrong scope — the LOAD-BEARING case)
- **Weak + mis-scoped** → BOTH #3 and #5 fire (co-firing)
