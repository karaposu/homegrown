## (A) SEEN facts — the paper's claims (relevance-tagged)

- **P-A [CORE] — the accessibility model (FOK is INFERRED, not directly accessed).** Koriat REJECTS Hart's trace-based "internal-monitor" view. VERBATIM (:212-217): *"the cues for the FOK are to be found in the very information that is activated or accessed during the course of the search-and-retrieval process. Thus, the computation of FOK judgments is parasitic on the processes of retrieval."* Relevance: DIRECT — this is the paper's thesis and the import candidate.
- **P-B — byproducts.** VERBATIM (:224-230): *"such clues, the by-products of the retrieval process, can be used to evaluate the likelihood that that answer is correct... even when retrieval fails, these very clues contain important information."* The FOK reads the debris of the attempt. Relevance: DIRECT.
- **P-C — accessibility = AMOUNT + intensity.** VERBATIM (:251-254): *"the amount of information activated or accessed and its intensity (e.g., its ease of access, vividness, specificity, and persistence)."* Relevance: DIRECT (the seed candidate lives here — QUANTITY as the signal).
- **P-D — fluency→illusion.** VERBATIM (:286-291): *"Fluency of processing can serve as a valid cue for remembering... However, fluent processing can also result from other sources, and when such sources are difficult to specify, fluent processing may lead to memory illusions."* Accessibility/fluency ≠ correctness. Relevance: DIRECT (maps to the confabulation guard).
- **P-E — no privileged access + the striking result.** VERBATIM (:327-332): *"subjects have no privileged access to an internal monitor... It is only by attempting to search for the solicited target that one can assess the likelihood."* The empirical punch (:2684-2704, :2735-2737): the mere NUMBER OF LETTERS recalled predicts recognition as well as FOK (72% vs 61% hit rate; FOK↔letters correlation .94); *"subjects' FOK judgments do not have access to information that is not already contained in the output of the retrieval attempt."* Relevance: DIRECT (the cheap-quantity-proxy seed).
- **P-F — accuracy as byproduct.** FOK accuracy depends on the extent to which accessibility at t1 predicts accuracy at t2 (:2758-2764); FOK FAILS when accessibility ≠ correctness. Relevance: DIRECT.
- **P-G — subject-vs-experimenter perspective.** Subjective (accessibility, computable now) vs objective (correctness, known only later) properties of memory (:344-355). Relevance: SUPPORTING (maps to Predictive-RC-now vs Retrospective-RC-later).
- **P-H — FOK→control feedback.** VERBATIM (:2932-2942): *"not only does the amount of accessible information affect FOK judgments but the latter may also feed back into the search process."* FOK drives search continuation/termination. Relevance: SUPPORTING (the monitoring→control link).
- **P-KEY-SELF-NOTE — Koriat grounds FOK on the availability heuristic.** VERBATIM (:243-246): *"This account of the mechanism underlying FOK is similar to the availability heuristic postulated by Tversky and Kahneman (1973)."* Relevance: DIRECT — this is the exact concept the canon already names as "the availability illusion" (see B/grasp_management:39).
- **P-QUARANTINE — the apparatus.** The 3 experiments, the letter-recall paradigm, the gamma/point-biserial correlations, Figure 1's link model (PI-C/PI-W/memory-strength links), the median-split hit-rates, the perseveration-error analysis — the scientific machinery. NON-transferable (reverse-lens quarantine). Relevance: PERIPHERAL (method, not a harness practice).
