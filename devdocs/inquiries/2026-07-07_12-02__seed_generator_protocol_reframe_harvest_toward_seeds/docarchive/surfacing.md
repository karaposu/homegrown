# Surfacing — seed_generator / reframe-the-harvest-toward-seeds

**Case:** artifact (existing harness files) + possibility (the design question). **Territory spec:** explicit-bounded (the harness's seed/breakthrough/harvest machinery + canon concept-set).

**HEADLINE (the one finding that reframes the whole dive):** the word **"seed" is already overloaded across three existing places in the harness**, and they are NOT the same concept. Any rigorous definition — and the seed_generator's very name — must reckon with this overload FIRST. Two of the three senses are *inputs* (launch-points a loop generates FROM); the user's proposal is the *third* sense (an *output* germ extracted from studying a paper). The reframe is **substantially warranted** (the harvest's value empirically lands in seeds + confirming, not breakthroughs), with **one firm correction** (the breakthrough/import test is not the wrong goal — it's the right GATE; keep it as the seed-quality filter, don't discard it).

## (A) SEEN facts — the existing machinery (each file-verified, with quotes)

### S-1 — THE "SEED" OVERLOAD (the central finding): three distinct senses, file-verified

**Sense 1 — the INNOVATE-seed (an INPUT trigger).** `.claude/skills/innovate/references/innovate.md:77-91`: *"Innovation starts with a seed — something that triggers the process."* It has a 7-TYPE taxonomy (`:81-89` table): **Gap** ("something should exist here but doesn't"), **Dissatisfaction** ("this works but something feels wrong"), **Constraint** ("we can't do X because of Y"), **Question** ("what if…?"), **Signal** ("pre-verbal sensing"), **Failure** ("this didn't work, but why?"), **Collision** ("two unrelated things in proximity"). → An innovate-seed is the **felt starting-point that generation works FROM**; upstream fuel. (Notably, the USER'S OWN request is an innovate-seed: a **Dissatisfaction** ["breakthrough-search feels not-useful"] + a **Gap** ["a seed_generator should exist"].)

**Sense 2 — the PARADIGM-SWEEPER seed / "seed-block" (a LAUNCH-POINT input).** `.claude/skills/paradigm-sweeper/references/paradigm_sweeper.md:55`: *"seed / seed-block — the per-entry, copy-pasteable loop input."* `:124`: *"Every admitted entry carries a seed-block … a copy-pasteable loop input, capped at ~6 lines — its fields pin the family's non-default commitments so a dive doesn't regress to the modal reading."* `:192`: *"divergence-pinning, not description."* → A sweeper-seed is a **copy-pasteable launch-block for a downstream dive**. It FEEDS sense 1 (the seed-block becomes the innovate-seed a loop generates from) — `:15` names an "S1 seed-time hook inside the innovation discipline." So senses 1 and 2 form a **producer→consumer chain of INPUTS**.

**Sense 3 — the HARVEST "breakthrough seed" (an OUTPUT germ).** From the paper findings + `non-sycophancy-both-ways.md:25-26`: a harvest-seed is *"a genuine LIVE germ — one that changes a future design decision — for an explicitly-UN-BUILT component,"* extracted FROM a paper. LIVE iff it **"changes a FUTURE design decision the harness hasn't already made"** (the change-a-decision test). Concrete: paper 17 planted a measurement-seed (dissociate the un-built quality-hunch's calibration into ranking-vs-bias); paper 19 planted a computation-seed (compute the hunch from a cheap quantity-proxy); paper 20's candidate collapsed. → A harvest-seed is a **downstream YIELD extracted from studying an external source**, anchored to a harness concept.

**THE DISAMBIGUATION (load-bearing):** senses 1+2 are **INPUTS** (launch-points: "explore the X approach to Y"); sense 3 is an **OUTPUT** (a yield: "paper 17 gives the harness a germ to grow"). The user's "seed_generator that searches papers for seeds" is **sense 3**. But the NAME "seed_generator" reads like senses 1+2 (paradigm-sweeper literally already "generates seeds" as launch-blocks). **This naming collision is the first thing the definition must resolve.** [FLAG for Critique: is sense-3 truly distinct from senses 1+2, or is a harvest-seed just an innovate-seed of type "Gap/Question" that happens to be sourced from a paper? Test the distinctness.]

### S-2 — THE IMPORT TEST is a GATE, not a GOAL (grounds the reframe-adjudication)

The import test — *a paper imports iff it NAMES something un-named OR RESOLVES a confusion; else confirming/decorative* — functions as a **quality gate**, not the harvest's purpose. Evidence: `non-sycophancy-both-ways.md:16` calls it *"the established neutral gate."* The harvest grades MOST papers NO on the breakthrough reading yet extracts value every time (seeds, confirming-plus, mirrors — the whole verdict-ladder at `:27`). Paper 20's finding shows the gate WORKING: the candidate seed *collapsed AT the gate* (file-verified against `evolving_quality_assetment_component.md:56`), which is precisely what keeps a manufactured seed out. → **The gate is the thing that makes a "seed" credible.** [This is the load-bearing fact for the non-sycophancy correction: the critique "breakthrough-search is not useful" conflates *"the breakthrough VERDICT is usually NO"* (true) with *"the breakthrough GATE is useless"* (false — it's the seed-quality filter).]

## (C) The gate-vs-goal fact-base (for the reframe-adjudication)

- **The critique's TRUE part:** the harvest's yield is seeds + confirming + mirrors, not breakthroughs (S-5). Framing success as "breakthrough-hunting" mis-sets the criterion. The user is RIGHT that the goal is mis-named.
- **The critique's OVERSTATED part:** "searching for breakthroughs is not useful" treats the breakthrough test as the GOAL. But it's the GATE (S-2) — the strict bar that makes a seed credible (paper 20's seed collapsed at it; 17/19's survived it). Discarding the gate would let manufactured seeds through (exactly the sycophancy the whole harvest guards against).
- **The honest synthesis (for Sensemaking to stabilize):** REFRAME THE GOAL (breakthrough-hunting → seed-harvesting) while KEEPING THE GATE (the import test becomes the seed-quality filter, not the success-criterion). The breakthrough verdict stays — demoted from "the point" to "the top of the ladder / a rare seed-that-already-grew."

## (D) Region-assessment

### The reframe-status fork: FORMALIZATION-with-a-reframe (dominant), not pure-NEW / pure-RENAME
- **REFRAME (of the goal): WARRANTED.** The success-criterion should shift from breakthrough to seed-yield (S-5 empirical support). Credit the user.
- **NEW (protocol): PARTIAL.** seed_generator would be a new NAMED artifact, but its mechanism is scattered-but-present (S-3) — not a blank slate.
- **RENAME: PARTIAL.** "breakthrough SEED or not?" → "seed_generator" renames the seed-half of the existing fork.
- **FORMALIZATION: DOMINANT.** The best reading: consolidate the scattered, already-working seed-extraction (the fork + routelister seed-routes + the change-a-decision test + the future-seed folder) into ONE named, deliberate protocol, with the success-criterion reframed and the import test retained as the quality-gate.

### The definitional landscape (what a rigorous seed-definition must cover)
1. **Disambiguate** the 3 senses; name sense-3 (output-germ) as seed_generator's target.
2. **The LIVE-test:** keep the change-a-decision test (a seed changes a future design decision the harness hasn't made).
3. **The anchor-constraint:** "relates to a harness concept" — already the LIVE-test's content (S-4); make it explicit (a seed names its harness anchor: a canon frame / un-built component / open question).
4. **A taxonomy of harvest-seeds** (candidate — borrow innovate's structure but for OUTPUTS): build-seed (17's measurement), compute-seed (19's proxy), frame-seed, measure-seed, … [to be developed in Innovation].
5. **Seed vs neighbors** on the verdict-ladder: import (names/resolves NOW) · confirming-plus (agrees) · mirror (reflects) · **seed (not-yet-but-could-grow)** · breakthrough (a new frame/practice — a seed already grown). The seed is the "germinal" verdict-kind.

### The seed_generator-relates-to-existing map
- **vs paradigm-sweeper:** sweeper generates INPUT launch-blocks (sense 2) from a *practice's paradigm-space*; seed_generator extracts OUTPUT germs (sense 3) from an *external source*, anchored to harness concepts. Different direction, different territory — but the sweeper's "enumerate, never select / the seed-block contract / the anchoring guard / the persistent index" are strong STRUCTURAL precedents to borrow.
- **vs the traverse "breakthrough SEED or not?" fork:** seed_generator formalizes + reframes the fork's seed-half (flips the success-criterion; keeps the gate).
- **vs routelister:** routelister already records seeds as typed routes — seed_generator's output could BE a routelister-style typed record (or feed it).
- **vs innovate:** innovate CONSUMES seeds (sense 1); seed_generator PRODUCES seeds (sense 3). A produced sense-3 seed could later become a sense-1 seed for an innovation dive — a producer→consumer link, like sweeper→innovate.
