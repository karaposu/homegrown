# Sensemaking — seed_generator / reframe-the-harvest-toward-seeds

## Cognitive anchors

- **A-1 (the seed overload):** "seed" already means three things — innovate-seed (INPUT trigger, 7 types), paradigm-sweeper seed-block (INPUT launch-point), harvest breakthrough-seed (OUTPUT germ). The user's proposal is the OUTPUT sense; the name collides with the INPUT senses.
- **A-2 (the reframe):** "breakthrough-search is not useful" — warranted-with-a-correction.
- **A-3 (the gate):** the import/breakthrough test is a quality-GATE (what makes a seed credible), not the harvest's goal.
- **A-4 (the LIVE-test = the anchor-constraint):** "changes a future design decision the harness hasn't made" IS "relates to our concepts" (a seed anchored to an un-built/undecided harness concept).
- **A-5 (the user's phrase — "interesting development and understanding seeds"):** possibly LOOSER than the strict change-a-decision test — it wants to capture illuminating developments, not only decision-changers. A real tension worth resolving, not flattening.
- **A-6 (scattered-but-present):** the seed-extraction mechanism already exists, scattered (the fork + routelister seed-routes + the change-a-decision test + a `docs/future-seed/` folder).
- **A-7 (the naming tension):** seed_generator "generates" outputs, but "seed" already names inputs elsewhere.

## Perspectives checked

- **Non-sycophancy both ways:** credit the reframe's true part (mis-set goal) fully; keep the gate (don't rubber-stamp the discard); don't defend the status quo.
- **The harness's own design philosophy:** typed-everything + the verdict-ladder — a "seed" should slot into the ladder as a typed verdict-kind with its own test.
- **The user's intent:** more yield from the harvest; capture interesting developments + understanding, not just rare breakthroughs.
- **The producer→consumer lifecycle:** a seed is an OUTPUT that can later become an INPUT (a recorded harvest-seed becomes the trigger of its own development run) — the paradigm-sweeper→innovate pattern.

## SV2 — the reframe adjudication: WARRANTED-WITH-A-CORRECTION (non-sycophancy both ways)

- **(a) The TRUE part — credit it fully.** The harvest's success-criterion, framed as "find breakthroughs," genuinely mis-describes where value lands. Empirically (surfacing S-5): the recent run (papers 16-20) yielded thin seeds (17, 19) + confirming-plus + mirrors + **zero breakthroughs**; the genuine imports were early (13, 15). If success = "breakthrough," the recent harvest reads as a long failure — but it wasn't; it was producing seeds and frame-confirmations. **The user has correctly diagnosed a mis-set goal.** This is not defended away.
- **(b) The OVERSTATED part — the correction.** "Searching for breakthroughs is USELESS" conflates two different things: the breakthrough *VERDICT* (usually NO — true) and the breakthrough *GATE* (the import test — the strict bar that makes a seed CREDIBLE). The gate is load-bearing: paper 20's candidate seed collapsed AT it (file-verified), papers 17/19's survived it. Without the gate, the harvest would manufacture seeds to please — the exact sycophancy the whole method guards against. So the gate is not the wrong goal; it's the right *filter*.
- **(c) The SYNTHESIS.** **Reframe the GOAL (breakthrough-hunting → seed-harvesting) while KEEPING the GATE (the import test becomes the seed-quality filter).** "Breakthrough" is demoted from *the point* to *the top of a ladder* — a seed that has already grown into a full new frame/practice. The success-criterion moves from the rare top to the reliable middle (seeds). **This is the honest both-ways landing: the user is right that the goal was mis-set, and right that we should hunt seeds — but the machinery that made the old verdicts trustworthy is exactly what makes the new seed-yields trustworthy, so it stays.**

## SV3 — the seed-definition [THE CENTER]: a two-grade, gated, anchored, output-germ

**The essence (one line):** *A seed is an under-developed idea, extracted from studying a source, anchored to a specific harness concept, that would change a future design decision if developed — a germ whose payoff is deferred but real.*

**The four defining properties:**
- **(i) It is an OUTPUT germ** — the yield of extraction, not the fuel for generation. This distinguishes it from the innovate-seed (INPUT trigger) and the paradigm-sweeper seed-block (INPUT launch-point). Direction is the primary discriminator.
- **(ii) It is ANCHORED to a harness concept** — a canon frame, an un-built component, or an open design question. This IS the user's "relate to our concepts" constraint, and it is already the content of the change-a-decision test (a seed changes a *future harness decision* → it is attached to an un-decided harness concept). Make the anchor EXPLICIT: every seed names its anchor.
- **(iii) It is GATED** — it must connect to something the harness does NOT already own (the import-test descendant: does it NAME / RESOLVE / SEED something un-owned, vs merely confirming/decorating?). The gate is retained verbatim from the harvest.
- **(iv) It has a MATURITY / payoff-timing** — this is what separates a seed from a full import. An **import** pays off NOW (it names/resolves something today); a **seed** pays off AFTER development (it needs growing). Payoff-timing is the seed's signature.

**The two grades (resolving the A-5 tension — "interesting development and understanding" vs the strict test):**
- **LIVE seed (act-grade):** clearly changes a future design decision the harness hasn't made (the strict test — papers 17, 19). Recorded AND actionable.
- **NASCENT seed (watch-grade):** an interesting development anchored to a harness concept that *might* change a decision later but doesn't clearly yet. Recorded and WATCHED, not yet acted on. (This is the harvest's existing "watch-note" — e.g. paper 20's single-data-point hypothesis — promoted to a first-class seed-grade.)
- **Why two grades is the honest synthesis:** the user's "interesting development and understanding seeds" wants a LOOSER net than "changes a decision" — it wants to capture illuminating things. The two-grade definition honors that (nascent = the looser catch) WITHOUT discarding the gate (nascent seeds are recorded/watched but must MATURE to live before they're acted on — the gate moves from "capture" to "act"). Neither rubber-stamps a loose net nor defends the strict one.

**The verdict-ladder, with the seed slotted in:**
`breakthrough (a seed already grown into a new frame/practice) > import (changes a decision NOW) > LIVE seed (would change a future decision) > NASCENT seed (interesting, anchored, might mature) > confirming-plus (strengthens an owned frame) > mirror (reflects by contrast) > confirming-nothing (decorative)`. The seed is the **germinal** region of the ladder — the reliable middle the reframe targets.

**Distinctness from senses 1+2 (prosecuted, flag 1):** is a harvest-seed just an innovate-seed of type "Gap/Question" sourced from a paper? **No — but they are lifecycle-linked.** Distinct because: direction (output-yield vs input-fuel), testedness (a harvest-seed has passed a gate; an innovate-seed can be "a maybe idea"), and anchoring (a harvest-seed names its harness concept; an innovate-seed is a free-floating trigger). **Linked because:** a recorded harvest-seed, when someone later develops it, BECOMES the innovate-seed (the "Question/Gap" trigger) of that development run — exactly the paradigm-sweeper-block → innovate-seed pattern. So sense-3 is a distinct verdict-KIND that can later serve as a sense-1 trigger. Clean.

**The naming tension (stated, not decided):** "seed_generator" reads like it generates INPUT seeds (paradigm-sweeper's job). It actually EXTRACTS OUTPUT germs. Two honest resolutions: (a) keep "seed_generator" but have its spec DECLARE up front "generates sense-3 output-seeds, distinct from innovate/sweeper input-seeds"; (b) rename to remove the collision — **seed_harvester** / **seed_prospector** / **seed_extractor** (harvester fits the existing "harvest" vocabulary best). Recommendation leans (b) seed_harvester, but this is Innovation/Critique's call, and ultimately the user's. Flagged, not decided.

## SV4 — the status verdict + the relation-map

**Status = FORMALIZATION-WITH-A-REFRAME (dominant), prosecuted against the alternatives:**
- **NOT blank-slate-NEW:** the mechanism is scattered-but-present (the fork extracts seeds; routelister records seed-routes; the change-a-decision test gates them; `docs/future-seed/` already exists). Inflating to "brand-new protocol" to please "create a protocol" would be dishonest.
- **NOT pure-RENAME:** it does more than rename the fork's seed-half — it flips the success-criterion (breakthrough → seed), consolidates four scattered pieces, adds the two-grade definition, and may add a deliberate run-mode (run seed_generator ON PURPOSE, not only as a paper-dive byproduct). Deflating to "just a rename" would undersell it.
- **= FORMALIZATION-WITH-A-REFRAME:** consolidate the scattered seed-extraction into ONE named protocol, with the reframed success-criterion and the retained gate. This is the honest middle — real design work, built on existing machinery.

**Relation-map (seed_generator's place):**
- **vs paradigm-sweeper:** both "generate seeds," OPPOSITE directions — sweeper makes INPUT launch-blocks from a *practice's paradigm-space*; seed_generator extracts OUTPUT germs from an *external source*. BORROW the sweeper's structural precedents (they're strong): *enumerate-never-select*, *the seed-block contract* (capped, divergence-pinning), *the anchoring guard*, *the persistent cross-run index*.
- **vs the traverse "breakthrough SEED or not?" fork:** seed_generator formalizes + reframes the fork's seed-half (keeps the gate; flips the criterion).
- **vs routelister:** a produced seed could BE a routelister-style typed record (grain × kind × engagement), or feed routelister directly. Strong structural overlap.
- **vs innovate:** seed_generator PRODUCES what innovate CONSUMES (a produced sense-3 seed later becomes a sense-1 innovate-trigger). Producer→consumer.
