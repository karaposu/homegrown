## Phase 2 — Generate (7 mechanisms × 3)

### 1. Lens Shifting (Framer)

- **1G — exhaust lens.** Route-production as the loop's EXHAUST: every finished inquiry emits its field as a by-product, no extra strokes. The teaching frame for the verdict (carries the user's "constant route list" insight in one word).
- **1F — inventory lens (focused, light).** Leaves→canopy as continuous restocking vs annual stocktake: the integrate-run absorbs fresh stock per delivery; the old design's navigational session was a periodic warehouse count. Seasoning only.
- **1C — the ceremony-creep skeptic (contrarian).** "A 7th step on a 6-discipline loop — identity bloat." Answer with structure: the step is POST-CONCLUDE — outside the cognitive pipeline (the inquiry is already COMPLETE; this is exhaust capture, not a discipline); it is droppable without breaking anything; it costs minutes; and it REMOVES work elsewhere (the navigational session sheds N per-inquiry enumerations for one integrate-run). **Net ceremony: negative.** Adopted into the verdict.

### 2. Combination (Generator)

- **2G — the route-map × the turn record (the round's biggest gain).** Under field-before-choice, a turn needs the field WRITTEN before the pick — and now every worker loop emits exactly that artifact. **The turn record's field-reference points AT the per-inquiry `routelister.md`: the route-map IS the canonical written field.** The Turn Architecture's validity condition gets its concrete file; the Expedition's spine reads it natively.
- **2F — the goal-phrase × the finding's structure.** The template quotes the finding's own Goal line (which CONCLUDE compiles from `_branch.md`): goal received from the artifact, mechanically, zero invention.
- **2C — the bootstrap × the shakedown.** The first-canopy bootstrap (one root-run over existing findings) does TRIPLE duty: births the corpus map (long missing), warms the Expedition spine (the navigation warm-up the shakedown needs anyway), and creates the canopy the integrate-runs extend. One session, three standing wants.

### 3. Inversion (Framer; depth-checked)

- **3G — start-side inversion.** "Routes at the loop's end" → invert: routes at the START (pre-work direction lists)? Absorbed: enumeration needs the finished territory (start-side openness is already articulate_simple's job — the considered-articulations); the asymmetry is justified, one clarifying line.
- **3F — the always-dropped inversion (focused).** "Droppable gracefully" → invert: what if it is ALWAYS dropped — nobody runs it, and the usage-gap recurs? This lands on the proposal's deepest value: **habit→mechanism** — the gap finding showed capabilities-unrun become gaps; putting the run IN THE RUNNER converts an unrun mode into an automatic one. The droppability is for failure-resilience, not for routine skipping. Adopted into the verdict's grounds.
- **3C — the no-canopy inversion (contrarian).** "One master index" → invert: leaves only; the spine reads per-inquiry maps directly. Adjudication: WITHIN one expedition's context, leaves + the spine's growing context suffice (the maps are in its intake); the canopy earns its place at **cross-expedition / cross-session / corpus scale** — its irreplaceable work is cross-inquiry identity merging (the same concept surfacing in five inquiries becomes ONE canopy identity with five manifestations — what complement-perception needs beyond a single context window). Adopted as honest scoping: the canopy is persistence infrastructure, not a per-turn requirement.

### 4. Constraint Manipulation (Framer; both directions)

- **4-ADD (generic).** **The runner-step text ≤ 10 lines** — it is an invocation, not a spec; bloat here would be its own ceremony-creep.
- **4-ADD (focused).** **Non-blocking:** if the route-run fails, log one line in `_state.md` History and finish — the inquiry remains COMPLETE; the map can be produced later by re-running. CONCLUDE's outcome never hostages on the exhaust step.
- **4-REMOVE (generic).** Drop the stance nudge (plain goal)? → loses the only guard on the one real bias surface (warm sessions under-listing reflexive routes about their own verdict) and saves nothing. Fails.
- **4-REMOVE (contrarian).** Drop the per-inquiry `_route.md` (route-map only; index only at canopy)? → violates the spec's own contract ("a run always produces TWO files, and routelisting writes both itself"); the per-inquiry index is also what a later depth-drill on that finding loads. Fails on spec-conformance.

### 5. Absence Recognition (Generator; both levels, bidirectional)

- **Patch-level:** (i) **the cold-resume case** — an inquiry resumed cross-session concludes in a COLD session; the step still runs (mechanics identical), only the warmth bonus is absent; one honest line. (ii) **Who runs the integrate-run TODAY** — no navigational session exists yet: the human/assistant on demand (formalizing the current manual practice); the Expedition's spine inherits it as its see-phase's read+compile. Named.
- **Redesign-level:** none — the composition holds.
- **Bidirectional (already-present):** CONCLUDE already prints Relationships + resume pointers — proto-routes in prose. The step SUPERSEDES the ad-hoc pointers with typed, indexed routes; the rhyme is noted (the pointers stay as human-facing summary; the route-map is the machine-grade field).

### 6. Domain Transfer (Generator; native source included)

- **6-native (software).** **CI artifacts:** every build emits its reports as exhaust (tests, coverage); dashboards AGGREGATE them; the build never decides what to build next. Maps exactly: loop emits the route-map; the canopy aggregates; choosing stays the Selector's. The one analogy.
- **6-different (forestry: leaf litter → soil).** Apt but REJECTED — analogy budget (CI is seated; "leaves/canopy" is already the architecture's own vocabulary, not an analogy).
- **6-different (library science: books ship with catalog cards; the union catalog integrates).** Structurally identical to CI — REJECTED as duplicate.

### 7. Extrapolation (Generator)

- **7G — the canopy as the future Selector's read.** At scale, the standing report's directions section IS the canopy — the Selector reads integrated identities, not raw findings. Consistent with the priors; one line.
- **7F — organ-ready composition.** If the supplement organ (the gap finding's pole 1) is ever built, its outputs — loaded challenges, invented alternatives — write into the SAME per-inquiry maps as new route kinds: the composition needs no rework to receive them. One line.
- **7C — the failure path.** If per-inquiry maps rot (quality drops, nobody reads them): the canopy's stale-flag machinery detects non-re-confirmation; the sunk cost is minutes per loop; rollback = delete one runner step. The cheapest reversible composition the corpus has adopted.
