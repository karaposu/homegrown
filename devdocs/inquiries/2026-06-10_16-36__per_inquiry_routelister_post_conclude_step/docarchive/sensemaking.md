## Phase 3 — Ambiguity Collapse

#### Ambiguity A1: Adopt, refine, or reject the proposal?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** reject as redundant — the finding already lists Next Actions and Open Questions; a route-map re-states them with extra ceremony, and the nav session could always just read findings.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** Next Actions are disposition-laden commitments (MUST/COULD = priority decisions, the author's seat); Open Questions are typed gaps — neither is a TYPED, GOAL-RELATIVE, INDEX-ACCUMULATING field. The index machinery is the difference: it is what lets N inquiries' directions INTEGRATE into one corpus map (identities re-confirmed, stale-flagged, enriched) instead of remaining N prose sections someone re-reads. The n=1 precedent shows the per-inquiry map adds typed structure prose lacks; and the gap finding's usage-gap rows establish that this exact mode-usage is what's missing.

**Confidence:** HIGH. **Resolution:** **ADOPT-REFINED** — the step is real value, with the refinements of A2–A5. **No longer allowed:** treating route-maps as redundant with Next Actions; treating the nav session's direct finding-reads as equivalent to index integration.

#### Ambiguity A2: Where exactly does the step sit, over what, with what goal?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** post-critique (pre-CONCLUDE), over the whole inquiry folder — the user said "just after critique," the context is hottest there, and more material means richer routes.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** pre-CONCLUDE the finding doesn't exist — the sweep would enumerate from discipline outputs about to be archived, producing routes anchored to artifacts no consumer reads later; CONCLUDE also reshapes content (refinements land in the finding). Post-CONCLUDE the session is still warm (minutes later, same session — the fresh-focus claim survives) and the territory is the distilled, self-contained artifact. **Confidence:** HIGH. **Resolution:** **a RUNNER step immediately after CONCLUDE: territory = `finding.md`; goal = received from the finding's own Question/Goal section ("advance from this finding toward [the inquiry's goal], including tests and refinements of this finding itself"); outputs = `routelister.md` + `_route.md` in the inquiry folder.** The step belongs to the runners' docs, not `conclude.md` (shared protocol stays runner-neutral). **No longer allowed:** pre-CONCLUDE placement; folder-wide territory; invented goals.

#### Ambiguity A3: What is the navigational session's run (the mode question)?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** a new "compile mode" is needed — neither root nor depth fits reading other maps.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** the spec's root mode + the index's INTEGRATE step already describe it: per-inquiry maps and findings are TERRITORY (material to sweep); the master corpus-scope `_route.md` is the run's own index (LOAD → INTEGRATE → PERSIST); the received goal biases the sweep ("direction mode" = the same run with a sharper goal — a parameter, not a mode). #### Ambiguity A4: Downstream-only? (the user's open question)

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** yes, strictly — routes about the finding itself are navel-gazing; the map should contain only onward work.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** "routes about the finding itself" conflates two classes. **Confidence:** HIGH. **Resolution:** **"downstream" = forward-looking engagements, BOTH onward and reflexive; excluded = backward re-description and re-litigation of settled priors.
