### 1. The consolidated definition

**IS.** Routelister is a **standalone, domain-agnostic** thinking discipline that **identifies the concept-identities in a territory that can be engaged as directions toward a goal, and lists each as a typed, prescriptive route — enumerating all of them (leaning to inclusion), without selecting which to take.**
- **Unit:** the **concept-identity** — the invariant that generalizes over a concept's manifestations/artifacts (a README's description of a feature and the implementation of it are two manifestations of one identity). A *route* = an identity (or, on a concept-space run, a manifestation) framed as a typed prescriptive direction (Direction + Movement-Type + reachability + guidance).
- **What qualifies as a concept** (the concept-target gloss): a thing whose engagement either **advances the goal** or **sharpens the understanding the goal rests on** (including a fuzzy goal itself); **abstractness is no bar** to inclusion.
- **Two traversal axes (one operation, two spaces):** **project-space (breadth)** enumerates the distinct concept-identities across the territory — the default discovery run, which stays compact because it lists identities, not their manifestations; **concept-space (depth)** takes one identity as input and enumerates its manifestations as routes, flagging **manifestation-divergences** (README-vs-implementation, deprecated-vs-active) as high-value **epistemic routes** ("reconcile / choose"). These two axes are routeman's existing staged parent/child (generic/directional) mechanism, grounded by which space each stage traverses.
- **Character:** routelister **traverses a semantic topology — the breadth of identities × the depth of each identity's manifestations — to emit routes.** It perceives that structure *by enumerating it*; it never constructs or delivers it as a model.

**IS-NOT.**
- Not **loop-bound / position-defined** — does not require a completed cognitive cycle and is not defined by sitting between cycles (the routeman mistake the diagnosis found; its identity is intrinsic, so it runs on any territory).
- Not a **selector** (it enumerates; it never picks the move to take).
- Not an **executor** (it lists "refine X" / "develop Y" as routes; performing them is downstream disciplines' work).
- Not a **descriptive model-builder** (`/comprehend`) or **relevance-tagger** (`/surfacing`) — it emits prescriptive routes, not a model or a relevance-ranked inventory.
- Not **generating-from-nothing** (routes are drawn from the territory's concepts).
- Not the **goal-setter** (the goal is exogenous, even if fuzzy).
- *(added via the ontology)* Not a **per-manifestation enumerator at the project level** — it generalizes to the concept-identity there, avoiding the manifestation-overload; manifestations are enumerated only on a concept-scoped drill-down.
- *(added)* Not an **inter-concept relational/dependency-graph builder** — routeman's NOT-list excludes cross-concept dependency graphs; routelister's "topology" is within-concept containment only.
- *(added)* Not a **topology constructor/deliverer** — it traverses a topology to emit routes; it neither builds nor outputs the topology (this is the reconciliation of the topology framing with not-model-builder).

**EXPECTED (as a discipline).** The full discipline anatomy per the project's canon — a **spec** (Definition/Philosophy including the NOT-list / Structural Components / Process Model / Failure Modes / Coverage Strategy) and an **output** (the Route-Map Transform / Progression / Telemetry / Frontier) — PLUS, as discipline-unique structural components, **the ontology** (concept-identity ↔ manifestations) and **the two traversal axes** (project-space / concept-space); PLUS, as a **required-but-process-deferred component, the identity-individuation mechanism** (how routelister decides two artifacts are manifestations of the same identity vs different concepts); PLUS the four universal properties (telemetry, frontier-awareness, failure-modes, self-application) and standalone/domain-agnostic status. It fills the forward-Boundary slot **by role** (a runner-owned composition fact), not as part of its identity.

**Carried from 10-25, unchanged:** the rename verdict — creating routelister as a fresh discipline is structural *conditionally* (re-derive its identity from the corrected understanding + carry routeman's re-tested machinery + supersede/archive routeman), and is **not strictly superior** to rewriting routeman in place (both reach the same corrected discipline); and the framing that routelister IS routeman's same route-enumeration operation corrected (now also: + its staged machinery grounded by space).

### 3. The seam, reconciled

The one place a careless consolidation could have produced an internal contradiction is the seam between 11-43's "semantic topology extraction" framing and 10-25's "routelister is NOT a model-builder." The reconciliation is precise and survives pushing: routelister **perceives the topology by enumerating it** (the same way `/surfacing` perceives a territory by drawing items — that is enumeration, not model-building), and **emits routes** (not a delivered model, which is what would make it `/comprehend`). And the "topology" is the *within-concept* identity→manifestation containment plus the breadth of identities — not an *inter-concept* relationship graph (which routeman's NOT-list excludes). With those two distinctions, the topology framing and the not-model-builder exclusion coexist coherently in the same definition.
