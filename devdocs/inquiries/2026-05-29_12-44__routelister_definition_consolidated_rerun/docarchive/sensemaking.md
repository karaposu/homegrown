### SV6 — Stabilized Model — THE CONSOLIDATED ROUTELISTER DEFINITION

**Verdict on the re-run:** the newer (ontology + space-traversal) understanding **REFINES** the 10-25 definition — it deepens every face and overturns nothing. The definition is therefore stable enough to base the structural spec on. Here is the single consolidated definition:

**IS.** Routelister is a **standalone, domain-agnostic** thinking discipline that **identifies the concept-identities in a territory that can be engaged as directions toward a goal, and lists each as a typed, prescriptive route — enumerating all (leaning to inclusion), without selecting which to take.**
- **Unit:** the **concept-identity** — the invariant generalized over a concept's manifestations/artifacts. A *route* = an identity (or, on a concept-space run, a manifestation) framed as a typed prescriptive direction (Direction + Movement-Type + reachability + guidance).
- **Concept-target** (what qualifies): the goal-relative two-axis gloss — a thing whose engagement either *advances the goal* or *sharpens the understanding the goal rests on* (including a fuzzy goal itself); abstractness is no bar to inclusion.
- **Two traversal axes (one operation, two spaces):** **project-space (breadth)** — enumerate the distinct concept-identities across the territory (the default discovery run); **concept-space (depth)** — given one identity as input, enumerate its manifestations as routes, flagging **manifestation-divergences** (e.g., README-vs-implementation, deprecated-vs-active) as high-value **epistemic routes**. These two axes are routeman's staged parent/child (generic/directional) mechanism, grounded by which space each traverses.
- **Character:** routelister **traverses a semantic topology (identity-breadth × manifestation-depth) to emit routes** — it perceives that structure *by enumerating it*, and never constructs or delivers it as a model.

**IS-NOT.**
- NOT **loop-bound / position-defined** — does not require a completed cognitive cycle; not defined by sitting between cycles (the routeman mistake; its identity is intrinsic).
- NOT a **selector** (enumerates; never picks the move to take).
- NOT an **executor** (lists "refine X" / "develop Y" as routes; does not perform them — downstream disciplines do).
- NOT a **descriptive model-builder** (`/comprehend`) or **relevance-tagger** (`/surfacing`) — it emits prescriptive routes, not a model or a relevance-ranked inventory.
- NOT **generating-from-nothing** (routes are drawn from the territory's concepts).
- NOT the **goal-setter** (the goal is exogenous, even if fuzzy).
- *(added via the ontology)* NOT a **per-manifestation enumerator at the project level** (it generalizes to the concept-identity — avoiding the manifestation-overload).
- *(added)* NOT an **inter-concept relational/dependency-graph builder** (routeman §1.3 NOT-list — the "topology" is within-concept containment only).
- *(added)* NOT a **topology constructor/deliverer** (it traverses a topology to emit routes; it neither builds nor outputs the topology — the reconciliation of the topology framing with not-model-builder).

**EXPECTED (as a discipline).** The full discipline anatomy per canon — a **spec** (Definition/Philosophy incl. the NOT-list / Structural Components / Process Model / Failure Modes / Coverage Strategy) and an **output** (the Route Map Transform / Progression / Telemetry / Frontier) — PLUS, as discipline-unique structural components: **the ontology** (concept-identity ↔ manifestations) and **the two traversal axes** (project-space / concept-space); PLUS, as a **required-but-process-deferred component, the identity-individuation mechanism** (how routelister decides two artifacts share an identity vs are different concepts).
