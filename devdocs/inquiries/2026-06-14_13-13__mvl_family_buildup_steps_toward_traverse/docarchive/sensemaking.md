## Phase 3 — Ambiguity Collapse

### Ambiguity 1 — What does "building steps" mean?
Chronological development history, OR logical/compositional layering (nested supersets)?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** They were *parallel experiments* — four independently-tried loop shapes — and "building steps" is just a post-hoc narrative imposed on what was really exploration.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** The affixes are *literally additive* on a common base (`MVL` + `w` + `a` + `r`), and the pipelines are *nested supersets* (C1) — `aMVLw` contains `MVLw`'s pipeline entirely, which contains `MVL`'s entirely. Parallel experiments would produce *overlapping-but-divergent* pipelines (like MVL+, which swaps E for A+Su — and which is correctly NOT on the spine). The spine four are strictly nested; nesting is the signature of additive construction, not parallel search. (MVL+ being the one genuine side-branch *proves* the test discriminates.)

**Confidence:** HIGH — the nesting is a structural fact, not a narrative.

**Resolution:** "Building steps" means **both, and they coincide**: the chronological order IS the compositional order. Each step = previous loop + one cognitive operation.

**Now fixed:** the spine is an additive chain `MVL→MVLw→aMVLw→aMVLwr(=traverse)`. **No longer allowed:** "co-equal sibling variants" framing. **Depends on this:** the entire keeper-argument (you keep the top of an additive chain, not "the best of several siblings"). **Model change:** M1 locked.

### Ambiguity 2 — What does "the only thing that should maintain" mean?
(a) keep installed/live; (b) keep evolving/improving; (c) keep referenced/exist.

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** "maintain" means "the only one that should *exist*" → delete the ancestors entirely.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** The user *already chose* non-active/ over deletion in this same work-stream (C3), explicitly to preserve reference history; and immutable findings legitimately cite the old names. Deletion would break the historical record the project's own principle (P3) protects. So (c)-as-deletion is structurally excluded by an already-made, evidence-backed decision.

**Confidence:** HIGH.

**Resolution:** "maintain" = **(a) live-on-the-menu + (b) evolve/keep-correct**. Only traverse stays installed and is the only spec we keep improving. (c) referencing the old names stays valid (historical), and they remain *readable* in non-active/ — that is not "maintenance."

**Now fixed:** maintain = install+evolve, exactly one runner. **No longer allowed:** treating "retire" as "delete"; spending spec-improvement effort on the ancestors. **Depends:** the canon-cleanup action (the stale docs must be fixed *because* only traverse is maintained). **Model change:** M2 sharpened.

### Ambiguity 3 (load-bearing concept test) — "completeness-as-keeper-criterion"
Is "keep the complete pipeline" the project's *actual* principle, or an external default the loop adopted?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** The **speed counter** — MVL (SIC) was explicitly the *fast/lightweight* lane (S1); "always the full pipeline" makes every simple question pay a 7-discipline tax (When_Is_Good_Enough measured ~28 min / 6-discipline run and named a "throughput inversion" where ceremony starves throughput). So completeness is *not* an unqualified good; a lightweight loop has real structural merit, which argues for keeping MVL.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds) — partially, and honestly:** Two structural facts blunt it but do not erase it. (1) The decision under question is which spec to **maintain**, not whether speed ever matters — and maintaining four near-duplicate specs has its *own* measured cost (the canon-staleness drift R2 we are literally cleaning up). A speed need is satisfiable by **one documented quick-mode flag on traverse** (run S→I→C only), which is *less* maintenance surface than a separate `MVL` spec. (2) Canon frames MVL/SIC as the *starting rotation* of a whirl that grows toward completeness (minimum_viable_loop.md), i.e. a bootstrap, not a permanent product to preserve. So "keep the complete one as the maintained loop" holds; the speed benefit re-enters as a *future feature of traverse*, not as a reason to maintain an ancestor.

**Confidence:** HIGH on "maintain only traverse"; the speed niche is a **genuine OPEN item** (flag, don't bury): if fast passes are needed, they belong as a traverse quick-mode, not as a revived MVL.

**Resolution:** completeness IS the keeper-criterion *for the maintained loop*, grounded in canon's own "each operation is needed" arguments (K3/K4) and the consumer-contract criterion (S4) — with speed re-homed as a possible traverse mode.

**Now fixed:** the complete pipeline is the maintained loop. **No longer allowed:** "keep MVL for speed" as a maintenance decision. **Depends:** any future quick-mode is a traverse variant. **Model change:** M2 grounded against its strongest adversary; the speed item is logged OPEN, not silently dropped (avoids Clean Resolution Trap).
