## Phase 1 — Cognitive Anchor Extraction

**Constraints**
- C1 — No must-have weakened; the five survive unchanged-or-sharpened (the scorecard's disqualifying force is the thing being protected).
- C2 — No must-list bloat: a new component is a must-have ONLY if its absence makes an analogy *actively misleading*; otherwise high-value (the finding's own kill).
- C3 — Cohere with committed canon (algorithm-family non-stationarity; Thesis path-bias; meaningful-traversal signals).
- C4 — Mine ALL ten (the low-scorers explicitly — they carry the rarest parts).

**Key Insights**
- KI1 — **The extractions sort into THREE operations, not one.** "Increase accuracy and variety" decomposes: **(a) SHARPEN** — refine an existing must-have's wording without adding a line (dialogic fit-search; bidirectional back-pressure; position-as-meaning); **(b) ADD** — a genuinely new component the finding lacks (attractor dynamics; the prune-record); **(c) RE-WEIGHT/CORRECT** — change a component's status or fix an over-claim (promote path-dependence; correct "clear traverser" to a spectrum). Accuracy = mostly (a)+(c); variety = mostly (b). Keeping these operations distinct is what prevents bloat (every extraction must declare which operation it is).
- KI2 — **The deepest correlation (landscape-reshaping in 5 paradigms) resolves a latent imprecision in must-have #2.** The finding's #2 is "non-stationary + path-dependent" with path-dependence as a *sub-clause*. But the five-paradigm recurrence (watershed erodes, succession builds soil, city locks in, language enables new words, accretion bends the field) shows path-dependence isn't a refinement OF non-stationarity — it's a STRONGER claim: the landscape doesn't just change (non-stationary), it changes *because of where you walked* (path-dependent). These are two distinct disqualifiers: a randomly-shifting landscape (non-stationary, not path-dependent — e.g., weather you don't affect) vs a landscape you carve. **Resolution: #2 splits into two probes under one must-have** — "does the territory change?" (non-stationarity) AND "does YOUR PATH change it?" (path-dependence) — the second is the rarer, deeper bar. Not a promotion to a sixth must-have (it's already #2's content) but a sharpening that makes #2 carry two distinct yes/no tests. (Accuracy gain; matches the Thesis's path-bias exactly.)
- KI3 — **The two new-component candidates pass the disqualifying-force test at HIGH-VALUE, not must-have.** *Attractor dynamics* (rich-get-richer + zone-clearing): an analogy lacking it is impoverished (misses why some concepts dominate) but not actively MISLEADING — high-value. *The prune-record* (slime mold's prune-half): same — its absence impoverishes (no dead-end memory) but doesn't mislead about what traversal IS. Both join the high-value tier. (Variety gain, anti-bloat respected.)
- KI4 — **The prune-record is more than a slime-mold gift — it ties to the gap finding's confirmation-shape risk.** The finding tracks kept/parked routes (`parked-as: gated-on(...)`) but has NO record of actively-abandoned ones. A prune-record (what was tried and rejected, with why) prevents re-exploring known dead-ends AND is anti-confirmation (it forces the loop to remember disconfirmations, not just keep what fit). This is the strongest single variety-addition because it connects three findings (this enrichment + the gap finding's confirmation-shape + the meaningful-traversal stuck-in-a-rut signal).
- KI5 — **The "clear traverser" correction is an accuracy FIX, not an enrichment — the finding currently over-claims.** The finding lists "a clear traverser mapping to the loop/Selector" as a high-value requirement, stated universally. But correlation (c) shows agency is paradigm-dependent: jazz/city have an agent; succession/watershed/R-D/accretion have NONE (pure emergence) — and those agent-less analogies still scored well (succession 5/5). So requiring an agent-traverser would wrongly penalize good emergent analogies. **Resolution: replace "clear traverser" with "a locatable locus of change on the agent↔field spectrum"** — the analogy must make clear WHAT changes (agent, or field, or emergent collective), but need not make it an agent. (Accuracy correction.)
- KI6 — **The drift-vs-selection extraction sharpens must-have #1 and connects to the algorithm-family finding.** The finding's "fit-search" forbids aimless-growth AND fixed-peak-optimization — but languages show traversal has TWO engines: undirected *drift* (exploration with no immediate fit-pressure) and *selection* (fit-driven). The algorithm-family finding already named explore/exploit. **Resolution: #1's probe gains a clause** — "fit-search, with room for both exploratory drift and fit-driven selection" — so an analogy showing PURELY greedy fit (no drift) is impoverished, and one showing pure drift (no fit) fails #1's aimless-growth sin. (Accuracy gain; ties to explore/exploit.)
- KI7 — **The temporal-source correlation (d) becomes a finding NOTE, not a requirement.** "Time-based analogies score higher (time natively supplies non-stationarity + local-horizon)" is an empirical pattern useful for the analogy HUNT, but it's not a requirement of the analogy itself (a non-temporal analogy that somehow passes all must-haves would be fine). **Resolution: a one-line note in the finding's vocabulary/seed area** — "empirically, time-based sources pass more must-haves for free; prefer them in the hunt" — guidance, not a tier entry.
- KI8 — **The active-inhibition extraction sharpens the merge/seam high-value entry; the bidirectional back-pressure sharpens must-have #3.** R-D's lateral inhibition: individuation may be ACTIVE (a strong concept suppresses near-duplicates), not just a passive "same or different?" judgment — the merge/seam entry gains "(maintained actively where needed — a dominant concept inhibits near-rivals)". Succession's enable-up/constrain-down: must-have #3's back-pressure is currently mono-directional (layers *constrain* each other); succession shows it's bidirectional (an earlier layer *enables* the later one that then constrains it) — #3's probe gains "co-revise (enable upward, constrain downward)". (Two accuracy gains.)

**Structural Points:** SP1 the three operations (sharpen/add/re-weight) · SP2 #2's two-probe split (non-stationary AND path-dependent as distinct tests) · SP3 attractor-dynamics + prune-record as HIGH-VALUE adds · SP4 the prune-record's three-finding tie · SP5 traverser → agent↔field spectrum (a fix) · SP6 #1 gains drift/selection · SP7 #3 gains bidirectional back-pressure; merge/seam gains active inhibition · SP8 temporal-source as a note.

**Foundational Principles:** FP1 declare each extraction's operation (sharpen/add/re-weight) — the anti-bloat gate · FP2 must-have only if absence actively misleads (C2) · FP3 mine the low-scorers — they carry the rarest parts (C4) · FP4 cohere with canon, and where an extraction MATCHES canon (path-dependence↔Thesis; drift/selection↔explore-exploit), that's confirmation not addition.

**Meaning-Nodes:** sharpen/add/re-weight · path-dependence as #2's deeper second probe · attractor dynamics · the prune-record · the agent↔field traverser spectrum · drift+selection · active inhibition · bidirectional back-pressure · the temporal-source note.

## Phase 3 — Ambiguity Collapse

#### Ambiguity A1: How do the extractions sort — what does "accuracy vs variety" mean operationally?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** they don't need sorting — just append every useful insight to the finding as new entries; more content = more accurate and more various.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** the finding's whole value is the disqualifying-force of its must-haves; appending indiscriminately bloats the must-list (the finding's own documented kill) and dilutes the grading instrument. Accuracy and variety are DIFFERENT operations on the finding: accuracy = sharper/corrected EXISTING requirements (fold-in, no new line); variety = genuinely new HIGH-VALUE perspectives (new line, gated by disqualifying-force). Conflating them is exactly how the scorecard would rot.

**Confidence:** HIGH. **Resolution:** three declared operations — **SHARPEN** (refine an existing must-have, no new line) / **ADD** (new high-value component, gated by the disqualifying-force test) / **RE-WEIGHT/CORRECT** (change status or fix an over-claim). Every extraction tagged. **No longer allowed:** indiscriminate appending; promoting refinements to new must-haves.

#### Ambiguity A2: Does path-dependence get promoted (the 5-paradigm correlation)?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** it recurs in five paradigms — make it a sixth must-have, the spine.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** path-dependence is ALREADY inside must-have #2; a sixth must-have would duplicate. But the correlation reveals a real imprecision: #2 currently reads "non-stationary + path-dependent" as if one claim, when they're TWO distinct disqualifiers (a landscape can shift without your causing it — weather — passing non-stationarity but failing path-dependence). The fix isn't promotion, it's SPLITTING #2 into two probes under one must-have, with path-dependence flagged as the rarer/deeper bar.

**Confidence:** HIGH. **Resolution:** **#2 carries two distinct yes/no probes** — "does the territory change as you traverse?" (non-stationarity) AND "does your PATH change what's reachable?" (path-dependence, the deeper, 5-paradigm-confirmed bar). One must-have, two tests. **No longer allowed:** a sixth must-have; treating the two as one undifferentiated claim.

#### Ambiguity A3: Are attractor-dynamics and the prune-record must-haves or high-value?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** they're so important (the prune-record ties to confirmation-shape; attractors explain concept-dominance) they should be must-haves.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** the disqualifying-force test: an analogy lacking attractor-dynamics (jazz doesn't show rich-get-richer) is impoverished but not MISLEADING about what traversal is — and jazz scored 5/5. An analogy lacking a prune-record (most do) is impoverished but doesn't teach something false. Must-have membership requires absence-actively-misleads; neither qualifies. They're high-value (their PRESENCE enriches).

**Confidence:** HIGH. **Resolution:** both join the **high-value tier**; the prune-record carries a note tying it to the gap finding's confirmation-shape risk and the meaningful-traversal stuck-in-a-rut signal (it's the richest single add). **No longer allowed:** promoting them to must-have; dropping the prune-record's cross-finding tie.

#### Ambiguity A4: Is correcting "clear traverser" a weakening (violating C1)?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** C1 says no requirement weakened; loosening "clear traverser" to "agent OR field" weakens it.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** "clear traverser" was an OVER-CLAIM, not a correctly-calibrated requirement — it would wrongly penalize agent-less analogies that score well (succession 5/5 has no agent). Fixing a false universal isn't weakening a true requirement; it's correcting an error. The replacement still REQUIRES something (a locatable locus of change on the agent↔field spectrum — the analogy must make clear WHAT changes), so it isn't loosened to nothing.

**Confidence:** HIGH. **Resolution:** **re-weight/correct** — "clear traverser" → "a locatable locus of change (agent ↔ collective ↔ field)"; the requirement is that the analogy NAME what changes, not that it be an agent. **No longer allowed:** the universal agent-traverser claim.

#### Ambiguity A5: What about the "extract the mechanism not the analogy" principle — does it change anything?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** it's obvious; every extraction already does this implicitly.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** it's not free — it's WHY the low-scorers contribute (R-D's chemistry is irrelevant; its lateral-inhibition MECHANISM transfers). Making it explicit is itself a finding-level meta-note: *a low-scoring analogy can donate a high-value mechanism; grade the analogy as a whole, but mine its mechanisms separately.* This guards the finding's future use (don't discard a 2.5/5 analogy's component because the analogy failed).

**Confidence:** HIGH. **Resolution:** a finding meta-note — **whole-analogy score and component-donation are separate; mine mechanisms from low-scorers.** (This IS the "from ALL of them" lesson, generalized.) **No longer allowed:** discarding a low-scorer's components with the analogy.
