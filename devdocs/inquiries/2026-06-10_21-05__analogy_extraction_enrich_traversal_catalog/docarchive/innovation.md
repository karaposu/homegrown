## Phase 2 — Generate (7 mechanisms × 3, compact)

### 1. Lens Shifting (Framer)

- **1G — the "patch-notes" lens.** Frame the deliverable as a versioned changelog against the finding (v1 → v1.1): each edit is a line with its operation, source, and rationale — makes the proposals scannable and auditable.
- **1F — the "two-probe instrument" lens (focused on #2).** A must-have with two yes/no probes is a richer test instrument — a candidate can pass non-stationarity yet fail path-dependence (weather), and the scorecard should SHOW that partial. Adopted: #2's two probes are scored separately (non-stationary: P/F · path-dependent: P/F), so #2 yields a 2-bit grade, not 1.
- **1C — the bloat adversary (contrarian).** "Five sharpenings + two adds + a correction + two notes + a section — that's the finding doubling in size; isn't THIS the bloat the finding warned against?" Answer with the operation discipline: the 5 sharpenings add ZERO lines (they fold into existing must-have text); only 2 new high-value lines + 2 short notes + 1 evidence section are genuinely added; the must-have COUNT stays 5. Bloat = more must-haves; this adds none. **Adopted: the finding's must-have count is explicitly unchanged (5); the growth is in precision (fold-ins) and the high-value tier (2 lines), which is the tier designed to hold richness.**

### 2. Combination (Generator)

- **2G — attractor dynamics × the prune-record = "concept economy."** Both are about WHERE traversal-attention/mass concentrates: attractors pull it in (rich-get-richer); pruning pushes it out (dead-ends starve). Together they're a single high-value entry — **concept economy: attention accretes to fit-rich concepts and starves from dead-ends** — one entry, two mechanisms, cleaner than two separate lines. Adopted (resolves the decomposition's open "two entries or one").
- **2F — path-dependence × the algorithm-family's "non-stationary."** The #2 split makes EXPLICIT what the algorithm-family finding stated compactly: non-stationary (landscape moves) is necessary; path-dependent (you move it) is the stronger sub-case the project actually lives in (the path-bias). The split is a faithful unpacking of committed canon, not a new claim — stated as such in the edit's rationale.
- **2C — the prune-record × the gap finding's confirmation-shape (the cross-finding bridge).** The prune-record isn't just slime-mold-tidy; it's the loop's anti-confirmation memory: recording what was tried-and-failed forces disconfirmations to persist, countering the confirmation-shape risk the gap finding named. Adopted as the prune-record's headline rationale (its richest justification).

### 3. Inversion (Framer; depth-checked)

- **3G — the "what would make this WORSE" inversion.** Inverting "enrich for accuracy" → what edit would DAMAGE accuracy? Promoting a high-value to must-have without the disqualifying-force test; weakening a must-have to fit an analogy. Naming these confirms the operation-gate is the safeguard. Absorbed.
- **3F — the traverser-fix inversion (focused, adopted).** "Require a clear traverser" → invert: the BEST analogies (succession, watershed) have NO traverser-agent, and that's a strength (emergence, no central planner — which matches the project's own no-orchestrator architecture). So the agent-traverser requirement was not just an over-claim — it was BACKWARDS for the high-scorers. **Adopted: the corrected entry positively VALUES the field/emergent end of the spectrum** (an agent-less analogy isn't deficient; it may better show emergence) — stronger than a neutral "spectrum" framing.
- **3C — the correlations-as-findings inversion (contrarian).** "The correlations justify the edits" → invert: the correlations may be MORE valuable than the edits — they're standalone insights about traversal (the landscape-reshaping universality; the agency spectrum) that belong in `thinking_space_dynamics.md` regardless of any analogy. Adopted: the correlations section is flagged as canon-candidate material, not just edit-justification.

### 4. Constraint Manipulation (Framer; both directions)

- **4-ADD (generic).** Each edit ships in a fixed shape: `[operation] · [target] · [new text] · [source analogy] · [disqualifying-force verdict]` — a uniform row, g**reppable and auditable.
- **4-ADD (focused).** The proposed-edits deliver as a TABLE (operation × target × text × source) so the user can accept/reject per-row.
- **4-REMOVE (generic).** Drop the correlations section (just ship the edits)? → loses the EVIDENCE that licenses each edit (why split #2? because 5 paradigms show path-carving) — the edits become assertions. Fails.
- **4-REMOVE (contrarian).** Drop the two notes (temporal-source; mine-mechanisms) as "not edits to the scorecard proper"? → they're the guidance that makes the scorecard usable in the hunt and that vindicates "from ALL ten"; cheap, high-leverage. Keep.

### 5. Absence Recognition (Generator; both levels, bidirectional)

- **Patch-level:** (i) **the enriched composite** — the original finding's composite (succession+jazz+watershed+R-D) should be RE-STATED with the enrichments (succession now carries bidirectional back-pressure; watershed now carries the ratchet; R-D now carries active inhibition; + concept-economy from accretion+slime-mold) — one updated sentence. (ii) **a "what each low-scorer donated" line** — the explicit ledger (R-D→inhibition; accretion→attractor; annealing→restart/temperature; gravitational→the local-horizon counter-example) so the meta-note has its receipts.
- **Redesign-level:** none — the edit-set form holds.
- **Bidirectional (already-present):** the finding ALREADY has a "necessary-not-sufficient; add must-haves as new misleadings surface" clause — the enrichment is the FIRST exercise of that clause (no new must-have was needed, but the high-value tier grew and the must-haves sharpened) — noted as proof the finding's growth mechanism works.

### 6. Domain Transfer (Generator; native source included)

- **6-native (the project's own).** The "calibrated-claims" tag grammar (the corpus tags claims [canon]/[repo-fact]/[test:]) — the edit-set's operation-tags (SHARPEN/ADD/CORRECT) are the same discipline applied to finding-edits. One framing line.
- **6-different (software versioning).** Semantic versioning — sharpenings = patch (precision, no API change); adds = minor (new high-value, backward-compatible); a must-have change would be major. The enrichment is a MINOR version bump (1.0→1.1): new high-value, sharpened must-haves, no must-have count change. Apt and clarifying — one line.
- **6-different (editing/redlining).** REJECTED — the patch-notes + semver framings already cover the "tracked changes" idea.

### 7. Extrapolation (Generator)

- **7G — the enrichment loop generalizes.** This (generate analogies → grade → mine ALL → fold back) is a reusable pattern: any catalog/scorecard can be enriched by generating instances, grading them, and mining even the failures for components. One line — the method, not just this result.
- **7F — the correlations feed canon.** (3C's stronger form) the four correlations are concrete thinking-space dynamics → a COULD toward `thinking_space_dynamics.md`. Adopted.
- **7C — the over-enrichment failure path.** If every future analogy-mining appends to the finding, it eventually bloats despite the discipline; so the enrichment ships with a watch: "high-value tier growth is fine; if it exceeds ~8 entries, re-tier (some become vocabulary)." One honest line.
