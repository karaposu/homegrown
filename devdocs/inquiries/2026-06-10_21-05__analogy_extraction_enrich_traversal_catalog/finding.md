## Finding

*Context: the prior finding is a scorecard for grading analogies of thinking-space traversal — five disqualifying must-haves, a high-value tier, a vocabulary pool. Ten analogies were then generated and graded. This inquiry mines all ten (and the comparisons among them) to make the scorecard more accurate and more various, while protecting the disqualifying-force that is its whole value.*

### 1. The three operations (the anti-bloat gate)

The user's "accuracy and variety" decomposes into three operations, and every extraction must declare which it is:
- **SHARPEN** — refine an existing must-have's wording. Adds no new line; cannot weaken the must-have, only increase its precision. (Accuracy.)
- **ADD** — introduce a genuinely new component. Gated by the disqualifying-force test: must-have ONLY if its *absence actively misleads*; otherwise high-value. (Variety.)
- **CORRECT** — fix an over-claim or mis-status. (Accuracy.)

This is the discipline that lets the finding grow more accurate AND more various without the bloat its own prior self warned against: bloat is must-have inflation, and no operation here adds a must-have.

### 2. The five sharpenings (each sourced; folded in, no new must-have)

Detailed in the Summary. The two that carry the most weight:

- **The #2 split** (licensed by correlation b — landscape-reshaping in 5 paradigms): the prior #2 read "non-stationary + path-dependent" as one claim. They are two distinct disqualifiers — a landscape can shift without your causing it (weather: non-stationary, NOT path-dependent). The split makes #2 a **two-part probe** (the other must-haves stay single-probe): the second probe (path-dependence — your path reshapes what's reachable) is the rarer, deeper bar, and it is a faithful unpacking of the Traversal Thesis's path-bias and the algorithm-family's non-stationary verdict, not a new claim.
- **#1's bounded drift** (licensed by *a living language*): traversal has two engines — exploratory drift and fit-driven selection (explore/exploit). But drift is bounded — it is exploration *under the goal's pull*, never the aimless growth #1 forbids. (Critique caught that an unbounded "room for drift" would license the very sin #1 exists to forbid; the bound is load-bearing.)

### 3. The one new high-value component: concept economy

Two analogies the prior scored low donate the two halves of a single new component about **where traversal-attention concentrates**:
- **Accrete to fit-rich** (gravitational accretion): concepts that gain "mass" (confirmed fit, accumulated findings) attract more attention and **clear their neighborhood** (a dominant concept suppresses near-duplicates — the astronomical "clearing the orbit," and a deeper individuation mechanism than the passive same-or-different judgment).
- **Starve dead-ends — the prune-record** (slime mold): traversal keeps a record of *actively-abandoned* routes, not just kept/parked ones. This is the richest single addition because it bridges three findings: it prevents re-exploring known dead-ends (efficiency), it is **anti-confirmation memory** (persisting disconfirmations counters the gap finding's *confirmation-shape risk*), and it operationalizes the meaningful-traversal *stuck-in-a-rut* signal.

The two are **one entry, two named sub-mechanisms** — a shared function (attention-allocation across concepts), not a claim that accretion and pruning are the same operation. Gated to high-value: jazz scored 5/5 without concept economy, so its absence impoverishes rather than misleads.

### 4. The one correction: the traverser

The prior listed "a clear traverser mapping to the loop/Selector" as a high-value requirement, stated universally. Correlation (c) shows agency is paradigm-dependent: the agent-less analogies (succession, watershed, reaction-diffusion, accretion) scored *well* (succession 5/5). Requiring an agent-traverser would wrongly penalize them. Corrected to **"a locatable locus of change (agent ↔ collective ↔ field)"** — the analogy must make clear WHAT changes, but the whole spectrum is legitimate; the field/emergent end is a strength (it shows emergence, matching the project's no-central-planner architecture), and the agent end is equally fine. Neither end is penalized — the original's agent-bias is removed without installing a field-bias.

### 5. The two notes + the correlations section

- **Temporal-source note** (correlation d): empirically, time-based sources (jazz, conversation, an embryo) pass more must-haves for free — time natively supplies non-stationarity and a local horizon. Guidance for the analogy hunt, not a tier entry.
- **Mine-mechanisms meta-note** (the generalized "from ALL of them" lesson): whole-analogy score and component-donation are separate — grade the analogy whole, but mine its mechanisms separately. Receipts: the donation ledger — reaction-diffusion → active inhibition; accretion → attractor dynamics; annealing → temperature/restart; gravitational → the local-horizon counter-example.
- **The correlations section** (the four patterns + the donation ledger) becomes the finding's new "what comparing the analogies taught" — and is flagged as canon-candidate material for `docs/canon/thinking_space_dynamics.md` (the patterns are concrete traversal dynamics independent of any single analogy).

### 6. The updated composite

The prior's composite (succession + jazz + watershed + reaction-diffusion's inhibition) stands, now carrying the enrichments: *meaning-first traversal is **ecological succession** (stages that build the ground the next grows in — enabling upward, constraining downward), walked by a **jazz soloist** (fitting each move to the evolving whole, with drift under the goal's pull and a local horizon), on terrain that **erodes as you cross it** (your path carves the channel it then follows — the ratchet), where neighboring growths **actively inhibit** each other to hold a seam (individuation), and where attention **accretes to fit-rich concepts and starves from dead-ends** (concept economy, with a prune-record as anti-confirmation memory).*

## Reasoning

**Why three operations, not one.** "Enrich for accuracy and variety" is two distinct moves on the finding, and conflating them is exactly how a scorecard rots (indiscriminate appending → must-have inflation → lost discrimination). Tagging every extraction sharpen/add/correct, and gating every add by the disqualifying-force test, is the structural guarantee that the finding grew sharper without growing bloated.

**Why the failing analogies were worth mining.** The standout result: the lowest-scoring analogies (reaction-diffusion, accretion, annealing) donated the parts the high-scorers lacked (active inhibition, attractor dynamics, restart). The lesson generalizes — whole-analogy score and component-donation are separate axes — and it's the structural vindication of the user's "from ALL of them."

**Significant kills.** *Indiscriminate appending* — killed: bloats the must-list, the finding's own documented failure. *Path-dependence as a sixth must-have* — killed: it's already inside #2; the fix is a two-probe split, not a new bar. *Two separate add-entries (attractor; prune-record)* — killed: merged into concept economy (one shared function, two sub-mechanisms). *A neutral "spectrum" traverser fix* — superseded: the corrected entry values the whole spectrum (no end penalized), stronger than neutrality. *Unbounded "room for drift"* — killed by critique: it would license #1's forbidden aimless-growth sin; bounded to drift-under-the-goal's-pull.

**Self-reference handling.** The finding enriches its own scorecard, with an obvious risk of self-flattering additions. Guards: every add gated by the finding's OWN disqualifying-force test; the #2 split checked against a real counter-category (weather: non-stationary, not path-dependent); the bounded-drift check against the explore/exploit canon; and the must-have count held at 5 (the bloat adversary's answer). The enrichment is precise about its own precision.
