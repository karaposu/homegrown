## Phase 2 — Generate: the four dives (guard-honored: each within its own family; no cross-family judgment until the comparison)

### Dive F4 — Entity-threaded registry → **"Route Threads as Views"**

**Mini-design (within pins: per-identity threads · extended-across-runs · query/glance read):** no new store. A route/concept's thread is a **derived view**, assembled on demand from artifacts that already exist: its map rows (first-seen, essentiality, ✓ state) + any choice-lines naming it + the pointers those carry. Realization: a read-side pattern — a `thread <identity>` assembly (a small script, or simply a warming instruction: "assemble the thread for X before touching X") rendering first-seen → selections → outcomes. The only persistent F4 objects remain the `_route.md` identity-sets already being written.

**Buys:** per-identity retrievability (the family's own criterion) exactly when traffic exists; the Selector's future per-identity history; zero write burden; zero duplication. **Costs:** read-time assembly; **parasitic on records existing** — with no choice-records, a thread renders map rows only.

**In-family inversion (mandated: "views are just grep — dignity-inflation; honest F4 = NOT NOW"):** partially stands, and the verdict carries it: F4's FULL form (a maintained registry-store) is honestly NOT NOW — the fresh-only ceiling is direct evidence, and the audit already deferred the cross-map registry with a named trigger. What survives is the entry-ramp: the views-pattern costs nothing to adopt (it is a reading discipline, not a build) and becomes valuable exactly when chains produce traffic. Calling the view "F4" is fair at the commitment level (the entity, not the event, organizes the read) — but the assessment says plainly: this is F4's cheapest possible instance, not its paradigm in force.

**E-scores:** E1 excellent (zero new parts) · E2 perfect (no habit) · E3 perfect (a view duplicates nothing) · E4 good at read-time. **Own-criterion (retrievability):** served conditionally on traffic. **Breakthrough verdict:** no — good accounting, nothing dissolves. **Overall: CONDITIONAL — adopt the view-pattern free; the store stays behind the audit's trigger (chains + cross-map traffic).**

### Dive F6 — Distilled ledger cascade → **"The Epoch Header"**

**Mini-design (within pins: rich capture · scheduled distill · layered summaries as the read-surface):** a ~10-line **rolling digest at the head of the memory file**, above the raw turn-lines. Written at two EXISTING gates: (i) at CONCLUDE — one line lifted from the finding's one-sentence answer + pointer (the distillation already happened; this is transcription); (ii) at the Allocation Rule's consultation cadence (~5 inquiries) — compress the oldest turn-lines into 1–2 trajectory-lines. Read by: **warming, first** (the header is the designed warm-start read — orientation in ten lines) and **the cadence's count** (the header carries the running inquiry-count — the count-shaped trigger finally gets a countable surface).

**Buys:** the missing cross-inquiry layer; warming acceleration; the trigger's food; the human's catch-up surface. **Costs:** the cadence-time compression is a real act (though it IS the cadence's own job); the CONCLUDE-time line is nearly free.

**In-family inversion (mandated: "written-never-read — a report generator, like `_route.md`'s cumulative half"):** the defense is structural — this design is **consumer-first**: the header exists BECAUSE warming reads it first; the read is designed-in, not hoped-for (whereas `_route.md`'s reader — a re-run — was hoped-for and never came). The honest residue stands: **if warming stays unpracticed, the header is unread and F6 fails its own memory-iff-consumed test.** The verdict carries the conditionality explicitly.

**E-scores:** E1 good (one header block) · E2 strong (rides two existing gates) · E3 clean (lines compress and point; nothing copied) · E4 excellent — the single best glance-surface any family offers. **Own-criterion (glanceability-at-scale):** served by construction. **Breakthrough verdict:** no — but the highest leverage-per-cost for the HUMAN reader of any family. **Overall: FELT SEAM, CHEAP FILL — memory-iff-warming-practices; the CONCLUDE-riding half is adoptable the day the memory file exists.**

### Dive F7 — Consumer-written memory → **"The Two-Phase Record"**

**Mini-design (within pins: successor writes predecessor's turn-memory · retro, outcome-aware · reception-not-emission):** the memory file is append-only; a turn contributes two lines, written by the two parties who actually know:

```text
[2026-07-03 act]  T7: chose <route> · because <one line>
[2026-07-05 warm] T7 meant: <what it turned out to mean> → <pointer>
```

The actor's half at act-time — F2's core, MINUS the impossible field (no `led-to` at act-time). The successor's half at warm-time: warming already reads what happened; while reading, it **closes open turns** — one meant-line per turn lacking one; the judgment is already formed in-context, so the write is transcription. Mechanics: the warming doctrine's "recent work" step gains one clause: *close open turns.*

**Buys:** the temporal glitch repaired honestly (each half written by whoever knows); **Retrospective-RC-shaped records** — outcome-aware judgments are the quality substrate's raw food; Selector-calibration gold (the delta between act-time `because` and warm-time `meant` measures rationale quality); staleness-resistance for the meant-half (written from outcomes, it can't be optimistic).

**Costs:** warming is unpracticed (roadmap item 2) — the full form rides a planned habit, not a practiced one; the successor line is a session-start act (bounded: only open turns).

**In-family inversion (mandated: "two unpracticed habits stacked — riding an unpracticed habit is riding nothing"):** partially stands and sharpens the verdict into a two-stage form: **(interim, free today)** repair the schema by DROPPING `led-to` from the act-time record — `chose · because` only; outcomes live in the territory; no dangling field (this is the pure-F2 fix, and it costs nothing); **(full, warming-gated)** the meant-line clause activates WITH warming — not a second habit but a clause IN the warming habit's own definition when it starts (same activation gate as the climb itself). Stacking dissolves: one habit, one clause inside it.

**E-scores:** E1 good (one file, two line-types) · E2 strong-when-warming (rides it; zero standalone habit) · E3 perfect (meant-lines point, never copy) · E4 good (open turns visible at a glance). **Own-criterion (hindsight-quality):** excellent by construction — the records ARE hindsight. **Breakthrough verdict (the register applied): the closest of the four** — exhaust-capture is real (the warm-time judgment is sunk cost; only transcription is added), and the meant-half dissolves act-time optimism structurally. Not zero-burden — the write remains — but the marginal burden approaches the size of one line. **Overall: THE LOCATED DISSATISFACTION'S ANSWER-CANDIDATE — an immediately-free schema repair + a warming-gated full form.**

### Dive F8 — Bitemporal record → **"Dated Append Lines"** (the light form)

**Mini-design (within pins: decided-time + learned-time · corrections append, never overwrite):** the file's FORMAT is the design. Every line carries its own date (= when-learned, for meant/correction lines); every line references a turn-id whose act-line carries when-decided. Both timestamps exist with zero machinery. Corrections are lines: `[date] T7 correction: <what changed> (supersedes the 07-03 line)` — nothing edited, ever. The HEAVY form (per-record dual-timestamp columns, query tooling) is deferred to a named trigger: calibration-time, when the Selector computes prediction-outcome deltas and the query patterns earn tooling.

**Buys:** belief-change auditability (the family's own criterion) at zero cost; the correction discipline the project already practices at finding-grain (refines-chains; stale-flags), extended down one grain by constitution rather than build. **Costs:** none in the light form; the heavy form's costs are deferred with its trigger.

**In-family inversion (mandated: "free-under-append-only means F8 contributes NOTHING — a file-format property, not a paradigm"):** partially stands and is clarifying — F8-light's contribution is a **discipline, not machinery**: the commitments (never overwrite; corrections cite what they supersede; dates on every line) are the file's CONSTITUTION, and constitutions are exactly what paradigm-bundles look like when their machinery cost hits zero. The assessment says both: nothing to build; something real to commit to.

**E-scores:** E1 perfect · E2 perfect · E3 perfect · E4 neutral. **Own-criterion (auditability-of-belief-change):** served at line grain. **Breakthrough verdict:** a small real dissolution — **the staleness burden at record grain genuinely disappears** (no record pretends to be current; supersession is explicit), which is the anti-rot property the June-22 finding wanted, now by format rather than vigilance. **Overall: ADOPT-THE-DISCIPLINE-FREE; machinery waits at the calibration trigger.**
