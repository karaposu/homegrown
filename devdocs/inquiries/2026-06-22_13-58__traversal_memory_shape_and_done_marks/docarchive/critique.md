## Phase 2 — Adversarial Evaluation

**C1 — D1 reconciliation soundness.** **Prosecution:** "decompose by where it lives" is a rhetorical move that relabels the disagreement. **Defense:** it makes a **testable location claim** per component (visits=folders, outcomes=findings/code, selection+rationale=nowhere) that holds under inspection, and it yields a DIFFERENT design (thin ledger + reconstruct) than either pole (fat log / pure session). Substantive. **Verdict: SURVIVE.**

**C2 — D2 "outcomes always already artifacts" (the load-bearing claim). 🔑** **Prosecution:** not every outcome is an artifact — a route can be **explored then abandoned** (no artifact), **deferred**, or **executed as a thought with no output**. The "point at the outcome" rule then points at nothing, and the information is lost. **Defense + collision:** these cases split cleanly: an outcome that **produced an artifact** (code, finding, spec edit, even a git diff) → **point** at it; an outcome that **produced no artifact** (abandon / defer / decide-existing-is-fine) is **itself a choice with a rationale** → it's held by the **ledger's rationale line** ("chose NOT to pursue R5 because X"), not a missing outcome-pointer. So nothing is lost — artifact-outcomes are pointed at, non-artifact-outcomes ARE rationales. **Verdict: SURVIVE + REFINE #1 (LOAD-BEARING):** state explicitly that "outcome" splits — *artifact-producing* → point; *decision/abandonment* → it is a choice+rationale the ledger already holds. This sharpens "point, don't re-describe."

**C3 — D3 L2 enrich-existing-links feasibility. 🔑** **Prosecution:** L2 (record the sliver as enriched relationship-links in existing artifacts) was oversold as "near-zero footprint" — but **not every selection spawns a child inquiry**: direct spec-edits, deferrals, and abandonments create no `_branch.md` to enrich. Where does their rationale go? **Defense + collision:** correct — L2 covers **inquiry-spawning** selections cleanly (the child's `CONTINUES FROM` + a rationale line); **non-spawning** selections (direct edits, defers, abandons) have no host artifact → they need a minimal central record (or a note in the parent's `_route.md`, where routelog already stamps `↗`). So the real design is likely **hybrid** — enrich links where a child exists, a thin central index for the rest. **Verdict: SURVIVE + REFINE #2:** L2 is the leanest option *where a host artifact exists*, NOT a total replacement; the design is hybrid (enriched-links + a thin central record for non-spawning selections) — and this is the gated/deferred choice, not settled now.

**C4 — D4 mark-reversal principled? 🔑** **Prosecution:** keeping the mark just defers to the user; the prior dropped it on identity grounds ("one enumerator, two controllers"). **Defense + collision:** the reversal is principled on TWO distinctions the prior missed: (a) **static-piece** — the prior's clobber/futility argument was about *regenerated* maps; a **concluded** piece isn't regenerated, so a mark on it is durable; (b) **authored-empty** — routelister authors the mark-slot *empty and never reads it* (more hands-off than the `Priority`/`Essentiality` slots it actively fills), so "one enumerator, two controllers" **still holds** — the consumer (routelog/hand) fills it. The prior over-killed by conflating the cheap mark with the fat log. **Verdict: SURVIVE + REFINE #3:** state the honest SCOPE — the mark is durable on **concluded** pieces (the common consumption case: route-maps are read after conclude); on a *still-iterating* inquiry's map it would still be wiped on re-run (but you don't mark routes mid-inquiry). Identity holds via authored-empty + consumer-filled.

**C5 — D5 prior re-test honesty.** **Prosecution:** the re-test soft-pedals how much the prior was wrong (it reverses two of its recommendations). **Defense:** the re-test is explicit — SURVIVES (routelister=enumerator-never-tracks; need-is-real; map-vs-record) and CORRECTED (the fat-log shape; the drop-✓), each with a structural reason. The spine genuinely survives (routelister is untouched); the two corrected items are sub-recommendations, not the core verdict. Honest. **Verdict: SURVIVE.**

**C6 — D6 worth-it / over-engineering.** **Prosecution:** a four-organ architecture for "keep a mark and don't write a fat log" is over-built. **Defense:** three of the four organs already exist (routelister, routelog, the orchestrator session as a canon role); the only new thing is the thin selection-record, and even it may be enriched-links (near-zero), and its form is **deferred** (gated ≥3 turns). The round commits PRINCIPLES (log choices not outcomes; keep the mark; reconstruct by warming) + a small re-test, not a build. **Verdict: SURVIVE.**
