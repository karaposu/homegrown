## Phase 2 — Generate

### Inversion (Framer — MANDATORY, the central commitment) 🔑

**Central assumption:** "the fix is to make WHY climb to the goal via a zoom-ladder generated by two meta-questions."

**L1 inversion — "Don't restructure WHY at all; it's already a 2-part field — just tell authors to fill the importance half."** (component-level)
- *Steelman:* the spec already says WHY = "territory-evidence · why-this-might-be-important." The goal-contribution IS the importance half. No new structure — just discipline.
- *Why it partially holds, partially fails:* TRUE that no *heavy* structure is needed (the importance half exists — so this is NOT three new sub-fields). But "just tell authors to do better" is **what is failing now** — the measured goal-band-drop happened *despite* the importance half existing. Without a **named, mandatory** goal-rung (a meta-question that must be answered), the band keeps dropping. → Inversion yields the kept refinement: **the change is minimal (not three sub-fields) but it must be a NAMED MANDATORY question, not a vague "fill it in better."** Lightest structure that *guarantees* the goal rung.

**L2 inversion — system-level: "Put the goal-reasoning WITH essentiality (a reasoning sub-block, like meaning-gaps sits with Confidence), NOT in WHY."** 🔑 (the real structural fork)
- *System reframe:* meaning-gaps live in Guidance, tied to Confidence ("by-product of Confidence"). By parallel, the goal-reasoning could be an essentiality-reasoning sub-block — keeping the rating↔reasoning pattern *physically* parallel.
- *Why it fails (structural grounds):* fragmenting the reasoning into per-rating sub-blocks (a WHY for Priority, a gaps-block for Confidence, an essentiality-block for Essentiality) **scatters** what is naturally **one continuous climb**. The local→neighbourhood→goal reasoning is a single line of thought; splitting its top rung into a separate block tied to essentiality breaks the continuity. And the user's actual ask is to enrich **WHY**, not spawn a new block. WHY already IS the per-route reasoning prose — the goal rung is its natural top, not a separate artifact. → **Keep it in WHY.** The meaning-gaps parallel is informative (it proves the rating↔reasoning pattern) but the goal-reasoning belongs in WHY because WHY is the continuous reasoning field. *Kept linkage:* the spec should NOTE that WHY's goal rung reads as the essentiality token's justification — linked, not fragmented.
- This inversion is the most valuable: it **confirms the SV6 placement against a real, spec-precedented alternative.**

**L3 inversion — "three literal sub-fields ARE simplest."**
- *Why it fails:* three forced sub-fields bloat (violate ceiling-not-floor) and the narrow one duplicates Move/Lands (measured). A single climbing field with a mandatory top rung is lighter and sufficient.

### Absence Recognition (Generator — both levels)

- **Redesign-level (from scratch):** if the record were designed from scratch with "every route justified up to the goal," WHY would climb by default; the flat single-scope WHY is the incremental artifact.
- **Redesign-level (already-present-in-different-form):** the goal-climb is **already in the best WHYs** ("the design's whole purpose is to de-risk the build"; "a money/trust board's value is exception-surfacing... preserves anti-god-mode"). **Formalize the good examples — don't invent.**

### Domain Transfer (Generator — native + different)
- **Native (writing/journalism): the "nut graf."** A news story has a paragraph stating why the story matters to the reader. WHY's goal rung is the route's **nut graf** — "why this matters to the goal."
- **Native (software): the commit-message body.** The record was already analogized to a commit; a good commit body says why the change matters to the system. WHY's goal rung = the body's "why this matters" line.
- **Different (management/OKRs): "line of sight."** Good objective systems require every task to show its **line of sight** to a top objective. WHY's goal rung is the route's *line of sight to the end goal*. (Clean crystallizing term.)

### Combination (Generator)
- the goal-rung **+ essentiality** → the goal rung is the essentiality token's **caption** (the rating + its one-line justification, read together).
- the two meta-questions **+ the lean rule** → a short route **fuses** them into one climbing clause ("X, so the goal gains Y").

### Constraint Manipulation (Framer — both directions)
- **ADD** "WHY may be only ONE sentence" → forces the climb into a single sentence ending at the goal ("does X locally, so that the end goal gains Y") — elegant, lean, and it *structurally guarantees* the goal rung.
- **REMOVE** "the goal rung is mandatory" → the band keeps dropping (the measured status quo). → the mandatory goal-rung is **load-bearing, not removable.**

### Lens Shifting (Framer)
- **Solo-human lens:** the goal rung answers "why am I doing this again?" at a glance.
- **Meta-loop-selector lens:** the goal rung gives a downstream selector the route's line-of-sight (machine-readable-ish), without WHY deciding anything.

### Extrapolation (Generator)
- Across many maps, a mandatory goal-rung builds a consistent **line of sight from every route to the goal** — anti-drift accumulates; a cross-map "are all my routes still pointed at the goal?" check falls out.

## Crystallizing Output

### The NAME: **Line of Sight** (WHY climbs from the route to the goal)

> The route record already says **what** a route does (`Move`/`Lands`) and **whether** the goal needs it (`Essentiality`). What's been dropping out is **why the goal needs it** — the route's *line of sight to the goal*. A flat WHY stops at the local justification; a good WHY **climbs** until it reaches the end goal. The fix: **every WHY must reach the goal** — the top rung is mandatory.

And the organizing pattern: **every rating has its reasoning, and WHY's top rung is essentiality's.**

> | rating (the glance) | its reasoning (the prose) |
> |---|---|
> | **Priority** (how pressing) | **WHY** — why it's pressing |
> | **Confidence** (how formed) | **meaning-gaps** — why it's not fully formed (a by-product of Confidence) |
> | **Essentiality** (whether the goal needs it) | **WHY's goal rung** — what the goal gains (why it's core) |
>
> The goal rung isn't a duplicate of the essentiality token — it's the token's *justification*, the third instance of a pattern the spec already runs twice.

### (a) The two domain-agnostic meta-questions

> **WHY climbs from the route to the goal, generated by two glance questions:**
> - *(neighbourhood — optional)* **"What does this unblock or protect around it?"**
> - *(goal — mandatory)* **"What does the end goal gain — what's the line of sight to the goal?"**
>
> The **local rung** ("what it does") is already `Move`/`Lands` — WHY does **not** restate it; it climbs *from* there.

### (b) Real WHYs rewritten

**A narrow-only WHY gaining its goal rung** (map `13-57`, the settlement-key route):

> *Before (narrow-only):* "the DB-level exactly-once guarantee + the missing refund type; one migration."
> *After (climbs to the goal):* "gives the settlement engine its DB-level exactly-once guarantee — **so the end goal, a trustworthy money loop, can't double-pay or strand funds.**"
>
> The bold clause is the mandatory goal rung (the line of sight). The local provision rides in `Move`/`Lands`.

**A crammed WHY reflowed into labeled rungs** (map `21-40`, the keystone route — only when dense; ceiling-not-floor):

> *Before (crammed run-on):* "the keystone — `capabilities_for` has exactly one caller, so the pivot is a one-endpoint change. It ships independently of the gate cutover and cannot break a money-path gate."
> *After:*
> - *(neighbourhood)* ships independently of the gate cutover; cannot break a money-path gate (one caller).
> - *(goal)* gives the product a role-correct authority surface **without risking the money path** — the goal's safety property.

A short route keeps it to one climbing clause; only a dense route earns the labeled rungs.
