## Phase 1 — Landscape

- **Viable:** a single WHY field that climbs to a mandatory goal-rung, generalizes across kinds, stays route↔goal, reflows rather than inflates.
- **Dead:** a rung that asserts route↔route dependencies; three forced sub-fields; a goal-rung that subsumes/duplicates essentiality; forced filler on peripheral routes.
- **Boundary:** the optional **neighbourhood (mid) rung** — real in the data but the place where dependency-graph creep lives.

### Candidate verdicts

**C1 — The line-of-sight WHY (mandatory goal-rung).** Prosecution: "WHYs already reach the goal sometimes." Defense+collision: the corpus shows the goal band dropping out (most WHYs stop at the local provision); making it mandatory surfaces it uniformly, and even peripheral routes get an honest (weak) line of sight. **Verdict: SURVIVE** (D1, D2 pass; external-anchor validated).

**C2 — D3 no-dependency-graph (the load-bearing identity prosecution).** Prosecution: the **neighbourhood (mid) rung** is the danger — "ships independently of the gate cutover" references another route/phase; that's a route↔route edge (forbidden §1.3). Defense: "ships independently / cannot break the money path" is an attributive *property* of the route (its isolation / blast radius), not a dependency edge ("A needs B"). Collision: the property reading is clean, BUT the rung *could* drift into dependency claims. **Verdict: SURVIVE + REFINE #1 (LOAD-BEARING):** the neighbourhood rung carries the route's **area-property** (blast radius / what it protects / isolation), framed as a property OF the route — NEVER a route↔route dependency edge. The goal rung (route↔goal) is always safe; the guard is specifically for the mid rung.

**C3 — D4 no-duplication, precisely scoped.** Prosecution: "if the goal-rung = essentiality's reasoning, and WHY is mostly the goal-rung, then WHY ≈ essentiality." Defense: WHY has THREE rungs; **only the GOAL rung is essentiality's reasoning/caption** — the neighbourhood rung is WHY's own (tied to no rating), and the local rung is Move/Lands. So WHY carries strictly more than essentiality's reasoning. Collision: the innovation was right but imprecise if read loosely. **Verdict: SURVIVE + REFINE #2:** state precisely — *only the top (goal) rung* is essentiality's caption; the neighbourhood rung is independent. Don't claim all of WHY is essentiality's reasoning.

**C4 — D6 placement (the L2 fork).** Prosecution: meaning-gaps is a sub-block in Guidance tied to Confidence; by exact parallel, essentiality's reasoning should be a sub-block too — consistency. Defense: the parallel is NOT exact — **meaning-gaps is a LIST of multiple facets** (plural, itemized → warrants a block), whereas **the goal line-of-sight is a SINGLE continuous clause** that is the natural top of WHY's reasoning prose (singular → belongs inline in WHY). Collision: the structural difference (list vs single clause) justifies the different placement; keep it in WHY. **Verdict: SURVIVE + REFINE #3:** the L2 rejection holds *because* the goal-reasoning is a single continuous clause, not an itemized list — the meaning-gaps parallel is informative (proves the rating↔reasoning pattern) but not binding on placement.

**C5 — D5 mandatory-ness vs filler.** Prosecution: forcing a goal-rung on every route manufactures filler on routes with no goal benefit. Defense: the LOW/peripheral routes above show the honest answer is "indirect / future-phase" — informative, not filler. **Verdict: SURVIVE + REFINE #4:** mandatory-ness means *state the line of sight even if weak* ("indirect — a future-phase nice-to-have"), NOT *manufacture a strong benefit*. A weak line of sight is a true signal (and confirms a peripheral essentiality).

**C6 — D5 lean/bloat.** Prosecution: WHY gains a clause after we've been trimming. Defense: the narrow rung is OFF-loaded to Move/Lands, so WHY may get *shorter* on routes that currently cram local detail; the goal-rung is one clause; labeled rungs only when dense (ceiling-not-floor). **Verdict: SURVIVE + REFINE #5:** honest accounting — roughly neutral (one goal clause added, local detail shed to Move/Lands), structured not crammed.

**C7 — the "line of sight" name + rating↔reasoning pattern.** Prosecution (substance): is "line of sight" real or borrowed gloss? Defense: it maps to measured cases (the best WHYs already climb — "the design's whole purpose is to de-risk the build"); the rating↔reasoning pattern is in the spec twice (Priority↔WHY, Confidence↔meaning-gaps). **Verdict: SURVIVE.**
