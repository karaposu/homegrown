# Finding: WHY Field — Give Every Route a "Line of Sight" to the Goal

## Question

In this project, the **routelister** discipline ends each inquiry with a "route-map": a list of next-step routes, each carrying a few fields. One field is **WHY** — the prose that justifies the route. (In the spec it is defined as *"territory-evidence · why-this-might-be-important."*)

The user's observation: the WHY field is **too narrow** — it tends to give only the *local* reason a route matters, not what the *end goal* gains from it. They want WHY to carry **narrow + mid + big scope**, and proposed simple sub-fields (`why-narrow` / `why-mid` / `why-big`). Their two questions: **do sub-fields make sense, or is there a better organization / a different axis paradigm — maybe with domain-agnostic meta-questions?**

The goal: a verdict plus the best structure for a multi-scope WHY — lightweight, domain-agnostic, consistent with routelister's identity, and reconciled with the **essentiality** axis just added in the prior inquiry (which also concerns the goal — so we must check for overlap).

## Finding Summary

- **Verdict: YES — the instinct is right, and measurably so. But the best form is NOT three literal sub-fields. It is a short "line of sight" — WHY should *climb* from the route up to the end goal.**

- **What "climbing" means — three rungs, but you don't write three lines:**
  - **local** ("what the route does") — *already covered by the `Move`/`Lands` fields*; WHY does not restate it.
  - **neighbourhood** ("what it unblocks or protects around it") — **optional**.
  - **goal** ("what the end goal gains") — **mandatory**. This is the band that was dropping out.

- **Generated by two domain-agnostic meta-questions** (what the user asked for): *"what does this unblock or protect around it?"* (optional) and *"what does the end goal gain?"* (mandatory).

- **Why the goal-rung doesn't duplicate the essentiality axis (the load-bearing point):** essentiality is the *rating* (core / supporting / peripheral — *whether* the goal needs the route); the WHY goal-rung is the *reasoning* (*what* the goal gains — *why* it's core). Rating and reasoning are different jobs. The spec already pairs them twice — **Priority↔WHY**, **Confidence↔meaning-gaps** — so the WHY goal-rung is just the third instance of a pattern already in use.

- **The name: "line of sight."** A flat WHY stops at the local justification; a good WHY has a clear line of sight all the way to the goal. The fix is simply: **every WHY must reach the goal.**

- **It's measured, not theoretical:** across real route-maps, most WHYs stop at the local provision ("one migration"; "the DB-level exactly-once guarantee") while a few already climb ("the design's whole purpose is to de-risk the build"). The goal band is real and it systematically drops out. The fix formalizes what the *best* WHYs already do.

- **It stays lean and identity-clean:** one clause on a short route (labeled rungs only when dense); the goal-rung is route-to-goal ("what the goal gains"), never route-to-route ("this enables route B") — which would be the forbidden dependency graph.

## Finding

### Why we are even discussing this

A route-map is meant to let a reader — including a cold reader picking the work up months later — decide what to do next. Each route says **what** it does (the `Move`/`Lands` fields), and after the prior inquiry, **whether** the goal needs it (the `Essentiality` rating). The WHY field is supposed to supply the *reasoning*. The user noticed that the reasoning keeps stopping short: it explains the local mechanics but rarely closes the loop to *what the end goal actually gains*. So you can read a route and still not see why it's worth doing for the thing you're building.

### The diagnosis: the goal band drops out

We checked the real WHY fields. The user's literal claim — "WHY is only narrow scope" — slightly overshoots: some WHYs already reach the goal ("a money/trust board's value is exception-surfacing... preserves anti-god-mode"; "the design's whole purpose is to de-risk the build"). But the coverage is **inconsistent and left to chance**, and the band that systematically goes missing is the **goal/benefit band** — most WHYs give the local causal role ("settlement has nothing to release without a hold") and never state what the *goal* gains. A second, smaller problem: when WHYs do reach multiple scopes, they often **cram** them into one run-on sentence that's hard to parse.

### The answer: WHY climbs to the goal (a "line of sight")

Rather than add three labeled sub-fields, restructure WHY as a short **climb** with three rungs — but only two of them live in WHY:

- The **local rung** ("what the route does") is already the `Move`/`Lands` fields. WHY does **not** restate it; it climbs *from* there.
- The **neighbourhood rung** ("what this unblocks or protects around it") is **optional** — included when there's a real area-effect worth naming.
- The **goal rung** ("what the end goal gains") is **mandatory** — this is the line of sight, and the band that was dropping out.

Two domain-agnostic meta-questions generate it (the user's explicit request):
1. *(neighbourhood — optional)* "What does this unblock or protect around it?"
2. *(goal — mandatory)* "What does the end goal gain — what's the line of sight to the goal?"

A short route answers in one climbing clause; only a dense route earns labeled rungs (the same "ceiling, not floor" rule the `Move`/`Lands` redesign settled).

### Why this isn't a duplicate of essentiality

This is the question the inquiry had to get right, because the prior inquiry just added an essentiality axis that also concerns the goal. The resolution is clean: **essentiality is the rating; the WHY goal-rung is the reasoning behind it.**

- **Essentiality** answers *whether* the goal needs the route — a one-token verdict (core / supporting / peripheral).
- **The WHY goal-rung** answers *what* the goal gains — the prose that justifies the verdict.

This is a pattern the spec already runs twice: **Priority** (a rating) is justified by **WHY**; **Confidence** (a rating) is justified by the **meaning-gaps** list (which the spec authors "as a by-product of Confidence"). The WHY goal-rung is the third instance — it is precisely the caption that explains the essentiality token. So the two complement rather than duplicate.

| rating (the glance) | its reasoning (the prose) |
|---|---|
| **Priority** — how pressing | **WHY** — why it's pressing |
| **Confidence** — how formed | **meaning-gaps** — why it's not fully formed |
| **Essentiality** — whether the goal needs it | **WHY's goal-rung** — what the goal gains |

A precise note (caught in review): *only* the goal-rung is essentiality's caption. The neighbourhood rung is WHY's own and is tied to no rating — so WHY is not wholly subsumed by essentiality.

### Two concrete examples

**A narrow-only WHY gaining its goal-rung** (a real settlement-key route):

> *Before (stops local):* "the DB-level exactly-once guarantee + the missing refund type; one migration."
> *After (climbs to the goal):* "gives the settlement engine its DB-level exactly-once guarantee — **so the end goal, a trustworthy money loop, can't double-pay or strand funds.**"

The bold clause is the mandatory goal-rung; the action ("one migration") rides in `Move`.

**A crammed WHY reflowed** (only because it's dense — a real authority-pivot route):

> *Before (run-on):* "the keystone — `capabilities_for` has exactly one caller, so the pivot is a one-endpoint change. It ships independently of the gate cutover and cannot break a money-path gate."
> *After:*
> - *(neighbourhood)* ships independently of the gate cutover; cannot break a money-path gate.
> - *(goal)* gives the product a role-correct authority surface **without risking the money path.**

### Five refinements the design carries (from adversarial review)

The design was stress-tested by applying the mandatory goal-rung literally to real routes spanning every engagement-kind and priority. It survived (the goal-rung produced a clean line of sight on all of them), with five things the eventual spec wording must get right:

1. **(Load-bearing) The neighbourhood rung is an area-property, never a dependency edge.** "Ships independently / cannot break the money path" is a *property* of the route (its isolation, its blast radius) — fine. "This route depends on route R2" is a route-to-route dependency edge — forbidden (it would build the dependency graph routelister must not build). The goal-rung is always safe (route-to-goal); the guard is specifically for the optional mid rung.
2. **Only the goal-rung is essentiality's reasoning.** The neighbourhood rung is WHY's own. Don't claim all of WHY is essentiality's caption.
3. **Keep it in WHY, not a separate essentiality block.** A tempting alternative was to attach the goal-reasoning to essentiality as a sub-block (mirroring how meaning-gaps attaches to Confidence). Rejected: meaning-gaps is an itemized *list* (which warrants a block), whereas the goal line-of-sight is a *single continuous clause* that is the natural top of WHY's prose. Splitting it out would fragment one line of thought.
4. **Mandatory means "state it even if weak," not "manufacture a benefit."** On a peripheral route, the honest goal-rung reads "indirect — a future-phase nice-to-have." That is not filler — it is *why* the route is peripheral, and it confirms the essentiality rating. The mandatory-ness is about always showing the line of sight, even when it's faint.
5. **Honest lean accounting.** WHY gains a goal clause, but it sheds the local detail to `Move`/`Lands`, so the net is roughly neutral — and structured, not crammed.

## Reasoning

**Why this answer over the alternatives** — the full field considered:

- **"Three literal sub-fields (why-narrow / why-mid / why-big)" (rejected).** It bloats (forces three lines on every route, against the lean rule), and the narrow rung duplicates the `Move`/`Lands` fields, which already say what the route does. A single climbing field with a mandatory goal-rung is lighter and sufficient.

- **"Just tell authors to fill the importance half better" (rejected, but it sharpened the answer).** WHY already has an "importance" half in the spec, so no heavy new structure is needed. But "try harder" is exactly what is failing now — the goal band drops out *despite* the importance half existing. So the change is minimal *but* must be a **named, mandatory** question, not a vague exhortation.

- **"Attach the goal-reasoning to essentiality as a sub-block" (rejected — the hardest alternative).** This mirrors how meaning-gaps attaches to Confidence, so it had real pull. It fails because meaning-gaps is an itemized list (warranting a block) while the goal line-of-sight is a single continuous clause that belongs inline at the top of WHY's prose. Fragmenting WHY's climb into a separate block scatters one line of thought.

- **"A consequence-chain WHY (what it enables → what that enables → … → the goal)" (rejected).** This chains route-to-route, which is the dependency graph routelister is forbidden to build. The line of sight stays route-to-goal ("what the goal gains").

- **What survived:** the line-of-sight WHY — local delegated to `Move`/`Lands`, optional neighbourhood rung (area-property only), mandatory goal-rung (route-to-goal, the essentiality caption), generated by two domain-agnostic meta-questions, reflowed not inflated. It survived adversarial testing applied literally to real routes of every engagement-kind and every priority — the goal-rung generalized to all of them (epistemic routes' line of sight is "what understanding the goal gains"; peripheral routes' is an honest "indirect"). The review was externally grounded in the real maps and the spec, not in the analysis agreeing with itself.

## Open Questions

### Monitoring
- **Will authors keep goal-rungs honest rather than filler?** Observable across the first maps written under the new spec. The risk is a route with a genuinely weak line of sight getting a manufactured strong-sounding goal-rung. The mitigation is already in the spec wording (a weak "indirect / future-phase" line of sight is the correct answer, and it should match a peripheral essentiality) — watch whether authors use it.

### Refinement Triggers
- **Reconsider the optional neighbourhood rung** if, in practice, it is either never used (drop it — WHY becomes a two-rung local→goal climb) or repeatedly drifts into route-to-route dependency claims despite the guard (tighten or remove it). Observable after the first several maps under the new format.
