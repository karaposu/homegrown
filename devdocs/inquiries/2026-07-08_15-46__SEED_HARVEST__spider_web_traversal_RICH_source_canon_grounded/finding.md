## Finding

### Why this dive existed

The project runs a "seed harvest": it reads outside sources (papers, and here a spoken metaphor) and asks what design-germs they provoke for its own machinery. Six dives in, every single dive had produced exactly one seed. The user then took one of those sources — the spider-web-predation metaphor for how thinking-space gets traversed — and spoke a long, developed version of it, and could see half a dozen distinct ideas in it where the harvest had banked one. That gap is the alarm: if the harvester under-generates, quality sources get wasted and real ideas get missed. This dive is the controlled test of what went wrong and whether a specific fix recovers the lost yield.

### The diagnosis this dive set out to prove

The thin dive's coverage table had crossed the spider metaphor against the harness's *machinery* — the surfacing step that pulls in relevant material, the relevance-router, the `_route` map, the seed index, the gate. That is a narrow axis, and it is downstream of the actual subject. The spider metaphor is not about the machinery; it is about *traversal* — how a searcher moves through an unknown space and pins down a target whose shape it does not yet know. The project has a whole canon model of exactly that, and the thin dive never crossed against it.

So the fix under test was precise: **ground the anchor axis in the canon model of traversal, not the harness plumbing.** Concretely, this dive built an 11-column anchor axis from four canon sources —

1. the **analogy family** (`docs/canon/thinking_space_traversal_analogies.md`): a set of natural-world pictures for traversal, currently the fungus (the search-MOVE: explore / exploit / backtrack) and the slime-mold and humanity's collective record (the RECORD layer);
2. the **movement vocabulary** (`docs/canon/thinking_space_dynamics.md`): the project's primitives for moves through the space (steps, charts, steering);
3. the **open question** (`docs/canon/what_is_meaningful_traversal.md`): a deliberately-unresolved question about what separates real traversal from spinning-in-place, with five candidate signals but no committed answer;
4. **SUSTRALL's unbuilt organs** (`devdocs/inquiries/2026-06-11_16-45__sustrall_next_main_steps_and_alternatives/finding.md`): the era-goal names three things it needs but has not built — traversal-memory ("zero instances today"), session-warming, and multi-head search ("the one organ that must be grown new").

The spider's 15 predation-and-traversal mechanics (from `a.md`) became the rows; these 11 canon anchors became the columns; the 165 cells were the coverage table.

### What the table produced

Of the 165 cells, 24 were genuinely attempted with a hypothesis, 13 collapsed to mirror-of-something-owned, and 128 were skip-reasoned (most of them the whole machinery column — the thin dive's entire axis folded here into a single already-covered column — plus the rows that only re-picture branching or iteration the project already has). The 24 attempts produced 11 raw candidates plus an explicit "maybe it's all mirrors" candidate held live for honesty.

The gate then ran unchanged. It is worth being concrete about how hard it bit, because the whole method-proof rests on the gate NOT having been loosened to manufacture a yield:

- **4 crossings were killed as mirrors.** The web-as-external-structure that senses for the spider re-describes a distributed-cognition idea the project already owns; the multi-head *picture* re-pictures branching the project already does; coarse-then-fine re-describes the loop's existing refine passes; and "tightening one part loses another" is a true trade-off observation but names no open design decision.
- **A "warming is needed" claim was demoted to mere affirmation.** The metaphor re-derives that SUSTRALL needs session-warming and traversal-memory — but SUSTRALL already *names* those needs, so re-noting them is confirmation, not a seed. Only the specific *shape* proposed for them (what they should store) survived as a seed.
- **The user's "big seed" was cut down by more than half** (detailed below).

That is four kills, a demotion, and a size-cap — on a run where the user had explicitly asked for *more*. The gate was not the thing that changed.

### The seven seeds, and the one frame that organizes them

The seven survivors are not scattered. They assemble into a single picture: **the spider models the "define" half of what the project calls explfine — explore-and-define.** The fungus already modeled the explore half (how you move); the spider models how a moving searcher pins down a target whose shape is unknown.

The centerpiece is **`p29-S2`, the shape-definition frame.** The analogy family had a real gap: it pictured how one search *steps* (the fungus) and how many searches *accumulate* (the record layer), but not how a traversal *defines* an unknown-shaped target. The spider fills exactly that — it converges by catching a feasible part first, tightening it, and letting that locked part constrain and reveal the rest ("it's definitely a mobile app" → the user-grouping into iPhone/Android follows). This is structurally the same move the sibling fungus dive made: that dive showed the family had been all-record until the *move* was named; this one shows it still lacked *shape-definition*. The frame is import-shaped — it names something the project half-does but never named — which is why it also lands on the ladder as an import, cross-referenced, not double-counted.

Around that frame sit five mechanism-seeds, each naming a distinct open design decision:

- **`p29-S3`, capture-order** — which anchor you lock first is consequential: lock the load-bearing one first, sample every dimension a little before deep-locking any, proceed gradually across parts; lock the wrong part first and the target "escapes" (you define around a wrong core). This is not the piece-ordering the decomposition discipline already does (that orders pieces of a *known* whole by coupling); this is anchor-selection while converging on an *unknown* target.
- **`p29-S4`, cross-head anchor-propagation** — a coordination shape for SUSTRALL's unbuilt multi-head organ: a head that resolves an anchor propagates it *during* the run to constrain the other heads, rather than each head running blind and merging at the end.
- **`p29-S5`, confidence-gated borders** — a candidate signal-family for the open "meaningful traversal" question: sense the target's borders with approximate confidence, start from the high-confidence borders, and leave low-confidence regions explicitly UNDECIDED (a first-class abstain state, not a forced guess). The five signals the canon already lists are about the walk's productivity; none is about confidence in having crossed a boundary.
- **`p29-S6`, anchors-as-portable-state** — the specific content for SUSTRALL's memory and warming organs: the resolved anchors ARE the compressed traversal-state, so they are both what traversal-memory stores and what session-warming carries into a fresh session.
- **`p29-S8`, the completion-criterion** — a candidate answer to the open question's termination half: a traversal is *done* when the shape is well-defined enough that you can say what it IS and what it is NOT (the negative space closed), rather than at a coverage-percentage or a step-budget. This one surfaced from the second-harvest backstop — the deliberate "what did the table miss?" pass — which earned its keep here. *(Later re-sized: a follow-on clarification dive showed the termination question is an eight-sub-question family, and this seed answers two of them — measure + decidability — not the whole; see the seed record below.)*

### The user's "big seed," resized honestly

The user singled out "sensemaking = thinking-space traversal at small scale" as a *big* seed. Taken at full strength — "sensemaking IS a scaled-down traversal" — it does not survive: the canon deliberately separates the *terrain* (the space the loops walk) from the *mover* (the within-a-step primitives sensemaking uses), and collapsing them is a category error. But the defensible core does survive, and it is narrower: the convergence *dynamic* — the shape-definition move of `p29-S2` — is scale-invariant, so a model developed for the sensemaking scale transfers to the loop-traversal scale and back. That is `p29-S7`, and it is recorded honestly as thin, contested (it still owes a resolution of the terrain/mover tension), and valuable mainly for the transfer it licenses rather than as a standalone insight. Keeping it (rather than killing it) honors the real structural content; resizing it (rather than banking it as "big") is the anti-rubber-stamp half of the same judgment.

### The fix-readout

The clean result: **same idea, 1 seed vs 7, with the gate held constant, and the only variable the anchor axis.** Every one of the seven sits on a canon-grounded column (the analogy family, the movement vocabulary, the open question, the SUSTRALL organs, the sensemaking-scale relation). Not one is reachable from the machinery axis the thin dive used — the thin dive's single seed was, quite literally, the only thing its narrow axis could reach. This is as close to a controlled comparison as a harvest gets, and it points hard at anchor-axis breadth as the lever. It does not *prove* the general diagnosis — the rich articulation and the deeper read co-varied with the axis, so the comparison is near-controlled, not clean — but it is strong, specific evidence for the deferred inquiry to build on.
