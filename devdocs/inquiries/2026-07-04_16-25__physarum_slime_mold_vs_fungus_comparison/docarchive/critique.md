## Phase 2-3 — Adversarial Evaluation + Verdicts

### Q3 (the over-integration fix) — the sharpest — REFINE

**Prosecution A (D2, D3):** the "fix" leans on calling a slime trail a "memory" — an anthropomorphism. Strip that and it is just chemical self-avoidance; no fix.
**Defense:** the published framing is externalized *spatial memory* — the trail is deposited information the organism reads to avoid re-searching, which is functionally a memory regardless of any cognition claim. The finding already scopes it to the *function* (anti-redundancy navigation), not cognition. **SURVIVES the anthropomorphism charge** — the scoping is present.

**Prosecution B (D1, D6 — the real catch):** is the over-integration wrinkle actually *fixed*, or just relocated? The mycelium fused persistent-memory with body/budget. Physarum separates the *external* slime trail — but its *internal* tubes are still simultaneously the body, the resource-conduit, and the working-state. So Physarum's internal side fuses working-memory + budget exactly as the mycelium did.
**Defense + collision:** true — and this sharpens rather than sinks the claim. **REFINE R1:** state precisely which axis is fixed. Physarum resolves the over-integration on the **persistent-external-record axis** (an external, persisting record held distinct from the live flow — the axis the mycelium fully fused and the project actually separates). Its internal tubes *do* still fuse working-state with budget — but that residual fusion is *faithful*, not a flaw, because in the project too the live working context and the compute/attention budget are one session-resource. So the finding should claim: Physarum fixes the *persistent-memory* axis of the over-integration (a better map than the mycelium there) and leaves the *working-memory/budget* fusion intact, which matches the project. This makes "partial fix" precise (which axis) and turns the residual into a virtue.

### The Emergent (record-layer's two functions; _route.md ≈ slime trail) — REFINE

Calibrate the claim from the start rather than asserting-then-walking-back. The two-record-functions refinement (accumulate-findings vs avoid-redundancy) is sound and correctly scoped as a candidate for the family/spec-slot, not a redefinition.
