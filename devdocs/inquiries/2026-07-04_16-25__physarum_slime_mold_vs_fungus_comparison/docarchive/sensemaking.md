## SV2 — The Physarum profile committed, and the structure-not-biology re-test

**Committed (honesty-bounded):** *Physarum polycephalum* in its plasmodial stage is a single giant multinucleate cell — one continuous streaming body, no internal walls (so, structurally, *more* one-body than the mycelium's many walled hyphae). It is motile: it flows, and can withdraw its whole mass and relocate. Its cytoplasm oscillates (shuttle streaming), redistributing toward attractants; its tube network thickens productive routes and prunes unproductive ones; there is no brain. Its demonstrated behaviours are citable as results — maze/shortest-path, the Tokyo rail network, anticipation of periodic events, and an externalized slime-trail memory it uses to avoid re-searching.

**A-SYNC re-test (structure-not-biology, with a SECOND organism):** the rule holds. The load-bearing claims (syncytial cell, motility, tube adaptation, no brain, and the experiments-as-results) are textbook or well-known published findings; the *interpretive* layer — "the slime mold decides / learns / thinks" — is bounded OUT of any load-bearing position. Cite the behaviours (path-finding, network-optimization, trail-avoidance), not a mind. So the refined rule ("real-mechanism-informs, model-carries-where-thin, contested-bounded-out") survives a second application intact — and its "contested-bounded-out" clause does real work here (the cognition debate is exactly what it excludes).

## SV4 — The two hypotheses adjudicated

**A-FIX — the over-integration wrinkle: PARTIALLY fixed (this is the dive's sharpest contribution).** The mycelium's wrinkle (15-56): it fuses memory and body/budget into one structure, where the project keeps the *external corpus* (memory) separable from *live context* (budget/flow). Physarum splits them natively: the **internal tube network** is the live flow / current allocation; the **external slime trail** is a persistent record left in the world, used for anti-redundancy ("don't re-search where I've been"). That is exactly the project's own separation — a written corpus / route-index kept *outside* the live traverse. So on the memory-separation axis, **Physarum is the better map than the mycelium**: it shows a brainless forager keeping an external, separable memory distinct from its live flow.

But scope it honestly, or it overclaims. The slime trail records *where it has been* (a been-there, anti-redundancy trail) — not *what it found and how much each find is worth* (a weighted, proportion-kept findings archive). So Physarum's external memory maps the **anti-redundancy function** of the project's record, not the full record. And it does **not** perform a proportion-correcting re-read either (avoid-where-been ≠ re-weight-the-whole). So the fix is *partial*: it resolves the over-integration on the *separation* axis (external record distinct from live flow — genuinely better than the mycelium), while the *proportion-keeping* gap remains open (and so the pairing rule still holds — SV6).

**A-GROUND — the grounding import: real, but a lesser tier.** The Tokyo rail is genuine evidence that a decentralized, brainless network can compute a near-optimal, robust resource-distribution structure. Tested against the 14-23 grounding standard (civilization *actually* resolving mysteries and curing disease), Physarum's existence-proof is **grounding of the mechanism, not of the whole architecture**: it demonstrates that the reallocation-mechanism produces optimal structure, at lab scale, on a single task — not that the whole traversal-architecture yields meaningful understanding. So Physarum imports a real slice of grounding the mycelium's plain foraging lacked — but it sits a tier below humanity's civilization-scale proof. Honest placement: *mechanism-grounding*, not *architecture-grounding*.

## SV5 — The relation-verdict: JOIN as complementary; the convergence becomes a constellation

**A-REL adjudicated on structural grounds: JOIN (complementary), not replace, not separate.**

Steelman **REPLACE** ("Physarum dominates — active computation, external memory, grounding; retire the mycelium"): rejected. Physarum does not dominate; it *trades.* It loses on the **persistent-body-as-record** (it retracts and migrates — its body is transient, where the mycelium *builds and keeps* a body that IS the accumulated trace) and on **multibody richness** (one cell vs many hyphae — the "many fronts" picture the user cared about is richer in the mycelium). A scene that loses two real mappings does not replace the one that holds them.

Steelman **SEPARATE** ("just pick whichever serves; don't carry both"): rejected. There is a structural reason to carry both — they map *different, both-real* properties of the project. The project genuinely *accumulates a persistent corpus* (the mycelium's strength) AND *actively reallocates live, with an external anti-redundancy memory* (Physarum's strength). Dropping either loses a real mapping.

So **JOIN** survives, and "both" is load-bearing by the same standard 15-56 used for the two multibody models: each scene names a real strength the other lacks. **Physarum = the active-computation / re-decider / external-memory sibling; the mycelium = the accumulator / persistent-body-as-record sibling.**

**The convergence-form becomes a small constellation.** The prior form was a PAIR (mycelium + humanity). Physarum joins the **live / intra-system** side, splitting its labour with the mycelium; humanity stays the **cross-session** view. The new form:
- **live / intra-system view:** the mycelium (accumulate + keep) **and** Physarum (compute + re-decide + external memory);
- **cross-session view:** humanity (independent mortal heads bridged by records).

**With a parsimony discipline, committed:** a family with three scenes risks over-population. The constellation is justified *only* because each scene carries a distinct, nameable contribution (mycelium = persistent-accumulation; Physarum = active-reallocation + external-memory + mechanism-grounding; humanity = mortal-independent-heads + architecture-grounding). If any scene stops carrying a distinct load, it should be dropped. Non-sycophancy cuts both ways here: Physarum *earns* its place by two contributions the others lack — and is admitted for that reason, not because it is newest.
