## THE CLAIM AXIS — major claims, table-ready (verbatim-anchored) [core]

- **L1. The unit-of-analysis principle** (HIGH — :66-84, :102-106, :880-888): cognitive processes are delimited by **functional relationships**, not skin/skull or spatial colocation; small sociotechnical systems (ship bridge, cockpit) are proper cognitive units; there is **no inside/outside gulf** to bridge (:870-888); central hypothesis: system properties are accounted for by *"the organization and propagation of constraints"* (:864-869).
- **L2. THE THREE DISTRIBUTIONS** (HIGH — :107-114): across **members of a social group**; across **internal and external structure**; **through TIME** — *"products of earlier events can transform the nature of later events."*
- **L3. Social organization IS cognitive architecture** (HIGH — :134-141): stable information-trajectory patterns reflect architecture; + the three group-cognition questions (:155-160): how group implements mind-like processes; group properties ≠ member properties; participation changes the members.
- **L4. Work materials BECOME cognitive-system elements** (HIGH — :186-192): *"work materials from time to time become elements of the cognitive system itself… part of the distributed system of cognitive control"* (the cane/microscope line).
- **L5. Culture accumulates partial solutions — and blinds** (HIGH — :211-221): *"culture is a process that accumulates partial solutions to frequently encountered problems"*; the residue enables building on others' success; *"culture may also blind us to other ways of thinking."*
- **L6. COGNITIVE ETHNOGRAPHY — event-centered observation** (HIGH — :229-270, :360-371): meanings in negotiated practices; *"we are interested not only in what people know, but in how they go about using what they know"*; **only observation discovers unanticipated use** (*"pilots routinely display the test pattern on the weather radar as a reminder that a final fuel transfer is in progress. There is no method other than observation that can discover these sorts of facts"*); **automated recording of interaction histories** as a rising data source (:259-261); domain expertise non-substitutable (:267-270).
- **L7. Ethnography-less design DESTROYS invisible supports** (HIGH — :283-312): the airspeed-tape case — features *"inadvertently removed"* because designers didn't know pilots use the dial as *"a material anchor for a conceptual space of meaningful airspeeds"* (they rarely read the number); the fix required a method to *"identify the critical features… and a theoretical language… sufficiently abstract that they could be moved to a very different display format."*
- **L8. THE INTEGRATED LOOP** (HIGH — :317-419): theory ↔ ethnography ↔ experiment ↔ design, mutually constraining, in cycles (*"observation to theory to design and back"*); the core principles (:328-335): people establish/coordinate environmental structure; **coordination takes effort**; **people off-load cognitive effort to the environment whenever practical**; social organization provides load-balancing; introducing a new work material is ITSELF an ethnographic experiment (:350-356).
- **L9. Representational-stance SHIFTING + non-referent encoding** (HIGH — :505-574): people shift between representation-as-thing and thing-represented (the fishermen's "flecks"; the flight engineer flicking the gauge); AND people *"manipulate the properties of a representation to encode information that does not pertain to and is not about the thing that the representation represents"* (files left near the trash as reminders; :553-557).
- **L10. HISTORY-ENRICHED DIGITAL OBJECTS** (HIGH — :603-683): physical use-histories are perceptually available (worn door handle) and support expertise (*"the side effects of use often provide resources for the construction of expert performance"*); such supports are *"sometimes actively, but mistakenly, designed out of 'clean' and 'simple' digital work environments"*; digital objects can record + display accrued interaction histories (attribute-mapped scrollbars; menu-brightness by use; source-code copy-history enabling a bug fixed in the copy AND its origin :635-641); data-ownership/privacy note (:670-673).
- **L11. INTELLIGENT USE OF SPACE** (HIGH — :757-839, Kirsh): spatial arrangements **simplify choice** (hiding/highlighting affordances), **simplify perception** (grouping, marking — the newspaper for bicycle parts, spoons by saucepans), **simplify internal computation** (offloading — the spaghetti sort; Tetris players manipulating forms *"to save themselves costly and potentially error-prone computations"*); *"the environment is one's partner or cognitive ally"*; external scaffolding constant and mostly unnoticed.
- *(minor, held: Pad++/multiscale zoomables (:686-756) — a design instance of L9/L10; the emergent-usage-structure point (:743-749) folds under L10.)*
