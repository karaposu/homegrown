### Collapse (a) — the deliberate-design disanalogy's SCOPE

This is exactly the protocol's existing rule (§3: "no property of the source other than its news-value raises a candidate's standing — not the source's fame"). The disanalogy is a **special case of the no-source-authority rule**, not a new killer.
**Confidence:** HIGH.
**Resolution:** the disanalogy is a **justification-filter, not a source-filter**. KILLS: "the web evolved efficiency X, so we should have X" (appeal-to-nature, contentless). SURVIVES to the gate: "the web embodies design-option X; here is why X might help our traversal *on its own merits*" — judged like any crossing, on the two-door news test.
**Now fixed:** every spider→traversal candidate must carry project-side warrant; "the spider does it" is source-support (provenance), never value.
