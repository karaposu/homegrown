## Phase 3 — Ambiguity Collapse

#### Ambiguity A1: What exactly IS the lone traversal loop, in committed terms?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** it's a new system — a meta-runner program with its own architecture, state machine, and components; defining it as "just turns" undersells what must be built.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** walk the description against the committed vocabulary — every element lands on an existing name: the cycle (the meta-loop), the validity condition, the Selector's choosing, *revisit*, the worker runners, the warming doctrine, explfine's outputs. The only state it needs is the turn record (already required by Tier 1) plus a ledger section. The existence proof clinches it: today's session ran the shape with a human keystroke as the continuation rule. What remains is a RUN-SHAPE specification, not a system.

**Confidence:** HIGH. **Resolution:** **expedition(T, G, N≥20)** — N consecutive committed turns over territory T toward goal G, with automatic continuation, the frontier ledger, perception-based complement detection, revisit-with-topology, and budget-primary stop. **No longer allowed:** treating the lone run as a new component or a new architecture.

#### Ambiguity A2: What does "by itself" commit to — and does it skip the gates?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** ≥20 autonomous choices IS Level-3 behavior; the gates demand earned graduation first; the goal as stated is a gate-skip and should wait months.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** the gates bind UNSUPERVISED trust. The committed mode — **supervised-autonomous** — keeps the human present and interruptible, removes only the per-turn approval requirement, and records every choice: which is precisely the runner pattern canon already accepts within inquiries (auto-chaining, informational checkpoints, redirect-anytime), applied one level up. Far from skipping the gates, the run is the gate-data FACTORY: 20 recorded selections with live or retrospective agree/disagree marks ≈ double the Tier-1 fuel in one event.

**Confidence:** HIGH. **Resolution:** "by itself" = **the continuation rule under supervised-autonomous mode** — the spine chooses and continues; checkpoints are informational; the human may interrupt; every choice is recorded for calibration. Fully-absent-human runs come later, calibrated. **No longer allowed:** per-turn approval as a requirement; ungated unsupervised runs.

#### Ambiguity A3: What are the mechanics — and does the persistent spine break the architecture's dial?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** the architecture (hours old) made fresh-seat enumeration the quality ideal and seats optional — a persistent growing spine quietly rebuilds the navigational-session-as-mandatory-seat idea the same day it was rejected.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** the architecture rejected seats as VALIDITY requirements, not as instances — it explicitly allowed seats to be added when a level demands; the expedition's demand is CONTINUITY (the topology and ledger must persist across 20 turns, which files alone serve poorly mid-run). The spine is **the dial made durable**: a standing warmed seat hosting see/choose/record, with per-turn fresh see-agents remaining the optional upgrade. What validity requires — field-before-choice — is session-independent and untouched. Mechanics: **Mode B primary** (spine + one worker-subagent per turn; ~300–350K of 1M for 20 turns), **Mode A fallback** (inline; proven ~7 loops today; comfortable to ~10–15), one **pretest** required for Mode B (a single worker-as-subagent loop, checking the worker's internal sequential depth).

**Confidence:** HIGH for the reconciliation; MED-HIGH for Mode B pending the pretest. **Resolution:** spine + worker-subagents, pretested; inline fallback; the dial intact. **No longer allowed:** calling the spine mandatory; running Mode B's first real expedition without the pretest.

#### Ambiguity A4: How do parking, complement-detection, and return actually work?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** these need designed mechanisms — a frontier database, a similarity-matching algorithm, a scheduling policy — which makes the goal months away.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** at N≈20 with a growing spine, all three collapse into the standing read: the **ledger** is a running list in the turn record (parked area + one line + source); **complement-detection** is perception in a context that holds both the new finding and the ledger (the precise thing the user's 1M-context premise buys — and what today's session demonstrably does when it says "this touches X from earlier"); **return** is the committed *revisit* move chosen while holding the accumulated map. Mechanized versions are L3+ futures, not prerequisites.

**Confidence:** HIGH. **Resolution:** ledger-in-record + perception-based detection at each see-phase + revisit-with-topology. **No longer allowed:** blocking the expedition on frontier algorithms.

#### Ambiguity A5: When does the run stop?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** "at least 20" is a vanity number; runs should stop exactly when signals say — anything else is ritual.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** the signals are placeholders BY DOCTRINE — making them the primary stop rule invents the formula canon forbids; the budget is the only decidable-today instrument, and the fuzziness doctrine's own external support (multi-objective non-stationary problems lack clean stopping criteria) says budget-primary is the honest form. Signals remain advisory and may legitimately end a run early — recorded as success-with-reason.

**Confidence:** HIGH. **Resolution:** **budget-primary (N=20 floor for the first expedition's ambition), signals-advisory** (goal-satisfied at declared resolution; ledger empty; dry-spell of k low-novelty turns) — all placeholder-marked. **No longer allowed:** formula-pretending stop rules; treating an early legitimate stop as failure.

#### Ambiguity A6: The name?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** keep the user's own coinage — "lone traversal loop" is his word for his goal; renaming it is the assistant overriding the owner.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** the coinage was offered tentatively WITH a request for a better name in the same breath; and "loop" now collides with vocabulary committed hours ago (the meta-loop IS the cycle; this thing is a bounded RUN of that cycle, not a loop) — keeping it would re-tangle the names the same day they were untangled. **Confidence:** MED-HIGH (veto standing — naming is the user's seat). **Resolution:** **the Expedition** — expedition(T, G, N): an event-noun (anti-regrowth-safe), metaphor that pays (base camps = parked frontiers; the growing map; leave-and-return), natural usage ("a 20-turn expedition over T"). Referent settled: the OPERATION (explore-define stays explfine; "thinking space" stays). **No longer allowed:** "loop" in the operation's name.
