## Phase 2 — Generate (7 mechanisms × 3)

### 1. Lens Shifting (Framer)

- **1G — mission-control lens.** The per-turn checkpoint as telemetry block: `Turn 12/20 — chose: revisit(parked-area-3) — why: T11's finding supplies the missing mechanism — ledger: +1 parked, −1 revisited (4 open) — moves so far: deepen×5 widen×3 revisit×2 different-approach×1 — budget: 8 remaining.` Concrete, glanceable, scroll-friendly.
- **1F — alpine lens (focused, light touch).** The walkthrough's narrative texture: warming = base camp; parked frontiers = supply caches; the budget = weather window. Kept to seasoning — the expedition image is already the chosen one.
- **1C — the saboteur lens (contrarian).** Where does a 20-turn unattended run SILENTLY fail? Candidate: **selection monoculture** — by turn ~8 the spine rubber-stamps one move type (all go-deeper, no widening), and nobody notices until the report. Mitigation adopted: the checkpoint's **move-distribution line** (the option-vocabulary tally so far) — one line of diversity telemetry that makes monoculture visible by turn 5, not turn 20.

### 2. Combination (Generator)

- **2G — checkpoint × the Allocation Rule's cadence.** Every 5th checkpoint is EXTENDED: the dashboard glance + a ledger review. The allocation rule's "consult every ~5 inquiries" maps one-to-one onto the run — no new rhythm invented.
- **2F — the run's end × explfine's outputs.** The expedition ends by compiling **the expedition report**: the map (the grown `_route.md`-class topology) + the findings index + the frontier (the ledger's surviving entries) + a telemetry annex (per-turn stats). The report IS explfine's output bundle plus instrumentation — and a structural echo worth stating: **turn : inquiry :: expedition report : finding** (the run-level CONCLUDE analog).
- **2C — the pretest × the joy-exception's economics.** The Mode-B pretest (one worker-as-subagent loop) runs on a REAL small question, so even the test produces a usable finding — no throwaway work.

### 3. Inversion (Framer; depth-checked)

- **3G — the rule's logical form (on the continuation rule).** "Continues automatically" → invert: stop-by-default (today's behavior) vs continue-by-default. The breakthrough is precisely the flip to **continue-unless**: *after a worker's CONCLUDE — if no stop-condition fires and budget remains, the spine proceeds to the next see-phase.* Adopted as the rule's exact statement; the inversion supplies the form.
- **3F — the first-territory inversion (focused).** "Own-frontier is the forgiving rehearsal" → invert: own-frontier is the HARDER first test (self-reference; the spine's priors pre-bias selection; complement-detection untested fairly on territory it already knows) — while fresh territory compounds unknowns (new mode + new ground). Resolution — ADOPTED as a structural improvement: **insert a SHAKEDOWN expedition** — N=5–8 turns on a small fresh territory — before the full N≥20 run. The shakedown tests the mode's mechanics cheaply; the full expedition then runs on the real target with practiced machinery. (Enrichment of sensemaking's ordering — the pretest expands into pretest + shakedown, within its de-risking intent; flagged for Critique.)
- **3C — the absent-human inversion (contrarian).** "Supervised" → invert: run it overnight, human absent. What breaks: nothing mechanically (checkpoints are records; interruptibility simply goes unused) — but the live agree/disagree calibration marks are lost, degrading the run's gate-fuel quality to retro-marking. Adopted as a note: **presence is for calibration value, not safety** — first expeditions prefer a present (even if passive) human; absent-human runs are tolerable later.

### 4. Constraint Manipulation (Framer; both directions)

- **4-ADD (generic).** **Checkpoint ≤ 6 lines** — the throughput inversion's lesson applied: ceremony that grows per-turn cost starves the run.
- **4-ADD (focused).** **The expedition procedure note fits ONE page** — it is a runner-level addendum (the continuation rule + checkpoint format + stop conditions + abort/resume), NOT a new architecture document.
- **4-REMOVE (generic).** Remove the ledger (let frontiers stay scattered in findings)? → complement-detection loses its read-target; leave-and-return degrades to luck. Fails — the ledger is load-bearing.
- **4-REMOVE (contrarian).** Remove the budget (run until signals say stop)? → placeholder signals as the sole stop = the fuzziness doctrine violated from the other side (an unbounded run on advisory instruments). Fails — budget-primary confirmed.

### 5. Absence Recognition (Generator; both levels, bidirectional)

- **Patch-level — two absences:** (i) **abort/resume** unstated: expeditions are interruptible BY CONSTRUCTION (every turn commits to files) — abort = stop + compile the report-so-far; resume = warm a fresh spine from the records (turn log + map + ledger ARE the resume state). One paragraph, adopted. (ii) **The worklist during a run:** the expedition IS the climb-default in motion; the allocation tripwires stay live mid-run (a consumer-failure = fix-grade pause, then resume) — one line, adopted.
- **Redesign-level.** The distance stated honestly: steps 0–4 ≈ 2 working sessions → shakedown; full expedition ≈ 3–4 sessions from today. The build-list carries the total.
- **Bidirectional (already-present).** The run-report shape half-exists: CONCLUDE compiles inquiry-level; the expedition report is its run-level analog — symmetry stated (2F), no new invention claimed.

### 6. Domain Transfer (Generator; native source included)

- **6-native (software).** **The soak test:** the expedition is also the system's first endurance run — does per-loop quality HOLD across 20 cycles? Adopted as the telemetry annex's degradation-watch: per-turn PROCEED-rates and structural-check passes, so drift is visible in the report (and mid-run at extended checkpoints).
- **6-different (exploration history).** Depots, survey legs, the return-leg discipline — the expedition image's native vocabulary; already the chosen frame (seasoning only).
- **6-different (gaming — roguelike runs).** Run-based progress with persistent meta-progression — REJECTED (analogy-inflation guard; the expedition image already carries the event-ness).

### 7. Extrapolation (Generator)

- **7G — expeditions as the L3 work-unit.** Once practiced, the project's rhythm becomes: expedition → report → dashboard consultation → next expedition. The run-shape scales without redesign — the same definition at every maturity.
- **7F — L2 arrives DURING practice.** Two-three expeditions' recorded selections (with live marks) ARE the Selector's calibration set — the L2 graduation emerges from expedition practice rather than as separate work. Adopted into the importance argument.
- **7C — the failure path.** If the shakedown fails (worker-subagent depth collapses; spine drifts): fall back to Mode A inline at N≤12, split across sittings — the goal survives at reduced N while Mode B is repaired. And a failed expedition still yields its records (turns, marks, report-so-far) — the calibration data survives the disappointment. Adopted as the risk note.
