## Finding

### 1. The law

The traverse loop is a portable instrument: its protocols are user-scoped and its runs demonstrably span projects. A portable instrument's specs may assume what the instrument itself defines — the inquiry-folder convention, its canonical files, the frontmatter it writes — because those travel with it. They may never assume another system's artifacts, because those don't. `docs/canon` is this project's; the tag design anchored to it would have broken (or silently no-op'd) everywhere else. The write side is therefore rebuilt with zero external references, and the constraint is stated as a standing rule candidate for the user's adoption — with the two existing debts in the current specs (the conclude protocol's conditional seeds-index line; its cognitive_harness pointers) named as eventual cleanup targets, and the seeds line's conditional wording ("when the inquiry ran the seed-harvester protocol") noted as the graceful-absence shape any project-flavored clause must use.

### 2. The portable write core (the replacement step, verbatim)

> **Tags (at CONCLUDE, after compiling the finding body):** write up to **7 freestyle `tags:`** into the finding's frontmatter, lowercase-kebab, as an **inline list** — `tags: [time-road, decision-slot]` — the concepts this finding ACTUALLY ENGAGES, in the dive's own words. Before writing, two glances, both inside the loop's own record: **(1)** the warm pass's anchor vocabulary (`articulate_warm.md` is among the outputs being compiled — its committed context-need names the dive's territory); **(2)** the record's existing tags — e.g. `grep -h '^tags:' devdocs/inquiries/*/finding.md | tr -d '[]' | tr ',' '\n' | sort -u` — **reuse an existing form when it honestly fits** (recall-then-verify, with the record itself as the verify-target); when nothing fits, coin freely. **Empty record → skip the glance and coin freely** (day one is freestyle by design). Tags locate; they never grade. Unclear → fewer or none. *This step references nothing outside the loop's own outputs.*

Why CONCLUDE and not warm: the carrier evidence, now measured twice. Concepts like "the second parse" and "the nursery" — the findings' centerpieces — appear zero times in their dives' warm files and are born at innovation/critique; warm's genuine richness (the territory) reaches the tags anyway because CONCLUDE reads the warm artifact by construction. The day-one walkthrough is the portability acceptance test: a brand-new project adopts the loop; the first dive's glance greps an empty record and skips; freestyle tags flow; the second dive sees the first's tags; the vocabulary grows with the record — no canon, no atlas, no setup, nothing missing.

### 3. This project's convergence layer (each project builds its own)

Behind the existing gates, in escalating cost: **normalization** (rides the offered finding-section parse — lowercase, kebab, conservative plural-fold); **the tag map** (a small project-side file mapping tags to canon areas, consulted by the adapter, tuned occasionally when the visible `unmappedTags` counter accumulates — demand-pulled and gracefully absent, which is what distinguishes it from the habit-fed records that die here); **the gated LLM pass, last** (generate-time batch; a reviewable tag→group mapping artifact as its only output; runs only on measured synonym-shaped residue with the user's go). The overlay lens groups by mapped-area where the map covers and by raw normalized tag where it doesn't; its ≥15-tagged-folders condition survives, counting freestyle tags. Other projects owe none of this — the tag field is the loop's interface; grouping is each host's implementation.

### 4. What this supersedes, and what it keeps

Superseded: the canon-anchored write (the `canon_areas:` field as a loop requirement; the vocabulary glance; the canon-vocabulary policy line; the pending policy blessing — void, its object dissolved). Kept and re-confirmed: the carrier (finding frontmatter at CONCLUDE — the argument never rested on canon); the honesty rules; the kills. Relocated: the canon join and its filter (the 06-55 bug-fixes live on there); the nursery (project-side watch: recurring tags surface as canonization candidates); the counters (`unknownCanonAreas` → `unmappedTags`). Untouched: C2, the second parse (one field rename), the atlas-domain rule (the loop no longer reads canon at all — the rule now governs only project-side consumers).
