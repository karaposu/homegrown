# Decomposition — enrichment mechanism (similarity-crossing) + traverse-loop-as-enricher

## Step 1 — Coupling Topology

The stabilized whole (SV6): *enrichment IS the harvest's own §2 source×target crossing, run with aspect-depth on a thin source; it unifies the 16-41 poles, bounded-corrects the finding, and needs a scaffolding-level decision bounded by the provenance floor + siting.* Coupling clusters:

- **The mechanism** (crossing = §2 · source-list from walking the phenomenon · target-list from canon · sensemaking-adjudicated match · aspect-depth) — tightly coupled internally (all facets of one operation), the foundation everything else consumes.
- **The correction** (what the mechanism does to the 16-41 finding) — depends on the mechanism's unification result; otherwise independent.
- **The architecture** (how much scaffolding a thin source needs) — depends on the mechanism-is-the-crossing fact; otherwise independent of the correction.
- **The guards** (provenance floor on every candidate + siting) — bind the architecture pick; distinct safety-anchor.
- **The application** (revise the finding/draft?) — consumes the correction + architecture + guards.

Coupling gradient: P1↔{P2,P3} strong (they consume P1's result); P2↔P3 weak (independent); P4↔P3 strong-but-distinct-anchor (binding conditions); P5↔{P2,P3,P4} strong (consumes all three).
