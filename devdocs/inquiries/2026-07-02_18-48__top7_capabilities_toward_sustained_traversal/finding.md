## Finding

### 1. Context: what kind of stuck this is

The project's era-goal is **SUSTRALL** — the SUStained TRAversal Loop of Loops: worker loop-runners (the hands), a fresh isolated navigational session running routelister over finished work (the eyes), and an orchestrator holding traversal memory (the will), revolving as a controlled whirl over thinking space. The loop already turns today at autonomy Level 0 — with **human** organs: the user reads the fields, selects, dispatches, remembers, and stops. SUSTRALL is not a thing to invent; it is the earned, per-axis transfer of those organs to the system.

That framing matters for the stuckness: the last breakthrough (routelister + its traverse integration) was an ORGAN-shaped invention, so "waiting for the next breakthrough" naturally feels like waiting for the next organ-shaped idea. But the canon anticipated this exact feeling — the bootstrap jump is "**a staircase wearing the name of a leap**." The next progress is step-shaped, and the first step is explicitly available: the north star's own next-step section says *"take the first recorded SUSTRALL turn… **No new build required**."*

### 2. The diagnosis — a starved default with an unpowered trigger

SUSTRALL's Allocation Rule (adopted June 10) says: **the default session is climbing** the tier path; loop-work (fixing/improving the worker disciplines) is demand-driven via tripwires; joy-exceptions are allowed, costed, and capped. Judged by that rule, the recent weeks were all legitimate classes — demand-driven checks (the routelister audits), costed joy-exceptions (the paradigm/movement theory chain, which upgraded canon). And in the 22 days since canonization, the default itself ran **zero executed climb-steps**: no memory artifact, no isolated session, no recorded turn.

Why does a default starve while exceptions thrive? The critique pass sharpened this into the finding's most useful line: the rule DOES have a trigger for climbing — the consultation cadence, "re-read the signals after every ~5 completed inquiries." But that trigger is **count-shaped**: someone or something must count completed inquiries. Nothing does — because counting-across-inquiries is exactly the between-inquiry attention layer that traversal memory would provide and that doesn't exist yet. Tripwires and joy are **event-shaped** — they fire themselves. So the default's trigger is real but unpowered, **starved by the very gap it is meant to close**. That is a bootstrap knot, and it has two cuts: an external trigger (put the cadence on a calendar) and the real one — take the first turn, so something finally exists that counts.

### 3. The ranking criterion — and how to read the list

**Ranked by breakthrough-enabling power toward long multi-loop traversal, under the canon's own dependency gates.** Three reading notes:

- **The grain is mixed on purpose.** The canon's own critical path mixes an artifact, a habit, a count, a graduation, a spec, and a build — forcing one grain would drop real items. Each item carries its shape-label.
- **Rank is not a calendar.** Ranks are unlock-order under gates. In practice: item 2 is item 3's practice-prerequisite; items 3, 4, and 5 interleave in time (turns accumulate while the spec authors; the Selector's gate arrives on the walk's data); item 6 is dependency-free and buildable in any idle session.
- **The bands are this finding's presentation** (not canon tiers — canon places the stop-judgment spec at Tier 3, for instance). They group by what the items DO: the Manual Era makes the paused goal real by hand; the Autonomy Climb transfers it.

### 4. The Seven

**— The Manual Era (make long traversal real, now) —**

**1. The First Turn** *(organ + integration — the routelister-shaped one)*
Create the first traversal-memory artifact ever — the cross-inquiry choice-record, in the shape the June-22 finding already settled: **one line per turn, `chose <route> · because <one line> · led-to → <pointer>`** — and adopt the turn-invariant (*a cycle counts as a SUSTRALL turn when its selection-rationale is recorded*). Step zero, hard-ordered: the indicator pre-registration file must exist first (§6). **Unlocks:** everything downstream — the Selector's calibration data, the telemetry the self-improvement metrics starve for, the count that powers the Allocation Rule's own trigger, and the system-detected improvement-trigger fraction finally leaving ≈0%. The fatigue objection is answered by design: the record is one line, not a report.

**2. Clean Eyes** *(habit — completes the Seeing cargo from ~80%)*
Run the navigational session as SUSTRALL defines it: a **fresh, context-isolated session, warmed on the terrain** (orientation → trajectory → recent work → target), running routelister over the finished work — instead of the worker session writing its own exhaust map, as every traverse does today. The organ itself is validated (this morning's 20-map audit); only the isolation habit has never been practiced once. **Unlocks:** uncontaminated route-fields — the worker's own biases stop pre-selecting the options — and the Navigator role existing in reality rather than on paper.

**3. The Long Walk** *(practice-milestone — the canon's ~10-turns step, named here and given a territory discipline)*
One territory, five to ten revolutions of probe → see → decide → dispatch → remember, with a **hard budget cap from day one**. This is normal inquiry work done in chain form with the two new habits — not extra work. One of its turns can deliberately be the first MTTP pattern invocation ever (the catalog has ready-made candidates). **Unlocks:** the ~10 recorded turns the Selector gate wants; the "Sustained" quality bar tested against reality (if the chain grinds, that is load-bearing data collectible no other way); and the direct answer to the question's pain — **this item IS long multi-loop traversal, at Level 0/1.**

**4. Knowing When to Stop** *(spec-build — placeholders + cap, never the formula)*
Author `devdocs/spec/meaningful_traversal.md` v1: the operational placeholder stop-signals (coverage, convergence, productivity, directedness, depth — plus the rung-based candidate the movement-vocabulary work already delivered to the canon note) and the budget cap formalized. The cap itself rides item 3 immediately; this item is the signals half, best authored once a few real turns exist to inform it. **Unlocks:** chains that end on judgment instead of fatigue — the difference between a sustained whirl and forever-or-arbitrary. The canon's own note: this substrate "now has a consumer waiting."

**— The Autonomy Climb (make it self-turning) —**

**5. The Selector's Apprenticeship** *(transfer — the first real autonomy handover)*
The system proposes the next route from each map; the human approves or overrides; every agreement/disagreement is recorded. Gate (shape committed, number placeholder): agreement reliably high over the recorded pairs — exactly the data item 3 generates. Two canon riders travel with it: the Tier-2 chore of refreshing the dormant meta-loop skeleton (gate: ≥3 recorded turns), and the selector-side memory schema, which by canon "advances with its consumer." **Unlocks:** Level 2 on the Selecting axis — the orchestrator's first system-held seat, earned the way the ladder demands.

**6. The Safety Floor** *(small build — designed, pending; dependency-free)*
Per-spec manifests + the advisory `tools/structural_check.sh` — the first Primitive-RC instance (the deterministic layer of the quality-awareness architecture), whose design is complete per canon. Every pipeline run today checks structure by hand — this very session included. **Unlocks:** mechanical structural safety on every run, and the trust-substrate automation needs before anything runs unattended. Buildable in any idle session without touching the list's order.

**7. The Carrier** *(organ + integration — the second routelister-shaped one; the Level-3 threshold)*
The mechanism that spawns and sequences sessions without a human typing each command — sequential chains first, multihead later. Deliberately LAST by its own canon gate: its design decision is gated on the Level-2 calibration data items 3 and 5 produce, because automating dispatch before the system can see, select, and remember is **automating blindness**. The orchestrator-decision-rules rider derives from the recorded choices here. **Unlocks:** the bootstrap boundary actually moving — the system turning the whirl within familiar territory while the human seeds and supervises.

## Reasoning

**Why this list over alternatives.** The five candidate readings from articulation (gap-driven, blocker-chain, breakthrough-pattern, tier-driven, portfolio) CONVERGED rather than competed: the canon docs already contain both the gap-list and its ordering (the minimum critical path), the gates form a real dependency graph (blocker-chain), the shape-labels expose the breakthrough-pattern filter, and the bands display the portfolio spread. The list is the docs' own answer, ranked — the inquiry's added value is the delta-check (what recent findings already de-risked), the two named interpolations (the Long Walk's territory discipline; the bands), and the diagnosis.

**Significant kills and refinements:**
- **"The carrier is the real blocker — automate first"** (steelmanned twice): killed on the canon's self-provisioning dependency — the Selector calibrates against recorded rationales and the carrier's design gate is Level-2 data; automating dispatch before see/select/remember exist is automating blindness. Its true kernel survives as item 1's one-line cheapness constraint and item 3's cap.
- **"The default has no trigger"** (this finding's own earlier line): killed at the letter by critique — the consultation cadence EXISTS. Replaced by the sharper truth: the trigger is count-shaped and unpowered because nothing counts between inquiries. The finding got better by losing the argument.
- **"Seven is padding — the honest list is three"** (memory, chain, stop): killed on the goal's own wording — three serves "long manual traversal"; the seven serve long traversal that becomes SUSTRALL (the eyes complete a cargo; the Selector is the first transfer; the floor is the trust-substrate; the carrier is the threshold). Its kernel became the band structure.
- **"Say 'we procrastinated with theory'"**: killed on evidence — the theory chain produced canon upgrades and a validated audit in allowed classes; "procrastinated" asserts avoidance the record doesn't show, and the mechanism-line (the unpowered trigger) is both truer and more actionable than the blame-line.
- **"Drop the pre-registration from the first move"**: killed on timestamp-physics — dropping doesn't defer it, it destroys it permanently.
- **"Quality floor earlier — cheapest wins"**: killed on the criterion (unlock-power, not annoyance-relief); its kernel became the rank-is-not-a-calendar note.

**Canon-consistency note** (the branch flagged the docs' commitments as standing bounds): the seven honor the fuzziness boundary (item 4 is placeholders + cap, never the formula), the June-22 choice-record shape (item 1 adopts it verbatim), the capability-not-phenomenology test (nothing here claims indicators — item 1's step-zero exists precisely so indicator claims stay testable), and SUSTRALL's payoff restraint (explfine stays the test, not a promise). Gate NUMBERS (~10 turns, ~80% agreement) are placeholders by the docs' own statement.

**Honesty note on grounding:** the two canon docs were read at source; the half-baked references they summarize (the autonomy ladder's gate designs, /intuit's phases, the 15 self-improvement questions) were consumed via the canon's own summaries — sufficient for a top-7 ranking; a per-gate design inquiry should read them at source.
