## (c) Phase 2–3 — Adversarial evaluation + verdicts

### C1 — The valve, prosecuted hardest at the Sweeper
**Prosecution:** a session that READS findings and writes "consider consolidating X" is one temptation away from running the consolidation "while it's here."
**Defense/Collision:** the recipe must foreclose in words, not vibes; the Landing Check verified separately (scripts write lines — no capability to act).
**Verdict: REFINE → applied (R1):** the Sweeper recipe gains the terminal line — *"the sweep session ENDS at note-writing: it never opens an inquiry, runs a ceremony, generates a view, or edits any protocol; its last act is the note."*

### C2 — Reactive-only, per archetype
**Prosecution:** anything predictive hiding?
**Defense/Collision:** Sentinel — countable drift over existing artifacts ✓; Landing Check — fires on an arrival that EXISTS ✓; Sweeper — collisions read landed findings; relitigation compares an EXISTING new inquiry to RECORDED kills ✓; Deep Auditor — rot measured over history ✓. No option infers a future task.
**Verdict: SURVIVE.**

### C3 — Pre-registration at the letter: the placement bug (the material catch)
**Prosecution:** alert-notes at `devdocs/views/_alerts/` sit INSIDE a tree the constitution says is never searched for sources — the warm-up must READ alerts while other rules teach never-read-views-as-source; a contradiction planted in a path.
**Defense/Collision:** the never-search rule is scoped to the GENERATOR by its wording, but the placement invites exactly the confusion the rule exists to prevent — and costs nothing to fix.
**Verdict: REFINE → applied (R2):** alerts move to their own home — **`devdocs/alerts/<date>.md`** — with the view-CLASS constitution applied (dated · pointed · quoted-basis · subordinate · never-cite · regenerate-not-edit) WITHOUT living inside the views tree. The wall-sentence unchanged.

### C4 — The recency-acknowledgment hole
**Prosecution:** "alerts newer than the previous session" — which session? Multiple per day; parallel sessions; the marker either derives from something real or a hidden state-file appears.
**Defense/Collision:** the marker dissolves into a WINDOW: alerts are dated files — the warm-up reads alerts from the last K days (default 7, aligned with the digest's recency shape); no marker, no state; grave items never rely on the window (the device grain exists for them).
**Verdict: REFINE → applied (R3):** recency-acknowledgment becomes **window-acknowledgment** (read `devdocs/alerts/` newer than K days; K a protocol constant); the which-session question never arises.

### C5 — The threshold's setter
**Prosecution:** a conditional line's K is a judgment; a watcher tuning its own K is a self-modifying trigger — valve-adjacent.
**Verdict: REFINE → applied (R4):** the walls gain one line — *"thresholds are protocol constants: set at build, changed by the user, never adjusted by a watcher about itself."*

### C6 — The fatigue QUANTITY bound
**Prosecution:** four stacked archetypes bound quality (precision rules) but nothing bounds VOLUME — an active day could emit unbounded notes + lines + banners.
**Verdict: REFINE → applied (R5):** the fatigue wall gains the volume rule — *"an active day's watchers emit at most ~3 alert-notes; overflow compresses into ONE summary-note ('+N more, listed inside'); header lines stay ≤5; the device fires at most once per grave."*
