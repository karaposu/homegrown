## Phase 2 — Generate (principal content + mandated inversions)

### (a) The identity statement (final form) + inversion

**Principal:**
> *The paradigm-sweeper makes **chart acquisition** runnable — the cartographic act the Movement Frame already defines ("no position changes; the map of the ways comes into existence"), operationalized as a skill. Given a stuck or unsatisfying topic, it recasts the topic as a practice ("whose practice, doing what?"), then perceives and maps that practice's paradigm-families — coherent, generative bundles of commitments, each passing the membership test — as a persistent map of 5–9 basic-level entries, each a loop-ready seed. It enumerates; it never selects.*

**Inversion (mandated: "chart acquisition is a metaphor — ground it or drop it"):** the term is not free metaphor — it is the committed vocabulary of the Movement Frame finding (cartographic acts are a defined category: acquire / extend / replace), now in canon-adjacent text. The identity sentence GROUNDS it inline (the operational unpacking rides in the same sentence: recast → map families → seed entries → never select). Dropping the term would orphan the design from the theory that licenses it. *(Survives with the grounding clause built in.)*

### (b) The should-be + the failure-mode catalog + inversion

**Process spine (inherited verbatim-from-findings, cited):** probes 0–5 — probe 0 scope-recast · probe 1 schools · probe 2 facet-walk (5+2 kit as engine; heuristic prior, never checklist) · probe 3 defaults/non-defaults · probe 4 coherent combinations {established | frontier — flagged and KEPT} · probe 5 fashion-loosening. Membership test as the family-filter. Depth is consumer-dialed in one line: the full pipeline is the default for any PERSISTED map; a quick in-session glance may stop early but may not call its output a sweep-map.

**Inversion (mandated: "six probes is ceremony — ship probe-1-only"):** the committed finding answers at the letter: *"Probe 1 alone reproduces the MODAL map — probes 2–5 exist for the regions probe 1 cannot see."* The tool's entire purpose is the non-modal regions (the founding case: the modal naming map was exactly what failed; the winning family lived off it). Probe-1-only rebuilds the bias the tool exists to break. *(Rejected; residue kept: the one-line depth-dial above — no mode machinery in v0.)*

**The failure-mode catalog (draft, house style — LAYER 1 operational / LAYER 2 identity-eroding):**

*LAYER 1 — operational:*
1. **Modal-Map-Only** — the sweep stops at probe 1; the map lists the schools anyone would name; the non-modal regions (the tool's purpose) are absent. *Corrective:* probes 2–4 are mandatory for a persisted map; telemetry reports the count of families probe 1 alone would not have produced.
2. **Checklist-Ossification** — the facet kit walked as boxes to fill; forced axes; empty discriminations. *Corrective:* the kit is a prior — an axis earns its place only by discriminating real variants; stretch-fits are reported, not hidden (the kit's own 09-30 concession is the model).
3. **Family-Hallucination** — entries that fail the membership test: options, styles, single moves, or lenses dressed as paradigms. *Corrective:* run the membership test per entry; failures demote to axis-values on the chart or drop — never pad the map.
4. **Anchoring-Dive** — judging or diving the first attractive family before the map completes, or judging any family before each has its batch. *Corrective:* the map completes before any dive; a fixed small batch per family before judging any (the founding finding's guard).
5. **Scope-Miss** — probe 0 skipped; an object-domain swept instead of the practice. *Corrective:* probe 0 is mandatory; the recast is recorded in the artifact header.
6. **Grain-Drift** — entries at the wrong grain: super-paradigm blobs or sub-variant shards instead of the 5–9 basic-level families. *Corrective:* the basic-level check — each entry must support a DISTINCT dive, and merging any two must lose a live distinction.

*LAYER 2 — identity-eroding:*
7. **Selection-Creep** — the map crowns a winner: ranks families, recommends one, or "just starts" the best dive. *Corrective:* enumerate-don't-decide; selection belongs to the consumer AFTER dives; attributive notes only (the routelister precedent, with its audit-proven teeth).
8. **Generation-Creep** — the sweeper starts solving inside entries: worked candidates, designs, or solutions appear in the map. *Corrective:* entries carry commitments, exemplars, and axis-positions — never worked solutions; the seed block points forward to the dive that will generate.
9. **Coverage-Theater** — the map presented as exhaustive. *Corrective:* "a coverage discipline, not a coverage proof" (founding finding, verbatim) — the artifact carries a frontier/bounds note; telemetry states what loosening (probe 5) did and didn't run.

**Principal (compact — capped at ~6 lines; divergence-pinning, not description):**


**Inversion (mandated: "seed blocks are bureaucracy — a bare family name + exemplar seeds fine"):** a bare name seeds the loop with the MODAL reading of that family — the downstream articulation re-derives the commitments from its priors, and the dive regresses to default. The block's fields are exactly the divergence-pins: the bundle fixes the non-default choices; axis-positions block regression; the goal-echo keeps line-of-sight; the tag sets novelty expectations. What the steelman wins: the CAP (≈6 lines — anything more starts doing the loop's own articulation work). *(Rejected; cap kept.)*

### (d) The map artifact sketch + the dive-done column + inversion

**Principal (borrow the house skeleton; own the columns):**
- **Header:** the practice recast (probe 0's output) · goal · dial (in-session / persisted) · date.
- **Index table:** `# · Family · one-line bundle · {est|frontier} · exemplar · dived ✓` — the **✓ is the dive-done consumer column**, inherited exactly from the routelister precedent: authored EMPTY by the sweeper, never read by it; filled by the consumer as dives complete (annotations legal — today's audit validated the annotated-tick pattern in the wild). It is the anchoring guard's operational display: one glance shows which families still lack their batch.
- **Per-entry records:** the commitment-bundle · exemplar-or-frontier grounds · axis-positions · the seed block (copy-pasteable).
- **Excluded (with reasons):** membership-test failures, demoted to axis-values or dropped — the anti-hallucination trail.
- **Telemetry:** probes run · families-beyond-probe-1 count · stretch-fits · demotions · bounds/frontier note · self-assessment (PROCEED/FLAG).

**Inversion (mandated: "no new artifact form — emit a routelister-style map"):** half right, absorbed: the SKELETON (index + records + excluded + telemetry + consumer-✓) is routelister's proven grammar, borrowed deliberately. The COLUMNS cannot be routelister's — routes and families are different units with different type systems (engagement verbs and Essentiality are route-typed; bundles, exemplars, and {est|frontier} are family-typed). Emitting families AS routes would misfile the unit — the exact wrong-grain failure both disciplines catalog. *(Absorbed as borrow-skeleton-own-columns.)*

### (e) The NOT-list (final phrasing)

1. **Not a pipeline stage** — traverse stays A→Su→S→D→I→C→R; the sweeper is invoked on demand, upstream.
2. **Not a selector** — it never ranks, recommends, or picks the winning family; cross-family selection belongs to the consumer, after dives. *(The identity wall.)*
3. **Not a candidate-generator** — it maps families; the DIVES generate. A worked solution inside the map is a failure mode, not a feature.
4. **Not the Paradigm-Sweep MTTP** — the catalog owns the S3 multi-loop orchestration; this skill is the S2 organ that orchestration would invoke.
5. **Not an innovate replacement** — innovate transforms seeds and shifts frames; the sweeper hands it (or traverse) the chart. The S1 seed-time hook remains innovate's own pending patch, untouched.
6. **Not a paradigm-shift engine** — it ACQUIRES charts; it neither replaces the reigning chart nor argues anyone off it (acquisition ≠ replacement).
7. **Not generic enumeration** — it does not re-implement surfacing's possibility case or routelister's mechanics; it operationalizes the paradigm-instruments and nothing else. *(The carried ban from the revised kill.)*
8. **Not a finished theory** — v0-provisional: its instruments carry 09-30's validation ceiling (single-mind runs; blind-topic test pending); the first invocation revises the spec.

### (f) The staging + the reusable pattern + inversion

**Staging:** (1) the pre-registration file → (2) the First Turn → (3) **build on go** (structural+process by convention; this finding's sketches make the build assembly-grade) → (4) **first invocation = the traversal-memory sweep, run as the Long Walk** — one territory, N family-dives, hard cap, every dive a recorded turn (dives-as-turns).

**The pattern, stated once for reuse:**
> **Sweep → Seed → Loop:** when a topic is stuck or its solutions feel forced — sweep the practice (acquire the chart); take seeds from the map (the consumer selects; the anchoring guard applies); run one loop per seed (traverse at full depth, or innovate for lighter dives); select across families only with dives in hand. The sweeper makes family-jumps cheap; the loops make them deep.

**Inversion (mandated: "build the skill NOW — splitting to another session is process theater"):** what follows from building now: the spec gets authored BEFORE this inquiry's own critique has tested the design — any critique-forced revision becomes spec churn (write-then-rewrite in one day); and the new-skill-on-go convention exists precisely so artifact-creation happens from a settled finding, not mid-pipeline. What the steelman wins: the build must be CHEAP — this innovation's sketches (catalog, seed block, artifact skeleton, NOT-list) are written at assembly grade so the build session is transcription+convention, under an hour. *(Rejected; the low-latency residue kept.)*
