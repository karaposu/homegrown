## Piece-Level Inversions (REQUIRED — the two load-bearing pieces)

### Q3 Inversion (claim axis) — "the availability illusion is a real import"

**Reverse:** *"Cold-navigation already fully covers this. The steering canon names the failure of under-informed sessions and prescribes warming. 'The availability illusion' is just a cuter label for cold-navigation — decorative, not a real import."*

**What follows if the reversal held:** naming it would add nothing; we'd have a synonym for cold-navigation.

**Re-defeat (the mechanism-vs-state distinction, verified against the canon):**
1. **The canon names the STATE and the FIX, not the WHY.** Verbatim (steering canon, lines 161–163): cold-navigation is *"The navigation session reads the latest finding, understands the local words, but does not understand the project well enough to know what move matters."* That describes an *under-warmed session* (a state) and implies the fix (*warm more*). It does **not** name *why a session under-warms* — because looking-up feels like having, so it never registers the need. The illusion names that cause. Cause ≠ symptom.
2. **The principle generalizes beyond the navigation session.** Cold-navigation is scoped to the navigation session. Reach ≠ grasp applies to *any* traverse (a worker "has" all canon available but operates only on loaded context) and to memory design at large (don't design assuming "it's lookup-able, so it's fine"). The canon states the narrow case; the illusion states the general law.

Naming the mechanism behind an existing symptom, and generalizing it past the one place the canon states it, is a real RESOLVES-a-confusion import — not a relabel. **Inversion tested, rejected; the claim survives, precisely bounded (mechanism-behind, not new-lack).**

### Q1 Inversion (frame axis) — "it's a cluster, not a peak"

**Reverse:** *"There IS a peak. The availability illusion is consolidation-scale — it reframes the entire memory design as defense-against-the-gap. Calling it 'one of a cluster' undersells it; this is the second breakthrough."*

**What follows if the reversal held:** the finding should headline the illusion as a breakthrough equal to consolidation.

**Re-defeat (warn-vs-build):** consolidation was *generative* — it unified five previously-scattered fixes into one process and located a missing lifecycle stage; it tells you what to *build*. The availability illusion is *cautionary* — it names a trap and a law (reach ≠ grasp); it tells you what to *respect*, not what to build. A caution, however sharp, is not a generative stage. Even the emergent it seeds (E1: our memory machinery is defense-against-the-gap) is a *unifying observation*, not a new buildable concept. So it is the brightest member of a cluster, not a peak. **Inversion tested, rejected; the cluster-not-peak frame survives — which is the finding's core honesty.**

*(Note: the Q1 inversion is the anti-inflation guard. That it was generated, tested, and rejected is exactly what licenses the finding to claim "cluster" without the claim reading as false modesty.)*
