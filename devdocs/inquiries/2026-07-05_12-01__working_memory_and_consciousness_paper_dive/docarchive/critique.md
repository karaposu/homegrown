### Q2 — The context window IS a working memory
- **Prosecution:** rich-but-poor is a loose analogy, or an obvious observation ("context windows are finite") dressed up.
- **Defense:** the identity is structural, not loose — a cache (fast, tiny, resident) over a backing store (the repo, vast, on-disk); the paper's *"see the vast riches… sample a tiny portion"* is exact. It grounds the prior finding's thin working-memory kind concretely.
- **Verdict: SURVIVE (graded supporting).** Real and load-bearing *for grounding the kind*, but modest as a standalone import — the finding should present it as grounding, not as a discovery.

### Q3 — The availability illusion [center; external-anchor]
- **Prosecution (hardest):** the steering canon already covers this. Line 147: reading one finding *"can produce tidy movement maps that miss what is actually being built"* — "tidy maps that miss" *is* the illusion (feeling complete while incomplete). So Q3 is decorative.
- **Defense:** the canon *gestures* at it ("tidy maps that miss") but does not **name the mechanism** — *why* a session feels complete: because lookup-ability feels like having (*"the ease… fosters the illusion"*). And the canon scopes it to the navigation session; the illusion generalizes (any traverse; memory design at large — reach ≠ grasp).
- **Collision:** the prosecution lands *more* than sensemaking allowed. The finding must not claim "the canon is silent on the why" — the "tidy maps that miss" line is a genuine *gesture* at the illusion. The honest import is **"names and generalizes what the canon only gestured at,"** which is weaker than "the canon is silent" but still a real RESOLVES-import (a gesture is not a name; a named mechanism is actionable where a gesture is not).
- **Verdict: SURVIVE + REFINE.** The center holds, but tighten the claim: acknowledge the canon's "tidy maps that miss" gesture, and frame the import as *naming + generalizing* it, not *supplying what was absent*. This is the single most important honesty tightening.

Under the capacity lens, consolidation shifts from optional to necessitated.
