# Finding: the best game-type reference for the traversal UI — the Outer Wilds ship log, and what survives of the rooms idea

## Question

The user: *"Based on our recent understanding, maybe our visualization and UI should be game-like — especially a game with freestyle map discovery where we are trying to uncover something. One example: there are rooms, and we enter rooms (venture) and define objects inside, defining the room's shape and connections to other rooms. Or probably there are better alternatives. Our focus should be to find the best game-type reference for thinking-space traversal."*

(Context: games were just settled as a genuine traversal domain — the analogy running at the *epistemic* layer, the player's discovered terrain — and the project's atlas is built: the Time-Road home view, with a designed-but-unbuilt semantic layer from the 10-03 finding, under standing honesty rails.)

## Finding Summary

- **The answer, in one breath: THE OUTER WILDS SHIP LOG.** It is the one shipped game-UI that renders exactly what our record is — *knowledge, not places*: entries appear only when genuinely discovered, links between them are drawn as learned, every entry carries a "there's more to explore here" marker, and the log GROWS as understanding grows (no fixed world to complete). It passes every honesty rail we have, several of which it practices as game mechanics.
- **The supporting cast, each with a distinct job:** *Etrian Odyssey's hand-drawn cartography* doesn't add UI — it NAMES what the record already does (routelister/_route/findings ARE maps drawn by hand while walking, validated by our gates instead of a game engine). *The deduction boards* (Case of the Golden Idol) win the third discovery-object — watching understanding form — and wait, slotted, for live ventures to be visible. *Return of the Obra Dinn* dies as a whole (its fixed 60-slot denominator can't survive our self-extending map) but lends two mechanics: the enumerated-unknown ledger form (which our open field already has) and batch-validation (kin to our gate).
- **The rooms idea survives in its bones and drops its dressing.** Rooms = ventures; the objects-you-define-inside = the `_route` identity registries (already authored per inquiry — defining objects to define the room is literally what routelisting does); doors = real edges plus open routes; more-to-explore = Open Questions; entering = the tee-up button. What drops: invented room GEOMETRY (shapes, floor plans, spatial adjacency) — nothing in the record supports it, and in a meaning-dense display invented geography gets read as meaning. The example's two original contributions survive as first-class: **the entering act** (the log has no doors; our tee-up is one) and **the per-room object inventory** (the log has no inventory; our registries are exactly that).
- **The gate's sharpest contribution — the THREE-ZONE model of our darkness:** LIT (walked terrain, drawn), LISTED DARK (the ledger — 492 enumerated, named, prioritized open routes, drawable as dark doorways), and the UNLISTED VOID beyond (questions never yet asked — real, acknowledged in text and key, and never rendered as if enumerated). Fog-of-war's black void mis-describes zone two but honestly names zone three.
- **Two landings, ordered:** **grade-0, free and immediate** — the discovery READING of the existing atlas (below; no pixels needed); **grade-1, gated** — THE EXPEDITION LOG lens (the ship log's form, project-native, with the frontier drawn IN PLACE as dark doorway stubs at each room's rim) — **queued behind the 10-03 foundation build ①a**, which is smaller and ships the KEY this lens extends.
- **One honesty clause the reference-borrowing requires:** Outer Wilds' markers are omniscient (the designers know where content is); ours are WORK-AUTHORED — declared known-unknowns that can be wrong or incomplete. They invite; they never promise.

## Finding

### 1. How the adjudication ran

Thirteen real game-UI systems in three families (spatial-discovery, epistemic-discovery, authorship) were tested against three discovery-objects — *seeing the record* (already served by the atlas), ★*entering the unknown* (the measured gap: the open field lives as a text flyout, un-spatial, invitation-less), and *understanding forming* (nothing renders it; needs live ventures) — under four hard filters: no fabricated geometry, no fixed denominators (our map self-extends), markers may invite but never arrow (the quest-marker deskilling warning, and our own enumerate-never-choose discipline), and lens jurisdiction (the road is never repositioned).

### 2. Why the ship log wins the primary object

The record is a body of *findings connected by typed links, with authored markers of what remains open* — which is a description of the ship log, not merely an analogy to it. Point for point: entries appear only from genuine discovery (our no-fabrication rail, as a game mechanic); entries are knowledge, not geography (our folders are findings, not places); links are drawn as learned (our six edge types are the record's only real geography); per-entry more-to-explore markers (our Open Questions and open routes, natively authored); and the log grows with knowledge — there is no "100% complete" (our field grows as it is explored; any completion-percentage would fabricate a denominator). Even the ship log's celebrated TWO MODES — the rumor-graph and the map-position view of the same knowledge — already exist here as vehicles: the chains lens and the Time-Road. What's missing is only the frontier rendered *in place*.

### 3. The rooms idea, honored precisely

The user's example decomposes onto real structure almost one-to-one, which is why it deserved better than a yes or a no. Its bones — rooms entered, objects defined inside, connections emerging — are ventures, `_route` registries, and edges: all real, all already authored by the work itself. Its dressing — room shapes, dungeon geography — sits on the fabrication frontier: the record has no spatial substrate, and the 10-03 no-false-signifiers obligation (in a meaning-dense display, variation gets read as meaning) kills invented floor plans; the archaeological dig-map seals it (a dig map is honest precisely because its geometry is the site's *real* geometry — excavated squares drawn, unexcavated squares gridded and numbered; we have no site geometry, so the honest form is ledger-plus-graph). **And the game-feel is not lost by dropping the dressing:** Outer Wilds is beloved *as a discovery game* through its log — the feel lives in genuine discovery rendered honestly, not in invented corridors.

### 4. Grade-0 — the discovery reading (free, lands with this finding)

> The atlas already renders a discovery game once you read it in the fifth picture's vocabulary: **the record is the revealed terrain** — every settlement a region you opened, every folder a room you entered and defined by what you found in it (its `_route` registry is the room's object inventory). **The teal pennants are "there's more to explore here" markers** — real ones, authored by the work itself. **The open field is the door ledger:** 492 doors you have seen and named but not walked through. **The staleness ramp is seen-but-stale fog:** ember rooms you stand in, ash rooms you mapped long ago and might not recognize now. And beyond the ledger lies the unlisted void — the questions not yet asked — which no honest map draws, and this one doesn't.

### 5. Grade-1 — THE EXPEDITION LOG lens (a gated offer, queued)

A new lens beside road/chains/flat (jurisdiction precedented), with one identity: the ship log's knowledge-graph, project-native. **Renders, all from today's data.json:** inquiries as entries (solid when concluded, pulsing when active), ventures as lateral LANES (their cluster form in this layout), the six real edge types as the drawn links. **The layout — READ-LANES, not a force graph** *(amended 2026-07-14 at the user's word; the adjudication lives in `devdocs/inquiries/2026-07-14_12-32__layout_meaning_paradigms_for_atlas_axes/finding.md`)*: X = time, READ — the same "right = later" the road trains and the scrub-strip repeats, so spatial intuition transfers between views. The lateral axis = CONCEPT LANES bound to a NAMED notion — v1: episodic membership (venture/chain; lane labels improve when the venture registry lands); topic-lanes or same-tag hulls unlock when the tag step ships (a declared primary-tag rule with annotations for multi-tag folders — or hulls over the time layout, repositioning nothing). Elevation: unbound and declared so in v1; height = docarchive-backing is the named v2 binding. Within-lane order is collision-avoidance only — declared. **The focus act rides the lanes:** clicking a lane isolates it and dims the rest (a view filter — nothing written, nothing auto-selected). **The trade, named:** the ship log's floating rumor-graph feel partially trades for legibility, stability, and honest axes — every organ below survives, and the log reads even more log-like when growth appends rightward. A **declared-optimized variant** (the floating topology view, for link-structure reading that lanes can obscure) stays legal as a secondary mode — its positions declared field-less, its use justified per the strengthening adopted with this amendment (a new fully-optimized layout must state why no real field could serve). **★The frontier, in place — the design's core:** each node's unticked open routes drawn as DARK DOORWAY STUBS at the node's rim (capped at six with a condensed marker, key-disclosed) — so a door is visible AT its room, with its neighborhood around it, instead of only in a sortable list; clicking a stub opens the route's text and the tee-up (entering = a `/traverse` resume command — composing, never firing). **More-to-explore badges:** open-route counts now; Open-Questions counts when the second parse ships. **Honesty built in:** layout declared PER AXIS in the KEY — bound axes named as bindings ("left→right is time"; "lanes group inquiries by venture"), optimized/decorative residues named as carrying no field ("order within a lane carries no meaning"), and no axis stays optimized where a real field could serve *(this per-axis form supersedes the original blanket line "node positions carry no meaning in this lens" — amended 2026-07-14: the blanket form was the honest move for a force layout, but it stated the case as if it were the law)*; the marker-provenance clause in the KEY (markers are declared known-unknowns — work-authored, fallible; they invite, never promise); the three-zone model (lit drawn, listed-dark stubbed, unlisted void acknowledged in the KEY, never rendered); honest counts only, never a completion-%. **Composes with the 10-03 layer:** the KEY is shared (this lens adds rows), the staleness ramp is shared, saved views can hold it; foundations and tiers stay road-side. **The map never writes the record; nothing arrows a next move** — the stubs enumerate; choosing stays human. **Gate:** the user's go — and the offer queues BEHIND the 10-03 ①a foundation+key build, which is smaller and ships the KEY this lens extends.
