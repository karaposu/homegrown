## Phase 2 + 3 — Adversarial Evaluation and Verdicts

Burden of proof: **high stakes** for anything touching `_route.md` or the record's identity (guilty until proven innocent); **low stakes** for wording guards.

### The assembly (ASM) — one principle sentence naming the reader; one trailing Basis line in Touches grammar with speech-act marks and a role clause; honesty-first (cite only what this run read; reasoned not-recovered); two-sided yardstick; index nearly untouched; dry-run before the spec edit

**Prosecution.** (1) *Substance-vs-label:* "zero new fields" is a label — a Basis line is a field in everything but name; calling it a refinement launders an addition. (2) *§1.4:* a sentence naming "a reader" names a consumer — an outward pointer. (3) *Dossier drift:* once every record carries evidence, the map reads as a case file, not a field of directions (Description-collapse by another door). (4) *User-perspective:* the user asked whether these are regressions; an assembly that answers "improvement" for all seven may be the rubber stamp the user feared. (5) *Specification gap:* "material reliance" and "durable original" are runtime determinations — are they specified?

**Defense.** (1) The house already treats a labelled line as a text convention, not a field: "keep it as this text convention until a consumer needs **reliable structured or cross-route-aggregate extraction**" (§5.2.1) — Touches and Meaning-gaps entered exactly this way; the honest description is *no new typed field, no new file, no new registry; one new line-type under the text-convention rule.* (2) The sentence names a **property of the artifact** — written for a reader who did not see the run — not a discipline, protocol or runner; §1.4's prohibition is on referencing "any other discipline, protocol, runner, or larger process", and the sentence references none. (3) K7 keeps basis subordinate; and the record already carries WHY, Meaning-gaps and Excluded reasons without reading as a dossier. (4) The per-behaviour verdicts are *sized* (Premise C) and one behaviour (G in the index) is REFINE, not SURVIVE — not a stamp. (5) Decomposition P1/P2 specify both determinations (materiality = removing the source changes Move/Lands/WHY or inclusion; durability = an original in the project record or at an accessible location; state-claim = the route asserts what the source *said at the time*).

**Collision.** Prosecution (1) wins on wording and loses on mechanism: the assembly must *say* it adds a line-type, not "zero new fields". (2)–(5) fail. **External anchors:** §5.2.1 quoted; §1.4 quoted; the Meaning-gaps precedent verified in the spec; the determinations verified in `decomposition.md`.

**Verdict: SURVIVE** — caveat on D6/D7 wording only: describe the change as *substantively an addition, formally a refinement (one line-type + marks + one principle sentence + one mode)*; never as "zero new fields".

### K1 + K2 — the Basis line in Touches grammar, with quotation / paraphrase / not-recovered marks

**Prosecution.** The grammar is underspecified — the innovation left the exact marks open; an author can still write `Basis: the user's correction` with no mark and satisfy the letter. *Substance-axis test (literal application to corpus routes):* the RICH map's R5 line "the user named seed-generation failure a 'big big problem'" — under the rule it becomes `Basis: user, in conversation: "big big problem" (establishes the goal's stakes) — original not in the inquiry folder`; the 07-03 map's "the user said 'before anything'" becomes `Basis: _branch.md Source Input, "before anything" (establishes ordering)` — a pointer, because the phrase *is* in that inquiry's `_branch.md`. Both are one line; neither inflates. **Defense.** The marks are a Structural-layer choice (out of this inquiry's scope by Layer Commitment); what is *fixed* here is that an unmarked attribution is non-compliant. **Collision.** Prosecution wins a REFINE-direction on the marks; the mechanism holds. **Verdict: SURVIVE** with a refinement target handed to the draft: *an attribution without one of the three marks is non-compliant.* (D2 pass by substance test; D4 pass — one line each.)

### K3 + K19 — not-recovered-with-reason; "consulted, nothing found" as a legitimate mark

**Prosecution.** Invites boilerplate; "original not recovered" becomes a stock phrase that hides sloppiness. **Defense.** Bounded by materiality (only material reliances need a line) and made visible by the telemetry count; the reason is required ("original in conversation; not preserved" vs "document consulted; passage not located"), as Excluded entries require reasons ("never silently dropped", §5.1). **Collision.** Defense holds; the count is the audit surface. **Verdict: SURVIVE.**

### K4 — cite only what this run read; conversation only as excerpt or not-recovered

**Prosecution.** (1) Unenforceable: RouteLister keeps no sweep trace. (2) Over-broad: an author who *knows* a canonical path from prior sessions would have to mark it "not recovered", *reducing* recovery. (3) Specification gap: what counts as "read"? **Defense.** The rule targets the one failure that makes the change a net harm — false precision — and the house already enforces rules by audit, not by trace; "no sight, no cite" is a standing convention elsewhere. **Collision.** (2) wins: the rule must be scoped. *Refinement:* **a pointer more precise than a path (a section, a line, a message location) and any quotation require the source in view during this run; a bare path may be cited from knowledge if it resolves (which is itself a check); anything else is an excerpt or not-recovered.** (3) is answered by that scoping. (1) stands as a known limit — enforcement is by the telemetry split and by the blind re-read. **Verdict: REFINE** — direction: scope the whitelist to precision-and-quotation; keep it the *governing* clause. (D3 critical: passes after refinement.)

### K5 — one sentence in §5 naming the reader

### K9 — the index carries no source content by default; optional found-in note; the invocation log gives reach
