## Phase 1 — Seed

**Seed (Question + Dissatisfaction).** *Given the `rl.md` proposal, what does it require of RouteLister, what does it mean for RouteLister's existence, and is each change a regression or an improvement?* — carried on the stabilized model: a backward-facing *basis* addition to a forward-facing record; identity line basis-in / engagement-out; pointer-vs-copy by durability of the original; honesty must outrank precision; per-map labels vs index references; the MN5 yardstick. The dissatisfaction: the brief calls this "strengthening an existing mechanism" and the spec has none; and the spec has two internal gaps the brief did not name.

**Direction (intuition).** What feels important, in order: (1) the one path by which the change makes RouteLister *worse* — precision demanded without honesty first; (2) whether "small, coherent refinement" can be true of something that is, substantively, an addition; (3) the identity line, and whether it is the project's own distinction; (4) the surprise that WHY once *was* the evidence field.

## Phase 2 — Generate

Each mechanism: **generic** (on the seed as a whole) · **focused** (on one piece's commitment) · **contrarian** (against the inherited frame). Candidates are labelled K1… for testing.

### 1. Lens Shifting (Framer)

- **Generic.** *Current frame:* the map is read by its author, soon. *Shifted:* the map is read by someone who never saw the run — the canon's context-isolated navigational session, a Selector, the user months later. Under the first frame provenance is dead weight; under the second it is the difference between a field of directions and a field of assertions. The canon commits to the second frame, so the proposal's value is not speculative. → **K5a: the value of provenance is condition-dependent on the reader, and the reader is already fixed by canon.**
- **Focused (P1).** *Condition A:* the territory is an inquiry folder (traverse runs) — most bases are files; pointer-form covers them and costs nothing where Touches already names the path. *Condition B:* the territory includes passages or a conversation (standalone, orchestrator-fed) — the excerpt form is the whole change. → **K6: territory-kind-conditioned weight** — the rule's cost scales with how much of the territory is ephemeral; artifact-fed runs pay almost nothing.
- **Contrarian.** *Condition:* RouteLister's readers should re-read the territory (surfacing's thin-artifact posture). Then the map should carry **no basis content**, only pointers; a conversation-only basis is honestly "unsourced", and the real fix is upstream — make the conversation part of the territory *record* (the runner persists mid-pipeline user turns beside `_branch.md`). → **K14: runner-side capture of mid-pipeline turns** (an ecosystem route; RouteLister then only ever points).

### 2. Combination (Generator)

- **Generic.** Touches' grammar ("the endpoint (items awaiting action)") × a source reference → **K1: a Basis line in the Touches grammar** — `Basis: <pointer | "quoted sentence" | paraphrase: …> (what it supports)` — one trailing line per material reliance; no new field *class* beyond a line-type the record already knows how to read.
- **Focused (P1).** seed_harvester's REQUIRED `source-support:` × RouteLister's Excluded-with-reasons ("never silently dropped") → **K3: not-recovered-with-reason** — when the basis cannot be shown, the line says so *and why* ("original in conversation; not preserved"), in positive form, exactly as Excluded entries carry reasons.
- **Contrarian.** routelog's ↗ manifestation stamp × the index → let **consumers** stamp source references into `_route.md` after the fact (as they tick ✓) and keep RouteLister writing none. *Residue that survives:* consumers may **backfill** references on old maps by hand, which is the forward-only rule's companion. → **K15: consumer backfill**.

### 3. Inversion (Framer) — with depth check

- **Generic.** L1: "provenance is *added* to the record" → "provenance is *already in* the record, hidden in WHY and Guidance prose; the change is to *mark* it." (component-level) · L2: "the record needs no new field; it needs a marking grammar — quotation marks, `paraphrase:`, `not recovered`." · L3 (system): "the record's defect is not a missing field but **unmarked speech acts**: it mixes what was *seen* (in the territory), what was *said* (by someone), and what the author *concluded*, without marking which is which; citations are the case where the mix costs most." *Multi-axis:* existence-axis — could the count of new fields be **zero**? Yes: a line-type plus marks. Identity-axis — a route is a perceived concept plus a prescription; the basis belongs to the perception half. → **K2: zero-new-field speech-act marking**; and **K17: record-wide seen/said/concluded marking** (beyond citations).
- **Focused (P4).** "RouteLister enumerates; recording is a controller's job" → "RouteLister has *always* recorded: the map is a record of perception, the index a record of identities over time; the only thing it never records is engagement." System-level: **the enumerator/controller split is perception-record vs action-record, not record vs no-record.** (Reaches Sensemaking's A1 by a different mechanism — mechanism independence.)
- **Contrarian.** "the change is an improvement" → "it is a regression: it turns a *field of directions* into a *dossier of evidence*; the reader's eye moves from what-to-do to why-believe-it; the prescriptive character erodes (Description-collapse by another door)." What follows: basis content must be **subordinate** — trailing, never leading; or collected in one map-level list. → **K7: subordinate layout guard.**

### 4. Constraint Manipulation (Framer) — both directions

- **ADD (generic).** Add "every material basis must be recoverable" as a hard constraint. - **REMOVE (focused, P3).** Remove "no third file" hypothetically → a per-inquiry `_sources.md` register. What survives: the register belongs **inside `routelister.md` as a section** (the Excluded precedent) — one of P3's two containers.
- **REMOVE (contrarian).** Remove self-containment (§1.4) → provenance could be process-aware (message IDs, turn numbers, "the runner's transcript"). Fails as re-fusion — but exposes the exact wording line that survives: the spec may accept **whatever pointer forms the territory itself provides** (a permalink in a Slack-fed project; a `_branch.md` line here) without knowing what a caller is. → **K8: source kinds open; pointer forms are the territory's own.**

### 5. Absence Recognition (Generator) — both levels, both directions

- **Patch-level (generic).** Missing today: a LAYER 1 mode for an unrecoverable basis; a telemetry count; a `not recovered` mark; a pointer form into `## User Input`; a sentence beside §3.1 that inventing a pointer is inventing a manifestation. (All land in P1.)
- **Redesign-level, "what's missing" (focused, P4).** Designed from scratch, a route would carry a *perceived-from* as naturally as Touches; the evidence slot was lost on the incremental path routeman → routelister → the June WHY reframe. Also absent: **the spec never says who the map is for.** The cold reader lives in a finding (06-21), not in §5. - **Redesign-level, "already present in different form" (contrarian).** The project already does most of this narratively: every map cites paths (72 with section anchors); authors quote the user in short verbatim phrases; `## User Input` captures the ask; the harvester's source-support is REQUIRED; conclude cites evidence. ### 6. Domain Transfer (Generator) — native + different

- **Native (computing).** *Version control:* the design history already uses it — rationale = commit message, outcome = SHA. Transfer one step further: a citation is a **ref**; a quotation is a **blob carried inline only when no ref resolves** — git's own content-addressed model says when to copy. *Provenance standards (W3C PROV):* an entity **wasDerivedFrom** other entities (basis), **wasGeneratedBy** an activity (the run — already the invocation log), **wasAttributedTo** an agent (the excerpt's speaker). The basis-vs-engagement line maps onto derivation-vs-activity exactly; RouteLister records derivation and attribution, never the activity's *decisions*. → **K4a: PROV as the sanity vocabulary** for P4.
- **Different (library cataloguing, focused P3).** Authority files — the model the index was built on — carry a **"sources consulted" note per heading** (the citation plus the information found), *and* a separate note for **sources consulted with nothing found**. Transfer: the index's identity row may carry a "found-in" note attached to the manifestation; and the `not recovered` mark has a cataloguing twin — "consulted X; nothing found" — so recording a *negative* provenance result is standard practice, not an apology. → **K9a: found-in note on the manifestation row (optional)** · **K19: consulted-but-absent as a legitimate mark.**
- **Different (journalism and legal citation, contrarian).** Attribution rules: direct quote vs paraphrase marking; "on background" — a source may be typed without being named; the **pinpoint cite** (page/paragraph precision) and the rule "no sight, no cite" (never cite what you did not read). Transfer: section-level precision = pinpoint; "no sight, no cite" = rl.md §5F's inspection-claim clause; and the contrarian angle — sometimes the speaker must *not* be identified — supports **speaker-type without identity** (rl.md already: "speaker or source type where available"). → **K18: speaker-type suffices; identity is optional.**
