**Principal (Absence Recognition + Lens Shifting):** the clean test — *an engagement-type must be partitionable by kind* (teleological = advances the goal / epistemic = sharpens understanding). The absence: there is no *kind* under which "defer/neglect/do-nothing" advances the goal or sharpens understanding — they're orthogonal to the partition. Lens: viewed as "operations on a concept's content," the nine verbs all qualify and the three don't. → **the three are disposition/priority/timing, a different category.**
