## P1 — THE RULE + THE CLAUSE RESOLUTION (principal, finding-ready)

**The two-halves rule (verbatim):**

> **Never lie with position — which was never "positions must mean nothing."** Two halves: **(i) READ where a field exists** — bind an axis to a real, mechanically-derived field and declare the binding in the KEY (this is the project's own law: "a primary dimension needs the primary channel"); **(ii) DECLARE where an optimizer or decoration places things** — force equilibria, within-lane ordering, collision offsets carry no field, and the KEY says so. **Preference: shrink the second class by binding** — and any NEW layout that spends a whole axis on optimization must state why no real field could serve (declaration alone is the floor, not the license).

**The amendment draft (replaces the disputed 10-26 §5 line):**

> ~~"layout declared meaning-free in the KEY ('node positions carry no meaning in this lens')"~~ → **"layout DECLARED per axis in the KEY: bound axes named as bindings (which field each position reads); optimized or decorative residues named as carrying no field ('within-lane order carries no meaning'). Nothing in this lens's positions is left undeclared, and no axis stays optimized where a real field could serve."**

**The live obligation (one sentence):** the shipped `chains` and `flat` lenses render positions from arbitrary indexes with no KEY on screen today — whatever else happens, their one-line declarations ride the ①a KEY build ("positions here are packing order — they carry no meaning").

**Inversion (REPEAL, mandated):** "a layout needing a 'means nothing' disclaimer shouldn't ship — make meaning mandatory for every axis." Tested: fails as stated — the optimized class cannot be emptied (collision offsets, within-lane order, label placement always exist; mandating meaning at residue grain is impossible, and pretending otherwise re-creates false signifiers). **True half adopted into the rule:** the default flips — new fully-optimized layouts must justify, not merely declare (the "floor, not license" sentence).
