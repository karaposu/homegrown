### Region B — the gate-crux [core, the center] — BOTH poles live

The hardest correction. Two genuinely divergent readings, both surfaced at full strength:

- **POLE-A — DROP the gate.** A seed is any unique, usable extract from a source; an affirmation is usable (it gives you confidence); therefore affirmation is a seed; therefore the "must-connect-to-something-un-owned" requirement was simply wrong. Clean, matches the user's words most literally, maximally inclusive.
- **POLE-B — GENERALIZE the gate.** A seed must change your EPISTEMIC STATE about something load-bearing — by NAMING something new (the old gate; inspiration-seed) OR by RESOLVING an uncertainty (the new half; affirmation-of-the-UNCERTAIN). An affirmation of something you were genuinely unsure about IS a seed; an affirmation of something already-certain stays decorative. The gate survives, widened from one door to two.

**The distinction Pole-B rests on [core]:** affirmation-of-the-UNCERTAIN vs affirmation-of-the-CERTAIN.
- *Uncertain, load-bearing:* paper 20's mirror affirmed the harness's typed-everything design IS sound — decision-relevant ("don't rebuild it; the philosophy holds"). You were not certain; the affirmation moved you.
- *Certain, decorative:* a paper that agrees with something you were already fully confident of. Adds nothing; changes no decision. This is exactly what the old gate rightly excluded — and what Pole-A would now let flood back in.

**THE KEY CANON GROUNDING [core] — surfaced from two files, and it reframes the whole crux:**
- The north star: **"the asymmetry that makes this tractable: regression is easier to detect than improvement; absence-of-failure signals are first-class"** (`project_north_star.md:61`). And the substrate sentence: **"quality awareness as the substrate that makes self-improvement trustworthy"** (`:10`).
- The quality architecture: **"There are 2 parts of this self quality assesment — Regression Detection [and] Improvement detection. The Predictive RC catches "things that *feel off*… a hunch with a confidence level" (`:56-58`).
- **What this grounds:** the harness's OWN value-architecture is TWO-HALVED — improvement-detection AND regression-detection — with **regression (≈ affirmation that nothing is broken / the design is sound) as the PRIMARY concern**. The prior gate ("connects to something un-owned") captured only the IMPROVEMENT half. The user's affirmation-seed points precisely at the REGRESSION half the gate was missing — and the harness treats that half as first-class, even primary. So Pole-B is not a face-saving hedge; it is the gate re-derived to match the harness's own two-halved architecture: a seed changes epistemic state either by improvement (name-new) or by regression-clearance (affirm-the-uncertain-is-sound).
