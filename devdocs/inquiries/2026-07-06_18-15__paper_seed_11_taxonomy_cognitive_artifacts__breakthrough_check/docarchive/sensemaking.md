**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** Heersmink's ecological species are defined by a specific MECHANISM — either info carried by a PERCEIVED-SPATIAL arrangement (you see the car keys on the spot; the perceived location, not a convention, carries the info) or info EMERGING from physical MANIPULATION of a structure (rearranging Scrabble tiles). The ecological mechanism (perceived-arrangement or manipulation-emergence) is ABSENT; what does the work is convention. So on the mechanism, our organizational artifacts are symbolic, not ecological. This is a structural defeat (the classifying mechanism), not a precedent citation. **Confidence: HIGH.**
