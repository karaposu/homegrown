### SV6 — Stabilized Model — Routelister's Route-Typology Logic

**routelister should NOT keep the same logic. The primary verdict is EXTEND — routelister generalizes the route-typing logic beyond the special case the 24 01-30 finding solved — with a REFINE-level continuity credited for the parts that genuinely carry.**

The reasoning, in five moves:

1. **The 24 01-30 logic categorizes a fixed, closed, inherited taxonomy.** routeman's 16 move-types were inherited verbatim from canonical /navigation; the finding's job was to organize that standing set into three families (Progression / Re-orientation / Coordination) and tag each type with six attributes. Its load-bearing premise: *a fixed taxonomy of route-types exists to be organized.*

2. **routelister has no such fixed taxonomy.** It perceives concept-identities in an *open* territory and lists each as a typed route. Its routes are territory-derived and open-ended — not drawn from a standing enumeration. The 24 01-30 premise is therefore absent, so the logic cannot stay the **SAME**.

3. **The content cannot transfer, because the roles it encodes are absent.** All six routeman attributes are loop/meta-loop/selector artifacts — `direction` (relative to a cycle), `intent` (loop-move intents), `autonomy_readiness_tier` (from the meta-loop autonomy ladder), `auto_class` (auto-emit vs flag-for-human — a *selector* role), `scope` (thread-relative), `has_sub_actions` (REVISIT-specific). routelister is not loop-bound, not meta-loop-bound, and does not select (it lists all routes without choosing among them). With those roles absent, the attributes have nothing to attach to; they drop.

4. **routelister types its routes AT EMISSION, along its own intrinsic dimensions.** Where routeman sorts a standing list in a separate organizing pass, routelister characterizes each route as it is perceived-and-listed — this is entailed by its perceive-by-enumerate character. Its dimensions are derived (not invented) from its settled identity: **kind** — teleological (advances the goal) vs epistemic (sharpens the understanding the goal rests on), from the 09-53 concept-definition finding; **grain** — project-space (an identity among many) vs concept-space (a manifestation-as-route), from the 11-43 ontology finding; plus **abstractness-no-bar**.

5. **routeman's categorization is the special case; two things carry over.** A closed, pre-enumerated list of move-types is simply a *frozen territory* — so routeman's "categorize a fixed taxonomy" is the special case of routelister's general "type open, territory-derived routes" where the territory happens to be a fixed list. (This is the same generalization shape the input-contract finding found, where routeman's completed-cycle "state" was a special case of routelister's "territory.") What genuinely **survives** as a REFINE-level continuity: (a) the **hybrid shape** — a primary type-axis plus secondary attributes — re-usable as the shape of routelister's per-route type-signature (kind as the primary axis; grain + abstractness as attributes); and (b) the **surface-don't-invent method** — routelister's dimensions are derived from its own identity, exactly as the 24 01-30 finding surfaced rather than invented routeman's families.

**Why EXTEND over REFINE (the one judgment call):** "the logic" of the 24 01-30 finding is the whole operation (premise + object + organizing pass + content), not just its shape. Premise, object (closed → open), operation-timing (post-hoc pass → type-at-emission), and 100% of the content all change; only the meta-shape and method survive. A change of that magnitude is a generalization/extension of the logic, not a tweak within it — so EXTEND is the honest headline, with REFINE accurately describing only the surviving shape+method.

**How SV6 differs from SV1:** SV1 suspected the fixed-taxonomy frame wouldn't transfer and that the work was REFINE-vs-EXTEND. SV6 establishes *why* it doesn't (the premise is absent; the attribute-roles are absent), *what* routelister does instead (type-at-emission along intrinsic kind/grain/abstractness dimensions), the general/special relationship (routeman = frozen-territory special case, paralleling state⊆territory), the precise survivors (hybrid shape + surface-don't-invent method), and the resolved headline (EXTEND, with REFINE-level continuity), with the label flagged for Critique.
