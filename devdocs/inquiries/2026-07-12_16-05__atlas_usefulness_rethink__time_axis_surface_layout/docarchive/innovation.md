## P2 — The vehicles, each written FOR itself

**V1 — THE GLOBE (the user's, in its best form).** Not naive longitude-as-time: a **spiral meridian** — the time-path winds from the north pole (May 23) down and around to the south pole (NOW), one revolution ≈ 2 weeks; ventures = raised archipelagos along the path; you spin the globe to travel time; the whole world = the whole record, a planet you own. Its real strengths: the strongest INHABITABLE feel of all candidates; a closed, learnable shape; spin is a beautiful scrub. *Its tests, stated as questions:* (a) wrap/adjacency — on a sphere the path's neighboring windings sit side-by-side: does May visually abut July (a false adjacency the no-lying norm must veto, or a soft one mitigated by winding gaps)? (b) hidden half — from any viewpoint ~50% of the record is behind the horizon: does the 30-second narration survive when half the story is invisible without rotation? (c) regrow — the corpus grows ~90/month: either the spiral tightens (everything re-positions each regeneration — the learnable shape un-learns itself) or the sphere grows (same). *5-test:* novelty high; scrutiny — the three tests bite hard (b) is near-fatal for task 11; fertility — the spin-scrub survives INTO other vehicles as a gesture; actionability — buildable. **Carried to the gate with its tests.**

**V2 — THE TIME-ROAD (terrain band).** A gently curved ribbon (slight lateral S-curves + rolling elevation) running left(past)→right(NOW); **X = time** (gap-form below), **elevation = weekly activity** (the [48,30,48,8,5,17,69,4] profile becomes literal terrain — a mountain range of effort), **ventures = settlements**: each chain's members clustered at its 0–4-day footprint, up to 3 gentle parallel tracks where bursts overlap, standalones as single roadside markers; the 23-giant = the largest town, LOD-labeled from far. The whole road visible from altitude (holistic in ONE glance — no rotation needed); extends rightward forever (growth = more road, nothing re-positions). *5-test:* novelty medium (it is the honest 3D descendant of a timeline); scrutiny — survives every R and every data constraint by construction (it was DERIVED from them); fertility — carries replay, chapter titles, the spin-scrub-as-dolly; actionability — a layout + camera change in the existing organ. **The leading candidate.**

## P3 — The scene-v2 spec (drafted for V2, the time-road; deltas noted)

**Geometry.** A ribbon mesh ~24 units wide, length = time-mapped; centerline gently S-curved (`z = 6·sin(x/40)`); **elevation = smoothed weekly node-count** (peak weeks = hills; the quiet Jun-20s = a valley — the pace-truth in terrain); the ribbon fades at both ends (pre-May mist; beyond-NOW void).

**Time mapping — the gap-form pair, developed:**
- *(a) Slotted scale:* each calendar DAY containing ≥1 node = one uniform slot (~3 units); empty days = a thin 0.5-unit seam. Pros: even density, no pileups, compact (≈90 active days ≈ 280 units). Cons: pace-truth lost — a 9.8-day silence looks like a pencil line; the narration says "then, immediately," when the truth was "then, after ten quiet days."
- *(b) True scale + folded gaps:* days proportional (~2.2 units/day); any gap > 3 days folds into a marked **pleat** — a visibly compressed accordion band labeled "· 10 quiet days ·". Pros: pace-truth kept AND told (the fold is content: "here the practice paused"); bursts read as genuinely crowded (true). Cons: burst days need local spreading (the 15-node day fans its nodes along the day's ~2.2 units + lane offsets + slight elevation jitter — acceptable at ≤15).
- *Leading: (b)* — task 11 is a NARRATION test and pace is part of the story; the fold makes gaps legible instead of lying by omission or by compression. (Gate confirms.)

**Placement.** Venture-settlements: each chain's members clustered at its date-footprint, laid as a short arc along the road's local direction (internal order = createdAt); concurrency ≤3 → up to 3 gentle tracks offset laterally (±6 units); standalones = single markers on the road's shoulders (alternating sides, at their dates); the 23-giant = the largest settlement, its arc allowed a local switchback.

**Furniture (mandatory, per R6).** Week tick-lines across the ribbon; **month pylons** with large labels (MAY · JUNE · JULY) readable from altitude; the **NOW terminus**: a beacon at the road's end (the active pulse lives here when an inquiry is ACTIVE); **venture labels at altitude**: the top-12 settlements by size/recency carry chips visible from far (the star-atlas economy, kept); ★**chapter titles (mechanical, from data):** per week, the largest venture's prettified name renders as a faint banner over that stretch — auto-derived, no fabrication (absence-redesign catch).

**Camera.** PRIMARY GESTURE = dolly along the road (wheel/drag-x/arrow keys) with inertia and soft snap to settlements; orbit constrained to a cone (±60° azimuth, 10–80° elevation) around the current road point; zoom envelope 6–140 units; **opens at a three-quarter aerial over the last ~2 weeks with the NOW beacon in the near field, looking back down the road**; double-click empty = return to the opening shot; `#node-id` deep-links fly along the road.

**LOD gates.** FAR (>90): terrain profile + month pylons + top-12 venture chips + the NOW beacon — the 30-second narration layer; MID (25–90): all settlements + their chips + week ticks + standalone markers; NEAR (<25): individual nodes + hover titles + selection neighborhoods. (The narration test on paper: from FAR, the eye reads — May: two big early towns; a long June ridge of steady work with a valley late June; a huge July mountain ending at the beacon — that IS the story, zero clicks.)

**Edges.** Continues-from: short warm arcs INSIDE settlements (the chain's spine) — and the rare **long back-arc** (a venture continuing something weeks old) rendered as a tall glowing arc over the road: visible story ("this reached back"). Related/other: on-selection only, unchanged. Raw-target edges: detail-panel only, unchanged.

**Materials.** The keep-set carried: fog, ember palette, chip sprites, glow; the ramp persists on nodes as the SECONDARY recency cue (now redundant with position — an honest double encoding).

**Deltas if the gate swaps vehicles:** GLOBE — same settlements/furniture/LOD on the spiral-meridian path; camera = spin+tilt; the wrap/hidden-half/regrow answers must be written into the spec. RADIAL — same, on rings; scrub = radial dolly.
