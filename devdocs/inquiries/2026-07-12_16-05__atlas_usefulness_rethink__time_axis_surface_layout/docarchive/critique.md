"Narrate in 30 seconds without one click" requires ROTATION, which is interaction; the only cure is an unrolled inset — **which is a road, conceding the point.** Severity: NEAR-FATAL on D-A (a CRITICAL dimension).
  3. **Regrow:** the spiral re-parameterizes each regeneration (+90 nodes/month) — everything repositions; the learnable-world virtue, the globe's core strength, **un-learns itself monthly.** Severity: MED-HIGH on D-F, and it attacks the defense's own pillar.
