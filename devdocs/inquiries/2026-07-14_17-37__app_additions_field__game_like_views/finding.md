Second: three NEW game-READINGS that ride existing or incoming surfaces rather than adding lenses — **the journey REPLAY** (the shipped ▶ play, staged: chapter banners announced at crossings, a bounded one-venture replay), **the RUNS-CARD format** (the region-ledger tile rendered as venture run-cards once real ventures land), and **the RE-TRAVERSAL upgrade** (the already-shipped back-arc arches gain declared dormancy meaning: "returned after ≥21 quiet days").

### 2. The three newcomers — game-readings on true fields

- **THE JOURNEY REPLAY.** The ▶ play control already drives the road; the addition is staging: chapter banners (already mechanical — the largest venture per week) ANNOUNCED as the cursor crosses them, and a **bounded one-venture replay** — play clamped to a settlement's span, the mechanical "tour" of any venture. The scrub-strip doubles as the route bar unchanged. **Clause: span-as-fact** — dates on the banner, never a duration callout, no fastest-anything (the games canon's speedrun row licenses the *reading*; a time-to-beat would smuggle a score through the one channel scores could hide in). No new binding, so no KEY row is required (one courtesy inventory line optional). An off-by-default attract-mode (auto-play on long idle) is a harmless option — staging only. **①a-independent**: disjoint code path; may run in parallel at your word without touching the queue.
- **THE RUNS-CARD FORMAT.** Not a new organ — the region-ledger tile's presentation once `ventures[]` lands: registry name · span (dates) · members · **end-status in canon vocabulary** (satisfied / handed-off / abandoned / dormant-open) · what-it-banked as COUNTS (canon promotions, seeds filed, routes opened→closed) · provenance×validation badges. **Clauses:** counts and status, never ratings; **default sort = recency** — count-sorts remain available as explicit acts, but ranking ventures by yield as the default would be a leaderboard in sort's clothing, and no "top ventures" framing exists anywhere.
- **THE RE-TRAVERSAL UPGRADE.** Half of this already ships unbranded: `road.jsx` draws tall glowing arches for long back-links. The upgrade DECLARES them (KEY rows) and adds dormancy meaning: an arch counts as a **re-entry** when a new folder chain-links (the canon chain set) into a settlement quiet **≥21 days — a declared convention, not a measurement**, and the KEY says so. A small ↩ glyph at the re-entered settlement (MID/NEAR zoom) is the v1.1; v1 may be KEY-rows-only. Live instances already on the road: the 32-day articulate2 recall (the archetype) and the May routeman thread's June return (found by today's pilot dig). The metroidvania reading — re-traversal under a grown mover — gets its first app-side instance; the marker records what happened and never prompts what to do next.
