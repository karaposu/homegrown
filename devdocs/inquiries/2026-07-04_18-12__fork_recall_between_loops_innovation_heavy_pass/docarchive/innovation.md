## Part 3 — Finding-Ready Pieces (folding the yields)

### Q1 — The reframe: the recalled object is the declared deferred fork [SUPERSEDES ordering]
The fork-recall's object is not "similar past work" but the **declared deferred fork** — a past traverse's stated re-entry point carrying a revival trigger and intent. The project already writes 391 of these (findings' `### DEFERRED` + routelisters' Excluded/@-gated routes), entirely **write-only** — nothing harvests them. This is grounded, not speculative. CONNECT-similarity is demoted to a *secondary catch* for undeclared resemblances (two traverses that rhyme, neither having deferred to the other).

### Q2 — The read architecture: publish/subscribe over a keyed condition-index [CENTER, SUPERSEDES scan]
Retrieval is **publish/subscribe**, not scan-the-past. Each traverse PUBLISHES its own forks into a shared, direction-keyed index as `(fork, direction-key, condition)` tuples (deposit's real work: extract a matchable germination-key from the prose trigger). A new traverse SUBSCRIBES by its candidate direction — a cheap lookup against a compact index, not a corpus re-surfacing. This resolves 17-45's open write-target (pub/sub *needs* a shared index) and makes the amortization concrete: the past did the work of declaring its forks; the present does a keyed lookup. *(Cold-start fallback: if the index is empty/stale, scan — scan is the degenerate case, not the design.)*

### Q3 — The verbs and the object's true nature: DEPOSIT / MATCH / GERMINATE; the conditional continuation [CENTER]
Three acts: **DEPOSIT** (publish forks), **MATCH** (fire the triggers the current direction satisfies), **GERMINATE** (resume a matched fork as a next-traverse seed). The recalled object is best named a **conditional continuation** — a resumable computation-point (the continuation lens: resume with new context) gated by a matchable condition (the spore lens: dormant until germination). The two lenses are one object: *spore* names its dormancy/pool/economy; *continuation* names its resume. A design axis follows — fork **fidelity**: thin (pointer+condition, re-derive on germination) vs thick (carries resume-state). Germination is a *right, not an obligation* (the options lens): matching surfaces forks; the selector chooses whether to germinate; and germination may **reconsolidate** (re-deposit a sharpened successor fork).

### Q4 — The write-back: append-only spent-markers; one-shot vs standing [REFINES the kill]
Spent-ness is tracked by **append-only events** ("traverse N germinated fork F"), never by rewriting the fork's home content. Forks are **one-shot** (a one-time condition — consumed on germination, marked spent) or **standing** (a recurring condition — never consumed, may re-fire). This refines 17-45's blunt "never mutate old" into "never rewrite old CONTENT; append-only status-events are correct and needed," wired to the avoid-redundancy function.

### Q5 — SELECT: reinforce ships; decay is abandonment-based and frontier [bounded EXTEND]
**Reinforce-on-germination** ships: a germinated fork's kind ranks up (a cheap, valuable signal). **Decay** is a frontier — and, corrected by the seed-bank insight, it must be **abandonment-based, not age-based**: a long-waiting fork is not stale (it may be a patient deep-question finally matchable); a fork on a genuinely abandoned direction is. This is a candidate 4th record-property (SELECT), flagged as frontier, not asserted as built.

### Q6 — Boundaries re-examined: watcher VINDICATED; read DIRECTION-TRIGGERED [F+G]
The watcher boundary holds, with a sharper reason: the fork-pool stays a **passive, pull-queried** index precisely to avoid merging into the watcher's active-monitoring (the watcher is direction-agnostic active-push; the fork-recall is direction-targeted passive-pull; the options lens agrees — pull-and-choose, don't auto-fire). The read is **direction-triggered** (fires when the candidate direction crystallizes: primarily the topic-read, optionally re-fired when the direction sharpens mid-traverse) — kept out of the excluded corpus-sweeps because it queries the fork-pool, not the corpus.

### Q7 — The honest delta-vs-17-45 ledger [the metric]
A supersedes-ordering (Q1) · C supersedes-architecture (Q2) · B/H6 extend (verbs + conditional-continuation + fidelity) · D refines-the-kill (Q4) · E bounded-extend (Q5) · F vindicates (Q6) · G refines (Q6). The floor's BONES stand (two halves; thin-write-in-loop/steer-in-nav; append-only over content-mutation; amortization — now leaner via pub/sub and event-sourcing).

- **E1 · THE FORK-POOL IS A MATERIALIZED VIEW OVER AN APPEND-ONLY EVENT LOG (the architectural unification).** Deposit, germinate, reinforce, spend are all EVENTS in one append-only log; the "index," the "open-pool," the "spent-set," and the "reinforcement-ranking" are all FOLDS (read-time projections) over it. This is a candidate NAME that supersedes both "deferred fork" (too vague) and "spore" (too biological) — a precise term that a Meaning-layer touch could adopt. **Delta: EXTENDS to a unifying name + dissolves the push/pull and spore/continuation dualities into one object.** [HIGH]
