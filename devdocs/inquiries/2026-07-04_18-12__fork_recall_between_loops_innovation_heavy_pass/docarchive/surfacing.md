## Cluster B — The new analogy: SPORULATION + GERMINATION (new to the chain)

- **B1 · The transfer** [HIGH — new analogy, carries new mechanism]. The chain used mycelial growth, anastomosis, the slime-trail, reinforcement — but NOT sporulation. Fungi produce **spores**: dormant packets that carry enough to restart growth, lie inert (sometimes years), and GERMINATE when conditions match. A deferred fork IS a spore: a dormant restart-point, deposited by a traverse, indexed by germination-condition (the revival trigger), activating when a passing traverse's direction meets it.
- **B2 · What the spore frame ADDS beyond "deferred fork"** [the mechanism, not a rename]. Three properties a plain link lacks: **dormancy** (the fork sits inert, costing nothing, until matched — not "surfaced each time"); **conditional germination** (a stated firing condition, not fuzzy similarity); **a viability window** (spores decay — see Cluster E). These are real mechanisms, not vocabulary.
- **B3 · Germination is the missing verb** [HIGH]. The chain had deposit (write) and read; sporulation adds GERMINATE — the event where a dormant fork becomes a live traverse-seed. "Reactivation" = germination. It is neither a pure read (it CONSUMES the spore, changes state) nor a pure write (it doesn't rewrite content) — a third act. → the operation may have THREE verbs, not 17-45's two.
