## Phase 2-3 — Adversarial Evaluation + Verdicts

### E1 (event-log-folds) — the strongest emergent, biggest framing risk — REFINE (R1)
**Prosecution (D1):** event-sourcing is an enterprise pattern; the project writes markdown files. "The pool is a fold over an event log" imports database vocabulary that doesn't fit — it reads as architecture-astronautics.
**Defense + collision → REFINE:** the CLAIM survives but the FRAMING must change. The load-bearing content is: *fork-state is DERIVED from append-only records, not stored mutably.* In the harness's idiom that means — forks live in each traverse's own append-only files; the "pool"/"index"/"spent-set" are COMPUTED by reading them at query-time; germination/spent are appended (to a small log or the germinating traverse's record). **R1: restate E1 in markdown-harness terms and explicitly DISCLAIM that it needs an event-store or database — it needs append-only records + read-time computation.** Without this, the pass's best idea looks like over-engineering. → fold into E1/Q4.

### The pile-on test — has "thin" ballooned? — REFINE (R4, the most important cut)
**Prosecution (D2):** the pass piled on keyed-index, event-log, germination-markers, one-shot/standing, reinforce, options-semantics, fidelity-axis, reconsolidation, decay. 17-45's *thin* write-half is now a heavy subsystem — the pass betrays the very principle it claims to keep.
**Defense + collision → REFINE:** the core IS still thin if separated from the frontier. **R4: the finding MUST foreground a MINIMAL v1 and relegate the rest explicitly.**
- **v1 core (thin, shippable):** DEPOSIT — each traverse publishes its already-written deferred forks to a shared, direction-keyed index; MATCH — at the topic-read, look up forks whose trigger the candidate direction fires; GERMINATE — the selector chooses one, spawns the next traverse, appends a spent-marker (one-shot).
- **Frontier (explicitly deferred):** reinforce, decay/SELECT, options-pricing, PageRank, thick-forks, reconsolidation, mid-traverse re-fire.
Without this separation the heavy innovation reads as scope-bloat. With it, the v1 is as thin as 17-45's, and the frontier is honest optionality. → the finding's spine.

### "Reconsolidation" (H5) — DEMOTE (R6)
**Prosecution (D2):** "germination re-deposits a refined fork" is presented as a special mechanism, but a germinated fork spawns a traverse, and *every* traverse deposits forks anyway. It's redundant.
**Verdict → DEMOTE:** reconsolidation is not a distinct mechanism — it's the ordinary deposit happening in the germinated traverse. **R6: remove it as a named mechanism; at most note that germinated traverses deposit their own forks like any other (the pool learns for free).** Prunes a cute-but-empty addition.

### Q2 (pub/sub) — is it really distinct from 17-45? — REFINE (R3)
**Prosecution (D3):** 17-45 already had "each traverse appends links; the reader reads the union." Is pub/sub-of-forks a genuine supersession or a rebrand?
**Defense + collision → REFINE:** it IS distinct on two axes — 17-45's links are BACKWARD ("I point to what I resemble," similarity), this pass's forks are FORWARD-PUBLISHED ("I offer my deferred branches, findable by direction," condition-carrying). Different object, different data-flow direction. **R3: state the distinction crisply — forward-published condition-forks vs backward-appended similarity-links — so "supersedes architecture" is visibly earned, not asserted.** → fold into Q2.

### E2 (conditional-continuation as a NAME) — REFINE (R2)
**Prosecution (D4):** under a PROCESS-scoped run, proposing a new canonical name is Meaning-layer overreach; and "conditional continuation" is more jargony than the intuitive "deferred fork."
**Defense + collision → REFINE:** keep it as the precise STRUCTURAL CHARACTERIZATION, not a replacement name. **R2: position "conditional continuation" as what a deferred fork STRUCTURALLY IS (which explains its behavior), while "deferred fork" stays the working/communicating name; adopting it as canon is a separate Meaning-layer inquiry.** The unification (it dissolves push/pull + spore/continuation) is the real value and survives; the rename is bounded to a proposal.

### E3 (collective-memory significance) — SURVIVE (bound intact)

### The 391 grounding — REFINE (R5, light)
