## The Six Pieces (finding-ready)

### (1) The yardstick — what made the two picks strong

> Before testing a new analogy, name what the last two got right. Four things did the work. **Close structural-fit:** the trio (problem / thinking / solution space) landed naturally, and the closer a mapping ran to literal, the stronger it felt — the citation graph in the humanity analogy was not *like* the project's record-graph, it *was* one. **The hero-constraint:** no character held the whole picture; understanding was approached only through a shared record (the comet-hunter has no hero to correct him — the dawn reading does). **A failure-arc that teaches the danger:** each scene carried its own cautionary tale (the comet-hunter, the divers who deny the treasure), so it taught what to fear, not just what to do. And, unique to the humanity analogy, **grounding:** it did not merely illustrate traversal, it proved the architecture works. Two more things helped — tellability and a vivid image — but those are multipliers on fit, not strengths on their own. **This is the yardstick the fungus is measured against.**

### (2) The fungus map — the counterparts, and what is new

> One ground rule first: this is about the *described* dynamic — pick a direction, grow toward resources, back off when stuck — not the biology of real fungi. The seed is the behavior, not the organism.
>
> The map is clean. The **problem space** is the nutrient landscape — where the food is, unknown until grown into. The **thinking space** is the soil volume, the field of possible growth-directions. The **solution space** is the resource-rich pockets — the shapes that feed, that fit. **Traversal memory** is the most striking mapping: the hyphal network *is* the record — the body you grew is the memory of where you searched, and reinforcing a path literally thickens it. One traverse is one growing tip; meaningful growth is reaching toward resource, chaotic growth is spreading at random.
>
> And here is what no prior scene had: a live **search dynamic**. Pick a direction under uncertainty (explore). Grow more where resources are found (exploit). Return to an earlier fork when a line dead-ends (backtrack). That triad — explore, exploit, backtrack — is new to the family.

### (3) The verdict — the fungus models the MOVE, not the record

> The fungus is the family's first picture of the search itself — **how a single traverse moves, not how many traverses are remembered.**
>
> This is the finding's spine. Every prior scene models the **record layer**: many traverses accumulating into a shared memory kept in proportion (the observatory's catalog, humanity's literature). The fungus models the **move layer**: one traverse's internal search — the directional logic of pushing a promising line, abandoning a dead-end, returning to a fork. The two are different *grains*, not competitors — one search process (the fungus, one head's session) versus many accumulating (the record-layer scenes).
>
> That grain-distinction settles the fit-test. **Judged as a record-layer scene, the fungus fails** — it has no proportion-keeping, no grounding, no many-heads. **Judged as what it is — a model of the search-move — it fits, and it fills a gap the family genuinely had.** No prior scene depicted the directional logic of a single search; you can point to that hole in the project's own structure, independent of the fungus. So the fungus is not a failed candidate, and the move-layer framing is not a rescue. The deeper reading: **the family was single-layer — all record — and the fungus reveals its own incompleteness.** The project half-knew this already (its disciplines *are* moves; "the catalog aims the next pointing" gestured at exploit; the fork-recall inquiries gestured at backtrack) — the fungus simply names it. It is an **addition, not a replacement**; it is never "better" than the picks, because it answers a different question.

### (4) The pairing rule — the fungus is the drive, the record is the brake

> One property is missing from the fungus, and it matters: **proportion-keeping.** The record-layer scenes re-read the whole and correct a find that has been over-weighted (the dawn reading catches the comet-hunter). The fungus never re-reads; it follows local gradients. That absence is not a hidden failure — proportion is a record-layer property, at the other grain, and the fungus is honestly out of that scope.
>
> But it forces a rule. Look at the fungus's own dynamic: reinforce where you find resources, and back off only when *stuck*. That is exactly the drive to over-invest in a rich seam — and it backs off only at a dead-end, never because a better seam might lie elsewhere. **Alone, the fungus is the comet-hunter's error taught as method:** double down on what's paying, and never re-read for proportion. So the fungus must be **paired**, never shown solo. The fungus is the drive (explore, exploit, backtrack); the observatory or the humanity scene is the brake (proportion, the dawn reading). Together they compose into the whole truth the project is after: **a good search-move, held in proportion by a re-read record.** Shown solo, the fungus would teach the very failure the family exists to warn against.

### (5) The fork-recall home — where the fungus is strongest

> The fungus's single best use is narrower than "an analogy for traversal," and naming it is a strength, not a demotion. Its sharpest mapping is the **backtrack**: when a line dead-ends, return to an earlier divergence and grow a different way. That is precisely the path-divergence-memory idea the project has circled before — the "fork-recall" thread (surfaced as SEED 2 in the earlier massage-brainstorm inquiry): a traverse that hits a wall should not restart, it should re-activate a prior fork and try another branch. No image in the family shows this as plainly as a mycelium abandoning a starved tunnel and pushing out from an old junction. So the fungus's **home is illustrating reinforcement and fork-recall** — the search-move mechanics — not carrying a whole-traversal account, where it is weak.

### (6) The honest losses, and what lands

> Two losses, stated plainly. The fungus is **one organism**, so it gives up the many-heads strength that carried both picks — and with it the mortality mapping that made the humanity analogy land: a mycelium is one continuous body, it has no per-head death, no session-boundary, no records bridging the deaths of separate minds. That transfer simply does not happen. What the fungus keeps is a *different* no-hero property — decentralized growth, no central brain directing it — which is real, but it is not the many-independent-minds picture.
>
> One clarification the losses produce: the fungus's network-memory and the record-layer's archive are **two different memories**, not rivals. The network is the *working* memory of one traverse (the search structure taking shape as you go); the findings corpus is the *persistent* memory across traverses. The project has both; the fungus lights up the first, the record-layer scenes the second.
>
> Two things land. The search dynamic (explore / exploit / backtrack) is a candidate **new axis for the analogy scorecard** — "does this scene depict the search-move, not just the record?" — a move-layer criterion the harness never had. And the observatory's disposition-pick (from the prior inquiry) is **untouched**: the fungus is a move-layer addition, orthogonal to how the observatory relates to the sonographer-and-potter pair. Nothing here re-opens or re-gates that choice.
