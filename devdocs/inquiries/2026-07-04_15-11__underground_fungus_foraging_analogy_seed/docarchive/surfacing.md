This is a genuinely novel, near-literal structural contribution.

### The distinctive NEW dynamic — the live SEARCH ALGORITHM [core, HIGH]

The seed foregrounds three moves the prior scenes never modeled:

- **EXPLORATION** — pick a direction under uncertainty (you don't know where the food is). `[core]`
- **EXPLOITATION / REINFORCEMENT** — grow more where resources are found (double down on a promising direction). `[core]`
- **BACKTRACK / ESCAPE** — when stuck, refocus growth from a prior path-divergence (return to an earlier fork, try another branch — escape a dead-end / local optimum). `[core]`

**Verified new-to-the-family `[core, HIGH]`:** the observatory modeled the RECORD (the catalog) + the CONFIG (what the instrument sees) + PROPORTION (the dawn reading); humanity added GROUNDING + the ladder; the pair modeled the FIT (the potter). **None modeled the live directional SEARCH-MOVE** — the explore/exploit/backtrack dynamic of *how* a traverser decides where to go next. The observatory's "the catalog aims the next pointing" gestures at it, but that is the *record* aiming, not the *search-strategy* itself. **The fungus's distinctive contribution is the MOVE.**

### The LAYER question (the central surfacing finding) [core, HIGH]

The fungus is picturable (it passes a felt-test as a scene) — but its *value* is as a **DYNAMICS-MODEL of the traversal MOVE**, a **different LAYER** from the static scenes, which model the **RECORD + PROPORTION**. So:

- **RECORD-layer** (what accumulates, stays in proportion, outlives the head) → observatory, humanity, the pair.
- **MOVE-layer** (how a single traverse decides direction, reinforces, backtracks) → the fungus.

**This reframes the whole fit-test:** the question may not be "is the fungus a *better* scene?" but "does the family need a *MOVE-layer* model, and is the fungus the right one?" — complementary, not competing.

### Fit-evidence FOR [core]

- **Captures the actual MECHANICS of moving** — directed search under uncertainty + reinforcement + backtracking — the layer the family lacked. `[core, HIGH]`
- **The network-as-its-own-memory is elegant and near-literal** (reinforced paths persist = the traversal record). `[core, HIGH]`
- **Decentralized growth, no central brain = the hero-constraint in a NEW form** — no controller directs the mycelium; growth is local gradient-following, so "no one holds total sight" is *native* to the mechanism. `[core, HIGH]`
- **The FIT property is native** — reaching a nutrient pocket = a solution fitting a problem-shape under conditions. `[sub, HIGH]`
- **Backtracking maps the fork-recall thread** — returning to a prior divergence to re-explore is *exactly* the path-divergence-memory idea surfaced in an earlier inquiry (the "fork-recall" SEED from 11-24). The fungus embodies it physically. `[core, HIGH]`

### Fit-evidence AGAINST — the five tensions (developed honestly) [core]

- **(i) Single organism vs multi-head.** The picks' strength included MANY independent heads whose separate deaths are bridged by records. The fungus is ONE organism. It *keeps* the "no central sight" property (via decentralization) but *loses* the "many independent heads + mortality bridged by records" property. **The mortality/session-boundary mapping does NOT map** — the mycelium has no per-head death; it is one continuous body. A real loss of one of humanity's strengths. `[core, HIGH]`
- **(ii) Proportion-keeping ABSENT (the sharpest tension).** The load-bearing strength of both picks was the dawn-reading / institutions that RE-READ the whole and keep findings in proportion (correcting the comet-hunter). **The fungus has no such mechanism** — it follows local gradients; it never re-reads its whole network to correct a misweighted branch. **This is a load-bearing strength the fungus is missing.** `[core, HIGH]`
- **(iii) Correction / failure-arc is reactive-local, not deliberate-global.** No comet-hunter-correction. The fungus can over-invest in a depleting direction and simply starve; the branch dies and resources flow elsewhere. That IS a kind of correction — **but correction-by-starvation, not correction-by-re-reading.** The family's teaching failure-arc (the *danger* of over-weighting a find, *corrected by a shared reckoning*) does not map; the fungus has a different, blinder self-correction. `[core, HIGH]`
- **(iv) Backtracking mapping — strong, with a caveat.** "Return to a prior divergence and re-explore" maps *well* to how traversal re-opens a fork (the fork-recall thread). Caveat: the fungus does it by resource-flow (physiological reallocation), not by a *deliberate decision* — the local-vs-deliberate distinction recurs. `[sub, HIGH]`
- **(v) Write-half weak or absent.** Under structure-not-biology (the user's forage/grow dynamic), the fungus FORAGES (searches/reads for food) and GROWS (builds structure) — but it does not TRANSFORM the world the way the potter shapes clay or medicine cures a disease. **It is mostly a READ/FORAGE half; the WRITE/WORK half is weak.** `[sub, MED]`

### Half-B synthesis

**Distinctive strength:** the fungus models the **MOVE-layer** (explore/exploit/backtrack) the whole family lacked, with a near-literal memory (the network IS the record), a novel no-hero form (decentralized growth), and a beautiful fit to the fork-recall thread. **Weaknesses against the picks' load-bearing strengths:** it loses proportion-keeping, loses the multi-head/mortality structure, has only a reactive-local failure-correction (no teaching failure-arc), and a weak write-half. **The frontier reading:** the fungus fits BEST as an **additive MOVE-layer / dynamics model** — capturing what the family couldn't — NOT as a replacement for the RECORD-layer scenes that carry proportion and grounding.
