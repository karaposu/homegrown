## Phase 3 — Ambiguity Collapse

#### A1 — The verdict-shape (the central adjudication).

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** the load-bearing strengths were extracted from RECORD-layer scenes and are RECORD-layer properties (proportion = re-reading many finds; grounding = a track record across many heads; multi-head = many independent minds). Judging a MOVE-layer model by RECORD-layer criteria is a grain-mismatch — like faulting a close-up lens for not being a wide-angle. The fungus is answerable to MOVE-layer criteria (does it depict directed search under uncertainty? reinforcement? fork-recall/backtrack? — yes, yes, yes), which no prior scene depicts at all. The gap it fills is real and independent (Technical perspective). So (b) fails: the fungus does not *fail* the test; it *answers a different question* the family had not yet asked.
**Confidence:** HIGH (the grain-distinction is structural, verified at SP1/SP2 and the frame-exit check, not merely elegant).
**Resolution:** primarily (a) — an additive MOVE-layer model — with a secondary (c): its explore/exploit/backtrack dynamic is *also* a candidate ELEMENT for the criteria-harness (a new evaluative axis: "does the analogy depict the search-MOVE, not just the record?"). Explicitly NOT (b); explicitly NOT a replacement for the picks.
**What is now fixed?** The fungus ships as a complement at the move layer; it is answerable to move-layer criteria.
**What is no longer allowed?** Presenting the fungus as "better than" or "a replacement for" the picks; judging it a failure by record-layer criteria.
**What now depends on this choice?** The pairing rule (A3); the fork-recall home (A4); the harness-element suggestion routes onward.
**What changed in the model?** The fit-test stopped being "pass/fail vs the picks" and became "which layer does it serve, and how well there."

#### A2 — Is the intra/inter-traversal layer real, or a convenient relabel?
**Ambiguity:** is a single mycelium's whole foraging life honestly "one traverse's internal dynamic," or a stretch to make the fit work?
**Strongest counter-interpretation:** "a whole mycelium living for months is nothing like one bounded session; you've relabeled a whole-organism life as a single move to dodge the multi-head loss."

So the relabel charge fails: the grain-match is at "one search process," which is exact, not stretched.
**Confidence:** HIGH.
**Resolution:** the intra-traversal (one search process) vs inter-traversal (many accumulating) distinction is real; the fungus sits at the former.
**What is now fixed?** The fungus = one-traverse-grain.
**What is no longer allowed?** Faulting the fungus for lacking many-head properties *as if that were a fit-failure* — it is a grain fact, disclosed.

#### A3 — The composition / pairing rule.

Presented solo, it therefore depicts the exploitation drive without its correction, i.e., it teaches the comet-hunter's error as the method (FP2).
