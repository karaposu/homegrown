## Phase 2 — Generate (7 mechanisms × 3, compact)

### 1. Lens Shifting (Framer)

- **1G — road lens.** Deferred = a road you chose not to take today; gated = a level crossing whose barrier lifts when the train passes; blocked = a washed-out bridge. One optional teaching line.
- **1F — task-system lens (focused).** Mature task vocabularies make the same cut: "someday/maybe" (chosen postponement) vs "waiting-for" (dependency) — one supporting clause that the distinction is battle-tested, not invented.
- **1C — the taxonomy-bloat adversary (contrarian).** "Four words for 'not now' — users will mis-tag." Answer: the words carry different MACHINE behavior (gated → polled; deferred → raised; blocked → untouched until external news) — they are not flavors of one state; and mis-tagging is LOW-STAKES + self-correcting at checkpoints (a polled deferred just does nothing; a raised gated asks one unneeded question). Adopted into the map's notes.

### 2. Combination (Generator)

- **2G — states × Selector rationale.** Machine-readable conditions make state-transitions choice-explanations: at L2+ a proposal can cite *"gated-on(meaning-stability: X) released this turn"* as its WHY. One consequence line.
- **2F — parked vs stale (the next tangle, pre-empted).** Two lifecycles, one clarifying line: **stale** (the canopy's flag) = no longer re-confirmed by the territory; **parked** = held by a state in the ledger. An entry can be parked and fresh; an identity can be stale and never parked. Adopted.
- **2C — blocked-on × the seats.** `blocked-on(user-naming-veto)` shows the architecture's seats inside the ledger — external-to-the-system includes decisions that are the human's by design. One line.

### 3. Inversion (Framer; depth-checked)

- **3G — rename-direction inversion.** Keep DEFERRED for gates and rename the template's usage instead? Absurd on age and correctness (the template's meaning is established and true); direction confirmed. Absorbed.
- **3F — bare-GATED inversion (focused).** Allow `gated` without a named condition? A bare gated entry cannot self-release (nothing to poll) — it degenerates into deferred-without-agency, the worst of both states. **Conditions-always-named confirmed as load-bearing.** Absorbed into the map's rule.
- **3C — poll-the-deferred inversion (contrarian).** Auto-revive deferred items too? Deferred revival IS a judgment (someone chose "not now"; someone re-decides) — auto-reviving converts a choice into a mechanism, the agency-falsification in reverse. Raised-only confirmed. Absorbed.

### 4. Constraint Manipulation (Framer; both directions)

- **4-ADD (generic).** **One canonical entry syntax:** `parked-as: <state>(<condition-or-reason>)` — e.g., `parked-as: gated-on(meaning-stability: concept-X)`. One shape, greppable, machine-checkable.
- **4-ADD (focused).** The map fits ONE table.
- **4-REMOVE (generic).** Fold BLOCKED into GATED? Already killed (the internality test carries chooser-relevant information). Stands.
- **4-REMOVE (contrarian).** Drop PARKED (let states stand alone)? PARKED names the ACTION and the LOCATION (the ledger write) — without it the states float verb-less and the guarantee loses its subject. Fails.

### 5. Absence Recognition (Generator; both levels, bidirectional)

- **Patch-level:** (i) **release recording** — when a gated entry's condition reads true at a see-phase, the release is logged in that turn's record as part of the field (no new mechanism; the ledger-read already happens). (ii) **children don't get entries** — the viability gate's "children wait" is implicit (unenumerated routes are nothing yet; only EXPLICIT routes park). One clarifying line each.
- **Redesign-level:** none.
- **Bidirectional (the unification):** the finding template's COULD-gating ("Depends-on… GATED") and the ledger's `parked-as: gated-on(…)` now speak ONE state language across both artifact families — findings and ledgers become cross-readable. Noted as the assembly's emergent.

### 6. Domain Transfer (Generator; native source included)

- **6-native (task systems).** The someday/maybe-vs-waiting-for cut (1F) — one clause.
- **6-different (air traffic / law).** Holding patterns; stays-pending-appeal — REJECTED (budget; the package needs no second analogy).

### 7. Extrapolation (Generator)

- **7G — the backlog query for free.** A state-tagged ledger is queryable: counting `gated-on(meaning-stability…)` entries = the materialization backlog per concept — a dashboard line with zero new machinery.
- **7F — Selector-rationale readiness** (2G's consequence at L2+).
- **7C — the mis-tag failure path** (1C): low-stakes, checkpoint-self-correcting; no enforcement needed.
