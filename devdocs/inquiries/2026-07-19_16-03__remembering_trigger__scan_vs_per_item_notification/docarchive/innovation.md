## P2 — The pipeline + the cost statement (finding-ready; shapes committed upstream)

**The pipeline.** The two modes are not rivals; they hand off — and his own sentence contains the handoff: *"I just kind of remembers"* (the cue-fired notice, unbidden, effortless) → *"maybe I should go back and check it out"* (the deliberate act) → *"and merge these contexts"* (the deliberate work the notice only initiates). Between notice and act sits the owned decision-slot, verbatim: *"whatever retrieval returns feeds a choice; nothing auto-runs."* The notification INITIATES; selection DECIDES; the deliberate mode EXECUTES.

**The cost derivation (why the difference is really important — his claim, confirmed with owned lines).** Scanning pays at READ time, per past item, out of the mover's span — "retrieval costs grasp" — and grows with the past's size (quadratic at full form: the spiral verdict). Resonance pays at WRITE time (links, cues, distinctive encodings, registered conditions) plus a small per-HIT noticing cost — it is not free: a hit (including a false hit) costs attention to receive and judge. Two precisions: **resonance replaces the FINDING cost, not the RETRIEVING cost** (the going-back still pays grasp; what's saved is the per-item search to learn that something relevant exists); and **the deep distinction is WHOSE BUDGET PAYS** — the mover's grasp (scarce, span-bounded) vs the substrate's structure (cheap, standing) — not serial-vs-parallel. An implemented lookup still computes, but it spends substrate budget; at the mover's ledger, resonance is scan-free.

**The consequence: a writing discipline.** Resonance fires only on encoding-overlap — a past thing encoded without the cues its future contexts will present NEVER fires (the miss side; failed reminding is common). So if dissolved context is ever to return unbidden, **the record must be written resonance-ready: encode with the cues future contexts will actually present** — links, distinctive phrasings, conditions stated in the vocabulary tomorrow's runs will carry. The architecture question turns out to decide how to WRITE, not how to read.
