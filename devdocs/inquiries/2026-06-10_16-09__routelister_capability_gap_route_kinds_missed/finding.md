*Context for a reader arriving fresh: routelister is the project's "see" discipline — given a territory (typically a finished inquiry's artifacts) and a goal, it enumerates typed routes ("directions one could take"), which the chooser (the Selector role — today the human) picks from. ### 1. The gap defined: three operations, one distinction, one architecture statement

**What enumeration is:** routelister sweeps a bounded territory and casts the concepts IT CONTAINS as typed routes, under a fixed nine-verb vocabulary — and, by deliberate spec identity, *"does not invent directions that the territory does not contain"* (the hallucination guard), receives its goal rather than forming one, and produces no control-flow moves. These exclusions are protective, not defects.

**What your mind adds at choose-time — the three operations:**
- **Collision:** your knowledge meets the artifact and produces a loaded objection. The territory cannot contain it, because half of the collision lives in you.
- **Generation:** you invent a candidate alternative that exists nowhere until you say it.
- **Rebinding:** you change what counts as the territory — jumping from one inquiry's folder to the whole corpus, or to an outside discipline — and perceive at the new scope.

**Slot-vs-load:** the spec's TEST and REFRAME verbs mean the unloaded FORMS of challenge and reframe are listable ("TEST the verdict" / "REFRAME this concept"). The LOADED forms — the actual objection, the actual candidate — require collision or generation. An unloaded slot rarely moves a chooser.

**The composite field (the architecture statement this corpus never wrote down):** what the Selector actually chooses from = enumerated concept-routes (routelister) + control-options (the Selector's own vocabulary: go-deeper, widen, different-approach, merge, revisit, unblock, stop) + supplement-routes (today: the human). The two vocabularies do not collide — routelister's verbs prescribe engagement with CONCEPTS; the Selector's moves steer the PROCESS. The gap stayed invisible precisely because you topped the field up by hand every turn. *(A gated candidate one-liner for canon, awaiting the naming veto and the user's seat: "The Selector's field is composite: enumerated concept-routes + control-options + supplement-routes — the third share currently human.")*

**One honest symmetry line:** the human also misses kinds routelister catches — systematic sweeps list the unglamorous due-diligence routes (consolidation, maintenance TEST-slots) that salience-driven attention skips *(plausible-typical, not verbatim-anchored: the corpus records what you picked, not what you skipped — observing the skip-class is part of what the tally is for)*.
