## Phase 2 — Generate (7 mechanisms × 3)

### 1. Lens Shifting (Framer)

- **1G — referral-letter lens.** The finding's teaching skeleton: symptom (the field is weaker than the operator's mind) → ruled out (organ defect; wrong organ) → diagnosis (a solitary organ doing a plural job) → referral (the fix inquiry, with requirements attached). Differential-diagnosis form keeps the routelister-is-innocent-with-precision tone.
- **1F — naming lens (focused on the kinds).** Durable names for the four hard-miss kinds, for use in measurement tags and the fix inquiry: **challenger routes** (collision-loaded objections), **reframe routes** (invented alternatives), **gestalt routes** (rebinding/cross-corpus patterns), **import routes** (external theory brought in). Short, kind-table-safe (each names a ROUTE class, not an organ or seat).
- **1C — the flattery adversary (contrarian).** "The diagnosis flatters the human: maybe supplements are rare and low-value; enumerated moves did the real work." Checked against the corpus: the supplements produced the project's INFLECTION events — the 2-shape answer correction, the MQ→ambiguities reframe, the canon demotion + Turn Architecture, the gradient corrigendum, the horizontal-dive pattern naming, the Expedition goal itself. Routine progress is enumeration-heavy; course-changes are supplement-heavy. **Adopted as the punctuation insight: supplements are LOW-FREQUENCY, HIGH-CONSEQUENCE** — expeditions without them do the gradualism fine and miss the punctuations.

### 2. Combination (Generator)

- **2G — taxonomy × the turn record.** The measurement's WHERE: when the human injects a move at an expedition checkpoint, the turn record's freeform content gains a one-line **supplement tag** (kind-named per the taxonomy's rubric). No new artifact; rides what the Expedition already writes.
- **2F — usage-gaps × run modes.** The two usage-gaps close with ZERO new capability — the spec's existing concept-space mode lists divergence-flags and depth-dive routes when someone RUNS it. Stated as diagnosis (the gap is usage); the caller-recipe itself stays in the fix space (one sentence in the seed, not a procedure here).
- **2C — the composite field × the canon gap.** The field statement is the architecture's missing sentence — drafted as the candidate one-liner for an eventual (gated, user's-seat) canon attachment: *"The Selector's field is composite: enumerated concept-routes (routelister) + control-options (the Selector's own vocabulary) + supplement-routes (today: the human; no organ exists)."*

### 3. Inversion (Framer; depth-checked)

- **3G — the symmetry inversion.** "Routelister misses kinds the human catches" → invert: **the human misses kinds routelister catches.** True, and one honest line keeps the finding from reading as tool-bashing: systematic sweep lists routes attention skips (completeness vs salience); the composite field needs BOTH organs. Adopted.
- **3F — the measurement inversion (focused; the round's biggest gain).** "Count supplements during future expeditions" → invert: **count them retrospectively, NOW** — the corpus's 78 CONTINUES-FROM chains already record the human's actual next-moves; tallying each chain-step against the taxonomy gives the gap's BASE RATE from data that already exists, before any expedition runs. Adopted: the measurement plan becomes two-part — **(A) retrospective corpus tally (available today)** + **(B) prospective expedition counting (the supplement tags)**.
- **3C — the seed inversion (contrarian).** "The fix = build the missing organ" → invert: **the fix = build nothing** — keep the human as the supplement organ permanently (the centaur position): autonomy runs on enumerable moves; supplements stay a human privilege channeled through interrupts and turn-seeds. A legitimate design pole, consistent with the gates. Adopted: the seed names TWO poles (organ-building vs centaur-formalization) and hands the fix inquiry the decision criterion from 7F — it does not presume organ-building.

### 4. Constraint Manipulation (Framer; both directions)

- **4-ADD (generic).** Each taxonomy card carries four fields: the verbatim corpus anchor · the OPERATION it needs (collide/generate/rebind) · the **current best substitute** (an unloaded slot / an unrun mode / none) · the measurement tag-name. The substitute column makes the cards actionable for measurement without designing fixes.
- **4-ADD (focused).** One screen per kind — cards stay compact.
- **4-REMOVE (generic).** Drop the by-design section (hard misses only)? → loses the field-composition honesty and re-invites the empire-creep into the Selector's and human's seats that the Turn Architecture just settled. Fails.
- **4-REMOVE (contrarian).** Fold the half-listable kind into hard-or-usage? → loses the split that makes epistemic probes actionable at the right binding. Fails.

### 5. Absence Recognition (Generator; both levels, bidirectional)

- **Patch-level:** (i) the package lacked a **frequency/consequence statement** — added via the punctuation insight (1C). (ii) Lacked a **downstream-purpose line**: the taxonomy serves three consumers — the fix inquiry (as requirements), the measurement (as rubric), the Expedition (as risk register). One sentence each.
- **Redesign-level:** none — the four-piece shape holds.
- **Bidirectional (already-present):** the corpus has a supplement-recording cousin — `devdocs/improvement_observations.md`'s run-end observation lines (human-injected metadata about runs). The supplement tags rhyme with it; noted as precedent, not merged.

### 6. Domain Transfer (Generator; native source included)

- **6-native (software).** **Code review's two channels:** linters enumerate known patterns; human reviewers collide outside knowledge with the diff ("this breaks under X"). The industry's answer to linter limits was never "smarter linters alone" — it was the two-channel review, lately a third (LLM reviewers). Maps exactly onto enumerator + human + (possible) generator organ. The finding's one analogy.
- **6-different (immunology).** Innate (pattern-matching) vs adaptive (novel-antigen response) immunity — apt but REJECTED: the analogy slot is taken (inflation guard).
- **6-different (security).** The red team — institutionalized collision is historically its OWN unit, never merged into the surveyor's job. One supporting line inside the seed's organ-pole. Adopted-as-line.

### 7. Extrapolation (Generator)

- **7G — architecture stability under either pole.** If the organ pole is ever built, the composite field becomes three-organ and the kind-table gains one row — the turn definition is already organ-agnostic (the field is whatever is WRITTEN before the pick). The Turn Architecture survives both fix poles unchanged. One reassuring line.
- **7F — the tally as the fix's decision criterion.** The retrospective count converts the fix inquiry from taste to data: if supplements are ~5% of moves but most inflections → organ-building is high-value; if rarer/lower-impact → centaur formalization suffices. The diagnosis hands the fix inquiry its own gate.
- **7C — the do-nothing path.** Without any fix: expeditions stay supervised indefinitely (the human channel as permanent component) — viable at small scale; caps between-inquiry autonomy near Level 2; the cost is stated, not dramatized.
