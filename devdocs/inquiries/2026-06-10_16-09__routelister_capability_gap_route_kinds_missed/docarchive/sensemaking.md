## Phase 3 — Ambiguity Collapse

#### Ambiguity A1: What exactly is the gap ("not so strong as my mind"), operationally?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** the gap is just scale/quality — routelister lists shallowly; a better prompt or more cycles would close it; no structural story needed.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** the missed moves differ in KIND, not degree: no amount of better sweeping over the gradient finding's folder produces "reasoning-as-pre-step-evaluation contradicts your oracle row" — the objection's content lives in knowledge the territory does not contain. Enumerate-not-invent is quoted spec text, not a quality setting. The corpus's hard cases all require one of three operations (collide / generate / rebind) that perception-of-the-given-territory cannot perform at any quality level.

**Confidence:** HIGH. **Resolution:** the gap = **routes whose content requires collision (outside knowledge × territory), generation (invented alternatives), or rebinding (scope/abstraction shifts) — three operations routelister deliberately does not perform.** Plus a softer second gap: enumeration that IS possible but never requested (stance, binding, depth runs). **No longer allowed:** treating the gap as a routelister quality knob.

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** list all eight surfaced candidates as routelister failures — the user asked for kinds it fails to list, and it lists none of them.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** three of the eight are moves the ARCHITECTURE deliberately houses elsewhere (control-moves → the Selector's option-vocabulary; goal-formation → the human's retained seat; operator-state → Selector input). Calling them routelister failures would re-tangle the kind-table the Turn Architecture just settled and aim the fix inquiry at organs that aren't broken. **Confidence:** HIGH. **Resolution — the taxonomy:**
- **Four hard-miss kinds (the gap proper):** **(1) Collision-loaded challenge routes** (K1b — outside knowledge contradicts the territory; e.g., the gradient objection); **(2) Invented-alternative routes** (K2 — novel candidate framings; e.g., the MQ→ambiguities reframe); **(3) Rebinding routes** (K3 — cross-corpus gestalts; e.g., "code is pregnant"); **(4) External-import routes** (K6 — outside theory brought to bear; e.g., the gradient-descent correlation probe).

#### Ambiguity A3: Where do the causes live (kinds-plus-causes — the depth question)?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** causes belong to the fix inquiry; including them violates the user's understanding-first sequencing.

#### Ambiguity A4: Does the Selector's option-vocabulary overlap routelister's verbs (the layer question)?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** "different-approach" (Selector) and REFRAME (routelister) are the same move listed twice — the vocabularies collide and one should absorb the other.
