#### P1 — Identity + 5 operations + intra-discipline ordering

**Principal candidate (authorable content):**

> *Identity:* cites P0's verb-meaning sentence.
>
> *Per-operation mechanism descriptions* (one paragraph each):
>
> - **Itemize** — input: the raw task statement. Mechanism: split the statement into distinct atomic items, where each item is one coherent ask. A statement that bundles multiple asks (e.g., "design X and also document Y") yields multiple items; a statement that's already a single ask yields one item. Output: an ordered list of 1 or more items.
>
> - **Meta-question** (per item) — input: one item. Mechanism: apply the canonical Meta-question set (P3) to the item, producing per-question answers. These answers serve as signals to downstream consumers (signaling whether the item needs external context) AND as constraints on the per-item Rephrase operation (preventing rephrasings from locking meaning in the wrong space). Output: a structured set of meta-question answers per item.
>
> - **Deconstruct** (per item) — input: one item. Mechanism: analyze the item into its constituent parts. At minimum: subject (what the item is about), action (what cognitive operation it asks for), deliverable-shape (what form the result takes). Output: a structured parts-record per item.
>
> - **MultiScope** (per item) — input: one item. Mechanism: produce versions of the item at multiple scales. At minimum: a small-scope version (the narrowest defensible interpretation) and a big-scope version (the widest defensible interpretation). The meta-question answers inform what "scope" means for this particular item. Output: 2 or more scope-versions per item.
>
> - **Rephrase** (per item, last) — input: one item + its Meta-question answers. Mechanism: produce alternative formulations of the item — different vocabularies, different emphases, implicit-rendered-explicit — constrained by the Meta-question answers so as not to lock meaning in the wrong space. Output: 2 or more rephrasings per item.
>
> *Intra-discipline ordering (4-stage flow):*
>
> 1. **Stage 1 (statement-level):** Itemize.
> 2. **Stage 2 (per item):** Meta-question.
> 3. **Stage 3 (per item, parallel):** Deconstruct + MultiScope. Both are item-internal analyses informed by the same Meta-question answers; they have no dependency on each other.
> 4. **Stage 4 (per item, last):** Rephrase. Constrained by the Meta-question answers from Stage 2.
>
> *Ordering rationale:* Itemize first because the rest are per-item operations. Meta-question second per item because its answers constrain Stage 4 (Rephrase) AND inform Stage 3 (Deconstruct + MultiScope). Stage 3's two operations parallelize because both consume the same Meta-question answers and produce independent outputs. Rephrase runs last because its content depends on the Meta-question constraint (per-item).
>
> *Per-item granularity:* Task-Define operates per item after Itemize splits. Cross-item operations are excluded (see P8).

**Piece-Level Inversion candidate:**
- *Inverted assumption:* the intra-discipline ordering is fixed.
- *What follows:* if the ordering were not fixed — operations could fire in any order per item — Rephrase could fire BEFORE Meta-question, removing the load-bearing constraint that prevents meaning-lock. The safety mechanism (A7 from sensemaking) disappears. Rephrase output becomes free-floating; meta-question loses its constraint role.
- *Scrutiny:* the ordering is structural, not stylistic. Removing it unwinds the K2 + FP5 commitments from sensemaking.
- *Principal SURVIVES.*

#### P7 — Lightweight stance + 5 enforcement criteria

> 5. **No ecosystem-knowledge reach.** Task-Define does not fetch deprecated-spec lists, currency-of-references information, project-history awareness, or any other ecosystem-state. The substrate is bounded to the task statement + LLM internal context.

> *Application rule:* at structural-layer authoring time, every operation + output + sub-section must pass all 5 criteria. A violation is a defect; the structural spec author should reject the addition or refactor it.
