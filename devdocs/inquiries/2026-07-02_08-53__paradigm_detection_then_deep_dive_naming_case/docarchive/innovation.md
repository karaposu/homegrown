**Mechanism gloss (Domain Transfer, computing/statistics-native source per the source-domain guard):** paradigm-detection is **stratified sampling for generative work** — partition the candidate-space into strata (paradigms), sample deliberately within each, and you get coverage guarantees no amount of unstratified sampling (however many mechanisms/temperature) provides. This explains WHY it works without overclaiming.

**Combination (generic→focused):** the operation = routelister's *individuation* + surfacing's *possibility-case*, pointed at a to-be-generated space; the paradigm map is a **route-map over a generative space** — paradigms are the routes, diving is engaging, and the map never chooses (map-not-gate inherits the harness's enumerate-vs-decide identity wholesale).

### Q6 — The worked naming map (content-production; principal)

**The AXES (coordinates of naming-space):**

**The FAMILIES (inhabitable regions; the user's five, typed, + the composed one the AI never visited):**

**The demonstration the map makes visible:** F6 — where the human's winning name lives — is a **composed** family (non-default A2 × non-default A1). A families-only listing misses composed regions; the axes generate them. And the modal region (F1/F3, the "startuppy compound" default) is exactly where unscaffolded generation clusters. *The map is challengeable and extendable — it decides nothing (map-not-gate).*

**The domain-agnostic detection meta-questions (the instruments, for ANY divergent generative task):**
1. *Family-first probe:* "What are the fundamentally different WAYS one could approach making this — different enough that a practitioner would call them different schools?"
2. *Axis-first probe:* "Along which dimensions do candidates differ in KIND, not degree?"
3. *Modal-exposure probe:* "For each axis — what's the default value everyone assumes, and what are the non-default values?"
4. *Composition probe:* "Which combinations of non-default values form a coherent, inhabitable approach with recognizable exemplars?" ← the F6-catcher
5. *Prior-loosening probe:* "Which regions are conventionally strong for this KIND of task — and which unfashionable regions might fit THIS case?"
Bounds: 5–9 entries, one line each; stop when fresh candidates keep re-landing in existing families (a convergence signal mirroring innovate's own stop-rule).

**Lens Shifting (focused):** under what conditions is the MAP itself the deliverable? When the chooser is a human with taste: the map is the **steering wheel** — the human points ("dive F6"), the machine covers. This realizes, at the domain level, the intuition-vs-mechanism complementarity innovate's spec already commits ("the human provides direction; the AI provides coverage").

**Inversion (voluntary; deliverable-form):** "the map should be a standalone reference doc, not a finding section." What follows: a reusable naming-paradigms doc — but premature at N=1 use; **DEFERRED** with trigger (map reused ≥2 times → extract to a reference doc). Finding-section form survives for now.
