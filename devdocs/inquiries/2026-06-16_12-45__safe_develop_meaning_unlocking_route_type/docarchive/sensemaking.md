Adding it as a verb also breaks the spec's explicit "small fixed vocabulary" design and sets a bloat precedent. The MTTP class exists *precisely* for multi-loop shapes.
**Confidence:** HIGH.
**Resolution:** it is a **multi-loop compositional pattern (MTTP candidate), not an engagement-type.** **Fixed:** home = the pattern class. **No longer allowed:** "add a 10th routelister verb." **Depends:** the routelister side gets a *pointer* (attribute), not a verb.

MTTP says members are "named by shape" — safe-develop has a distinct shape + trigger, so it earns a name as a **specialization**.
**Confidence:** MEDIUM (the instance-vs-member line is genuinely soft).
**Resolution:** a **named specialization of Branch-and-Synthesize** — "Meaning-First Staged Development" / keep the user's "Meaning-Unlocking Develop"; admit at **candidate / research-frontier** tier (N=1, no empirical instance) per the MTTP 5-criterion test.
