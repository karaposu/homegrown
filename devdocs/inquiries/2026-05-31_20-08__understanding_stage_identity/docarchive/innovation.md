## Meaning-level Brainstorm (Generator-weighted — the user's ask; process/structural still deferred)

*These are meaning-level shape ideas, not process/structural commitments — flagged for the deferred design inquiry.*

- **The composition shape (internal flow, sketched):** comprehend-the-input → (perceive N requests + order) → [hand each to framing/branch-creation] → verify-each-framing-vs-intent → (re-frame on mismatch). So the stage *brackets* branch-creation: it comprehends + splits *before*, and verifies *after* the framing is written. (The verify-after is what catches *wrongly-phrased*, which a before-only stage couldn't.)
- **Naming the role (it's a stage, so name the role/purpose, not a cognitive verb):** candidates — **intake** (the front-door that takes in + shapes the request), **input-comprehension stage**, **framing-prep**, **pre-flight** (check before the loop "takes off"), or keep the user's **understand(ing) stage**. *Lean:* **intake** or **understand-stage** — "intake" cleanly signals "a front-door role that composes," avoiding the "new cognitive discipline" misread; "understand-stage" honors the user's word while the "-stage" suffix marks it as composition, not a discipline.
- **Thin-to-mature trajectory:** early it can be *comprehend + a light fidelity check*; the *request-split* + the *spawn-parallel/sequential* machinery mature as the bundled-request failures recur. (Phase-dependent; matches K15.)
- **The two-half observation:** the stage arguably has two separable halves — a *fidelity half* (comprehend + verify the framing) and a *shaping half* (split into requests). They could be one stage or two sub-stages; a meaning-level open question for the structural design.
