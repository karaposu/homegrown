**Prosecution:** the ~3.5k figure assumes 5 views (~1,250 lines) — but TODAY zero views exist; the honest today-number is 120 × 22 ≈ **2,640 lines, all frontier, no views**. Presenting 3.5k as "measured today" launders a projection.

The deeper honest point the prosecution surfaces: **views only PAY once consolidations shrink the frontier** — at zero ceremonies, the full-read is already feasible at summary grade; the formula's 10× claim is against the RAW read (34k), which stands.
**Verdict: REFINE → applied (R1):** the finding presents three labeled numbers — raw 34k (infeasible) / today's summary-grade frontier 2.6k (feasible NOW, zero views) / maturity projection ~3.5k with the frontier shrinking as ceremonies run.

### C2 — The frontier definition vs refines-semantics (the material catch)
**Prosecution:** measured live — the corpus's edges are **135 refines / 8 supersedes / 32 corrects**. `refines:` in this house EXTENDS: the prior's commitments still bind (today's own chain: the protocol constitution binds through three refinements). Edge-coverage treats all 46 targets as "content lives downstream — skip raw," which is TRUE for supersedes and corrects but only half-true for refines: the refined prior is compressed away while still binding.
**Defense/Collision:** the defense rides a house mandate, and honestly: every refining finding MUST carry an `Inherited Commitments Re-test` naming the prior's commitments as confirmed / revised / invalidated (CONCLUDE's rule). So reading the chain's NEWEST link — which the full-read always does (it's frontier or view-headed) — reads a document that by construction declares what it preserved from the compressed prior. Awareness-grade coverage therefore holds **exactly as far as the re-test discipline holds**. What does NOT hold: binding-grade reading (a worker needing the prior's full constraints) — but that was always topic-read territory (drill the chain).
**Verdict: REFINE → applied (R2):** the coverage definition gains its honest clause — *"edge-coverage is awareness-grade and rides the re-test mandate: a refined prior is safe to meet as one line because its refiner must carry its commitments; where a refiner skipped its re-test, the compression is unsafe — one more reason the mandate is load-bearing."* (The frontier definition itself stands; the semantics note prevents over-trust.)
