**Routelister is the corrected concept-as-route discipline, re-derived under a clean name so its identity is intrinsic by construction — carrying routeman's re-tested sound machinery, explicitly excluding routeman's loop-relational mistake, and superseding routeman.**

**OT1 — what routelister IS.** A standalone, domain-agnostic thinking discipline whose operation is: *identify the concepts in a territory that can be engaged as directions toward a goal, and list each as a typed, prescriptive route — enumerating all such concepts (leaning to inclusion), without selecting which to take.* Its unit is the route (a concept framed as a typed prescriptive direction: Direction[=concept] + Movement Type[=how to engage] + reachability + guidance). Its concept-target is "a thing in the territory worth drawing in as a route, whose engagement either advances the goal or sharpens the understanding the goal rests on (including a fuzzy goal itself)." Its identity is defined by this OPERATION — not by any position in a loop. (The name "routelister" names that operation: it lists routes.)

**OT2 — what routelister is NOT.** (a) NOT loop-bound — it does not require a completed cognitive cycle and is not defined by its position between cycles (the explicit exclusion of routeman's diagnosed mistake; it runs on any territory). (b) NOT a selector — it enumerates, never picks. (c) NOT an executor — it lists "refine X" / "develop Y" as routes; performing them is downstream disciplines' work. (d) NOT a model-builder (/comprehend) or relevance-tagger (/surfacing) — it frames concepts as prescriptive directions, not as a descriptive model or a relevance-ranked inventory. (e) NOT generating-from-nothing — routes are drawn from the territory's concepts, not invented beyond it. (f) NOT the goal-setter — the goal is received (even if fuzzy).

**OT3 — what is EXPECTED of it as a discipline.** The full discipline anatomy (canon): a **spec** with Definition/Philosophy (the verb-meaning + transform + the NOT-list), Structural Components (its own — concept-identification, route-framing/typing via the carried movement taxonomy, the two-axis admission test, adaptive guidance), Process Model (receive state+goal → identify concepts → frame/type as routes → attach guidance → assemble), Failure Modes (its own — including collapse-into-descriptive, skip-the-abstract, over-generalization, and the carried routeman LAYER-1/2 modes after re-test), and Coverage Strategy (enumerate-all / asymmetric-failure / convergence); and an **output** with Transform (the Route Map), Progression, Telemetry, Frontier. Plus the four universal properties (telemetry, frontier-awareness, failure-modes, self-application) and standalone+domain-agnostic per canon. It fills the forward-Boundary taxonomy slot BY ROLE (a runner-owned composition fact), NOT as part of its identity.

**OT4 — is the from-scratch rename structural or cosmetic?** **Structurally justified — conditionally.** It is structural if and only if: (a) routelister's identity is **re-derived** from the corrected findings (concept-as-route + two-axis), not copied from routeman's spec; (b) routeman's **sound machinery is carried after re-test** (the 16-type movement taxonomy, the asymmetric-failure/enumerate-all principle, the Route Map output, the typed-reachability + adaptive-guidance mechanisms — which the diagnosis found mature), not discarded; and (c) routeman is **superseded + archived** to `non-active/`, not left to coexist. The rename's value is the *forced clean re-derivation* (a clean spec built from the corrected, intrinsic identity has no defective sections to half-patch — so the loop-relational mistake cannot return) plus the supersession (one discipline, no doubled baggage). The value is NOT the label itself. Absent (a) the rename is cosmetic (the defect rides along); absent (b) it over-rejects and loses mature work + the three inquiries' corrective gains; absent (c) it doubles the baggage it was meant to escape. This is the 2nd application of the project's rename-as-design-act methodology (after /navigation→routeman); executed under the three conditions, it confirms the methodology generalizes.

**The crucial nuance (why "can't trust routeman fully" doesn't mean "trust nothing"):** the diagnosis already localized routeman's defect to its IDENTITY, not its machinery. So the calibrated distrust is: **distrust the identity (re-derive it clean); re-test-and-carry the machinery; trust-but-re-test the recent corrected findings.** Routelister is therefore not a blank-slate reinvention — it is the corrected identity expressed cleanly + the mature machinery carried forward + routeman retired.
