## The Answer (scope of Inquiry Elaboration, meaning layer)

**Decision rule (C*):** a job is IN iff it operates on the **request/inquiry-framing** (object) AND it **perceives/emits** rather than acts (mode). Out otherwise.

**ACHIEVE:** turn a raw, imperfect request into a **loop-ready, fidelity-verified inquiry framing** — an *elaborated-inquiry spec* (the articulated 5 meta-aspects + goal + scope), a *request-structure verdict* (single / N-parallel / N-sequential + order), and a *fidelity verdict* — emitted as a perception bundle, so the loop never runs on a misframed or wrongly-bundled query.

**COVER (in-scope) — three coupled, tailored phases on the request:**
1. **Comprehend & articulate** the request multilayer (real intent behind imperfect phrasing) → the framing content; **depth-limited** to "enough to frame faithfully," never a problem model.
2. **Perceive request-structure** — one request or several, parallel or sequential, and their order (from request-level signals, not problem-coupling).
3. **Verify framing-fidelity** — framing faithfully matches intent, across three axes: internal-consistency, faithfulness-to-user-input (incl. transcription + Source-Input preservation), external-validity of cited references.

**NOT COVER (borders, each = a C* failure):**
- understanding/stabilizing the **problem** → `/sense-making`
- partitioning the **problem** into pieces+interfaces → `/decompose`
- drawing items from a **territory** → `/surfacing`
- generating solution **candidates** → `/innovate`; judging them → `/td-critique`
- **spawning** inquiries / creating folders / **gating** branch-creation / **encoding** into `_branch.md` → the runner + `branch_inquiry`
- **meta-routing** (Layer-Commitment; Synthesis-Trigger→CONCLUDE) → the runner

**UPPER BOUND:** every in-scope phase is tailored + coupled; if a phase can only be realized by wholesale re-invocation of `/decompose` or `/td-critique`, IE has become a mini-runner — revisit.

**MIGRATION (principle; exact list deferred to structural):** comprehension + fidelity move INTO IE (the 5-meta-aspect shaping, Source-Input, Scope-Check, Step 3.5 transcription audit, Step 3.6 reference-authority audit); orchestration STAYS with the runner (spawn, Synthesis-Trigger, Layer-Commitment). branch.md thins to encoding + orchestration.
