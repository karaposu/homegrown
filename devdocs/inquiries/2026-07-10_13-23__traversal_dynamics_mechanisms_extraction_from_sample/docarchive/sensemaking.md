## Phase 3 — Ambiguity Collapse

### Ambiguity 1 — Is consolidation-first the honest frame, or a flattening of the user's ask?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** the user said "extract mechanisms we can build" — a consolidation catalog under-delivers invention; the sample is rich enough to deserve new designs.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** the readiness states are file-verified (C1) — presenting half-built things as new inventions would falsify provenance and duplicate seeds/designs (violating the seed cross-ref discipline and the June-22 decomposition). And the frame does NOT zero out invention: the absent-list survived adversarial checking from both sides (perspectives a+b) and frame-exit rigor (the expectation-anchors absence verdict narrowed but held). "We can build" is served *more* literally by a catalog that says what is one command away vs one design away vs genuinely new.

**Confidence:** HIGH.
**Resolution:** consolidation-first, with the named absent-list as the invention tier.
**What is now fixed?** The five rungs as the spine; the absent-list as real.
**What is no longer allowed?** An invention-first catalog; re-minting seeded/designed mechanisms as new.
**What now depends on this?** The catalog's structure; the finding's honesty claims.
**What changed?** "Extract" is re-read as "surface + organize + complete," not "invent."

### Ambiguity 2 — What is a catalog ENTRY (granularity)?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** one entry per §6.5 requirement (six memory entries) — clean traceability to the sample.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** requirements are not buildable units — req-2 (recall) alone spans the write-half wiring + signature storage + a retrieval read; conflating them would produce entries nobody can act on (violating the deliverable's "what building it means" field). The seed index's own granularity rule (distinct anchor ⇒ distinct record) points the same way.

**Confidence:** HIGH (for the principle); the exact count stays open.
**Resolution:** **one entry = one buildable/adoptable unit** with one layer, one form, one rung, one payoff-timing — requirements map onto entries many-to-many via the overlap map.
**What is now fixed?** Entry = actionable unit. **No longer allowed:** per-requirement entries; family-blob entries. **Depends:** Decomposition's piece-cut; Innovation's per-entry development. **Changed:** the catalog's unit is settled before its membership.

### Ambiguity 3 — Does the first-move candidate (adopt routelog / create selections.md) pre-empt the June-22 deferred design?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** June-22 deferred the full choice-record design ("likely hybrid... deferred (gated)") — adopting routelog now commits half the design prematurely.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** the RLU finding (20-48) *post-dates* June-22 and already adjudicated adoption with corrections ("Verdict on routelog: ADOPT... routelog writes to `devdocs/selections.md` plus a bare pointer into `_route.md`") — the design decision is a settled prior; this dive's extend-don't-relitigate constraint (C2) forbids re-opening it. **Confidence:** HIGH on the design-settledness; the timing itself is deliberately NOT resolved (enumerate-never-select).
**Resolution:** first-move stays a CANDIDATE with its trigger-facts attached (zero turns; Tier-1; zero construction).
**Fixed:** the candidate's grounding. **Not allowed:** this dive choosing it. **Depends:** the offered build-order. **Changed:** the candidate is design-clean, purely a timing call.

### Ambiguity 4 — Does the catalog claim these should be built NOW?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** the operator performed all ten acts well; mechanizing now is cost without payoff (the cost view's challenge).

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** it fails only as a blanket claim — the payoff-timing split (perspective d) shows record-side mechanisms pay immediately (cheap, compounding, prerequisite-data for everything later; canon's telemetry-birth note) while detector-side ones pay at autonomy (canon's own severity note: masked-by-operator, scales-with-autonomy). The catalog asserts readiness and payoff-timing per entry; it does not assert "build now" globally.

**Confidence:** HIGH.
