## SV1 — Baseline

The user wants the record's meanings readable from geometry instead of text. Surfacing measured both sides: the demand (aboutness, backing, kind, conclusions, essentiality-mix, identity — all text-only today) and the supply (shape, texture, under-node volume, orientation, Z all free; position/color/size/elevation taken). The design question: bind gaps to free channels honestly, with the backing-fill (cubes) as first candidate, and settle what "modes and custom views" are architecturally.

## Phase 1 — Anchors

**Constraints:** the honesty rails (no fabrication; counts-never-scores; atlas-domain; no-LLM-at-render) · the LOD constraint (nodes hidden at FAR → per-node encodings live at MID/NEAR only) · the channel budget (composite-glyph cap ≤3–4 channels before noise) · the legend cost (unlabeled encodings decay into decoration; the app has NO legend today) · data tiers (B1 encodable-tonight / B2 derivable / B3 parse-pending / B4 aboutness-unshipped) · the overlay law (scoped: it governs the ROAD's grouping — "never a repositioning").

**Key insights:**
1. **The pennant precedent** — flag-height ∝ open-route count already ships: meaning-in-geometry is proven in-house, not hypothetical. The whole ask = generalize the pennant move.
2. **Pixels say WHEN/HOW-MUCH/HOW-RECENT/HOW-CONNECTED; text says WHAT-IT-IS/WHAT-IT-SAYS** — the measured complaint (12-task audit).
3. **Discrete stacked units read as counts** — the cube idea's honest form is MORE literal than sketched: N cubes = N countable things; continuous volume is both misread and score-shaped.
4. **Backing splits into two countable directions** — support-below (what this rests on) vs load-above (what rests on this).
5. **Z is spent meaninglessly** (lane = collision avoidance; shoulder side = parity; S-curve = aesthetics) — and in a meaning-dense display, *unused variation gets read as meaning*: today's arbitrary Z variation is a latent lie surface the moment users start reading geometry semantically.
6. **Aboutness is doubly absent** — zero pixels AND zero shipped data (tags unshipped; C2 unbuilt). It can be slotted, not shipped.

**Structural points:** the three LOD bands are three *meaning budgets* (FAR = aggregate/terrain/furniture; MID = per-node glyphs; NEAR = detail + text) · the binding = demand × supply × zoom × data-tier · modes = a composition architecture OVER bindings, not more bindings.

**Foundational principles:** the position-law ("a primary dimension needs the primary channel") · the ordered/unordered rule (quantities → ordered channels [size/height/count]; categories → nominal channels [shape/hue]) · redundancy is legitimate (pennant height + flyout count) · pixel provenance (every pixel mechanically derived from a parsed field).

**Meaning-nodes:** the channel ledger · the gap list · altını doldurmak (the foundation image) · custom views · the legend.

## SV2 — Anchor-informed

The ask resolves into: **generalize the pennant precedent into a semantic layer** — a phased binding table (which countable rides which free channel at which zoom), with the under-node foundation as the flagship new binding, plus a composition architecture for modes, plus the previously-unneeded legend. The insight that reframes SV1: the constraint structure (LOD budgets, data tiers, honesty rules) does most of the design work — the space of honest designs is small.

## Phase 2 — Perspectives

- **Technical:** all candidate encodings are InstancedMesh-cheap (the pennants prove the pattern at 231 nodes); a binding-picker is feasible *because* the fields are typed counts; 3D small multiples are expensive, but the scrub-strip proves cheap synchronized 2D companions.
- **Human/User:** the saying's image is literally "filled UNDERNEATH" — the empty under-node slot is its native home; a new glyph vocabulary is learnable once IF there's a key (maps work because legends exist); the de-texting articulation = the acceptance TEST (re-run the 12-task audit against the design).
- **Strategic:** design the SYSTEM so future data slots in without redesign — tags arrive → the reserved nominal channel fills; the parse ships → the B3 encodings activate. One-off encodings that must be re-designed when data arrives are waste.
- **Risk:** fabrication-by-aesthetics (arbitrary variation read as meaning — anchor 5); occlusion (under-node geometry can hide in terrain from low angles); double-encoding drift (same channel accidentally driven by two fields); legend absence → decoration; perspective size-distortion in 3D (near objects read bigger — count comparisons across road distance are unreliable; counts compare reliably only locally or via the flyout).
- **Resource:** the encodable-tonight set (B1+B2) already covers backing-below (docarchive count, events, iterations), backing-above (inbound chain edges, descendants), kind (flowType), essentiality-mix (openRoutes fields), venture extent — the design ships value before any new parse.
- **Definitional / internal consistency:** the overlay law read in its own words — it was settled about the *grouping lens* ("groups by canon area as OVERLAYS… never a repositioning") with position=time as the road's primary. It does NOT govern other *vehicles*: chains/flat already reposition as separate lenses beside the law. So: meaning-layers on the road = overlays (law-native); position-rebinding views = new LENSES (law-silent, precedented by chains/flat). The law needs no amendment — it needs its scope stated.
- **Frame-exit check** (gating fires: "meaning" is used across types in this dive's own structures): meaning-kinds enumerated project-wide — state/time/quantity/relation (served in-atlas), aboutness/quality/kind (this design's targets), and CONTENT-meaning in other homes (seeds/canon/memory — excluded by the atlas-domain rule: names+counts only) and PER-CLAIM backing inside a finding (source-support at claim grain — below node grain; the atlas renders node-grain; honest boundary, not a gap this design must fill). No re-location needed; the boundary is stated.
- **Phase/calibration:** count domains must be checked per encoding — docarchive 0–7 ✓, iterations 1–3 ✓, inbound edges 0–~10 (cap/bin like the pennants' min(count,8)), degree ≤16 (already log-capped). The relative-domain practice (staleness auto-fit) generalizes: discrete encodings bin, never rescale silently.

## SV3 — Multi-perspective

The design space is a **bounded table, not an open canvas**: three zoom budgets × four data tiers × a short list of free channels × honest-form rules. Two perspective yields reshape it: (1) the *no-false-signifiers* pressure — adding semantic geometry OBLIGATES cleaning or regularizing today's arbitrary variation (Z, S-curve read as meaning once users learn to read geometry); (2) the law survives by scope, dissolving the warm fork without amendment.

## Phase 3 — Ambiguity Collapse

#### Ambiguity 1: scope-of-"meanings" (which meanings are IN this design pass)
**Strongest counter:** design ONLY for the full future substrate (tags + parse) — a complete semantic layer in one pass, shipped when data arrives; partial now-bindings will need rework.
**Why it fails:** the phased structure is *additive by construction* — channels are reserved per meaning-kind, so tonight-bindings and future slots never collide; and B1/B2 already carry the user's own flagship (backing) — deferring everything to data-day forfeits shippable value for no design gain. The counter's "rework" premise is false under a reserved-channel table.
**Confidence:** HIGH. **Resolution:** the ask lands as a SYSTEM design with PHASED bindings: IN-NOW = backing (both directions, B1/B2), kind (flowType), essentiality-mix, venture extent, event-density; SLOTTED = re-tests/MUST-COULD-DEFERRED/triggers/citations (B3), aboutness (B4 — a reserved nominal channel); OUT = per-claim backing (below node grain), other homes' content (atlas-domain).
**Fixed:** the deliverable = a phased binding table + composition architecture. **No longer allowed:** all-or-nothing designs; unreserved ad-hoc channel grabs. **Depends:** D's piece structure; I's table content. **Model change:** scope↔endpoint MQA overlap resolved — the ask is the SYSTEM, with the cube candidate inside it.

#### Ambiguity 2: the backing direction (support-below vs load-above)
**Strongest counter:** encode BOTH as under-node stacks (two distinguishable unit types) — each is honest; diplomacy aside, more information.
**Why it fails (structural):** legibility — two stacked count systems under one node exceeds the glyph budget at settlement density (Chernoff cap) and the two directions have DIFFERENT natural geometry: support is *beneath* (the saying's literal image: what holds it up), load is *upon/toward* (consequence — already partially carried by size∝degree and inbound arcs). Duplicating load-above into a second under-stack double-encodes degree while crowding the one empty slot.
**Confidence:** HIGH. **Resolution:** the under-node slot belongs to SUPPORT-BELOW (the saying's own direction: pipeline completeness, events, iterations, re-tests-when-parsed); LOAD-ABOVE rides existing/refined channels (size, arcs — refinement open for I, e.g. distinguishing inbound-refinement from mere relatedness).
**Fixed:** under-node = support-below family. **No longer allowed:** a second under-stack for load-above. **Depends:** I's foundation design; the legend. **Model change:** altını doldurmak gets its literal geometry; "how solid" splits into two honestly-separate readings with separate homes.

#### Ambiguity 3: the "cubes instead" referent
**Strongest counter:** cubes replace the spheres — the node ITSELF becomes a stack of cubes; one object carries identity + backing.
**Why it fails:** it conflates two channels — the node's shape is the natural NOMINAL slot (kind: flow-type/node-kind), while backing is a QUANTITY (ordered); merging them spends the shape channel on a count and makes same-kind nodes look like different species at different backing levels (a category misread, violating the ordered/unordered rule). Also node-size already carries degree; cube-stack-as-node would fight it.
**Confidence:** HIGH. **Resolution:** cubes = discrete count-units UNDER the node (foundation courses); the node's own shape stays reserved for nominal kind (whether spheres stay or a shape vocabulary arrives is I's shape-channel question, adjudicated separately).
**Fixed:** foundation-under, not node-replacement. **No longer allowed:** count-driven node shapes. **Depends:** I's glyph design. **Model change:** the cube idea lands as the *first course* of a general foundation encoding.

#### Ambiguity 4: modes/custom-views vs the overlay law
**Strongest counter:** "custom views" implies full user freedom including repositioning the road — the law must be amended or it blocks the ask.
**Why it fails (structural):** the law's own text governs the road's *grouping* rendering ("overlays… never a repositioning; position=time is the settled primary"); repositioning vehicles ALREADY exist beside it as lenses (chains/flat) — the architecture the law lives in has always had law-free lenses. So custom views decompose without contradiction: meaning-LAYER toggles + presets = road overlays (law-native); any position-rebinding view = a new LENS (law-silent, precedented).
**Confidence:** HIGH. **Resolution:** modes architecture = overlay toggles + saved presets on the road; small multiples as synchronized 2D companions (scrub-strip precedent); position-rebinding only ever as a separate lens. The law stands unamended, its scope now stated.
**Fixed:** the law's scope; the composition shape. **No longer allowed:** silent law-breaking on the road; law-amendment without new evidence. **Depends:** I's modes design; the Inherited Commitments Re-test. **Model change:** the warm fork dissolves — modes are composition, not layout.

#### Ambiguity 5: FAR-zoom meaning (the narration layer)
**Strongest counter:** leave FAR as-is (terrain + text banners) — the 30s narration passed acceptance; don't spend design on a solved layer.
**Why it fails (partially — this collapse is a NARROWING, not a full kill):** the narration passed for STRUCTURE (terrain/pleats/beacon) but its CONTENT rides text (banners, chips — C3 measured); the user's complaint explicitly targets text-carried meaning; and venture-level pixels at FAR are the one place "holistic understanding of ventures" can live. BUT the counter is right that per-node encoding at FAR is impossible (nodes hidden) and text banners genuinely work.
**Confidence:** HIGH on the constraint; the specific FAR design stays OPEN for I.
**Resolution:** FIXED CONSTRAINT — FAR meaning is aggregate-only (settlement-glyphs ∝ venture countables, terrain skins, furniture); text banners stay until a pixel form proves itself (redundancy first, replacement only on felt acceptance).
**Fixed:** the FAR budget. **No longer allowed:** per-node glyph plans at FAR. **Depends:** I's settlement-glyph candidates. **Model change:** the holistic/venture goal localizes to FAR aggregate encodings.

**Load-bearing concept tests:** *binding table* — structural, not proxy (it IS the deliverable: demand×supply×zoom×tier). *Support-below/load-above* — the first matches the user's language ("how solid it is backed"/underneath); the second is loop-derived — labeled DERIVED, secondary. *The gap list (C4)* — specific-vs-pattern: derived from the complete 12-task frame (the standing usefulness definition), so pattern-complete within the frame; new tasks can add gaps (monitoring, not a hole).

## SV4 — Clarified

What's clear: the deliverable is a phased binding table + a composition architecture + a legend, under three zoom budgets; the foundation (support-below, discrete units) is the flagship; the law stands by scope; aboutness is slotted awaiting data. No longer viable: continuous-volume solidity, composite scores, cube-nodes, second under-stacks, per-node FAR plans, law amendment, aboutness-now.

## Phase 4 — Degrees-of-Freedom Reduction

**Fixed:** zoom bands as meaning budgets · phased tiers (now/slotted/out) · honest forms (discrete units for counts; ordered↔quantity, nominal↔category; bins capped like pennants) · the law's scope · a LEGEND is now REQUIRED (new encodings must be keyed — the first two shipped unlabeled; a third unlabeled one tips into decoration) · no-false-signifiers (semanticizing geometry obligates regularizing today's arbitrary variation or assigning it meaning).
**Eliminated:** continuous volume · composite indexes · node-shape-as-count · position rebinding on the road · LLM at render · aboutness before its data.
**Open for I/C:** the exact binding assignments (which B1/B2 countable → which channel) · the foundation's concrete geometry (courses/pilings/plinth) · the shape-channel question (kind vocabulary vs spheres) · settlement-glyph candidates at FAR · the modes composition (which toggles, preset shape, picker-or-not) · the legend's form · load-above's refinement.

## SV5 — Constrained

Solution space = fill a small table: ~6 in-now meanings × ~5 free channels × 3 zoom budgets, under stated form rules — plus one architecture choice (overlay toggles + presets, small-multiple companions optional) and one new UI obligation (the key/legend). Everything else is phased slots or named exclusions.

## Phase 5 — SV6 — Stabilized Model

**The atlas already knows how to do this — once.** Pennant-height ∝ open-count is a live meaning-in-geometry binding; the user's question generalizes it into a SEMANTIC LAYER: a phased binding table that assigns the measured gap-meanings (backing, kind, essentiality-mix, venture aggregates now; re-tests/verdict-counts when the parse ships; aboutness when tags flow) to the measured free channels (under-node volume, shape, texture, Z-reallocation, settlement geometry) within three zoom budgets (FAR aggregate-only, MID per-node glyphs, NEAR detail), under standing honesty rules given visual form (discrete units = counts; ordered/nominal fit; bins; provenance-pure pixels), with **the foundation under each node — altını doldurmak literalized as stacked discrete courses of support-below countables — as the flagship binding**, modes/custom-views as *composition over meaning-layers* (toggles + presets; lenses for anything that repositions; the overlay law intact by its own scope), and a **legend as a new first-class obligation**.

**Delta from SV1:** SV1 held an open canvas ("which channel for which meaning?"); SV6 holds a bounded, phased, law-compatible system where the constraint structure did the designing — plus two yields SV1 lacked: the no-false-signifiers obligation (arbitrary variation becomes a lie surface once geometry is semantic) and the legend obligation (the third unlabeled encoding tips into decoration).

**Saturation:** perspectives produced destabilizing anchors (risk → no-false-signifiers; definitional → law scope; human → legend) — not just confirmations; all 5 mandated ambiguities collapsed with counters tested; SV6≠SV1 structurally. **PROCEED.**
