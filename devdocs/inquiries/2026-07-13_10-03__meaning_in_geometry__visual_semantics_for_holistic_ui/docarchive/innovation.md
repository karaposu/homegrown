## P2 — THE FOUNDATION (flagship, worked)

**Geometry:** square slabs **0.66 × 0.14 × 0.66**, stacked with a 4% per-course inset (a subtle taper — masonry read); earth-stone palette (`#8a7a5f` family, road-native, NOT teal); the node sphere **sits on top of its stack** — total lift = 0.14 × courses (0 → ~1.0). One slab per canonical discipline file present in `docarchive/` (articulate_simple, surfacing, articulate_warm, sensemaking, decomposition, innovation, critique — 0–7). v2 (parse): a second course material (darker, chamfered) for re-tested-commitment count, binned min(n,3), stacked beneath the discipline courses (re-tests are the deepest footing).

**Why under survives its inversion (mandated: "under is the worst place on terrain — use fill-level inside, or beside"):** fill-INSIDE fails at scale — the node radius is 0.36–0.71; an internal fill level is sub-pixel at MID (radius 25–70), exactly where the encoding must read. BESIDE fails on crowding — the pennant pole owns the beside slot; a second beside-object blurs both. UNDER, done as a stack the node STANDS ON, is not buried in terrain (the old design floats nodes 0.45 above the surface; the stack occupies and extends that gap upward): at the rig's low-oblique angles (phi→1.35) stacks read as silhouettes against the ribbon; at overhead angles (phi→0.15) the square footprint distinguishes backed from bare; and the lift produces a free preattentive bonus — **well-backed findings literally stand taller**. The inversion improved the design (stack-as-plinth-lift, not sunk foundation) and then died. *(Camera-tested against road.jsx's actual rig: target y = elev+2.2, phi ∈ [0.15, 1.35], radius ∈ [6, 140]; MID band 25–70.)*

**Zero-state:** a node with an empty docarchive sits directly on the terrain — grounded, flat, visibly bare. Absence looks like absence (nothing rendered, nothing invented); superseded nodes keep their stacks (history stands) at their 40% opacity.

**Pennant differentiation:** flags = thin vertical pole + small cone, teal family, BESIDE the node; courses = flat square slabs, earth family, UNDER the node. Different silhouette, palette, position — no blur.

**Occlusion:** slabs sit ON the ribbon (never inside it); the stack's top course is always at the node's base (self-shadowing irrelevant at these sizes); back-arcs pass well above (lift ≥ 0.8+span·0.08).

**Scale (extrapolation, 500 nodes):** ≤ 7×500 slab instances in one InstancedMesh + the v2 material in a second — trivial; crowded widened days stay legible because slabs are thinner than the day-spread nudge (0.85).

*(5-test: novelty ✓ [a new binding, in-genre]; scrutiny ✓ [camera + occlusion + zero-state answered; every slab = one named real file — counts-never-scores native]; fertility ✓ [course-types extend to parse data; births task 13; a "sort by solidity" lens candidate]; actionability ✓ [one adapter field + one InstancedMesh]; mechanism-independence ✓ — three routes converge: combination (saying × empty slot × discrete units), domain transfer NATIVE cartography (graduated point symbols) + DIFFERENT construction (footings sized to load; plinths; the saying's own domain — buildings show their support by standing on it), and the discrete-unit principle from D4.)*
