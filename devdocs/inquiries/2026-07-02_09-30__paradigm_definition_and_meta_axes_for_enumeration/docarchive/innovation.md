## Phase 2 — Generate (per piece: principal + mandated Inversion + tests)

### Q1 — The definition (principal)

**The statement:**
> **A paradigm — relative to a practice — is a WAY the practice is done: a coherent, generative bundle of commitments, one of several distinct attractors in the practice's approach-space. Commitments on the inside; a recognizable family of outcomes on the outside.**

**The one-glance membership test (a runner applies it to any candidate family):**
1. **Bundle?** — does it commit ≥2 cohering choices? (a single choice is an *option*, not a paradigm)
2. **Generative?** — can you work NEW instances from it? (a pattern you can't generate from is a *style-description*)
3. **Anchored?** — can you point at an exemplar, and would a practitioner recognize/name it? (evidence, not essence — a nascent paradigm may lack both; their presence is the strongest detection signal)

**The NOT-list (with one-line reasons):** an **option** (one choice — no bundle) · a **style-description** (no generative capacity) · a **move/mechanism** (a transformation you APPLY, not a way you WORK — the TRIZ/innovate-mechanism contrast) · a **lens** (how you evaluate, not where you generate from — carried from the prior finding).

**Grain norm:** name paradigms at the **basic level** — where practitioners naturally talk ("microservices," not "software"; not "microservices-with-Kafka-on-K8s").

**Inversion (mandated: "re-heading a one-hour-old commitment is churn, not depth"):** what follows if region-headed stands? (i) practices whose outcomes aren't artifacts (skill-acquisition — Demo B) become unstatable; (ii) the probes' mechanics stay mismatched (they elicit approach-descriptions, then derive regions); (iii) causality stays inverted (the bundle explains the region). And the churn charge fails on inspection: **every clause of the prior definition survives** — "committed combination of generative choices" IS the bundle; "region of the candidate-space" IS the image; "inhabitable" reads better on the practice ("you can work within it"). **Rejected — with a refinement:** the finding must SHOW the clause-by-clause survival (a small continuity table) so the non-churn is visible, not asserted. *(5-test: survives; the continuity table is the constructive residue.)*

### Q2 — The scope (principal)

Paradigm-enumeration applies to **practices** — an agent-kind approaching a task-kind with genuine approach-variation. **Step-zero:** *"Whose practice, doing what?"* Honest failures exist: "paradigms of the number seven" survives no recast; "paradigms of rocks" resolves only by changing the subject to a practice (classifying rocks → genetic vs compositional vs textural classification — real paradigms, belonging to the classifying). The prior finding's divergent-generative gate = this scope's special case for the innovate hook.

**Inversion (mandated: "too restrictive — 'paradigms in physics' / 'paradigms of consciousness' are legitimate object-domain asks"):** steelman inspected: "the Newtonian paradigm" is a paradigm of DOING physics (Kuhn's original case is a practice-paradigm!); "paradigms of consciousness" = paradigms of EXPLAINING consciousness (functionalism, dualism, IIT — ways of theorizing). Every legitimate-sounding object-domain ask resolves, on inspection, to an implicit practice. **The inversion strengthens the scope rather than breaking it — with a refinement:** step-zero usually CONFIRMS an implicit practice rather than repairing an error; the finding words it as a clarifier, not a rejection ("which practice did you mean? — usually obvious"). *(Survives.)*

### Q3 — The trichotomy (principal)

Circularity-kill recorded: categories-as-families would use the output to generate the output. Vocabulary guard: **approach-axes** (paradigm-space dimensions) ≠ **answer-axes** (the Horizontal Dive pattern's configuration dimensions).

**Inversion (role-assignment reversed: "the three words are loose synonyms — collapse to 'axes'"):** what follows: the membership test and the cross-topic recurrence lose their names and reappear unnamed inside "axes" prose — the collapse doesn't remove the roles, it hides them. Each word carries non-redundant work; and answering the user's own three words directly is the friendlier shape. **Rejected.** *(Survives.)*

### Q5 — The pipeline as probes 0–5 (principal)

**Pipeline:** step-zero → facet-walk → axes → default/non-default values → coherent + exemplified combinations (filtered by the membership test) → **the map** (families + axes; 5–9 entries; stop when fresh candidates re-land).

**The probes (glance-level):**
- **0 — scope:** "Whose practice, doing what?" (topic-is-a-thing → "doing what with it?")
- **1 — schools:** "What would practitioners call the fundamentally different WAYS of doing this?"
- **2 — axes via the categories:** "Walk the five: material? unit? move? relation? criterion? — what dimensions of variation does each expose?"
- **3 — defaults:** "Per axis — the default everyone assumes, and the non-defaults?"
- **4 — composition:** "Which non-default combinations cohere AND have real exemplars?"
- **5 — loosening:** "Which regions are fashionable for tasks like this — and which unfashionable ones fit THIS case?"

**Inversion (mandated: "prompt-fluff — 'list the schools' gets 90%"):** tested on the anchor case: probe 1 alone yields naming's F1–F5 (the standard families) and **misses F6** — the composed region where the actual winner lived. Probe-1-only reproduces the MODAL map: it inherits community fashion (probe 5 exists to loosen exactly that) and sees no composed families (probe 4's job). The single question gets 90% of the ENTRIES and misses the under-explored regions that are the operation's entire point. **Rejected — with the refinement that the finding says this explicitly:** probe 1 is the anchor; probes 2–5 exist for what probe 1 cannot see. *(Survives.)*
