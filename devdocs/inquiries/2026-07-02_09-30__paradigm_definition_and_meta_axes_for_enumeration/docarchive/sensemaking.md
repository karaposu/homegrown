## Phase 3 — Ambiguity Collapse

#### Ambiguity A1: Is a paradigm the practice (way-of-working), the image (artifact-region), or a two-sided unity — and which side heads the definition?
**Strongest counter-interpretation:** "Region-first suffices operationally — detection inspects candidates and their families; practice-talk adds nothing a detector can use, and the prior finding worked fine with region as head."
**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** (i) the detection probes themselves elicit PRACTICE descriptions — "different schools," "ways one could approach making this" — and derive the region from them; (ii) composed families are found by composing CHOICES (probe 4 operates on commitments, not on surveys of artifacts); (iii) practices without artifact-spaces (learning a skill; understanding a codebase) have paradigms but no artifact-region — region-first cannot even state them, while practice-first covers both (the image generalizes from "artifact-region" to "recognizable family of outcomes"); (iv) causality runs practice→image (the bundle explains the region, never vice versa).
**Confidence:** HIGH.
**Resolution:** two-sided unity, **practice-headed**: the paradigm IS the coherent generative bundle of commitments; its IMAGE is the recognizable family/region of outcomes. The prior's definition survives as the image-side reading (its own words already contained both sides; the head noun moves).
**Fixed:** the definitional head. **No longer allowed:** region-only definitions. **Depends:** the scope resolution (A5); the re-test entry for the prior's definition. **Model change:** "inhabitable" now reads on the practice ("you can work within it") — cleaner than inhabiting a region.

#### Ambiguity A2: Are the six ingredients necessary criteria or family-resemblance features?
**Strongest counter-interpretation (i):** all six necessary — a crisp checklist beats graded vagueness.
**Why (i) fails:** community-recognition fails necessity (a new paradigm precedes its name — the first practitioner has one); exemplar-anchoring fails strict necessity for nascent paradigms (though a paradigm without even a thought-exemplar is hard to individuate); plurality is a property of the FIELD (paradigms come as alternatives), not of one paradigm.
**Strongest counter-interpretation (ii):** pure family resemblance — nothing necessary (Wittgenstein all the way).
**Why (ii) fails:** two ingredients do real boundary work and cannot be dropped: **commitment-bundle** (≥2 cohering choices) is what separates a paradigm from a mere OPTION (dark-mode-vs-light-mode is a choice; agile is a paradigm), and **generative capacity** is what separates a paradigm from a mere style-DESCRIPTION (you can work new instances from it; alchemy retains the capacity structurally even with no practitioners). Removing either admits things nobody calls paradigms in any sense.
**Confidence:** HIGH on the two-necessary core; MED on the exact placement of exemplar/community (typicality vs evidence — the distinction matters little operationally).
**Resolution:** **hybrid logical form** — two necessary ingredients (coherent commitment-bundle; generative capacity), two typicality/evidence features (exemplar-anchoring; community-recognition — the strongest DETECTION signals), one entailment (constrains-and-enables follows from bundle-ness), one field-property (plurality belongs to the approach-space, not the individual paradigm).
**Fixed:** the definition's logical form + the option-vs-paradigm boundary. **No longer allowed:** all-six checklists; structure-free resemblance. **Depends:** the attributes station of the trichotomy (A3); the kit's coherence filter. **Model change:** the definition gains a sharp NOT-list entry (option ≠ paradigm; style-description ≠ paradigm; move/mechanism ≠ paradigm per the TRIZ contrast).

#### Ambiguity A3: The trichotomy — what are "categories," "attributes," and "axes"?
**Strongest counter-interpretation:** categories = the FAMILIES themselves (the map's rows; the paradigms).
**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** the user's phrase is "meta paradigm categories/attributes/axes that can be used to GENERATE enumeration" — generator-side machinery. Families are the enumeration's OUTPUT; reading categories as families makes the user's sentence circular (using the output to generate the output). The generator-side reading assigns each word a distinct working role with no leftovers.
**Confidence:** HIGH.
**Resolution:** three ROLES in one generator pipeline — **attributes** = the paradigm-hood criteria (A2's settled form: what makes a candidate a paradigm at all — the membership test); **axes** = the given topic's dimensions of approach-variation (Zwicky parameters; topic-specific); **categories** = the recurring axis-KINDS — **meta-facets** — that recur across topics and are used to FIND a topic's axes (the only genuinely "meta" member, matching the user's "meta paradigm categories").
**Fixed:** the trichotomy. **No longer allowed:** treating the three words as synonyms; categories-as-families. **Depends:** A4 (the meta-facet set must actually exist). **Model change:** the pipeline becomes statable: meta-facets → axes → values → coherent combinations → families (the map).

#### Ambiguity A4: Does a recurring, domain-agnostic meta-facet set actually exist — and what is it? (Derived, not asserted.)
**Derivation across three unlike topics.** Naming's axes: identity-semantics (what the name speaks about), source-language (material drawn from), morphology (the forming operation), transparency (the output's relation to its audience). App-architecture's axes (monolith / microservices / serverless / event-driven): decomposition-unit, communication-mode, state-management, operational-responsibility. Learning-a-skill's axes (drill / immersion / project-based / mentorship): unit-of-practice (sub-skill vs whole-task), feedback-source, sequence (parts→whole vs whole→parts), knowledge-entry (theory-first vs practice-first), social-structure.
**The kinds that recur:**
1. **SOURCE / MATERIAL** — what the approach draws on (language pools · infrastructure substrate · prior knowledge/theory)
2. **UNIT / GRAIN** — the approach's working unit (morpheme/word · service/function · sub-skill/whole-task)
3. **OPERATION / MOVE** — the characteristic forming action (compound/mutate · decompose/orchestrate · drill/immerse)
4. **COORDINATION / RELATION** — how parts or participants relate (word-parts' semantic relation · service communication · learner-teacher-environment structure)
5. **CRITERION / VALUE** — what counts as good inside the approach (phonetic appeal vs descriptiveness · scalability vs simplicity · speed vs depth) — special: paradigms carry their OWN success definitions (Kuhn's deepest point, and why cross-paradigm comparison is hard)
6. *(secondary)* **SEQUENCE / TEMPORAL SHAPE** — the approach's ordering (parts-first vs whole-first; build-then-split vs split-first) — strong in learning/architecture, weak in naming
7. *(secondary)* **RESPONSIBILITY-LOCUS** — who/what carries the load (self vs platform; solo vs mentored) — strong where agents/infrastructure vary
**Strongest counter-interpretation:** the "recurrence" is pattern-matching after the fact — with enough squinting any axis maps to any kind (the procrustean risk realized).
**Why the counter partially STANDS (honest resolution):** the mapping did require judgment (naming's transparency → CRITERION is a stretch-fit); the set is therefore a **heuristic prior, not a complete basis** — its claim is "walking these kinds reliably surfaces candidate axes you'd otherwise miss," not "every axis instantiates exactly one kind." That claim survived the derivation: each topic's axis-set is recoverable by walking the five primary kinds.
**Confidence:** HIGH on existence-as-heuristic + the five primary members; MED on completeness (open list by design; secondaries flagged).
**Resolution:** the meta-facet set = 5 primary + 2 secondary kinds, shipped as heuristic-and-extendable.
**Fixed:** the kit's core content. **No longer allowed:** presenting the set as a complete basis or a required checklist. **Depends:** the instrument's presentation (innovation's job). **Model change:** the user's "categories" now has real content.

#### Ambiguity A5: What does "for any topic" honestly mean? (The scope bound.)
**Strongest counter-interpretation:** the paradigms-attach-to-practices hypothesis is a dodge — "any topic is recastable as a practice," so the bound excludes nothing.
**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** the recast is a REAL transformation with a visible effect: it changes what the paradigms attach to. "Paradigms of rocks" has no answer; "paradigms of CLASSIFYING rocks" does (phenetic vs genetic classification…) — and those paradigms belong to the classifying, not the rocks. The recast can also honestly FAIL: a topic with no agent, no task, or no genuine approach-variation ("paradigms of the number seven") yields nothing after recasting — the bound excludes real cases. Operationally the bound becomes the kit's **step-zero question**: "whose practice, doing what?" — forcing the clarification instead of hiding it.
**Confidence:** HIGH.
**Resolution:** paradigm-enumeration applies to **practices** — an agent-kind approaching a task-kind with genuine approach-variation; object-domain requests are repaired by the step-zero recast.
**Consequence for the prior finding (frame revision, feeding the re-test):** the prior's 3-conjunct gate (divergent generative tasks) is the **special case** of this scope for the innovate hook — correct where it lives (innovate is a generative discipline) but not the concept's boundary: non-generative practices (understanding a codebase; classifying) have paradigms too.
**Fixed:** the scope + step-zero. **No longer allowed:** unlimited "any topic"; treating the innovate gate as the concept's boundary. **Depends:** the kit's question sequence. **Model change:** "any topic" becomes "any practice (or topic recast as one)" — a stronger claim for being honest.

#### Ambiguity A6 (load-bearing coined term): Is "meta-facet" structural or proxy?
**Counter:** it's Ranganathan cosplay — a borrowed label.
**Why the counter fails:** the term names a real level verified by derivation (axis-KINDS recurring across topics whose concrete axes differ); dropping the term loses the distinction between a topic's axes and the kinds that generate them (exactly the confusion in the user's original breath-list). **User-language (H9):** the user said "categories" — the finding presents "**paradigm categories (meta-facets)**," their word first.
**Confidence:** HIGH. **Resolution:** keep, dual-labeled.

#### Ambiguity A7: Do the 5 probes survive the deepening?
**Counter:** the meta-system supersedes them — retire the probes.
**Why the counter fails:** each probe grounds 1:1 in the settled system (probe 1 ↔ community-recognition evidence; probe 2 ↔ axes; probe 3 ↔ modal values; probe 4 ↔ cross-consistency + exemplar-anchoring; probe 5 ↔ community/fashion loosening) — they ARE the kit's question-form, now with explanations of why each works. The deepening adds **probe 0** (the step-zero practice-naming recast, from A5) and upgrades probe 2 (walk the meta-facets to find axes).
**Confidence:** HIGH. **Resolution:** probes grounded-and-extended (0–5), not orphaned.

## Phase 5 — Conceptual Stabilization

### SV6 — Stabilized Model

**The definition (practice-headed, two-sided):**
> **A paradigm — relative to a practice (an agent-kind approaching a task-kind) — is a coherent, generative bundle of commitments about how the practice is done: one of several distinct attractors in the practice's approach-space. Its image is a recognizable family of outcomes.**
> Necessary: the **commitment-bundle** (≥2 cohering choices — a single choice is an *option*, not a paradigm) and **generative capacity** (new instances can be worked from it — a non-generative pattern is a *style-description*). Typical and evidential: **exemplar-anchoring** (a model instance to imitate — the word's original Greek sense) and **community-recognition** (practitioners can name it — the strongest detection signal). Entailed: it **constrains and enables**. Field-level: paradigms are **plural** — they come as alternatives that can compose (multi-paradigm is normal).

**The meta-system (the user's trichotomy, settled as the generator pipeline):**
- **Attributes** — the paradigm-hood criteria above: the membership test any candidate family must pass (coherent? generative? ideally exemplified and nameable?).
- **Axes** — the topic's own dimensions of approach-variation (topic-specific; found, not given).
- **Categories = meta-facets** — the recurring axis-KINDS used to find a topic's axes: **SOURCE/MATERIAL · UNIT/GRAIN · OPERATION/MOVE · COORDINATION/RELATION · CRITERION/VALUE** (+ secondary: SEQUENCE, RESPONSIBILITY-LOCUS) — a heuristic prior, extendable, never a checklist.
- **Pipeline:** step-zero (name the practice) → walk the meta-facets to find the axes → expose each axis's default and non-default values → compose combinations, filtering by the attributes (coherent + generative + exemplified) → the map (families + axes). The 5 probes are this pipeline's question-form, now grounded and joined by probe 0.

**Scope:** paradigm-enumeration applies to **practices**; object-domain asks are repaired by step-zero ("whose practice, doing what?"). The prior finding's divergent-generative gate remains correct FOR THE INNOVATE HOOK but is the special case, not the concept's boundary.

**Differences from SV1:** SV1 guessed the general shape; SV6 grounds each piece structurally — the option-vs-paradigm boundary (from the necessity tests), the practice-headed unity (forced by non-artifact practices + probe mechanics + causal direction), the trichotomy's circularity-kill of the rival reading, the meta-facet set DERIVED from three unlike topics with its procrustean limit stated, and the scope bound that can honestly fail. The prior finding's commitments enter the re-test with: definition **confirmed-but-re-headed** (frame revised), gate **confirmed-as-special-case** (frame revised), probes **confirmed-and-extended**, axes/families duality **confirmed** (it was the practice/image duality in miniature), lens≠paradigm **confirmed** (now joined by option≠paradigm and move≠paradigm).
