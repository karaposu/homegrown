## Finding Summary

- **The definition (practice-headed, two-sided):** a paradigm — relative to a **practice** — is a **coherent, generative bundle of commitments** about how the practice is done: one of several distinct attractors in the practice's approach-space. *Commitments on the inside; a recognizable family of outcomes on the outside.* The prior finding's region-formulation survives as the artifact-producing special case.

- **Two ingredients are necessary; the rest are evidence or consequences.** Necessary: the **commitment-bundle** (at least two cohering choices — a single choice is an *option*, not a paradigm) and **generative capacity** (new instances can be worked from it — a pattern you can't generate from is a *style-description*). Typical and evidential: **exemplar-anchoring** (the word's original Greek sense — a model shown beside, to be imitated) and **community recognition** (practitioners can name it — the strongest detection signal, but nascent paradigms exist before names). Entailed: it constrains and enables. Field-level: paradigms are **plural** — distinct attractors that can **compose** (multi-paradigm is normal, not exceptional).

- **The user's three words are the three stations of one generator.** **Attributes** = the paradigm-hood criteria above (the membership test any candidate family must pass). **Axes** = a given topic's own dimensions of approach-variation (found per topic, not given). **Categories** = the **meta-facets**: recurring axis-KINDS that show up across topics and are used to FIND a topic's axes — the genuinely "meta" member, and exactly what the user's phrase "meta paradigm categories" was reaching for.

- **The meta-facet kit (5 primary + 2 secondary), derived — not asserted — from four topics:** **SOURCE/MATERIAL** (what the approach draws on) · **UNIT/GRAIN** (its working unit) · **OPERATION/MOVE** (the agent's characteristic action) · **COORDINATION/RELATION** (the structure among parts or participants) · **CRITERION/VALUE** (what counts as good inside it — special: paradigms carry their *own* success definitions, which is why cross-paradigm comparison is inherently hard); secondary: SEQUENCE and RESPONSIBILITY-LOCUS. Shipped as a **heuristic prior, never a checklist** — walking the kinds reliably surfaces axes; not every axis maps to exactly one kind.

- **The generator pipeline, as six glance-level probes:** probe 0 — *"whose practice, doing what?"* (the scope question); probe 1 — the schools; probe 2 — walk the five categories to expose the axes; probe 3 — defaults and non-defaults per axis; probe 4 — which non-default combinations cohere, split into **established composed families** (exemplified) and **frontier candidates** (coherent, no exemplar yet — flagged and KEPT, never dropped); probe 5 — fashion-loosening. Output: the map (families + axes, 5–9 entries, basic-level grain). Probe 1 alone reproduces the MODAL map — probes 2–5 exist for the regions probe 1 cannot see.

- **The scope, honestly bounded:** paradigm-enumeration applies to **practices** — an agent-kind approaching a task-kind with genuine approach-variation. Object-domain asks resolve via probe 0 ("paradigms of rocks" → paradigms of *classifying* rocks), and inspection shows every legitimate-sounding object-domain ask was an implicit practice all along ("the Newtonian paradigm" IS a way of doing physics). One adjacent use stays outside: paradigm-as-**era-pattern** ("the feudal paradigm" — a system's organizing configuration) — not an enumeration target, though it re-enters when a practice (worldbuilding, institution design) takes such patterns as material.

- **Validation: four topics, three rediscovered composed families, one honest ceiling.** The kit was exercised on naming (the prior anchor), app-architecture, skill-acquisition, and — adversarially, chosen mid-critique — psychotherapy. In each, the facet-walk produced real axes without forcing, and probe 4 REDISCOVERED a known composed family from the axes alone: the **modular monolith** (architecture), **deliberate practice** (learning), **third-wave CBT** (psychotherapy). Rediscovery is the right validation for a mechanism meant to find unknown regions (you validate a metal detector on coins you buried). The ceiling: all four runs are single-mind, same-session — the **blind-topic fresh run** remains the designated clean test.

- **The prior finding's commitments were re-tested, not absorbed** — two "confirmed but frame revised" (the definition's head; the gate's location), three confirmed (the probes, extended; the axes/families duality; lens ≠ paradigm, now joined by option ≠ paradigm and move ≠ paradigm). Nothing in the prior was found wrong within its own scope.

## Finding

### 1. Why this inquiry exists

The immediately preceding inquiry (the `refines:` target above) established a missing operation — paradigm-detection before generation — and committed a working definition of "paradigm" grounded in one case (naming an app). The user then asked the natural next question: what IS a paradigm, as a definition — and what are the meta-level categories/attributes/axes that could generate a paradigm-enumeration for *any* topic? This finding answers both, grounding in the word's established senses (the Greek original; grammatical paradigms; Kuhn's scientific paradigms; programming paradigms; the vernacular), in three formal traditions (Zwicky's morphological analysis; Ranganathan's faceted classification; Rosch's prototype theory with Wittgenstein's family resemblance), and in live application to four unlike topics.

### 2. The definition

All the established senses of "paradigm" share a recognizable core. The Greek original (*paradeigma* — "a pattern shown beside") and the grammatical paradigm carry it exemplar-forward: a model instance you imitate to produce new instances. Kuhn's scientific paradigm carries it commitment-forward: a constellation of commitments (his "disciplinary matrix") that tells a community how to work, anchored by shared exemplars. Programming paradigms sit between: coherent commitment-bundles (state-and-mutation vs pure composition) with exemplar languages, and — importantly — freely composable (multi-paradigm languages are normal). One structure, two carriers:

> **A paradigm — relative to a practice — is a WAY the practice is done: a coherent, generative bundle of commitments, one of several distinct attractors in the practice's approach-space. Commitments on the inside; a recognizable family of outcomes on the outside.**

**The logical form (what's necessary, what's evidence).** Two ingredients are necessary, and each does boundary work:

- **The commitment-bundle** (≥2 cohering choices — "cohering" meaning held together in real practice, mutually supporting toward the paradigm's own criterion). This separates a paradigm from an **option**: "dark mode" (one toggle) is an option; "dark-first design" (contrast strategy + elevation-via-lightness + OLED economics, held together) is a paradigm of the UI-theming practice. Same words nearby, two different things.
- **Generative capacity** (new instances can be worked from it). This separates a paradigm from a **style-description**. Capacity, not activity: alchemy has no practitioners left, yet remains a paradigm structurally — one *could* work within it.

Two more ingredients are typical and serve as the strongest **detection evidence**, but are not essence: **exemplar-anchoring** (a model you can point at — grammar's inflection table is the exemplar CARRYING the bundle: "follows the -are conjugation" commits a speaker to a whole cohering system of endings) and **community recognition** (practitioners can name it — but a nascent paradigm exists before anyone names it). "Constrains and enables" follows from bundle-ness and needs no separate clause. And **plurality** belongs to the field, not the individual: paradigms come as alternatives, and the alternatives compose (event-driven microservices; third-wave CBT).

**The membership test (one glance, for any candidate family):** (1) *Bundle?* — at least two cohering choices? (2) *Generative?* — can you work new instances from it? (3) *Anchored?* — an exemplar to point at, a name practitioners would recognize? (1) and (2) decide; (3) strengthens, and its absence marks a **frontier** candidate rather than a rejection.

**The NOT-list:** an **option** (one choice — no bundle) · a **style-description** (no generative capacity) · a **move/mechanism** (a transformation you APPLY — the innovation discipline's seven mechanisms, TRIZ's principles — not a way you WORK) · a **lens** (how you evaluate, not where you generate from; carried from the prior finding).

**The grain norm:** name paradigms at the **basic level** — the grain where practitioners naturally talk ("microservices," not "software," not "microservices-with-Kafka-on-Kubernetes"). This is Rosch's most-informative-level insight applied to approach-space, and it is why maps stay at 5–9 entries.

**Why practice-headed (and what happens to the prior's region-headed form).** Three structural reasons force the head: (i) practices whose outcomes are not artifacts — acquiring a skill, running therapy — have paradigms but no artifact-region; a region-headed definition cannot even state them, while "a recognizable family of outcomes" covers process-shapes and capabilities. (ii) The detection probes elicit practice-descriptions ("the different WAYS of doing this") and derive regions from them. (iii) Causality runs practice→image: the bundle explains the region, never the reverse. Every clause of the prior definition survives the re-heading — "a committed combination of generative choices" IS the bundle; "a region of the candidate-space" IS the image; "inhabitable" reads even better on the practice side ("you can work within it"). For artifact-producing generative tasks (the prior's entire scope), the region-headed form remains the correct specialization, and detection still reads the image first where artifacts exist.

### 3. The scope — where paradigm-enumeration applies

Paradigms attach to **practices**: an agent-kind approaching a task-kind with genuine approach-variation. The generator's **probe 0** asks it directly: *"Whose practice, doing what?"* In use this is usually a clarifier, not a rejection — inspection shows that legitimate object-domain-sounding asks were implicit practices all along: "paradigms in physics" = ways of DOING physics (Kuhn's own case); "paradigms of consciousness" = ways of EXPLAINING consciousness (functionalism, dualism, IIT); "paradigms of mathematics" = ways of doing math (constructive vs classical proof — a CRITERION-facet split: what counts as a valid proof).

The bound can honestly fail, which is what makes it a real bound: "paradigms of the number seven" survives no recast. And one adjacent use of the word stays deliberately outside: paradigm-as-**era-pattern** — "the feudal paradigm" names a period's organizing configuration, not anyone's way of working. Such system-patterns are not enumeration targets — until a practice takes them up as material (worldbuilding; institution design), at which point they become families in THAT practice's map.

The prior finding's 3-conjunct gate (divergent generative task + family structure + no existing partition) is this scope's **special case** for the innovate hook — still correct where it lives, since the innovation discipline is a generative practice by construction.

### 4. The meta-system — the user's three words, settled

The trichotomy resolves as the three stations of one generator (the rival reading — categories as the families themselves — dies on circularity: the user asked for what GENERATES the enumeration; families are its output):

- **ATTRIBUTES** — the paradigm-hood criteria of §2: the membership test. They filter which candidate combinations are real paradigms.
- **AXES** — the topic's own dimensions of approach-variation. Topic-specific; found, not given. (Vocabulary guard: these **approach-axes** are not the **answer-axes** of the Horizontal Dive + Vertical Refinement + Synthesis pattern — that pattern's axes are dimensions of one composite ANSWER; these are dimensions of the APPROACH-space. Both vocabularies are live in this project; keep them distinct.)
- **CATEGORIES = META-FACETS** — the recurring axis-KINDS used to find a topic's axes:

| # | Category (meta-facet) | The glance question | naming | architecture | learning | psychotherapy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **SOURCE / MATERIAL** | what does it draw on? | language pools | runtime substrate | theory vs examples vs attempts | past / cognition / behavior / body / the relationship |
| 2 | **UNIT / GRAIN** | the working unit? | morpheme / word | process / service / function | sub-skill / whole task | thoughts / behaviors / person / family-system |
| 3 | **OPERATION / MOVE** | the agent's characteristic action? | compound / mutate / borrow | evolve / decompose / orchestrate | drill / immerse / imitate | interpret / restructure / expose / reflect |
| 4 | **COORDINATION / RELATION** | how do parts/participants relate? | word-parts' semantics | in-process / sync / async | solo / mentor / environment | expert-led / collaborative / client-led |
| 5 | **CRITERION / VALUE** | what counts as good inside it? | appeal vs descriptiveness | simplicity vs autonomy vs elasticity | accuracy vs fluency vs transfer | insight vs symptoms vs functioning |
| 6* | *SEQUENCE (secondary)* | what order does it impose? | — | build-then-split vs split-first | parts→whole vs whole→parts | phase-structured vs open-ended |
| 7* | *RESPONSIBILITY-LOCUS (secondary)* | who carries the load? | — | self-managed vs platform | self vs mentor | therapist vs client vs system |

Two clarifiers earned by adversarial testing: **OPERATION vs COORDINATION** — the operation is the move the AGENT makes; coordination is the structure AMONG participants/parts (immersion's move is attempt-whole-tasks; its coordination is feedback-from-environment). And **CRITERION is special**: paradigms carry their own definitions of success (Kuhn's deepest point, in miniature) — which is why cross-paradigm comparison is structurally hard and why a paradigm map never ranks families.

**Bounds, carried in the claims:** the kit is a **heuristic prior, never a checklist** — walking the kinds reliably SURFACES axes (that claim held on all four topics), but not every axis maps cleanly to one kind (naming's transparency→CRITERION was a stretch-fit, conceded). The kit also obeys its own norms: five-plus-two is the facet-list's own basic level — three ("what/how/why") loses real discriminations (UNIT vs MATERIAL separates live paradigm differences), and ten-plus demands empty slots.

### 5. The generator pipeline — probes 0–5

**Pipeline:** name the practice → walk the categories to find the axes → expose defaults and non-defaults → compose combinations, filtered by the membership test → the map (families + axes; 5–9 entries; stop when fresh candidates re-land in existing families — "same family" judged by matching axis-signatures at the map's grain).

**The probes (glance-level):**
- **0 — scope:** "Whose practice, doing what?" (a topic that is a thing → "doing what with it?")
- **1 — schools:** "What would practitioners call the fundamentally different WAYS of doing this?"
- **2 — axes via the categories:** "Material? unit? move? relation? criterion? — what dimensions of variation does each expose?"
- **3 — defaults:** "Per axis: the default everyone assumes, and the non-defaults?"
- **4 — composition:** "Which non-default combinations cohere — and which have real exemplars?" Output splits: **established composed families** (coherent + exemplified) and **frontier candidates** (coherent, unexemplified — flagged `frontier`, kept on the map, never dropped).
- **5 — loosening:** "Which regions are fashionable for tasks like this — and which unfashionable ones fit THIS case?"

Two design notes from the adversarial pass. First, **probe 1 alone reproduces the modal map**: tested on the naming anchor, "list the schools" yields the five standard families and misses the composed region where the actual winner lived — probes 2–5 exist precisely for what probe 1 cannot see. Second, **probe 4's frontier split repairs a real internal tension**: as originally worded it required exemplars, contradicting the definition's exemplar-as-evidence-not-essence — and would have silently dropped nascent coherent regions, which are the operation's whole prize. Coherent-but-unexemplified regions now stay on the map as first-class frontier entries.

### 6. The demonstrations — the pipeline run on unlike practices

Both maps below were drawn BY the pipeline with the facet-walk shown (and a third — psychotherapy — was drawn adversarially during critique, chosen as hostile ground: interpersonal, clinical, non-artifact). They are presented as **mechanism-rediscovery validation**: in each, probe 4 re-derives a known-but-non-obvious composed family from the axes alone — the correct test for a mechanism built to find unknown regions, since the rediscovered targets are independently confirmable.

**Practice A — structuring a server-side application.** Facet-walk → axes: deployable grain (MATERIAL→substrate also yields an axis), communication-mode, state-locus, operational-responsibility, optimization-target. Defaults: one process · in-process calls · simplicity (the classic monolith is the modal region). Families: Monolith (Rails-classic) · Microservices (Netflix-style) · Serverless (Lambda-centric; platform-carried ops) · Event-driven (async events as the organizing commitment — composes with either grain) · **Modular monolith** (*rediscovered by composition*: one deployable × strong internal boundaries × team-autonomy — Shopify) — the composed family the flat school-list misses, and per probe 5 the unfashionable-but-fitting region for small teams.

**Practice B — acquiring a skill.** Note: the outcomes here are process-shapes and capabilities, not artifacts — this is the case a region-headed definition cannot state, exercised deliberately. Facet-walk → axes: practice-grain, knowledge-entry, feedback-source, social-structure, progress-criterion. Default: theory-entry × course-shaped ("take a course" is the modal region). Families: Drill (scales; spaced repetition) · Immersion (language immersion) · Project-based (learn-by-building) · Apprenticeship (imitate-a-model × mentor-feedback) · Theory-first (the textbook course) · **Deliberate practice** (*rediscovered by composition*: sub-skill grain × mentor-designed tasks × at-edge-of-ability × accuracy — Ericsson) — assembled entirely from non-defaults, famous precisely because someone NAMED a region practitioners hadn't isolated.

**Practice C — psychotherapy (the adversarial pick, run during critique).** The walk produced five real axes with zero forcing (locus-of-material; unit-of-intervention including the family-SYSTEM; characteristic move; therapeutic-relationship structure; progress-criterion — where the paradigms famously disagree, making the incommensurability note live). Composed rediscovery: **third-wave CBT** (ACT/DBT) = CBT-material × acceptance-criterion × mindfulness-move — a historically real composition.

**The validation ceiling, stated plainly:** four topics, one adversarially chosen — but all run by a single mind in a single session that already knew the domains. The clean test is a **blind-topic fresh run** (recorded as the designated next validation; the route-map's R1).

### 7. What was deliberately NOT concluded

- **A necessary-and-sufficient six-criterion definition** — killed: community-recognition and exemplars fail necessity for nascent paradigms; the hybrid form does the boundary work without false exclusions.
- **A pure family-resemblance definition** (nothing necessary) — killed: bundle-ness and generative capacity are what keep options and style-descriptions out; dropping them admits things nobody calls paradigms in any sense.
- **Categories-as-families** — killed on circularity (the output cannot be the generator).
- **A three-facet or ten-plus-facet kit** — killed both directions: three loses real discriminations; ten-plus forces empty slots. Five-plus-two is the kit's own basic level.
- **"One question suffices" (probe-1-only)** — killed on the anchor case: it reproduces the modal map and misses composed regions — the operation's entire point.
- **Unlimited "any topic"** — killed: the practices-bound with honest failure cases is stronger for being real; the era-pattern penumbra is acknowledged, not annexed.
- **CORRECTS as the relationship to the prior finding** — killed under a deliberately high-stakes burden: nothing the prior asserted within its own scope is false; the revision traces to new territory (non-artifact practices), not to relaxed standards. REFINES is honest.

## Reasoning

**Why practice-headed won.** Three independent structural forces converge: statability (non-artifact practices exist and have paradigms — the skill-acquisition demonstration is one), probe mechanics (detection elicits ways-of-working and derives regions), and causal direction (bundles explain regions). The strongest counter — "region-first suffices operationally; the prior worked fine" — fails on the first force alone: a definition that cannot state half its scope is not sufficient, however well it worked on the artifact-producing half. The churn charge (revising a one-hour-old commitment) was answered by the clause-by-clause continuity shown in the re-test: nothing was discarded; the head was generalized.

**Why the kit is five-plus-two and heuristic.** The derivation across four topics found five kinds that recur strongly; both compressions (three) and expansions (ten-plus) were tested and killed — the first loses live discriminations, the second forces empty slots. The procrustean counter ("the recurrence is post-hoc pattern-matching") was PARTIALLY CONCEDED and shaped the claim: the kit ships as "walking these kinds reliably surfaces axes," never as "every axis is one kind" — and the one admitted stretch-fit (naming's transparency→CRITERION) is documented rather than smoothed.

**Why the frontier split is the pass's most valuable repair.** Critique caught a genuine internal contradiction: probe 4 required exemplars while the definition holds exemplars to be evidence-not-essence — so the instrument would have silently dropped nascent coherent regions, which are precisely the under-explored value the whole operation exists to reach. The repair (family | frontier entry types) aligns the instrument with its own definition AND with the project's asymmetric-failure principle (silently missing a region is the worse failure).

**Why the demonstrations are worded as rediscovery.** The composed "finds" (modular monolith, deliberate practice, third-wave CBT) are famous — an expert's probe 1 would list them. The demonstrations therefore claim only what they show: the mechanism re-derives known composed families from the axes alone, without expert recall — the metal-detector-validated-on-buried-coins test. Novel discovery remains for live use (the deferred frontier pursuit).

**Significant kills** are listed in Finding §7; the two worth re-stating: categories-as-families died on circularity (the cleanest kill of the run), and CORRECTS-as-relationship died under a deliberately raised burden — protecting the finding corpus from both silent absorption and drama-inflation.
