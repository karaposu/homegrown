# Sensemaking — advanced seed generation (source expansion / enrichment)

## SV1 — Baseline
The harvester under-produces on thin sources (paper 29: 1 sentence → 1 seed; a.md: the same idea hand-expanded → 7). The ask: make expansion of a thin source a protocol capability. Naive read: "add a source-expansion step."

## Phase 1 — Cognitive Anchor Extraction

**Structural points (the machinery of the problem):**
- **SP1 — the crossing has two inputs.** seed = source-claim × project-anchor (seed_harvester §2). Two enrichable inputs. Anchor-side enrichment (canon-grounding; 19-14 H1; the a/b/c fix) addresses input-2. Source-side expansion (19-14 H4) addresses input-1. Distinct levers. Both distinct from *crossing-generation* (foreignness gradient — more candidates from GIVEN inputs).
- **SP2 — the 19-14 already owns a source-expansion axis.** ★Load-bearing correction found while stabilizing: the 19-14 diagnosis's 2×2 has **claim-richness** as one of its two axes ("how much the source has been drawn out"). Source-expansion is NOT absent from the 19-14 — it is one of its two named axes, and it designed the guided-expansion pre-pass + both guards (provenance + source-worth-containment), user-gated, UNAPPLIED.
- **SP3 — two MODES of expansion.** anchor-DRIVEN interrogation (19-14: "the anchors supply the questions") vs source-DRIVEN engagement (stretch the phenomenon along its OWN aspect-structure). Different reachable material.

**Key insights (the non-obvious):**
- **KI1 — richness is not a scalar; it has aspect-DEPTH structure.** ★The dive's sharpest anchor. The 19-14 treats claim-richness as a quantity ("how much drawn out"). But the thin dive expanded to ~12 spider features (high *quantity*) and still got 1 seed. Its expansion was **aspect-FLAT** (a list of static features). a.md's expansion was **aspect-DEEP** (it covered dynamics, failure-modes, ordering, scale, boundaries). So the lever is not *how much* you expand — it is *along how many ASPECTS*. Quantity-rich ≠ aspect-deep.
- **KI2 — anchor-driven interrogation is bounded by its existing anchors; source-driven expansion can exceed them.** Anchor-driven can only ask about anchors it holds. The canon had NO "shape-definition" anchor → anchor-driven interrogation could not have asked about the spider's convergence-dynamic → yet engaging the spider's dynamics-aspect (source-driven) surfaced it, which THEN revealed the analogy-family gap (the import-shaped p29-S2). Source-driven expansion can surface material that reveals NEW anchors, not just fill old ones.
- **KI3 — this answers the 19-14's OWN open frontier.** The 19-14 left open: "can the machine reach the deepest source-insights or only shallow project-directed claims?" KI1+KI2 answer it: yes, IF expansion runs along the aspect-kit (source-driven), not by flat enumeration.

**Constraints (the guards):**
- **C1 — provenance floor (OWNED).** Every expanded claim must carry honest support; never fabricated behavior (19-14 provenance guard). Inherited, not to be re-invented.
- **C2 — source-worth containment (OWNED).** If no source-supported crossing emerges, stop; don't manufacture (19-14). Inherited.
- **C3 — do NOT re-invent the 19-14 design.** Extend it; the MQ4 exclusion.

**Foundational principles:**
- **FP1 — expansion is bounded by a real referent.** A source that points at an external real phenomenon (spider→biology) can be legitimately engaged; a self-contained claim (a paper's specific finding) has its referent exhausted by its text. This is the *source-type licensing* candidate — the deep form of C1/C2.

**Meaning-nodes:**
- **MN1 — "advanced" = deep, not just more.** The user's "stretch in multiple aspects" ≙ aspect-depth (KI1), which ≙ the 19-14's open frontier (KI3). The three collapse to one concept.

*Meta-inspection (H4 concept-names, H5 motivating-examples): "aspect-depth" is a coined term — validated below against the concrete a.md-vs-thin evidence (H5), not asserted. "Source-driven vs anchor-driven" matches the 19-14's own "anchors supply the questions" language (H9 alignment holds).*

## Phase 3 — Ambiguity Collapse

### Ambiguity A3 (THE load-bearing one) — does source-driven aspect-depth HOLD as distinct, or FOLD into the 19-14's richness axis?
**Strongest counter-interpretation (the 19-14-author):** the thin dive engaged the phenomenon and enumerated ~12 spider behaviors, yet produced 1 seed. So "engage the phenomenon on its own terms" is already covered by the richness axis AND already shown insufficient — aspect-depth is just richness re-labelled.

**Why the counter fails (structural, not precedent):** the thin dive's 12 items were **static features** (web-transmits-vibrations, spider-wraps-prey) — a list with no temporal/causal dimension. The import-shaped seed p29-S2 did NOT come from any static feature; per the rich finding it came from the spider's **process-dynamic** — "catch a feasible part first → it will define → the rest fills in." A flat feature-list **structurally cannot contain** a process-dynamic (a list has no ordering/causality). So "expand to 12 features" and "expand along the dynamics aspect" are *different operations reaching different material* — the counter conflates quantity of features with coverage of aspects. This is checkable against the actual artifacts (the thin dive's feature-list vs a.md's "define-then-fill" dynamic), not asserted from the spec.
**Confidence: HIGH** on the distinction's existence (evidence-grounded in the actual seed-origin), **but SIZED:** aspect-depth is NECESSARY-not-sufficient — a.md's process-dynamic still needed the canon analogy-family anchor to become p29-S2. So the honest claim is **"aspect-deep source-expansion is the missing complement to canon-grounding for reaching DEPTH; neither alone suffices"** — NOT "source-expansion is the lever."
**Resolution:** HOLDS, sized. Aspect-depth is a real structural refinement of the 19-14's richness axis (richness has aspect-structure; the lever is aspect-coverage, not quantity), and the source-driven aspect-kit is the method that achieves it.
**Now fixed:** the contribution = sharpening richness→aspect-depth + the source-driven aspect-kit; grounded in p29-S2's actual origin.
**No longer allowed:** claiming source-expansion is un-addressed (19-14 owns the axis); claiming aspect-depth alone yields seeds (needs the anchor-crossing).
**Depends on this:** A4 (the aspect-kit is the method), A6 (sequence).

### Ambiguity A6 — "one move" (19-14) or a SEQUENCE?
**Counter:** the 19-14 says canon-grounding + expansion are ONE move (interrogate against anchors); splitting them re-introduces complexity it deliberately unified.
**Why the counter partially fails (structural + fold-reopen):** the 19-14 fused them because IN ITS analysis expansion was anchor-driven. But KI2 names a DIFFERENT site — source-driven aspect-expansion that must PRECEDE anchor-crossing to exceed the known anchors (a new site; new mechanism-evidence = p29-S2's origin; candidate anchored there → the three fold-reopen conditions hold). So a DEEP-source case needs: **(phase 1) source-driven aspect-stretch → (phase 2) the 19-14's anchor-guided crossing + coverage table.** The 19-14's move is phase 2, unchanged; this adds a phase 1 BEFORE it for thin/deep cases.
**Confidence: MEDIUM.** It's an ADDITION-before, not a refutation — the 19-14's "one move" stands for the anchor-crossing itself. Sized to avoid over-claiming a contradiction.
**Resolution:** a SEQUENCE for the thin-deep case (aspect-stretch → anchor-crossing); the 19-14's move is the second phase, intact.

### Ambiguity A5 — does source-type licensing SHARPEN the owned guard or just restate it?
**Counter (pragmatist):** the owned source-worth containment already says "if nothing source-supported emerges, stop" — licensing-by-source-type is redundant.
**Why it partially holds as a sharpening:** the owned containment is a REACTIVE per-claim check (expand, then stop if nothing supports). Source-type licensing is a PROACTIVE source-level pre-check (before expanding, ask: does this source point at a real external referent, or is it a self-contained claim whose referent is exhausted?). Different grain — a pre-gate vs a post-filter. It adds efficiency + clarity (don't waste expansion on unexpandable sources) but the SAFETY is already floored by C2.
**Confidence: MEDIUM.** Real but adjacent; the gate should size whether it earns a distinct edit or folds into C1/C2 as a clarifying line.
**Resolution:** candidate refinement — a source-level pre-check that sharpens (not replaces) the owned floor.
