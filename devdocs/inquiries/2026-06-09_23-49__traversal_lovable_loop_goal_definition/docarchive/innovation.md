## Phase 2 — Generate (7 mechanisms × 3)

### 1. Lens Shifting (Framer)

- **1G — organism lens.** Read the assembly as an organism rather than a machine: hands (workers), eyes (navigator), will (orchestrator), memory — and the whirl as its metabolism. Under this lens "lovable" stops being decoration: organisms you love are organisms you keep feeding. Presentation-layer candidate for the identity card.
- **1F — trust lens (focused on the jump).** Re-evaluate "certain degree of automation" under TRUST rather than AUTOMATION: what crosses the boundary is not code but **earned delegation per axis** — the ladder's gates are trust instruments, not feature flags. The jump's crisp meaning: a delegation ladder climbed on evidence.
- **1C — product lens (contrarian).** Evaluate TLL as a tool a stranger points at their repo (explfine as the demo). Under this lens the acceptance test must be statable without homegrown-internal jargon. Survives as a CONSTRAINT on explfine's articulation (territory-generic phrasing), not as a redefinition of TLL's research identity.

### 2. Combination (Generator)

- **2G — orchestrator × MTTP library.** The orchestrator's "decide" step doesn't only pick the next single probe — it can invoke a multi-loop PATTERN from the MTTP library (fan-out/diagnose/refine shapes). Definition consequence: the orchestrator's repertoire = the 7 loop-control moves × route selection × **pattern invocation**. Enriches the component map with already-committed machinery.
- **2F — explfine × routelister's two run modes.** Explfine's mechanism IS the project-scale alternation of routelister's existing modes: root/breadth runs (identities at increasing resolution) interleaved with concept-target/depth runs + worker probes (per-concept definition). The acceptance test runs on shipped machinery — no new discipline required.
- **2C — lovable × telemetry.** Make lovable measurable: turn-cost (human minutes per revolution), interruption-cost (is stopping ever punished?), artifact-trust incidents (how often outputs get re-checked). Tested below — survives as OPTIONAL observability for the bar, not as its definition (a humane bar reduced to a dashboard stops being humane).

### 3. Inversion (Framer; depth-checked)

- **3G — the seed's central belief.** "TLL is the thing we must BUILD." L1 invert: TLL must be GROWN from the loop already turning. L2 (system-level): **TLL already runs today — at L0, with human organs.** Every component exists as behavior this very session (probe ran; human read the field; human selected; human dispatched; human remembers). The goal is **organ replacement, not loop creation**. Multi-axis check — existence-axis: what is genuinely NEW? Only traversal-memory artifacts and the automation carrier; everything else is transfer. Identity-axis: TLL = the same loop on a different per-axis substrate. This becomes the package's unifying claim.
- **3F — focused (component map).** "The navigator must be an isolated SESSION." Invert: navigation as a standing ARTIFACT VIEW (the cumulative map consulted instead of a session run). What follows: cheaper turns but lost fresh-eyes isolation — the session-ness is load-bearing against contamination (the cross-run doc's whole argument). Survives as refinement: **eyes = a fresh session whose persistent OUTPUT is the standing map** (session for perception; artifact as its cache).
- **3C — contrarian (identity/position).** "TLL is the era-goal." Invert: TLL is a DISTRACTION — the era-goal should be quality-awareness (the RC layers), since untrusted automation is worthless. Tested: the strongest contrarian in the set. It loses on one structural fact: the ladder's lower rungs are DESIGNED to generate the calibration data the upper rungs consume (L1 records selection rationales precisely so L2's selector can be trusted) — quality-awareness for traversal grows BY climbing, not before it. Killed as a redirection; survives as a refinement: the jump must state explicitly that **the staircase is self-provisioning**.

### 4. Constraint Manipulation (Framer; both directions)

- **4-ADD (generic) — the turn-invariant.** ADD: "a revolution counts as a TLL turn only if its selection-rationale is recorded." What opens: TLL becomes operationally checkable from the very first manual turn (and the L1→L2 gate's data accrues by definition, not by discipline). Strong candidate for the jump piece.
- **4-ADD (focused) — the detail-line.** ADD an operational boundary for "implementation-detail-free": the map MAY name components, responsibilities, interfaces, contracts, integration relations; it may NOT name file paths, languages, frameworks, or code constructs. Gives explfine a testable property instead of a vibe.
- **4-REMOVE (generic).** REMOVE "the human seeds the goal" (Goal-formation axis) → L5 autonomy. Explored: out of TLL's era by the ladder's own boundary; becomes a NOT-list entry — **TLL deliberately stops below goal-formation**.
- **4-REMOVE (contrarian).** REMOVE "one orchestrator" → competing selectors (a market of orchestrators arbitrated by outcomes). Fails parsimony and has no evidence need at this era; killed; seed preserved as an L5-adjacent research frontier.

### 5. Absence Recognition (Generator; both levels, bidirectional)

- **Patch-level absences (in the definition territory):** no name for the turn unit (fixed: *revolution*); no TLL-achieved checklist (created in Test: gates + explfine demo); no explicit statement of what TLL does NOT automate (the Predictive/Retrospective quality judgments stay human at threshold); the dormant draft is stale against current names (revival note, not a rewrite here).
- **Redesign-level.** Designed from scratch today, the goal would be stated **capability-first** ("point at any project → explored-and-defined") with the architecture as its grounding — which is exactly the user's framing, inverted from canon's architecture-first (Open Question 4). The definition package should LEAD with explfine and ground in the assembly.
- **Bidirectional (already-present-in-different-form).** The project already performs TLL turns manually (this session is one: probe → human read the field → human selected → human dispatched); `_route.md` is explfine's output class (i) in embryo; the L1 procedure exists in the ladder. The absence is **identity + memory + carrier**, not mechanisms — the definition must name, not over-build.

### 6. Domain Transfer (Generator; native source included)

- **6-native (control systems / aviation).** The autopilot certification ladder: manual flight → flight-director cues (system SHOWS, pilot flies = L1: navigator maps, human selects) → autopilot with pilot monitoring (= L3) → autoland under certification gates (= L4). "Certain degree of automation" inherits a mature staging vocabulary where each mode is EARNED by demonstrated reliability — and where the pilot's role never disappears, it elevates to monitoring. Also **SLAM** (simultaneous localization and mapping): explfine is cognitive SLAM — exploring unknown territory while building the very map used to navigate it; "explored" = coverage confidence, "defined" = map fidelity.
- **6-different (cartography).** National triangulation surveys: primary → secondary → detail triangulation (staged resolution, the corpus's own 10→100→200 vision), permanent benchmarks (findings as survey markers), and published map SERIES at declared scales — where a 1:100,000 map omits houses **by contract, not by failure**. "Implementation-detail-free" = a declared map scale.
- **6-different (organizational).** The chief-of-staff pattern: principal sets intent (human seeds); chief-of-staff runs the operating rhythm (orchestrator: agenda = selection, meetings = revolutions, minutes = memory); staff sections execute (workers); intel briefs (navigator). Lovable = the operating rhythm that survives contact with fatigue — rhythms persist when they return more energy than they cost.

### 7. Extrapolation (Generator)

- **7G — throughput.** Remove the human bottleneck from selection/dispatch and revolutions run at session-availability pace — which is exactly where the dormant draft's "uncontrolled continuation" failure mode bites. Consequence for the definition: TLL's value claim is **sustained DIRECTED speed**, so stop-signals and lovable (review tolerability) become MORE load-bearing as automation rises, not less.
- **7F — measurement (focused on consequences).** With traversal memory recording every selection + outcome: after ~30 revolutions, selector-agreement statistics exist (the L2→L3 gate's data); after ~50, miscalibration patterns become Baldwin seeds. The self-improvement curve's data plumbing arrives WITH the architecture — "telemetry birth" is quantitative, not rhetorical.
- **7C — commoditization (contrarian).** If substrate vendors ship native repo-understanding agents, explfine's MAP output commoditizes; the durable value migrates to the METHOD: auditable reasoning trails, declared coverage, frontier honesty, resumability. The acceptance criteria should anchor these differentiators, not just the artifact list.
