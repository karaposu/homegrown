- **SUSTRALL's identity:** the named, testable, assembled end-state of the canon's meta-loop program — worker loop-runners (the hands) + a navigational individual session (the eyes) + an orchestrator (the will, holding traversal memory) — revolving as what the canon already calls the "controlled whirl" over thinking space. - **The components, mapped and bounded:** the **loop runners** execute one inquiry each (per-cycle control only). The **navigational session** is a fresh, context-isolated, warmed session that runs the enumerator (routelister) over finished work — it produces the field of directions and NEVER chooses; its persistent product is the standing concept-map. The **orchestrator** is the cross-inquiry controller: it decides the seven loop-control moves (terminate / widen / merge / re-run-deeper / unblock / different-approach / revisit), selects among enumerated routes, can invoke multi-loop patterns from the pattern library, dispatches the next session, and is custodian of **traversal memory** — the one component with zero instances today (no `_meta_state.md` has ever existed).

The staircase is **self-provisioning**: the lower steps generate exactly the calibration data the upper steps require.

- **Explfine, operationalized:** point SUSTRALL at any *bounded, readable* project territory with a goal (which may be fuzzy). The whirl alternates breadth (the eyes sweep concept-identities at increasing resolution) and depth (the hands define each concept) and returns three artifact classes: (i) a cumulative, **implementation-detail-free concept-map** (components → sub-components → sub-concepts, with integration relations — may name interfaces and responsibilities; may not name file paths, languages, or code constructs); (ii) **per-concept definition findings**; (iii) an honest **frontier list**. ### 2. What SUSTRALL is

**One sentence:** SUSTRALL is the assembled, trust-graduated traversal system — worker loop-runners, a navigational individual session, and an orchestrator with traversal memory — revolving as a controlled whirl over thinking space, achieved when its per-axis autonomy gates are passed and it can explore-and-define an arbitrary readable territory with the human contributing only seed and review.

**Reference paragraph.** A **revolution** (one whirl turn) is: *probe* (a worker loop produces a finding) → *see* (the navigational session enumerates the field of directions over the finished work) → *decide + select* (the orchestrator makes a loop-control decision and picks the next route — possibly invoking a multi-loop pattern) → *dispatch* (the next session starts) → *remember* (traversal memory updated) → *assess* (traversal signals checked; continue or stop). SUSTRALL is this revolution running at Level-3+ autonomy: the system turning the wheel, the human seeding goals and reviewing.

**What SUSTRALL is NOT:** not the end-goal (the consciousness-gradient north star remains the asymptote; SUSTRALL is this era's vehicle); not a new thinking discipline (it composes the existing ones); not a rename (the canon meta-loop concept survives every re-test — SUSTRALL adds name, test, and bar); not exhaustive discovery (bounded meaningful traversal, per canon); not — in this era — a UI (the navigate-by-intent interface is a later view layer); and **deliberately stopped below goal-formation** (the human keeps the seat that chooses what to care about; that boundary is where SUSTRALL's era ends and the gradient's later work begins).

### 3. The components, precisely

**The loop runners (the hands).** The existing worker runners — `/MVL`, `/MVLw`, `/aMVLw` — each executing one inquiry at full depth. Their control scope is per-cycle only (conclude-or-iterate inside one inquiry). Nothing about them changes under SUSTRALL.

**The navigational individual session (the eyes).** A **fresh, context-isolated session**, warmed on the terrain (project orientation → long-run trajectory → recent work → the target artifacts), that runs the enumerator — routelister — over a finished cycle or territory. It produces the typed field of directions. Two boundaries are load-bearing: it **never chooses** (the corpus's most re-confirmed seam: "Navigation sees; it does not choose"), and it is a *session*, not a standing document — fresh perception each time, uncontaminated by the worker's local context. Its **persistent product** is the standing concept-map (`_route.md` lineage), which is the cache of its perception, not a replacement for it.

**The orchestrator (the will).** The cross-inquiry controller — the component that today is 100% human. Its repertoire: the **closed seven-move loop-control vocabulary** (decided, never enumerated — per the committed one-enumerator/two-controllers architecture), **route selection** among what the eyes enumerated, and **pattern invocation** from the open multi-loop pattern library (fan-out, diagnose-chain, branch-and-synthesize shapes). Its organ: **traversal memory** — the cross-inquiry record of what was visited, what was selected, why, and what came of it. Its body (at maturity): the **automation carrier** — whatever mechanism lets it actually spawn and sequence sessions without a human typing each command. The carrier is named here as a dependency and deliberately not designed (structural/process layers).

**The triple ↔ nine-axis correspondence.** The user's three-part decomposition and the autonomy ladder's nine-axis frame are two resolutions of one architecture: loop runners ↔ the Worker role; navigational session ↔ the Navigator role; orchestrator ↔ Selector + Runner (+ Evaluator at Level 4) **plus** the Memory axis (its organ) **plus** the Reflect-channel axis (it learns from its own recorded rationales and outcomes); the Multi-head axis is the orchestrator's dispatch dimension (how many hands turn at once); the Goal-formation axis stays **outside SUSTRALL** — the human's retained seat. The user's parenthesis "maybe this is meta loop" resolves cleanly: the meta-loop is not one slot in the triple — **it is what the assembled triple IS**; at Level 4 the orchestrator internally differentiates into distinct Selector / Runner / Evaluator seats.

### 4. The bootstrap jump

**From:** Level 0/1. Automation lives *below* the inquiry boundary — within one inquiry, a runner auto-chains six disciplines reliably. Above the boundary, the human is everything: reads the field, selects, dispatches, remembers (the ladder's own words: cross-inquiry traversal "lives in the user's head"), stops.

**To:** Level 3 as the user's "certain degree" — the system selects and dispatches sequential chains within familiar territory, holds memory, consumes its own reflections; the human seeds and supervises. Level 4 as the full whirl — parallel worker heads, cross-head evaluation, merge decisions.

**What crosses (the five cargos):**
1. **Seeing** — already artifact-borne (routelister's maps); needs only the isolated-session habit.
2. **Selecting** — from human gut to a system Selector, calibrated against the recorded rationales of earlier human selections.
3. **Dispatching** — from human typing to the automation carrier.
4. **Remembering** — from the user's head to traversal-memory artifacts.
5. **Stop-judging** — from human fatigue to traversal signals (coverage, convergence, productivity, directedness, depth) — flagged honestly: these are placeholders by canon's own design.

**Three properties of the jump:**
- **It is a staircase wearing the name of a leap.** Each cargo graduates separately on the nine-axis dial, each step gated by evidence. The gates' *shape* is committed (recorded selections → agreement statistics → graduation); their *numbers* (≥10 maps, ≥80% agreement) are placeholders by their source document's own statement — committed shape, placeholder values.
- **The staircase is self-provisioning.** The strongest objection this inquiry faced — "build quality-awareness first; untrusted automation is worthless" — is answered structurally: the lower steps EXIST to generate the calibration data the upper steps consume. Recording selection rationales at Level 1 is what makes a trustable Level-2 Selector possible. Trust for traversal grows by climbing, not before it.
- **The first turn is available immediately, and it has a definition.** **The turn-invariant:** a revolution counts as a SUSTRALL turn when its selection-rationale is recorded into traversal memory. By that invariant, the very next manually-orchestrated cycle — run the navigator on this finding, choose a route, write one line of why — is already a SUSTRALL turn at Level 0/1. The definition starts the climb by being adopted. Pacing note: gates constrain *order*, not *calendar* — deliberate, honest data-collection can pass them quickly; manufacturing rationale records cannot (that defeats the only purpose the records have).

### 5. Explfine — the acceptance test

**Signature:** `explfine(P, G?)` — point SUSTRALL at territory `P` (any **bounded, readable** project territory; scale is handled by staged resolution, not denied) with goal `G` (may be fuzzy; defaults to "understand P").

**Mechanism-shape:** the whirl alternates the eyes' breadth (concept-identities enumerated at increasing resolution — the corpus's old staged vision: ~10 top concepts, then 50–100, then ~200) with the hands' depth (worker probes that answer "what IS this concept; what are its boundaries; what should it integrate with"), the orchestrator routing between them until signals say stop.

**Output — three artifact classes:**
1. The **concept-map**: components → sub-components → sub-concepts, with integration relations. **The detail-line (its hard boundary):** the map may name components, responsibilities, interfaces, contracts, and integration relations; it may NOT name file paths, languages, frameworks, or code constructs. Like a survey map's declared scale: a 1:100,000 map omits houses by contract, not by failure.
2. **Per-concept definition findings** — each visited concept gets a stabilized definition artifact.
3. The **frontier list** — what remains unexplored, declared honestly.

**Semantics:** "explored" = swept and visited at declared resolution (coverage auditable from the traversal record); "defined" = each visited concept has its definition artifact. **Doneness** is adjudicated by the traversal signals — placeholder-honest. **Differentiators** (what keeps explfine valuable even when generic repo-understanding agents commoditize): the auditable reasoning trail, declared coverage, frontier honesty, and resumability — the method, not just the map.

Teaching analogies, flagged as analogies with no guarantee-import: explfine is *cognitive SLAM* (exploring unknown territory while building the map you navigate by); the jump's staging is the *autopilot certification ladder* (manual → flight-director cues → monitored autopilot → gated autoland — the pilot's role never disappears; it elevates).

**The SUSTRALL-achieved checklist:** SUSTRALL is achieved when (a) the per-axis gates are passed at recorded-evidence level for sequential chains (Level 3), multihead where needed (Level 4), AND (b) an explfine demo completes on a fresh, bounded, readable territory with the human contributing only the seed and reviews — producing map + findings + frontier with declared coverage.

### 7. What achieving SUSTRALL means for self-improvement analysis

**Baldwin cycles ride SUSTRALL revolutions.** The project's improvement mechanism (run → observe → detect pattern → propose change → evaluate → encode into spec) currently advances only when the human aims attention at it. SUSTRALL gives it a substrate that turns on its own:

- **Trigger phase — split honestly in two.** *Process-level* improvement needs (a traversal spinning, a stalled line, a coverage hole, a frontier never picked up) become **system-detected**: the whirl watches its own work through traversal signals and the navigator running over the project's own artifacts. The self-improvement-rate framework's first question — what fraction of improvement cycles are system-detected, today ≈ 0% — finally moves, **for the process class**. *Quality-level* needs (a discipline producing subtly worse findings) still await the Predictive quality layer (`/intuit`-class capability); SUSTRALL does not replace it and this finding does not pretend it does.
- **Speed phase.** Detection-to-correction latency currently contains a dominant human-attention term (an issue waits until the user happens to look). With the orchestrator dispatching, a detected process-issue can become the next probe in the same revolution.
- **Magnitude and Retention phases.** Cycle counting becomes mechanical (traversal memory records every encoded change's journey), and revisit-moves make retention checkable (the whirl can re-test whether an improvement stuck — the currently-uncollectible slow-drift and cross-discipline-transfer measurements become reachable because something finally operates across inquiries).
- **Telemetry birth.** Nearly every starved measurement in the self-improvement-rate framework presupposes exactly the records traversal memory keeps: selections, rationales, outcomes, latencies, revisits. The measurement framework and SUSTRALL are two views of one artifact.
- **Reflexivity.** Self-analysis collapses into the capability itself: **explfine(self)** — pointing the explore-and-define whirl at the project's own harness. The same-day sibling inquiry (the spec-corpus declaration layer) is, in this light, a manual prototype of exactly that.
