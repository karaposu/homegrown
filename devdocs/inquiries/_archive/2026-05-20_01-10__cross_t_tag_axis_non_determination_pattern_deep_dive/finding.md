### 2. Three unity dimensions, three different answers

The cross-T-tag pattern's structural status depends on distinguishing three dimensions of "unity" that look similar but answer different structural questions:

- **Observational unity (YES).** Across all 4 sub-patterns S1-S4, the same SHAPE recurs — mechanism applied at one axis/direction; load-bearing axis/direction missed at same piece. Each sub-pattern is discriminable in mechanism logs (with axis annotation per /innovate's existing per-piece axis-distribution telemetry). The shape is reliably identifiable.

- **Conceptual unity (YES).** All 4 sub-patterns share the underlying phenomenon — at a meta-decision piece, axis-specificity requires deliberate selection. /innovate's mechanism vocabulary is generic (axis-agnostic at piece-application time); without rules that FORCE specific axes at specific piece types, Innovation defaults to the most-available axis (content; LADDER-direction; depth-only; etc.) and misses load-bearing axes that require deliberate selection. The phenomenon is a single underlying cause.

- **Fix-wise unity (NO).** Each sub-pattern maps to a DIFFERENT primary fix mechanism (see Section 3 for the mapping). Per the failure-fix asymmetry argument committed at 00-15 — wrong-axis-Inversion requires axis-spec fix; Inversion-absence requires rule-firing fix — generalizing to N=4 shows ALL 4 sub-patterns have distinct primary fixes. No single rule fixes all 4.

**The structural lesson — silent widening is a cost, not a saving.**

The 22-00 synthesis silently widened ADD-MULTI-AXIS-REQUIREMENT's strict T4 + wrong-axis-Inversion revival trigger to encompass the wider cross-T-tag pattern, counting Pair 12 toward ADD-MULTI-AXIS's cumulative threshold despite Pair 12's failure pattern being Inversion-absence (Q3 territory, not ADD-MULTI-AXIS territory). The 00-00 inquiry corrected this on preserved-frontier-promotion discipline grounds.

The structural lesson the silent-widening incident teaches: **conflating observational + conceptual unity (YES) with fix-wise unity (NO) produces silent-widening incidents.** When the pattern is observationally + conceptually one but fix-wise distributed, treating it as one for promotion purposes maps the conceptual unity onto a rule that cannot honor the fix-wise distribution. The result is over-application or rule conflation.

Distinguishing the 3 dimensions explicitly is the structural prevention.

1. **(a) Sub-pattern count grows beyond N=4.** A new sub-pattern S5 (or more) is observed and added to the OPEN enumeration.

**The framing is SIGNAL, not COMMITMENT.** The conjunctive signal triggers re-examination of META-RULE feasibility — it does NOT commit to producing a META-RULE.
