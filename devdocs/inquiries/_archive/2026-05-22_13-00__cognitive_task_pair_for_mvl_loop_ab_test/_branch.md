# Branch: Loop-Level Meaningful Task Pair Design for /MVL+ vs /MVL2+ Comparison

## Question

What 10 paste-ready prompts (5+5 by nature, where each prompt presents a genuine cognitive task — decide / diagnose / design / strategize / analyze — rather than a surface-level enumeration or mapping task) would let the user run `/MVL+` versus `/MVL2+` on identical forked sessions and observe BOTH (a) the individual differences between `/explore` and `/surfacing` at their respective upstream-output stages AND (b) the cumulative effect of the upstream-discipline choice on the full E→S→D→I→C loop's final finding?

## Goal

A deliverable the user can act on immediately:

- 10 paste-ready prompts where each is a genuine cognitive job (not "find every X" or "list all Y"), such that:
  - The upstream discipline (`/explore` or `/surfacing`) does its mapping/surfacing work as PART of the cognitive task, not as the whole task.
  - Sense-making, decomposition, innovation, and critique each must do substantive cognitive work to advance the task.
  - The finding the loop produces is a meaningful work product (a decision with reasoning, a diagnosis with evidence, a design with components, a strategy with trade-offs).
- Each prompt annotated with: which axes of `/explore` vs `/surfacing` divergence the upstream-stage will exercise, what the loop's final finding should look like in shape, and at which downstream stage(s) the upstream-difference is expected to propagate most visibly.
- A warming protocol (carried over from the prior inquiry, possibly refined).
- A pre-committed comparison rubric measured at TWO levels:
  - **Upstream-output level** — compare the per-fork output of stage 1 (exploration.md vs surfacing.md). What does each variant surface? At what granularity? With what relevance discipline?
  - **Finding level** — compare the per-fork final finding.md. What did the loop conclude? With what depth, precision, and robustness?
- Caveats and self-reference vigilance carried over (with whatever refinements are warranted by this corrected framing).

The user will run the test by: opening one session, running the warming protocol, forking into two identical sessions, prefixing prompts with `/MVL+` in one fork and `/MVL2+` in the other, then comparing per-fork outputs at upstream-stage AND at finding-stage.

## Scope Check

Question covers goal. The question asks for the 10 prompts; the goal asks for the 10 prompts + per-prompt annotation + dual-level comparison rubric + warming + caveats. The supporting elements are operationally necessary for the prompts to function as loop-level test prompts.

Specific-vs-pattern check: SPECIFIC — the deliverable is 10 concrete cognitive-task prompts for this exact A/B comparison. Broader patterns (how to construct meaningful test tasks for ANY cognitive-loop comparison; what distinguishes a discipline-test from a loop-test in general) may emerge as observations but are not foreground.
