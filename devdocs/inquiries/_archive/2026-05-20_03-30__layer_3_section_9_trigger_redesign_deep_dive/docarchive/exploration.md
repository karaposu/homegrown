### R7: Trigger-mechanism epistemology — what counts measure / fail to measure

The count-based trigger has an implicit epistemology:
- **What it measures:** discrete event occurrences (override recordings) tracked across an unbounded inquiry stream.
- **What it asserts:** when the event count crosses threshold N, the discipline mechanism (override compliance criterion) may need investigation for formulaicness.
- **What it ASSUMES:** event-rate IS the appropriate proxy for formulaicness. (Counter: a single highly-formulaic override is enough evidence; 5 well-reasoned overrides are not formulaic.)
- **What it FAILS to measure:** (a) the QUALITY/PATTERN of reasoning across overrides (formulaicness IN the reasons, not in the events); (b) the absence-of-events (TRIVIALLY SATISFIED — Property (v) doesn't fire — entire mechanism is bypassed); (c) the ACTIVE-NO-OVERRIDE outcome (Property (v) fires + alternative genuinely considered + accepted as inapplicable WITHOUT entering the override path).

**Structural blindness pattern.** The count tracks ONE outcome (override recording) of THREE possible outcomes (default / mode-switch / override) of TWO possible operational states (Property (v) fires / doesn't fire). The count sees roughly 1/6 of the operational behavior. The other 5/6 are operationally invisible to the count.

**Cross-frontier observation.** This pattern generalizes:
- ADD-MULTI-AXIS revival trigger: N=3 T4 + wrong-axis-Inversion cases → trigger. Counts a specific failure-mode-event-type; does NOT count successful prevention of that failure mode. If discipline prevents the failure mode, count stays zero. (Substantively distinct from Layer-3 §9 — discipline preventing the failure IS the desired outcome.)
- Cross-T-tag conjunctive count+coverage trigger: counts pattern instances + spec coverage. Conjunctive design measures TWO axes; this is structurally more robust than single-count designs because it requires correlation, but still depends on event-occurrence.
