### 1. Two-layered protective purpose + state-conditional mechanism-redundancy

The Methodology-Mode Consideration refinement note at the innovate discipline's Phase 1 Seed (committed at `cognitive_harness/innovate/references/innovate.md` lines 281-302) protects against TWO STRUCTURALLY DISTINCT failure modes.

**Layer A (operational protective purpose).** The rule's 4-step procedure (identify inherited mode / generate ≥1 alternative / state what follows / decide default-or-mode-switch-or-override) protects against Innovation runs executing under the wrong methodology-mode distribution. This is what the Pair 8 origin diagnostic at `devdocs/inquiries/2026-05-18_loop_diagnose__innovation_missed_contrarian_rethink_methodology_mode/finding.md` committed to address — the weak prior at `2026-05-16_10-50__finding_md_format_redesign` ran balanced default mechanism distribution when the seed framing actually called for Framer-weighted contrarian-rethink mode; the user's correction at `2026-05-16_11-30__contrarian_rethink_finding_md_format` operationalized what Innovation's seed-time consideration could have produced internally. Layer A protects this operational surface.

**Layer B (meta-operational protective purpose).** The N=5 RECORDED-OVERRIDES trigger threshold (documented by Pair 12 per `devdocs/inquiries/2026-05-19_00-00__add_multi_axis_requirement_design_and_promotion_gate/finding.md` line 309's reference to "Pair #12 noted that 5 consecutive overrides would trigger an explicit follow-on investigation"; framed monitoring-style by the 22-00 synthesis at `devdocs/inquiries/2026-05-18_22-00__innovate_improvement_synthesis_from_recent_diagnostics/finding.md` line 271's "before N=5-6 with rote application erodes the intentional-friction purpose") protects against the override mechanism (Layer A's escape valve) degrading into formulaic template-filling — overrides becoming ritual rather than genuine consideration. Layer B protects this meta-operational surface.

**Scope of the trigger redesign.** The redesign question scopes to Layer B specifically. Layer A's 4-step procedure + override path + compliance criterion are preserved unchanged. The redesign addresses how the project detects Layer A's escape valve becoming formulaic, not how Layer A's procedure itself runs.

**Mechanism-redundancy is calibration-state-conditional.** Under the current high-discipline calibration state (post-23-00; characterized by sensemaking consistently adjudicating methodology mode at SV3-SV4-SV6 depth across diverse content types), the §9 rule's Layer A purpose is operationally redundant — upstream sensemaking adjudicates methodology mode at the seed-framing level, and §9's procedure becomes ceremonial compliance (Innovation reads the framing's methodology-mode commitment, generates the required alternative as procedural form, and rejects the alternative without override because sensemaking has already adjudicated). This is observable as the 5/5 ACTIVE-NO-OVERRIDE production cases across the cumulative inquiry pattern (Sub-Inquiry A at `devdocs/inquiries/2026-05-19_02-00__innovate_spec_edit_subinquiry_a_inherited_frame_audit/finding.md`; Sub-Inquiry B at `devdocs/inquiries/2026-05-19_03-00__innovate_spec_edit_subinquiry_b_piece_level_rules_methodology_mode/finding.md`; Sub-Inquiry C at `devdocs/inquiries/2026-05-19_04-00__innovate_spec_edit_subinquiry_c_mechanism_refinement_telemetry/finding.md`; the Pair 5 Q1 deep dive at `devdocs/inquiries/2026-05-19_05-00__pair_5_q1_inversion_definitional_clarification_deep_dive/finding.md`; the Pair 5 Q4 deep dive at `devdocs/inquiries/2026-05-19_06-00__pair_5_q4_failure_mode_prevention_deep_dive/finding.md` — collectively recording explicit methodology-mode-alternative consideration at Phase 1 Seed + structural-grounds rejection without entering the override path).

Layer B's operational silence under current discipline follows the same pattern: override events don't accumulate because Layer A's override path isn't entered; therefore the N=5 threshold's count doesn't advance. Under hypothetical lower-discipline calibration states (different inquiry runners; weaker sensemaking depth; emergency contexts; methodology-mode vocabulary that has evolved past the inherited mode's text-signal coverage), Layer A's procedure would become load-bearing — and Layer B's protective purpose would re-emerge with it.

**The rule is NOT removed.** The frame-level alternative ("§9 is operationally redundant under current state; remove the rule") was tested at this inquiry's Sensemaking (Ambiguity 5) and rejected on state-conditional necessity grounds. The rule's protective purpose remains valid across calibration states; removing it would lose protection in lower-discipline states. The structural commitment: preserve the rule; redesign the trigger; treat calibration-state-dependency as an explicit design parameter going forward.

### 2. Why the current count-based trigger is structurally inadequate + design-intent shift

The current Layer B mechanism is count-based — it tracks `Methodology-mode-alternative-marked-inapplicable` event recordings and fires investigation into compliance-criterion strengthening when the count reaches N=5 RECORDED OVERRIDES. The mechanism is structurally inadequate for three coupled reasons.

**(1) Mechanism-purpose mismatch.** The count tracks event-occurrence-rate; the Layer B protective purpose is pattern-detection (formulaicness in override reasoning). Event-occurrence-rate is a proxy for formulaicness when formulaicness manifests as accumulation; but the 22-00 synthesis's observation of a "shared structural pattern ('upstream-discipline-boundary + calibration-cost; rotating specific references per case')" was made at N=4 — BEFORE crossing the N=5 threshold, and through human inspection of the override reasons' content, not through automatic detection. The proxy doesn't see what the purpose targets. Even without disciplined operation (e.g., during the 4 historical overrides at Pair 8 / Pair 12 / 22-00 synthesis / 23-00 A1 branch experiment design), the proxy reports event-count without detecting whether the reasons share structure. The mismatch is intrinsic: count measures the wrong thing for the purpose.

**(2) 1/N operational-blindness.** The §9 rule's 4-step procedure produces three enumerated outcomes (default / mode-switch / override) operating across two operational states (Property (v) of the Meta-Decision-Piece Criterion fires vs Property (v) doesn't fire). With the operational evidence of a 4th implicit outcome (default-with-stated-rejection-reason; see Section 7 below), the path-space is 4 outcomes × 2 states = 8 operational paths. The count-based trigger observes EXACTLY ONE of these paths (override-recording-with-Property-(v)-firing). The other 7 paths are operationally invisible to the count. Concretely, in the 9-cumulative-inquiry pattern that motivated this inquiry: 4 inquiries produced TRIVIALLY SATISFIED outcomes (Property (v) doesn't fire — 4 of the 8 paths) and 5 inquiries produced ACTIVE-NO-OVERRIDE outcomes (Property (v) fires + alternative considered + rejected without entering the override path — 3 of the 8 paths). None of these 9 outcomes is observable to the count, despite all of them being relevant to Layer B's pattern-detection purpose. The blindness is intrinsic to the count's design, not contingent on discipline.

**(3) 9-cumulative-inquiry pattern interpretation.** The pattern at N=9 (the 00-00 ADD-MULTI-AXIS design-and-promotion inquiry as TRIVIALLY SATISFIED Path-C-Documentation; Sub-Inquiries A/B/C as ACTIVE-NO-OVERRIDE Production at 02-00 / 03-00 / 04-00; the Pair 5 Q1 deep dive at 05-00 as ACTIVE-NO-OVERRIDE Production; the Pair 5 Q4 deep dive at 06-00 — counting iterations 1+2 as one inquiry per the 06-00 finding's own framing — as ACTIVE-NO-OVERRIDE Production; the ADD-MULTI-AXIS understanding deep dive at 00-15 as TRIVIALLY SATISFIED Documentation; the cross-T-tag pattern deep dive at 01-10 as TRIVIALLY SATISFIED Documentation; the preserved-frontier codification deep dive at 02-15 as TRIVIALLY SATISFIED Documentation) is empirical evidence of the mechanism-purpose mismatch — NOT evidence the trigger is succeeding-by-silence. The strongest counter-interpretation — "the trigger is structurally correct; under disciplined operation override events don't accumulate; therefore the trigger correctly doesn't fire; non-firing is success-by-silence" — fails on structural grounds: the trigger's protective PURPOSE (detect formulaicness) is different from the trigger's MECHANISM (count override events); when discipline produces non-override outcomes that are nonetheless relevant to formulaicness detection, the trigger is mechanism-blind to the relevant signal. Discipline producing the pattern shows that even when the protective purpose is operationally relevant (Property (v) fires; alternatives are considered), the count cannot observe what would be detected. The pattern is structural evidence of the count's blindness.

**Design-intent shift: late-detection of accumulated formulaicness → early-detection of discipline-weakening.** The current trigger is structurally a late-detection mechanism — it fires after enough override events accumulate to suggest the pattern has stabilized. The redesigned trigger should be an early-detection mechanism — it fires on early signals that the rule's protective purpose is becoming operationally relevant (i.e., that the calibration state is shifting toward a state where Layer A's procedure becomes load-bearing). The signals an early-detection mechanism would observe include: rate-of-Property-(v)-firing-with-vs-without-override; pattern-recurrence in override reasons; cadence of new methodology-mode types added to the discipline's vocabulary; rate of inquiries where sensemaking did not adjudicate methodology mode upstream; emergence of edge cases (e.g., inquiries where Property (v) fires but neither default nor mode-switch nor override is structurally clean). This is a substantive purpose-shift — the trigger's design intent itself changes, not just its mechanism. The implication: the redesigned trigger is calibration-state-aware, observing state-transitions rather than tracking discrete events.

The 22-00 synthesis line 271's framing (N=4 = "MONITORING THRESHOLD REACHED"; N=5-6 = the action threshold; "before N=5-6 with rote application erodes the intentional-friction purpose") implicitly committed to multi-stage threshold design (monitor + act distinct). The current single-threshold framing in `docs/unanswered_frontiers.md` Frontier #7 ("N=5 fires investigation") collapses these stages. The redesigned trigger preserves the multi-stage framing: monitor stage detects discipline-weakening signals; act stage fires investigation when signals cross the act threshold (see Section 5 below).

### 3. Outcome enumeration gap — 3 enumerated vs 4 operational

The §9 rule's step (iv) at innovate.md lines 281-302 enumerates three outcomes: (i) default (use inherited mode); (ii) mode-switch (record `Seed-time-methodology-mode-switch: <new-mode>; reason: <specific reason>`); (iii) override (record `Methodology-mode-alternative-marked-inapplicable: <specific reason>`). The compliance criterion at the artifact-observable level requires the seed/preamble section to record the inherited mode, ≥1 alternative, the "what follows" description, and the decision.

The operational evidence across the 5 ACTIVE-NO-OVERRIDE production cases — Sub-Inquiry A at 02-00; Sub-Inquiry B at 03-00; Sub-Inquiry C at 04-00; the Pair 5 Q1 deep dive at 05-00; the Pair 5 Q4 deep dive at 06-00 — shows a fourth operational outcome the spec does not enumerate.

**Outcome (iv-implicit): default-with-stated-rejection-reason.** The runner (a) considered the alternative; (b) rejected the alternative; (c) documented reasoning for the rejection in narrative form within the seed/preamble section; (d) proceeded with the inherited mode without recording the rejection via the override pattern's specific `Methodology-mode-alternative-marked-inapplicable: ...` format. The compliance criterion accepts this operational behavior (the decision is recorded; the consideration occurred; the reasoning is present) but doesn't structurally distinguish it from outcome (i) default-as-baseline (which is when the runner chose default without generating a substantive alternative-and-rejection narrative).

**The gap is structural, not interpretive.** The 4th operational outcome's existence is determined by what the compliance criterion verifies (which accepts narrative reasoning) versus what the procedure's step (iv) enumerates (which lists 3 outcomes). The compliance criterion is more permissive than the enumeration; the operational behavior occupies the gap.

**The 4th outcome's structural relevance to the trigger redesign.** Outcome (iv-implicit) is structurally near outcome (iii) override — both reject the alternative with documented reasoning. The structural difference is the format of the rejection (narrative vs override-pattern). The count-based trigger observes only outcome (iii); outcome (iv-implicit) is invisible. The trigger's Layer B protective purpose (formulaicness detection) is concerned with the rate and pattern of consideration-with-rejection; this is observable at outcomes (iii) + (iv-implicit) combined, not at (iii) alone. The trigger redesign's FIRING axis (Section 5.A below) must consider observing combined outcomes, not only override events.

**Closure deferred.** The gap closure (whether to tighten the rule to 3 outcomes by requiring override for all rejections, or loosen the rule to 4 outcomes by enumerating the implicit one) is a STRUCTURAL inquiry concern. This MEANING-layer artifact acknowledges the gap; the future STRUCTURAL inquiry that commits the trigger redesign spec text should address this gap as a coupled commitment. Reasoning for the deferral: the gap closure is rule-redesign substance (changing the rule's structural model), not trigger redesign substance (changing how Layer B's pattern detection mechanism works); attempting both in one structural commit would over-scope.

### 4. Cross-frontier coupling with T-A — META only, substance-distinct

The typed framework codification at the 02-15 inquiry's finding Section 5 named the META-level coupling between Layer-3 §9's count-based trigger and T-A's cumulative-N evidence threshold (T-A is the capability-gap template in the typed framework; its canonical worked example is the A1 Inherited Frame Audit). Both are count-based; both face trigger-design questions. The 02-15 finding distinguished them as substance-distinct: T-A counts EVIDENCE-ACCUMULATION (advances organically with new diagnostic cases; not suppressed by disciplined operation); Layer-3 §9 counts INCIDENT-RECORDING (advances only with override events; suppressed by disciplined operation).

The substance-distinction has direct implications for whether the trigger redesign's substance propagates between them.

**T-A's count-based design may be structurally correct given its substance.** T-A's threshold counts cases-that-advance-the-warrant — each new diagnostic case where the relevant pattern surfaces is direct evidence that the candidate should be promoted. The count IS a useful proxy for evidence-strength because evidence-strength advances exactly as cases do. Discipline doesn't suppress new cases; new diagnostic cases are produced by the project's continued operation on novel content (different problem domains; different inquiry types; different runners). The count-based shape fits the count-based substance.

**Layer-3 §9's count-based design is structurally incorrect given its substance.** The count tracks events-that-proxy-formulaicness. Events are not proxy-able to formulaicness when discipline prevents events (by adjudicating methodology mode upstream) without preventing formulaicness (formulaicness can emerge in other paths or be silent until late). The count-based shape does not fit the formulaicness-targeting substance.

**The substance answer does NOT propagate from Layer-3 §9 to T-A.** The redesign commitment (non-count FIRING taxonomy; multi-stage threshold with per-stage actions; investigation-into-rule-redesign output) is specific to Layer-3 §9's substance. T-A's design may legitimately stay count-based; it's not a redesign target unless separately analyzed.

**The META framing applies to both.** The trigger-mechanism epistemology question — "what does this count measure and what is it actually targeting?" — is a META analysis applicable to ANY count-based trigger in the project. The framing transfers; the answers diverge per trigger's substance. Future trigger-mechanism work should apply per-trigger structural-substance analysis, not a uniform "all count-based triggers need redesign" answer.

**Cross-frontier transferability summary.** META coupling is real and useful (trigger-mechanism epistemology applies project-wide). Substance non-propagation is real and structurally grounded (different what's-being-counted produces different right-answers). Caveat: if T-A's design is later questioned by an inquiry surfacing a structural issue specific to T-A's substance (e.g., evidence-accumulation slowing in unexpected ways), separate analysis is required; the META framing CAN frame the question, but the answer requires substance-specific work.

This frame is consistent with the typed framework's recognition (per the 02-15 finding Section 5) that template-specific structure is preserved across redesign questions; the typed framework itself is structurally adequate without requiring uniform answers across templates.

### 5. The 3-axis trigger design at MEANING-layer (TAXONOMY level)

The trigger redesign has 3 independently adjudicable design axes. The MEANING-layer adjudication is at TAXONOMY (or category) level — committing to classes of design choices; specific candidates within the classes are STRUCTURAL inquiry concerns downstream.

#### 5.A — FIRING axis

The FIRING axis answers "what signal fires the trigger?" The MEANING-layer adjudication is at taxonomy level.

**Four-form FIRING taxonomy** (synthesized from the 8 alternative forms surfaced at this inquiry's Exploration, grouped by observation kind):

1. **Count-based** — observes event-occurrence-rate (one specific event type, like override recordings). Example: the current N=5 RECORDED-OVERRIDES trigger.
2. **Condition-based** — observes a specific structural pattern's presence. Examples: structural-ambiguity-detection (pattern-recurrence in override reasons); calibration-state-detection (state shift in the project that makes the rule's purpose operationally relevant); mechanism-redundancy-detection (upstream consistently adjudicating).
3. **Conjunctive-signal-based** — observes multiple partial signals together; fires when conjunction crosses threshold. Example: combine override-count + pattern-in-reasons + ratio-of-fire-vs-trivially-satisfied.
4. **Self-referential-observation-based** — observes discipline-level meta-observations from external reviewers (e.g., critique discipline flags formulaicness; reviewer notes the absence-of-events pattern).

**Note on period-based.** The Frontier #7 seed taxonomy at `docs/unanswered_frontiers.md` named period-based (fires after N inquiries regardless of count). Period-based observes elapsed-inquiry-cadence — structurally a count-form on a different countable (inquiries elapsed vs override events). It can be subsumed under count-based as a sub-case (same mechanism-purpose mismatch applies: cadence-counting doesn't directly detect formulaicness patterns) or treated as a 5th observation kind (temporal/cadence-based, distinct from event-occurrence). Either way, the MEANING-layer commitment to non-count taxonomy rejects period-based for the same structural reason as count-based: cadence is a proxy for what discipline-state-shift might produce, not direct pattern-detection. The choice between subsumption and separate-taxon framing is a STRUCTURAL inquiry concern.

**MEANING-layer answer: NON-COUNT.** Either condition-based (directly addresses Layer B's pattern-detection purpose; structurally aligned with formulaicness-as-pattern) or conjunctive-signal-based (combines partial signals for robustness against single-mechanism failure). The specific candidate within the chosen taxon is DEFERRED to the future STRUCTURAL inquiry. The MEANING-layer commitment: count-based taxonomy is rejected; the alternative taxons are open with structural rationale provided.

Rationale for non-count commitment: per Section 2 above, the count's mechanism-purpose mismatch is structural — count tracks event-occurrence-rate while Layer B's purpose targets formulaicness-as-pattern. Condition-based or conjunctive-signal-based forms align structure with purpose. The future STRUCTURAL inquiry selects between condition-based and conjunctive-signal-based (and within each, the specific candidate) based on operational feasibility, cost, and integration with existing project-level discipline mechanisms.

Self-referential-observation-based is open as supplementary or alternative. It could be the primary FIRING form (the trigger fires when a discipline-level observer flags formulaicness) or a supplementary signal within a conjunctive form. The choice depends on the future STRUCTURAL inquiry's analysis of who-observes-what.

#### 5.B — OUTPUT axis

The OUTPUT axis answers "what does the trigger produce when it fires?" The MEANING-layer adjudication is at category level.
