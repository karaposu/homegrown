Maintenance candidate for B4 (user-words-as-constraint default) operates BEFORE the pipeline starts, not at CONCLUDE; it's pre-pipeline interpretation rule.

Both pass the "would they need a separate maintenance piece?" test. Therefore: NEW DIMENSIONS.

**Confidence:** MEDIUM (each has 1 clear instance in the 3-prior chain; structural distinctness is clear but generalization beyond this chain is not yet evidenced).

**Resolution:** B6 becomes Dimension 6 (Synthesis-as-Validation); B4 becomes Dimension 7 (Speculative Tooling on User Permission). Framework grows to 7 dimensions.
