## 3. Inventory per Region

### R1 — Typed framework substance (D3)

**Working characterization:** "A typed framework of preserved-frontier resolution templates that classifies any preserved-frontier candidate into one of N templates (currently 3 named; OPEN enumeration) + dispatches to the corresponding promotion-path discipline + revival-trigger semantics."

The framework's structural identity has 4 components:
1. **Template enumeration** — the named templates (T-A, T-B, T-C; potentially T-D at a different level).
2. **Operational distinguishing tests** — the checks that select which template applies to a candidate.
3. **Promotion paths per template** — what the candidate's path from preservation to either commit or named-reference looks like.
4. **Revival trigger semantics per template** — what conditions advance the candidate's status.

**Operational value of the codification itself** (independent of the templates' individual values):
- **Prevents future silent-widening incidents.** The 22-00 silent-widening (subsumed Pair 12 under ADD-MULTI-AXIS's strict trigger; corrected at 00-00) was caused by lack of explicit template-selection discipline. Codification provides the discipline.
- **Future-inquiry-author checklist value.** Any future preserved-frontier candidate is adjudicated through the framework; no ad-hoc per-candidate adjudication.
- **Project-level transferability.** The framework's structural identity is innovate-independent.

**Open question at R1:** is the framework characterization itself OPEN (does it allow future structural categories beyond "template enumeration + tests + paths + triggers"), or is the 4-component structure a closed set? Given the OPEN-enumeration commitment at the template level, the 4-component structure is likely also open (analogous to cross-T-tag's OPEN sub-pattern vocabulary).

### R2 — T-A capability-gap template at depth (D3; A1 worked example)

**Structural identity:** the candidate addresses a scope where NO existing rule operates. The candidate ADDS a new defense-in-depth layer at a previously-uncovered vantage. New capability; not consolidation.

**Operational distinguishing test:** scope-coverage check returns NONE.

**Promotion path:** **branch-experiment-design → committed at convergence threshold.**
- Candidate originates from a diagnostic case (e.g., Pair 9's A1 origin at 09-20).
- Cumulative evidence accumulates across subsequent diagnostics (Pair 9's A1 at N=3 at 16-30; N=4 at 18-00; N=8 at 22-00 synthesis).
- At convergence threshold (project-level heuristic: ~N=8 for A1; varies per candidate scope), the candidate is PROMOTED to "ACTIONABLE-AS-BRANCH-INQUIRY."
- A dedicated branch-experiment inquiry (e.g., 23-00 for A1) DESIGNS the candidate's operational specification — components (predicate; orchestration; override path; evaluation gate; integration map) + empirical validation (e.g., 8/8 in-scope cases fire correctly).
- A downstream spec-edit inquiry (e.g., 02-00 Sub-Inquiry A for A1) COMMITS the candidate to /innovate spec via direct text edits.

**Revival trigger semantics:** **cumulative-N count.** The trigger advances by counting diagnostic cases that evidence the same capability gap. The threshold (N=8 for A1) is heuristic, not rigid. Convergence-driven.

**Worked example reference:** A1 (Inherited Frame Audit) — committed at /innovate spec lines 416-549 via 02-00. Source for the path stages: 09-20 (origin) → 16-30 (promoted) → 18-00 (strengthened) → 22-00 (N=8 declared) → 23-00 (designed) → 02-00 (committed). Six stages; ~2-month elapsed time.

### R3 — T-B structural-consolidation template at depth (D3; ADD-MULTI-AXIS worked example)

**Structural identity:** the candidate addresses scope where MULTIPLE existing rules operate at related scopes. The candidate would UNIFY them under a single meta-rule. Consolidation, not new capability.

**Operational distinguishing test:** scope-coverage check returns PARTIAL (or FULL — see edge case) + role check returns UNIFY.

**Promotion path:** **preservation-with-strict-trigger.**
- Candidate originates from a specific diagnostic case (e.g., Pair 7's ADD-MULTI-AXIS-REQUIREMENT origin via recursive self-application).
- Candidate is PRESERVED at origin with a STRICT REVIVAL TRIGGER (specific failure-pattern + specific T-tag + specific count threshold).
- The candidate is NOT promoted by general accumulation; only by the strict trigger firing.
- If the trigger fires, a dedicated promotion inquiry adjudicates the commit.
- If the trigger doesn't fire, the candidate stays preserved indefinitely.

**Revival trigger semantics:** **strict specification.** The trigger has substance-precise conditions (e.g., "3+ future T4 cases at meta-decision pieces firing properties iv/v where single-axis specification proves insufficient on wrong-axis-Inversion failures"). Adjacency evidence doesn't count.

**Anti-silent-widening discipline applies acutely:** the 22-00 synthesis silently widened ADD-MULTI-AXIS's strict trigger to encompass adjacent cases (Pair 12); 00-00 corrected via strict-reading restoration. Future T-B candidates must honor original revival triggers.

**Worked example reference:** ADD-MULTI-AXIS-REQUIREMENT. Path: Pair 7 (origin; preservation) → 22-00 (silent widening to N=2 attempted) → 00-00 (strict reading restored to N=1) → Path C committed (preservation with strict trigger). Currently at N=1 strict; trigger NOT fired.

**Operational distinguishing test:** scope-coverage PARTIAL + role UNIFY + fix-wise unity NO.

**Promotion path:** **named-reference preservation** with **NO RULE-PROMOTION PATH.**
- Candidate is NAMED + tracked as META-observation.
- Sub-patterns enumerated (OPEN list); per-sub-pattern fix-mapping documented.
- Accumulation tracked across diagnostics.
- Anti-silent-widening discipline enforced (sub-patterns explicitly NOT counted toward other templates' promotion triggers).

**Revival trigger semantics:** **conjunctive-signal threshold for META-RULE re-examination** (NOT promotion). Conditions: (a) sub-pattern count grows beyond known N AND (b) live constellation coverage observed inadequate. Re-examination outcome may extend the framework OR commit a META-RULE OR preserve the constellation's redundancy.

**Worked example reference:** Cross-T-tag axis-non-determination pattern. ### R5 — T-D candidate (4-alternative-analysis) adjudication (D3)

**The candidate:** 4-alternative-analysis calibration framework (Alt D full / Alt A mid / Alt B see-also / Alt C status quo) per-sub-piece, established at 05-00 Q1 deep dive + reused asymmetrically at 06-00 Q4 deep dive.

**Question:** is this a fourth template at the same level as T-A / T-B / T-C, OR is it a DIFFERENT category?

**Analysis:**
- T-A / T-B / T-C answer: "WHICH TEMPLATE CLASSIFIES this preserved-frontier candidate's promotion path?" Operates at per-candidate scope.
- 4-alternative-analysis answers: "GIVEN a candidate is being committed, AT WHAT FORM is the commit?" Operates at per-sub-piece scope WITHIN a candidate's commit.

These are at DIFFERENT LEVELS:
- T-A/B/C: candidate classification → promotion path.
- 4-alt-analysis: commit form within promotion → per-sub-piece calibration.

**Verdict:** 4-alternative-analysis is NOT a fourth template at the same level. It is a **CALIBRATION-DISCIPLINE MECHANISM** that applies within commit decisions, orthogonal to template classification. Conceptually:
- A T-A candidate (capability-gap; branch-experiment) when committed via the branch-experiment-derived spec edit MAY use 4-alternative-analysis per-sub-piece in the commit text (e.g., Sub-Inquiry A's A1 commits at full form; no see-also pointer needed for the canonical sub-section).
- A T-B candidate (structural-consolidation), IF the strict trigger fires + promotion happens, MAY use 4-alternative-analysis per-sub-piece (e.g., a hypothetical future ADD-MULTI-AXIS commit might use Alt B for some sub-pieces if the substance is already encoded elsewhere).
- A T-C candidate has NO rule-promotion path — 4-alternative-analysis doesn't apply because no rule is committed.

**Alternative framing — T-D as parallel meta-mechanism:** the framework could acknowledge 4-alternative-analysis as a META-MECHANISM at a different scope-level. Naming it explicitly (e.g., "calibration-discipline mechanism" or "commit-form-template") preserves its operational value without conflating with T-A/B/C's candidate-level templates.

**Recommended framing:** the typed framework has 2 LEVELS:
- **Level 1 — Candidate-level templates (T-A / T-B / T-C; OPEN enumeration):** classify preserved-frontier candidates by structural identity + dispatch to promotion path.
- **Level 2 — Commit-form mechanisms (4-alternative-analysis; possibly more):** within a commit decision, calibrate the form per-sub-piece.

The 2-level structure is a meaningful structural extension surfaced by this inquiry.

### R6 — Operational distinguishing tests + decision-tree structure (D3)

**Tests (per 00-15 + 01-10):**
- **Test 1 — Scope-coverage check:** does any existing rule operate at the candidate's scope? Result: NONE / PARTIAL / FULL.
- **Test 2 — Role check** (if Test 1 ≠ NONE): would the candidate UNIFY existing rules (consolidation) or ADD a new vantage (capability gap)?
- **Test 3 — Fix-wise unity check** (if Test 2 = UNIFY): does a single rule fix the candidate's pattern, or are fixes distributed?

**Decision tree:**

```
Scope-coverage check
├── NONE → T-A (capability-gap)
├── PARTIAL → Role check
│   ├── ADD → T-A (with partial-coverage acknowledgment)
│   └── UNIFY → Fix-wise unity check
│       ├── YES (single rule) → T-B (structural-consolidation)
│       └── NO (distributed fixes) → T-C (distributed-fix META-observation)
└── FULL → edge case (research-frontier per 01-10; possibly "redundant" status)
```

**Edge cases (per 01-10 Section 4):**
- **FULL-coverage candidate:** existing rules fully cover scope; candidate is redundant. The 3 templates don't classify this; future-inquiry territory.

**Operational test surface:** the 3 tests reduce to:
- Test 1: 3-way (NONE / PARTIAL / FULL).
- Test 2: 2-way (UNIFY / ADD).
- Test 3: 2-way (YES / NO).

Total enumerated paths: 3 × 2 × 2 = 12 leaves, of which 4 are named (T-A from NONE; T-A from PARTIAL+ADD; T-B from PARTIAL+UNIFY+YES; T-C from PARTIAL+UNIFY+NO) and 1 is edge-case (FULL → future). 7 leaves are unreachable under current test logic (since tests are gated by prior outcomes).

**Subtle point:** Test 1 = FULL is reachable but classifies to edge-case, not to any template. This is an honest gap in the framework.

### R7 — Revival triggers per template + common structure (D3)

**Per-template triggers:**

| Template | Trigger style | Substance | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| T-A | Cumulative-N count | Diagnostic cases evidencing the capability gap; convergence-driven heuristic | A1: N=8 across 8 in-scope diagnostics |
| T-B | Strict specification | Substance-precise conditions (failure-pattern; T-tag; count threshold; failure-mode) | ADD-MULTI-AXIS: 3+ T4 wrong-axis-Inversion at meta-decision pieces; N=1 currently |
| T-C | Conjunctive-signal for META-RULE re-examination (NOT promotion) | (a) sub-pattern count growth AND (b) constellation coverage inadequacy | Cross-T-tag: N=4 sub-patterns; both conditions for re-examination not yet met |

**Common structure across triggers:**
- All triggers are **observation-driven** (track empirical evidence; no design-event advances the count).
- All triggers honor **strict reading** (no silent widening; original specifications preserved).
- All triggers operate at **per-template granularity** (T-A's N=8 ≠ T-B's strict trigger ≠ T-C's conjunctive signal in style or semantics).

**Fundamental differences:**
- **T-A trigger leads to COMMIT** (via branch-experiment + spec edit).
- **T-B trigger leads to PROMOTION INQUIRY ADJUDICATION** (which may commit OR may further preserve).
- **T-C trigger leads to META-RULE RE-EXAMINATION** (which is NOT commit; may extend the META-observation OR commit a META-RULE OR preserve the constellation's redundancy).

The trigger-outcome semantics differ per template — the framework should articulate this explicitly to prevent conflation.

### R9 — Transferability beyond innovate (D2)

**Necessary conditions for a discipline to use the framework:**
1. **The discipline must have preserved-frontier candidates.** The concept of "candidate rule that emerged from diagnostic cases but isn't immediately ready to commit" must exist.
2. **The discipline must have evidence-accumulation mechanisms.** Cases must accumulate across multiple inquiries (LOOP_DIAGNOSE-like; cross-case observation).
3. **The discipline must have a way to commit rules.** Spec edits or analogous structural commits must be possible.

**Application to other disciplines:**
- **Intuit:** if intuit has preserved-frontier candidates emerging from cases, the framework applies. Adaptation: intuit's "fix" vocabulary differs from innovate's mechanism + axis vocabulary.
- **Comprehend:** likely applies; comprehend's anchor + model vocabulary would substitute for innovate's mechanism vocabulary in the operational tests.
- **Sense-making refinement:** the discipline's anchor-extraction + perspective-checking framework has had refinements (some preserved; some promoted). The typed framework's 3 templates may apply, though the canonical worked examples would differ.
- **Protocols (CONCLUDE; LOOP_DIAGNOSE):** protocols typically don't have preserved-frontier candidates in the same sense (protocols are operational specifications, not refinement-accumulators). The framework may not apply directly; might need adaptation.

**Verdict:** the framework's structural identity is discipline-agnostic at the meaning layer. Concrete application requires per-discipline adaptation (worked examples; per-discipline vocabulary), but the 3-template structure + 3-test diagnostic + per-template promotion paths are transferable.

## 5. Jump Scan — different direction

Before declaring convergence: scan in alternative directions.

**Direction 1 — Are there templates beyond T-A / T-B / T-C the project has implicitly used but not named?**

Looking at the project's spec evolution: the 22-00 synthesis identified 4 composition patterns + 3 unnamed (per seed #6 — "spec evolution composition patterns"). These are SPEC-EVOLUTION patterns (how spec edits compose), distinct from PRESERVED-FRONTIER RESOLUTION patterns. Not template candidates at the framework's level.

**Direction 2 — Is the framework a meta-discipline or a meta-protocol?**

A discipline has refinement-note structure + failure modes + Standard Analysis Protocol. A protocol has operational steps + state transitions. The framework is closer to a META-PROTOCOL — it operates ACROSS inquiries + classifies candidates + dispatches to template-specific protocols. Structurally a protocol at the meta level. Option (b) location (cognitive_harness/protocols/) gains some structural support from this framing.

**Direction 3 — Does the framework apply to non-preserved-frontier candidates?**

Examples of non-preserved candidates: ad-hoc spec edits without diagnostic origin; direct user-corrections; out-of-scope items. The framework's structure is specifically for PRESERVED-FRONTIER candidates (those that emerged from cases but weren't immediately committed). It does NOT apply to non-preserved candidates. Bounded scope.

**Direction 4 — Could the framework absorb the cross-T-tag pattern's sub-pattern enumeration?**

The cross-T-tag pattern (T-C worked example) has sub-patterns S1-S4 enumerated. Could the framework's Level 2 (commit-form mechanisms) absorb sub-pattern enumerations as a third level? Possibly — the framework could become 3-level:
- Level 1: candidate-level templates.
- Level 2: commit-form mechanisms (4-alternative-analysis).
- Level 3: sub-pattern enumerations within Level 1 templates (only applies to T-C currently).

Probing: Level 3 is T-C-specific; doesn't generalize to T-A or T-B (those don't have sub-pattern structure). The 3-level expansion would be a non-uniform extension. Better to keep Level 3 as a T-C-specific feature, not a general framework level.

**Jump-scan result:** No surprises that redraw the verdict. The 2-level framework structure (Level 1 candidate-templates; Level 2 commit-form-mechanisms) is stable + non-uniform extensions are bounded to specific templates.
