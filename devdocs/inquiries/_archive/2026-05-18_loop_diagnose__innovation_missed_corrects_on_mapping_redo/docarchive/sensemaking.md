Keep inverting until you reach a statement about the SYSTEM, not about a COMPONENT."* — the refinement REQUIRES depth-iterated Inversion which by definition operates on multiple targets, including system-level targets. The strict reading (one-Inversion-per-seed) is inconsistent with the spec's own depth-check refinement. The counter-interpretation fails on structural grounds: the spec's existing rules contemplate piece-level mechanism application, even if they don't say so explicitly.
