**Why the counter wins (structural grounds):** "Substance" is an informal noun that doesn't appear elsewhere in the explore.md spec. "Deliverable" connects to the discipline-output framing (§5 Transform; §5.4 Frontier-deliverable-to-downstream). The trigger should specify what kind of OUTPUT the inquiry demands — and what triggers form-(ii) selection is the kind of output. "Deliverable" / "required output" is sharper.

**Confidence:** HIGH (the structural counter has clear support; vocabulary aligns with discipline-output framing).

**Resolution:** Replace "substance requires comparing options on shared criteria" with "deliverable requires per-candidate assessment on shared criteria." Apply A3 canonicalization ("options" → "candidates" in same edit).

**What is now fixed:** form-(ii) trigger phrasing uses "deliverable" not "substance."

**What is no longer allowed:** vague trigger phrasings ("substance," "intent," "the task").

**What now depends on this choice:** the form-(ii) sub-bullet wording.

**What changed in the conceptual model:** trigger condition operationally clearer; aligns with discipline-output framing.
