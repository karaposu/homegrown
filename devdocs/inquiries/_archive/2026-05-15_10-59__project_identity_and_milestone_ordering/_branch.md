# Branch: Project Identity and Milestone Ordering

## Question
What is the Homegrown project at full ambition — a thinking harness that, when mounted on an AI model, lets it traverse thinking space and run long complex tasks the way humans do — and what is the meaningful sequential order of its developmental milestones (the capabilities the user named being self-maintenance, auto-navigation, meta-loop, and materialization, treated as examples of a broader pattern rather than the full list)?

## Goal
A reframing of Homegrown that does justice to its full ambition, plus a sequential ordering of its meaningful milestones such that:
- A reader who only sees the project surface (install scripts + markdown skills) understands why the markdown specs add up to something more than "prompt engineering distribution" — specifically, what the harness IS as a cognitive object and what it does FOR AI capability when mounted.
- The named milestones (self-maintenance, auto-navigation, meta-loop, materialization) are correctly placed inside the broader pattern of milestones the project is actually moving through, with each milestone explained for WHAT IT REALLY MEANS (the capability it unlocks, not just the artifact it produces).
- The order respects real dependencies — what unlocks what — rather than being a wishlist.
- The output is usable as the project's own self-description and roadmap orientation; not a marketing summary, not a feature list.

## Scope Check
**Question covers goal.** Both halves (identity reframing + meaningful milestone ordering) are present in the question and required by the goal. Answering the question well produces the goal artifact.

**Specific-vs-pattern check:** the user explicitly stated "not exactly these but what they mean" about the four named milestones. The inquiry therefore addresses the **broader pattern** of meaningful milestones the project is actually moving through, treating self-maintenance / auto-navigation / meta-loop / materialization as illustrative examples that pin the pattern but do not exhaust it. The inquiry must surface additional milestones the named four imply (e.g., quality awareness, real-time hunch/intuition, regression detection, ignition, autonomy ladder rungs, cross-run cognitive steering, multi-head loops) by reading the actual source files, not by guessing.

## Frame to surpass (anti-pattern)
The prior small_summary framed Homegrown as "a prompt-engineering distribution + an aspirational research effort." That frame is technically defensible from a code-counting perspective but misses the project's actual claim: that the markdown specs ARE the engineering — they are an executable cognitive harness whose operations on the LLM substrate produce the additional intelligence. "Prompt engineering distribution" loses two load-bearing ideas:
1. The harness is a **runtime cognitive architecture**, not a collection of prompts. It defines movement-in-thinking-space, not just instructions-to-the-model.
2. The milestones form a **graduated capability ladder** with real dependencies, not a feature roadmap.

The inquiry must produce a frame that names both correctly.
