**Defense.** The cues are listed concretely ("applies to a class of cases," "should be reusable," "applies regardless of context"). Pattern-matching against listed phrases is operationally clear for non-borderline cases. **Collision.** Behavioral divergence on borderline cases is real. Defense holds for non-borderline; borderline cases need deployment-monitoring.

**Verdict.** SURVIVE with **R1**: add "track two-reader divergence rate on borderline cases" to the REPAIR's evaluation gate.
