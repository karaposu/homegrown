### A3 — Aggregate verdict shape (FQ3)

**Resolution:** **Weighted aggregate:**

1. Count A-wins + B-wins + ties + both-weak per criticality tier.
2. CRITICAL tier dominates: if A wins ≥ 2 of 3 CRITICAL AND B does not, A is the aggregate winner. (Mirrored for B.)
3. If CRITICAL split (1-1 or with ties), HIGH tier breaks the tie similarly.
4. If HIGH split, render as TRADE-OFF (no clean winner).
5. MEDIUM tier is informational; doesn't change the verdict but informs the trade-off acknowledgment.

The verdict statement names the winner with explicit per-criticality-tier evidence.
