### 3. The capability-gap-vs-structural-consolidation asymmetry diagnostic

**Structural consolidation.** Multiple existing /innovate rules operate at related scopes that the candidate would UNIFY under a single meta-rule. The structural role is consolidation of partially-covered territory.

The diagnostic's **2 operational checks:**

1. **Scope-coverage check.** Does any existing /innovate rule operate at the candidate's scope? Coverage may be NONE, PARTIAL, or FULL.
2. **Role check.** If coverage is PARTIAL or FULL, would the candidate UNIFY existing rules (consolidation) or ADD a new vantage (capability gap)?

### 5. Loop-back reliability — empirically unresolved

The 00-00 inquiry's BLOCKER-downgrade for ADD-MULTI-AXIS rests on a contingent assumption: the axis-coverage check at /innovate spec line 593 (Phase 3 Test Assembly) reliably fires in practice + the runner reliably re-runs Phase 2 Generate when single-axis bias is flagged. If the loop-back is not reliable, the residual gap (axes other than intervention-shape at non-property-(v) meta-decision pieces) could surface as a real coverage hole, and ADD-MULTI-AXIS's BLOCKER status would be revisited.

**The contingency has 2 separable components:**

1. **The check's firing reliability** — does the axis-coverage check actually fire when single-axis bias exists in a candidate set?
2. **The runner's response reliability** — given a flag, does the runner actually re-run Phase 2 Generate with the alternative axis surfaced?

The check is OPERATIONALLY OBSERVATIONAL (it FLAGS); the bias-countering ACTION (re-run Phase 2) is the runner's response, not the check's own action. Both components must be reliable for the loop-back to work end-to-end.

**The empirical status post-00-00 is UNRESOLVED.** Across the inquiries that ran after 00-00 (the sub-inquiries A/B/C committing per-axis rules; the 05-00 Q1 deep dive; the 06-00 Q4 deep dive iterations 1+2; this inquiry), NO loop-back-skip patterns have been observed in saved Innovation outputs. This absence-of-evidence is consistent with TWO interpretations:

- **(a) Reliable firing.** The axis-coverage check fires + the runner reliably responds. The BLOCKER-downgrade contingency is satisfied.
- **(b) Absence of bias.** The axis-coverage check has NOT fired in any post-00-00 inquiry — no single-axis bias was present in subsequent candidate sets to detect.

Without runtime telemetry distinguishing the check's firings + runner responses, the inquiry CANNOT distinguish (a) from (b). Both interpretations produce the same observable evidence (no loop-back-skip observation).

**Signal-distinction logic — what would confirm vs refute reliability:**

- **Confirms (a) reliable firing:** runtime telemetry showing the axis-coverage check fired in inquiry X + the runner re-ran Phase 2 Generate with alternative axis surfaced; observed across multiple post-flag instances.
- **Refutes (a) reliable firing:** runtime telemetry (or a diagnostic of an inquiry's failure) showing the axis-coverage check fired but the runner did NOT re-run; observed in 2+ post-flag instances → loop-back-skip pattern confirmed → 00-00's optional secondary trigger condition active.
- **Consistent with (b) absence of bias:** no axis-coverage check firings recorded across multiple inquiries' Innovation outputs; runs may have produced multi-axis-balanced candidate sets without the check needing to flag.

**Commit position: empirically UNRESOLVED.** This finding does NOT falsely confirm reliability based on absence-of-loop-back-skip. The BLOCKER-downgrade remains contingent + the contingency is open. Future inquiries should track the check's firings + runner responses with deliberate telemetry to resolve the ambiguity.

### 6. Axis vocabulary OPEN + load-bearing predicate semantics

ADD-MULTI-AXIS-REQUIREMENT references "load-bearing axes at a meta-decision piece." The current evidence-grounded enumeration of axes from across the diagnostic series + the live /innovate spec is:

- **Content axis** — what the piece SAYS (e.g., the direction of a check; the content of an enum's values). Source: Pair 7's M1 piece in inquiry 14-00 logged Constraint Manipulation + Absence Recognition + Inversion all at content axis.
- **Intervention-shape axis** — what KIND of intervention the piece commits to (e.g., ADD-TEST vs REPAIR vs REVERT vs REFRAME-AS-BUG). Source: Pair 7's Q3-extension at /innovate spec lines 399-414 covers this axis at property-(v) pieces.
- **Cardinality axis** — single-valued vs multi-valued semantics. Source: Pair 12's P1 schema-commitment piece in inquiry 10-50 missed this axis.
- **Scope axis** — what range of cases the piece's commitment applies to. Source: 22-00 synthesis referenced this as one of CORE 2's relevant axes.
- **Direction axis** — for mechanisms with directional sub-modes (Constraint Manipulation both-direction; Absence Recognition redesign-level vs patch-level). Source: 22-00 synthesis Pattern E.
- **Depth axis** — vertical depth-check at the Inversion mechanism (system-level vs component-level). Source: /innovate spec's existing Inversion depth-check refinement.
- **Existence axis** — could the count/quantity be ZERO instead of N? Source: /innovate spec's multi-axis system-level check at line 170.
- **Identity axis** — what does this thing fundamentally consist of? Source: spec line 170.
- **Openness axis** — closed enum vs open enum at schema-commitment pieces. Source: Pair 12.
- **Reading-commitment axis (META-axis)** — at schema-commitment pieces, which READING the schema uses (body-section-shape reading vs content-mention reading) determines cardinality downstream. Source: Pair 12 META-axis observation.

**The list is OPEN, not closed.** The 10 axes above are evidence-grounded — each surfaced in a specific diagnostic case or in the /innovate spec's multi-axis machinery — but the enumeration is intentionally non-exhaustive. Future diagnostic cases may surface additional axes (e.g., temporal axis at process-staging pieces; structural axis at form-vs-content pieces; audience axis at reader-vantage pieces); future promotion inquiry can extend or rename the vocabulary based on the empirical evidence at that time. Closing the vocabulary at the current sample would either preclude future axes from being recognized (false-completeness) or force them into existing categories inappropriately (categorical misfit).

**The "load-bearing" predicate is judgment-dependent but operationally signal-guided.** A piece's axis is load-bearing when the piece's commitment on that axis (a) changes downstream-piece behavior, OR (b) the alternative direction at that axis would produce a meaningfully different artifact, OR (c) the axis is at the same hierarchical level as other identified load-bearing axes (not a meta-axis when base axes are also active, unless explicitly tested for meta-level structure). The signal-guided judgment is the middle ground between two over-extremes — NOT fully algorithmic (over-constraining the determination) and NOT fully arbitrary (under-constraining it). Future promotion inquiry will need to specify the operational determination mechanism (HOW the runner identifies axes per piece); this is one of the three explicit gaps for that future inquiry (see Section 4 Gap 1).
