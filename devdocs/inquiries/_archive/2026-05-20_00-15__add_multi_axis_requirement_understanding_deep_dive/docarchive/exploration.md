## 3. Inventory per Region

### R1 — Operational substance (D3)

**The candidate rule in plain English.**

"When a piece in the inquiry's piece-list is a meta-decision piece (fires properties iv or v of the Meta-Decision-Piece Criterion, AND multiple load-bearing axes are identifiable at this piece, then apply Inversion across ALL identified load-bearing axes simultaneously — not just one axis."

**What "axis" means here.** An axis is a dimension along which a piece's commitment varies. Pair 7 surfaced two specific axes:

- **Content axis** — "what the piece SAYS" (e.g., the direction of a check; the content of an enum's values).
- **Intervention-shape axis** — "what KIND of intervention the piece commits to" (e.g., ADD-TEST vs REPAIR vs REVERT vs REFRAME-AS-BUG; ADD-NEW-ARTIFACT vs EMBED-IN-EXISTING vs CREATE-PARALLEL).

Pair 12 surfaced an additional axis-of-importance:

- **Cardinality / reading-commitment axis** — at schema-commitment pieces, the load-bearing decision is the READING (single-value vs multi-value semantics; body-section-shape reading vs content-mention reading). Cardinality is a downstream consequence of reading-commitment.

Other axes referenced across diagnostics + spec:

- **Depth axis** (Inversion mechanism's existing depth-check — system-level vs component-level statements).
- **Existence axis** (Inversion mechanism's multi-axis system-level check, line 170 — could the count be ZERO instead of N?).
- **Identity axis** (line 170 — what does this thing fundamentally consist of?).
- **Scope axis** (mentioned in 22-00 synthesis as one of the relevant axes per CORE 2's Pattern D).
- **Direction axis** (mentioned in 22-00 — e.g., Constraint Manipulation both-direction; Absence Recognition redesign-level vs patch-level).
- **Openness axis** (mentioned in Pair 12 — closed enum vs open enum at schema-commitment pieces).

**What the rule would CONSTRAIN if promoted.** At a meta-decision piece firing properties iv or v WITH multiple identified load-bearing axes, Innovation could not satisfy the rule by inverting ONE axis (current state under Q3-extension at intervention-shape only; or under Q3 at any single axis). The compliance criterion would require inverting EACH identified axis, producing one Inversion-candidate per axis. The output would carry N Inversion-candidates where N = number of load-bearing axes at the piece.

**What would change in /innovate's runtime.** Currently, the live Piece-Level Inversion Rule (Q3) requires one Inversion-candidate at meta-decision pieces. The live Intervention-Shape-Axis Inversion (Q3-extension) requires the Inversion-candidate to target the intervention-shape axis specifically when property (v) fires. Under ADD-MULTI-AXIS, Innovation would identify ALL load-bearing axes at the piece + produce one Inversion-candidate per axis. This is GENERATION-TIME enforcement of axis-exhaustiveness.

**Subtle but important point.** The rule asks for Inversion at multiple axes "when Inversion is applied." If Inversion is NEVER APPLIED at a piece (Inversion-absence case — Pair 12 at the P1 schema-commitment piece), ADD-MULTI-AXIS has nothing to constrain. That case is Q3 territory (require piece-level Inversion at meta-decision pieces). This distinction is load-bearing for the strict revival trigger (focal point 2 below).
