## Phase 2 — Perspective Checking

### Perspective 1 — Definitional / Internal Consistency (required)

Does the proposed third top-level category contradict the spec's existing 2-operation Generation+Framing distinction?

The spec says: *"Innovation has two structural operations: Generation — creating novel ideas, concepts, or connections that didn't exist before; Framing — finding or constructing the conditions under which a novel idea becomes valid and useful."*

Both operations target *novel ideas* — that is, conceptual content. The split is between *what content* (Generation) and *under what conditions* (Framing) — but both stay within the conceptual-content frame.

Loop-meta moves target the *loop's own procedure* — not the conceptual content. They are not "novel ideas" in the spec's sense; they are *novel procedural directives*.

Internal-consistency check: if we add a third operation "Loop-Meta" alongside Generation+Framing, the new operation does NOT contradict the existing two — it operates on a different target (procedure rather than content). The spec's existing definitions of Generation and Framing don't claim to exhaust all of innovation; they describe what innovation does for content.

But: the spec's overall framing — *"Innovation is the process of creating something that doesn't exist in the current evaluation frame — a new idea, approach, condition, or combination — and making it viable enough to survive scrutiny"* — uses "approach" and "combination" which COULD admit procedural moves. The existing definition is broad enough.

**Internal-consistency verdict:** adding a third top-level category (Loop-Meta) does not contradict; it extends. Acceptable.

But there's an alternative: add Loop-Meta moves as a NEW seed type ("Procedural Failure" or "Loop Meta-Concern") and let existing mechanisms operate on that seed. This preserves the 2-operation distinction without adding a third top-level category.

**New anchor (I7):** the third-operation choice has a more conservative alternative — add Loop-Meta as a new SEED TYPE, then re-express the loop-meta moves as outputs of existing mechanisms operating on that seed. This preserves the existing structure but may stretch existing mechanisms (e.g., asking Combination to generate procedural directives).

### Perspective 3 — Phase / Calibration-State (required)

## Phase 3 — Ambiguity Collapse

### Ambiguity 1 — S4 + S6 + S10 collapse or distinct?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** collapse into one "Loop-Meta-Awareness" mechanism — they all touch the same territory.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):**
- S4 is INPUT-side — reads prior loop history as input to current reasoning. Mechanism shape: input contract change.
- S6 is OUTPUT-side — produces procedural directives as output. Mechanism shape: output contract change.
- S10 is OPERATION-side — applies the known failure-mode list to label prior outputs. Mechanism shape: a specific transform.

Collapsing them loses operational distinctness. A future agent reading the spec would not know whether "Loop-Meta-Awareness" means read-history OR produce-directives OR apply-list.

**Confidence:** HIGH.

**Resolution — KEEP DISTINCT.** S4, S6, S10 are three operationally distinct mechanisms with different I/O contracts. They share the substrate "loop-meta territory" but are not interchangeable.

For top-5 selection: S4 and S6 are most distinct (input + output pair). S10 partially overlaps with S6 (failure-mode self-labeling IS a kind of procedural directive — "label this run as exhibiting failure X"). S10 may consolidate into S6 OR be added as a sub-mode of S6, freeing a slot for a Tier-α candidate.

**What is now fixed:** loop-meta is a CLUSTER, not a single mechanism. S4 and S6 stay distinct; S10 either merges into S6 or holds as a sub-mode.

### Ambiguity 2 — Categorization shape (Generator/Framer addition vs sub-types vs new category)

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** add new mechanisms only as *sub-types within existing mechanisms* (e.g., Existence-Counter as a sub-mode of Absence Recognition; Altitude-Shift as a sub-mode of Lens Shifting; Procedural-Meta as a sub-mode of Constraint Manipulation). **Why the counter partially holds and partially fails:**
- Holds for some candidates: S1 (existence-counter as inverse of Absence Recognition) could plausibly be a sub-mode of Absence Recognition. S2 (altitude-shift) could be a Lens Shifting sub-mode.

Forcing them into existing mechanisms would distort the existing mechanisms' definitions.

**Confidence:** MEDIUM-HIGH.

**Resolution — HYBRID.** New T2 framer-suite candidates enter as either (a) sub-modes within existing Framers when structurally close, or (b) new mechanisms within the Framer category when structurally distinct.
