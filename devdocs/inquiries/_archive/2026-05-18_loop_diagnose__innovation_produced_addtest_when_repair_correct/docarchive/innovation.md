> *Add to `/innovate` reference at §"Phase 2 Generate" (before the variations-per-mechanism rule), or as a new top-level Section §8 "Intervention-Shape Vocabulary":*
>
> **§8 — Intervention-Shape Vocabulary.** When Innovation generates a candidate (especially a maintenance candidate for a spec or a fix for an identified problem), the candidate has an intervention shape — the form of action it proposes. The shape is distinct from the candidate's content. Two candidates with the same content can have different shapes; two candidates with the same shape can have different content. Recognized shapes:
>
> | Shape | Operation on the target | Cost / risk profile | Example downstream effect |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | **ADD-TEST** | Append a new test or check that runs alongside existing text | Lowest risk; no existing behavior changes | Catches the failure when it occurs; doesn't prevent it |
> | **ADD-DIMENSION** | Append a new evaluation dimension to existing evaluation framework | Low risk; expands evaluation surface | Surfaces previously-uncovered failures via new dimension |
> | **REPAIR** | Modify existing text that causes the failure; preserve function while removing the bias | Medium risk; existing behavior changes structurally | Removes the failure at its source |
> | **REVERT-REGRESSION** | Roll back current text to a prior version that did not exhibit the failure | Low-to-medium risk (depends on prior version's other properties) | Removes the failure via historical-state restoration |
> | **REMOVE** | Delete the failing text entirely without replacement | Medium risk; the function the text provided is also removed | The failure cannot occur because the failing element no longer exists |
> | **REFRAME-AS-BUG** | Reclassify the failure from a generic pattern to a localized bug; fix-and-move-on rather than building defenses | Variable risk (depends on whether classification is correct) | Avoids over-engineering a defense for a one-off case |
> | **DO-NOTHING** | Accept the failure as out-of-scope, insufficient evidence to act, or worth-the-cost | No risk; no change | The failure may recur; documented as accepted-cost |
> | **REORGANIZE-WITHOUT-ADDING** | Restructure existing sections without adding new content | Low risk; presentation-only | Improves readability or accessibility without changing semantics |
> | **CONTRARIAN-RETHINK** | Question the framing entirely; treat the prior conclusion as a candidate to invalidate | High risk if applied to load-bearing prior decisions | Surfaces fundamental reframing alternatives |
>
> **Extensibility.** New shapes may be added as evidence accumulates. Revival trigger: when 3+ inquiries surface a maintenance candidate whose shape doesn't fit one of the above, add the new shape with operational description and cost/risk profile.
>
> **Cross-references.** §"Phase 2 Generate" piece-level Inversion rule (Q3 from `2026-05-18_loop_diagnose__innovation_missed_corrects_on_mapping_redo/finding.md`, extended per this finding's Q3) requires Inversion-candidate generation at the intervention-shape axis when the piece commits to a shape from this vocabulary. §"Determination Mechanism for Meta-Decision Pieces" (Q2, extended per this finding) fires property (v) when a piece commits to a named shape from this vocabulary.
