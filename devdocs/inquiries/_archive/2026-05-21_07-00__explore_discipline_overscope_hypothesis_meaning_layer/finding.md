### Surrounding context — why this MEANING-layer inquiry exists

This finding emerged from a foundational concern the user raised after the just-completed 21-05-00 §3.1 redesign: "/explore is doing too much... should be just relevance mapping and content reading; shouldn't attach itself to the solution space." The user's concern was that /explore's current scope — particularly the post-21-05-00 §3.1 axis-minimum prescription — extends into territory that belongs at /sense-making + /td-critique per their own discipline identities.

The question is foundational because it questions the discipline-attribution at THE meaning-layer. If the user is right, the recent 21-05-00 redesign is wrong-placed (cognitive investment retained but discipline-location wrong). The MEANING-layer commitment must be settled before any STRUCTURAL or PROCESS work follows.

### 1. The /explore spec's own §1.5 declaration grounds the user's hypothesis

The /explore spec at `cognitive_harness/explore/references/explore.md` explicitly commits its discipline boundary at multiple sections:

**§1.1 Verb-meaning:**
> "To explore is to perform purposive open-mode surfacing of a territory... /explore discovers WHAT/WHERE; sense-making discovers WHY/HOW."

**§1.3 NOT-list** (first of 5 exclusions):
> "Meaning of items as concepts — conceptual-structure meaning, anchor extraction" — EXCLUDED from /explore.

**§1.4 clarification on "meaning":**
> "The first entry ('no meaning extraction') refers specifically to conceptual-structure meaning — anchor extraction, relational claims among anchors, interpretive role assignment within a conceptual model. It does NOT exclude identifying-labeling content — functional one-lines, surface forms, structural adjacency facts."

**§1.5 vocabulary (the load-bearing declaration):**
> "Labeling is /explore's per-item descriptive content at surface granularity — what each surfaced item IS at the inter-rater-agreement level. Anchor is sense-making's conceptual-structure unit — extracted via sense-making's Phase 1. The shorthand: **labels are observed; anchors are extracted. /explore produces labels. Sense-making consumes labels to extract anchors.**"

**§4.4 labeling-vs-meaning heuristic:**
> "Would a scanner who reads the item but has NOT yet built a conceptual-structure model of the territory produce roughly the same description? High agreement = labeling (acceptable for /explore); low agreement = meaning-extraction (belongs to sense-making)."

**These five sections constitute /explore's own commitment to a specific discipline boundary.** The user's hypothesis ("just relevance mapping + content reading") aligns directly with this commitment.

### 2. The 21-05-00 §3.1 redesign added anchor-extraction content to /explore in apparent violation of §1.5

The 21-05-00 redesigned §3.1 added (among other things):

> "**Comparison-axes dimension** (form ii only, when present). Enumerate the comparison axes explicitly BEFORE populating per-candidate cells. The axis list names at minimum: (a) the inquiry-question's criteria — both stated and implicit in the question's framing; (b) **project-architecture invariants relevant to the candidate's domain** (placement, coupling, installability boundaries; folder-independence commitments inherited from project structure); (c) constraints inherited via the inquiry's Synthesis Trigger (when applicable)..."

Determining which "project-architecture invariants" are RELEVANT to which candidate's DOMAIN requires interpretive role assignment within a conceptual model. The runner must JUDGE:
- Which project-architecture invariants exist for this project.
- Whether each invariant is RELEVANT to the candidate's specific domain.
- Whether the candidate's domain has the structural properties (placement, coupling, installability) that the invariants govern.

Per /explore §1.4, this is "conceptual-structure meaning — anchor extraction, relational claims among anchors, interpretive role assignment within a conceptual model." Per §1.3 NOT-list, that's EXCLUDED from /explore.

Per §4.4 heuristic: would multiple naive scanners (without conceptual model of the project's architecture) produce the same axis list? NO — surfacing this axis requires the conceptual model. The §4.4 heuristic CONFIRMS axis-minimum (b) is meaning-extraction (sense-making's territory), not labeling.
