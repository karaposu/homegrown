### R5 — /explore §3.1 (post-21-05-00 redesign) — DOES IT VIOLATE §1.5?

The §3.1 (post-redesign) commits the following at /explore:

- Form-(ii) "Comparison structure — candidates × axes."
- "Enumerate the comparison axes explicitly BEFORE populating per-candidate cells."
- "The axis list names at minimum: (a) the inquiry-question's criteria — both stated and implicit in the question's framing; (b) **project-architecture invariants relevant to the candidate's domain** (placement, coupling, installability boundaries; folder-independence commitments inherited from project structure); (c) constraints inherited via the inquiry's Synthesis Trigger (when applicable)."

**Inspection per §1.5 + §1.3 + §4.4:**

- **Axis-minimum (a) "the inquiry-question's criteria — both stated and implicit in the question's framing":**
  - "Stated criteria" = explicit text in the inquiry's question. Surfacing them is closer to LABELING (literal-text identification).
  - "Implicit criteria in the question's framing" = REQUIRES INTERPRETIVE JUDGMENT (which criteria are implicitly framed? interpretive role assignment). This is ANCHOR EXTRACTION = sense-making territory per §1.3 + §1.5.
  - **PARTIAL violation:** the "implicit" half violates the NOT-list.

- **Axis-minimum (b) "project-architecture invariants relevant to the candidate's domain":**
  - Determining which invariants are RELEVANT to which candidate's DOMAIN requires interpretive judgment (interpretive role assignment within a conceptual model).
  - Per §1.4: "anchor extraction, relational claims among anchors, interpretive role assignment within a conceptual model" — EXCLUDED from /explore.
  - **FULL violation:** axis-minimum (b) requires interpretive anchor extraction; the NOT-list excludes it.

- **Axis-minimum (c) "constraints inherited via the inquiry's Synthesis Trigger (when applicable)":**
  - Synthesis Trigger inheritance is partly procedural (the trigger fires + constraints flow in). The constraints themselves are pre-existing claims from prior findings.
  - "Constraints" naturally fits as a Sense-making Phase 1 anchor type (per /sense-making spec line 47).
  - Surfacing inherited constraints is borderline — if the inherited constraints are CLEARLY STATED (labeling-compatible), OK at /explore; if they require interpretation (anchor-extraction), they belong at sense-making.
  - **PARTIAL violation:** depends on whether constraints are pre-stated or require extraction.

- **Form-(ii) "Comparison structure" SHAPE recognition:**
  - Recognizing that an output is structured as candidates × axes is closer to LABELING the output shape (a structural observation).
  - Per §4.4 heuristic ("Would a scanner who reads the item but has NOT yet built a conceptual-structure model of the territory produce roughly the same description?") — multiple naive scanners would recognize a pros/cons table as "comparison structure" without needing the conceptual model. PASS.
  - **No violation:** form-(ii) shape-recognition is labeling-compatible. The PRESCRIPTION of axis-MINIMA is what crosses the boundary.

**Summary of §3.1 (post-redesign) violations of /explore's own NOT-list:**
- Form-(ii) shape recognition: OK at /explore.
- Axis-MINIMUM-PRESCRIPTION mechanism: VIOLATES /explore's NOT-list (anchor extraction).
- Specifically: (a)-implicit half + (b) entirety + (c) implicit-inheritance half all violate.
