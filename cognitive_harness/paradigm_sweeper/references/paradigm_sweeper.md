# Structural Paradigm-Sweeping — A Thinking Discipline

> **Provisionality note.** This spec is **v0-provisional**: its instruments are inherited from committed findings with a live validation debt (§6). The first invocation's critique step is this spec's named checker. Treat every clause as revisable on first-run evidence — through the spec-edit route, never by silent drift.

## What Paradigm-Sweeping Is

**The paradigm-sweeper makes chart acquisition runnable.** Chart acquisition is the cartographic act the movement vocabulary defines: *no position changes; the map of the ways comes into existence.* Given a stuck or unsatisfying topic, the sweeper recasts the topic as a practice ("whose practice, doing what?"), then perceives and maps that practice's **paradigm-families** — coherent, generative bundles of commitments, each passing the membership test — as a persistent map of 5–9 basic-level entries, each a loop-ready seed. **It enumerates; it never selects.**

Why this operation exists: generation disciplines transform a seed or shift its frame — moves on or near ONE candidate, within a chart they never acquire. You cannot jump to a family you have never seen on a map. The sweeper supplies the map; the loops that consume its seeds make the jumps deep.

One operation, consumer-dialed scales: **S1** — the in-run seed-time hook inside the innovation discipline (its own gated design; it CITES this file's §2). **S2** — this skill: one enumeration run producing a persistent map. **S3** — the full **Paradigm-Sweep** multi-loop orchestration (detect → N per-paradigm loops → cross-family selection), which is the MTTP catalog's member. *Naming pair:* this skill is `paradigm-sweeper` (the S2 instrument); "Paradigm-Sweep" names the S3 pattern. They are one program at two scales, not two features.

## 1. Identity

### 1.1 Verb-meaning (the cognitive operation)

To paradigm-sweep is to **perceive the family structure of a practice's approach-space** — to acquire its chart. The sweep is perception, not generation: its output describes ways-of-working that exist as attractors in the practice (plus coherent frontier regions), it does not produce candidate solutions. Position-neutral by construction: after a sweep, nobody has moved; everybody can see.

### 1.2 The unit — the practice

Paradigms attach to **practices**: an agent-kind approaching a task-kind with genuine approach-variation. The input topic may arrive object-domain-phrased ("paradigms of X"); **probe 0 recasts it** — "whose practice, doing what?" — and the recast is recorded in the map's header. Object-domain asks resolve to practices on inspection ("paradigms in physics" = ways of DOING physics); a topic that survives no recast ("paradigms of the number seven") is out of scope, and saying so is a correct sweep result. One adjacent sense stays outside: paradigm-as-**era-pattern** ("the feudal paradigm" — a period's organizing configuration, not anyone's way of working) — not an enumeration target until a practice takes such patterns as material.

### 1.3 The output unit — the paradigm-family

> **A paradigm — relative to a practice — is a WAY the practice is done: a coherent, generative bundle of commitments, one of several distinct attractors in the practice's approach-space. Commitments on the inside; a recognizable family of outcomes on the outside.**

Necessary (each does boundary work): the **commitment-bundle** (≥2 cohering choices, mutually supporting toward the paradigm's own criterion) and **generative capacity** (new instances can be worked from it — capacity, not activity). Typical and evidential, not essential: **exemplar-anchoring** and **community recognition** — their absence marks a **frontier** candidate, never a rejection. Plurality belongs to the field: paradigms come as alternatives, and alternatives compose.

Every map entry is one family, at the **basic level** — the grain where practitioners naturally talk ("microservices," not "software," not "microservices-with-Kafka-on-Kubernetes"). Entries carry a status: **`est`** (established: coherent + exemplified), **`frontier`** (coherent, unexemplified — first-class, kept), or **`stale(reason)`** (a prior entry a later run found mis-individuated or dissolved — appears only under §3.6's rules).

### 1.4 NOT-list (what paradigm-sweeping is not)

1. **Not a pipeline stage** — the traverse loop is unchanged; the sweeper is invoked on demand, upstream of loops.
2. **Not a selector** — it never ranks, recommends, or picks the winning family; cross-family selection belongs to the consumer, after dives. *(The identity wall — see §4.3.)*
3. **Not a candidate-generator** — it maps families; the DIVES generate. A worked solution inside the map is a failure mode (§4.2).
4. **Not the Paradigm-Sweep MTTP** — the catalog owns the S3 multi-loop orchestration; this skill is the S2 organ that orchestration would invoke.
5. **Not an innovate replacement** — it feeds charts to whatever generates; the S1 seed-time hook remains the innovation discipline's own patch, which cites this file rather than duplicating it.
6. **Not a paradigm-shift engine** — it ACQUIRES charts; it neither replaces the reigning chart nor argues anyone off it (acquisition ≠ replacement).
7. **Not generic enumeration** — it does not re-implement other disciplines' machinery; it operationalizes the paradigm-instruments (§2) and nothing else.
8. **Not a finished theory** — v0-provisional; the instruments carry their validation ceiling openly (§6).

### 1.5 Vocabulary

- **practice** — an agent-kind approaching a task-kind with genuine approach-variation; the sweep's unit.
- **paradigm / family** — one WAY the practice is done (per §1.3); one map entry.
- **frontier candidate** — a coherent, unexemplified combination; kept on the map, flagged `frontier`.
- **facet** — one of the 5+2 recurring axis-KINDS (§2.2) used to FIND a practice's axes.
- **axis** — one of the practice's own dimensions of approach-variation; found per topic, not given. *(These approach-axes are not answer-axes: an answer-axis spans one composite answer's dimensions; an approach-axis spans ways of working.)*
- **chart / map** — the sweep's artifact: the practice's families + axes.
- **dive** — a downstream loop run on one family's seed (not this discipline's act).
- **seed / seed-block** — the per-entry, copy-pasteable loop input (§5.2).

### 1.6 The dial

Depth is consumer-dialed: **`persisted`** (default) — the full pipeline (probes 0–5), emitted as a map file; **`in-session`** — a quick glance that may stop early but **may not call its output a sweep-map** (an early-stopped glance is a modal sketch, not chart acquisition — see §4.1's first failure mode).

## 2. The Instrument System

*This section is the instruments' single source of truth — inherited verbatim-in-substance from the defining finding. Other artifacts (including the innovation discipline's seed-time hook) CITE these subsections rather than duplicating them. Citation convention: dual-anchor — §-number plus title (e.g., "§2.2 The meta-facet kit"), so either anchor survives a drift.*

### 2.1 The membership test (the family-filter)

For any candidate family, one glance:

1. **Bundle?** — at least two cohering choices, held together in real practice?
2. **Generative?** — can you work new instances from it?
3. **Anchored?** — an exemplar to point at; a name practitioners would recognize?

**(1) and (2) decide; (3) strengthens** — and its absence marks a `frontier` candidate rather than a rejection. What the test excludes: an **option** (one choice — no bundle: "dark mode" is an option; "dark-first design" — contrast strategy + elevation-via-lightness + OLED economics, cohering — is a paradigm); a **style-description** (no generative capacity); a **move/mechanism** (a transformation you APPLY, not a way you WORK); a **lens** (how you evaluate, not where you generate from). Failed candidates are **demoted, with the failed conjunct named** — to an axis-value on the chart, or dropped — into the map's Excluded section. Never pad the map.

### 2.2 The meta-facet kit (probe 2's engine)

The recurring axis-KINDS used to find a practice's axes — **a heuristic prior, never a checklist**: walking the kinds reliably SURFACES axes, but not every axis maps cleanly to one kind, and an axis earns its place only by discriminating real variants. Report stretch-fits in telemetry; never force an empty slot.

| # | Meta-facet | The glance question |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **SOURCE / MATERIAL** | what does the approach draw on? |
| 2 | **UNIT / GRAIN** | what is its working unit? |
| 3 | **OPERATION / MOVE** | what is the agent's characteristic action? |
| 4 | **COORDINATION / RELATION** | how do parts or participants relate? |
| 5 | **CRITERION / VALUE** | what counts as good inside it? |
| 6* | *SEQUENCE (secondary)* | what order does it impose? |
| 7* | *RESPONSIBILITY-LOCUS (secondary)* | who carries the load? |

Two clarifiers: **OPERATION vs COORDINATION** — the operation is the move the AGENT makes; coordination is the structure AMONG participants or parts. **CRITERION is special:** paradigms carry their own definitions of success — which is why cross-paradigm comparison is structurally hard, and why a sweep-map never ranks families.

### 2.3 Probes 0–5 (the pipeline)

- **Probe 0 — scope:** *"Whose practice, doing what?"* (a topic that is a thing → "doing what with it?"). Mandatory; the recast is recorded in the map header.
- **Probe 1 — schools:** *"What would practitioners call the fundamentally different WAYS of doing this?"*
- **Probe 2 — axes via the facets:** *"Material? unit? move? relation? criterion? — what dimensions of variation does each expose?"*
- **Probe 3 — defaults:** *"Per axis: the default everyone assumes, and the non-defaults?"*
- **Probe 4 — composition:** *"Which non-default combinations cohere — and which have real exemplars?"* Output splits: **established composed families** (coherent + exemplified → `est`) and **frontier candidates** (coherent, unexemplified → `frontier` — flagged, kept, never dropped).
- **Probe 5 — loosening:** *"Which regions are fashionable for tasks like this — and which unfashionable ones fit THIS case?"*

**The modal note (why the pipeline exists):** probe 1 alone reproduces the MODAL map — the schools anyone would name — and misses the composed regions where off-modal winners live. **Probes 2–5 exist for the regions probe 1 cannot see.** A persisted map that stopped at probe 1 is the discipline's first failure mode.

### 2.4 The kit's bounds

The facet-walk's claim — walking the kinds reliably surfaces axes — held on four validation topics (naming, app-architecture, skill-acquisition, psychotherapy), with rediscovery of a known composed family in each (modular monolith; deliberate practice; third-wave CBT). Honest bounds: not every axis maps to one facet (stretch-fits happen and are reported); the kit is the facet-list's own basic level (three facets lose real discriminations; ten-plus force empty slots); and **all validation to date is single-mind, same-session — the blind-topic ceiling of §6.**

## 3. Process Model — recast → sweep → admit → seed → persist

*The process runs §2's instruments, in order.*

### 3.1 Probe 0 — the recast

Run probe 0 on the raw topic. Record both the raw topic and the practice-recast in the map header. If no recast survives, stop and say so — an honest out-of-scope verdict beats a forced sweep.

### 3.2 The sweep — probes 1–5

Run the probes in order. Probe 1 drafts the modal families. Probe 2 walks the facet kit to expose the practice's axes (name each axis; note which facet surfaced it; flag stretch-fits). Probe 3 marks each axis's default and non-defaults. Probe 4 composes non-default combinations and splits them {est | frontier}. Probe 5 loosens fashion — note which regions are conventionally strong for this task-kind and which unfashionable regions fit THIS case (a note on entries, never a ranking).

### 3.3 Entry admission

Run §2.1's membership test on every candidate — the probe-1 schools included. Admit passers as entries at the basic level. Demote failures to the **Excluded** section with the failed conjunct named and the disposition (axis-value on the chart | dropped).

### 3.4 Seeding — and the anchoring guard

Every admitted entry carries a **seed-block** (template in §5.2): a copy-pasteable loop input, **capped at ~6 lines** — its fields pin the family's non-default commitments so a dive doesn't regress to the modal reading; anything longer starts doing the downstream loop's own articulation work.

**The anchoring guard (canonical statement):** *when multiple families are to be dived, generate a fixed small batch WITHIN each family before judging ANY across families.* The map completes before any dive; judgment waits until every dived family has its batch. Cross-family selection belongs to the consumer, after dives — never to this discipline.

### 3.5 Convergence

The map is done when fresh candidates keep re-landing in existing families ("same family" = matching axis-signature at the map's grain) — normally **5–9 entries**. The grain check: each entry must support a DISTINCT dive, and merging any two entries must lose a live distinction. Fewer entries plus a rich Excluded section is a correct outcome on demotion-heavy practices; padding to reach a count is not.

### 3.6 Re-runs — the lazy pair

A sweep-map is **static once emitted** — history, never edited (the Dived ✓ column depends on this). Evolution happens by re-running the sweep on the same practice:

**On any re-run, create `_sweep.md` beside the maps if absent**, seeding its identity-set from the prior map's index — **including its dived column, seeded from the prior map's ✓s with per-run provenance (`dived @run1`)** — then load it, and emit a NEW map (`paradigm_sweep_v2.md`, `_v3`, …). In the index that travels forward: enrich entries with what dives taught; **stale-flag** mis-individuated or dissolved families with a reason — never delete, never renumber. `stale(reason)` values appear **only on run-≥2 maps, authored at emission** — never edited into an existing map. **Every new map's ✓ column is authored empty** — per-run engagement lives on the map; cumulative dive-history lives in the index. Prior maps stay untouched. `_sweep.md` carries only the family-identity-set and an invocation log — no process state (schema: §5.4).

## 4. Quality

### 4.1 LAYER 1 — operational failure modes

1. **Modal-Map-Only** — the sweep stops at probe 1; the map lists the schools anyone would name; the composed regions (the tool's purpose) are absent. *Corrective:* probes 2–4 are mandatory for a persisted map; telemetry reports the count of families probe 1 alone would not have produced.
2. **Checklist-Ossification** — the facet kit walked as boxes to fill; forced axes; empty discriminations. *Corrective:* the kit is a prior — an axis earns its place by discriminating real variants; stretch-fits are reported, not hidden.
3. **Family-Hallucination** — entries that fail the membership test: options, styles, moves, or lenses dressed as paradigms. *Corrective:* §2.1 per entry; demote with the failed conjunct named; never pad.
4. **Anchoring-Dive** — judging or diving the first attractive family before the map completes, or judging any family before each dived family has its batch. *Corrective:* the map completes first; the §3.4 guard governs dives.
5. **Scope-Miss** — probe 0 skipped; an object-domain swept instead of the practice. *Corrective:* probe 0 is mandatory; the recast is recorded in the header.
6. **Grain-Drift** — entries above or below the basic level: super-paradigm blobs or sub-variant shards. *Corrective:* the §3.5 grain check (distinct dive per entry; merging loses a distinction).

### 4.2 LAYER 2 — identity-eroding failure modes

7. **Selection-Creep** — the map crowns a winner: ranks families, recommends one, or "just starts" the best dive. *Corrective:* enumerate-don't-decide (§4.3); probe-5 fit-notes are attributive, never ordinal; selection belongs to the consumer, after dives.
8. **Generation-Creep** — the sweeper starts solving inside entries: worked candidates, designs, or solutions appear in the map. *Corrective:* entries carry commitments, exemplars, and axis-positions — never worked solutions; the seed-block points forward to the dive that will generate.
9. **Coverage-Theater** — the map presented as exhaustive. *Corrective:* **a coverage discipline, not a coverage proof** — the artifact carries a bounds note; telemetry states what loosening (probe 5) did and did not run.

### 4.3 The asymmetric principle

The identity wall is **enumerate-don't-decide**. Operational failures (LAYER 1) degrade a map; identity failures (LAYER 2) change what the discipline IS — a sweeper that selects becomes a chooser wearing a surveyor's badge, and its maps stop being trustable as maps. When in doubt, the asymmetry rules: prefer an honest smaller map with a rich Excluded section over an impressive map that quietly ranked, solved, or claimed completeness.

### 4.4 Self-assessment

Every run ends with a verdict: **PROCEED** (the map is a usable chart: recast recorded; probes run per the dial; entries membership-filtered; seeds attached; bounds stated) or **FLAG** (something needs the consumer's eyes before use — name it: an unresolvable recast, a facet-walk that forced axes, a map that couldn't converge).

## 5. Output

### 5.1 The sweep-map — `paradigm_sweep.md`

One file per run. **Required sections (checkable):** the header · the index table · one record per index row · Excluded · Telemetry.

- **Header:** `# Paradigm Sweep — <the practice>` · **Raw topic** (verbatim input) · **Practice (probe-0 recast)** · **Goal** · **Dial** · **Run** (number; `fresh` or `re-run per §3.6`) · date.
- **Index table:** `| # | Family | Bundle (one line) | Status | Exemplar | Dived ✓ |` — Status ∈ `est` · `frontier` · `stale(reason)`; **`stale` appears only on run-≥2 maps, authored at emission, never edited into an existing map.** The **Dived ✓ column is consumer-filled**: the sweeper authors it EMPTY and never reads it; the consumer ticks (and may annotate) as dives complete.
- **Records, Excluded, Telemetry:** per §5.2 and §5.5.

### 5.2 The entry record schema + the seed-block

```markdown
### F<n> — <family name>
- **Bundle:** <the 2–4 non-default choices, pinned>
- **Grounds:** <exemplar: name it | "frontier — coherent per <axes>">
- **Axis-positions:** <facet-or-axis: value · axis: value · …>
- **Seed-block:**
```

```text
seed: explore the <FAMILY> approach to <practice + goal>
commitments: <the bundle, pinned>
exemplar: <name it | "none — coherent per <axes>">
axis-positions: <axis: value · …>
goal-echo: <the shared goal, one line>
guard: generate within this family before judging across families (§3.4)
```

The seed-block is **divergence-pinning, not description**: the commitments pin the non-default choices so the dive doesn't regress to the modal reading; the cap (~6 lines) keeps the block from doing the loop's own articulation work. The Excluded section's line-form: `- <candidate> — failed <conjunct> → demoted to axis-value on <axis> | dropped`.

### 5.3 Worked micro-example

*Provenance: entry one is cited from the founding naming map (the paradigm chain's first worked map); entry two is an **illustrative frontier composed in that map's axis vocabulary for format display** — the founding map itself carries no unexemplified entry. Replace or supplement with a first-invocation excerpt when one exists.*

- **F3 — Compound-functional** *(est)* — **Bundle:** name speaks what-it-does; two-word compound morphology; descriptive transparency. **Grounds:** exemplar — "TalkMenu" (the founding map's own example). **Axis-positions:** identity-semantics: what-it-does · morphology: compound · transparency: descriptive.
- **F× — Market-native phonetic mutation** *(frontier)* — **Bundle:** source-language = market-native; morphology = phonetic mutation of a real word; transparency = suggestive. **Grounds:** frontier — coherent per source-language × morphology × transparency; no exemplar on the founding map. *(The founding case's actual winner lived in the adjacent composed family — translingual-metaphor, "Masajan" — showing how off-modal composed regions are where sweeps earn their keep.)*

### 5.4 The persistent index — `_sweep.md` (lazy)

**Born at the second invocation, not the first** — an index only earns existence when a second run loads it. On birth: seed from the prior map's index (including dived-state from its ✓s, with `@run<n>` provenance). Schema: an **identity-set table** (`family · status · grounds-pointer · dived · first-seen · last-touched`) + an **invocation log** (one line per run: date · dial · entries · demotions · verdict). Within-family content only — no process or control-flow state. Maintained per §3.6: enrich; stale-flag with reasons; never delete, never renumber.

### 5.5 Telemetry

One block at the map's tail: **dial** · **probes run** (0–5; loosening included or skipped) · **families:** N (**beyond-probe-1:** k — the count probe 1 alone would not have produced) · **stretch-fits** (axes that didn't map cleanly to a facet) · **demotions** (count; the Excluded section carries the detail) · **bounds note** (what this sweep did NOT cover — a coverage discipline, not a coverage proof) · **Self-assessment: PROCEED | FLAG**.

## Execute the Paradigm-Sweeping Process

### 1. Receive

Input: a **raw topic** (may be object-domain-phrased — recasting is this discipline's first act, never the caller's duty) + optional **goal** + optional **dial** (`in-session | persisted`; default `persisted`) + optional **out-path** (an inquiry chain passes its folder; default `devdocs/sweeps/<practice-slug>/`).

### 2. Recast (probe 0)

Run §3.1. Record raw topic + practice-recast in the header. No surviving recast → stop with an honest out-of-scope verdict.

### 3. Sweep (probes 1–5)

Run §3.2 in order. For a `persisted` map, all probes are mandatory (the Modal-Map-Only corrective).

### 4. Admit & demote

Run §3.3 — the membership test per candidate; demotions to Excluded with the failed conjunct named.

### 5. Seed

Attach the §5.2 seed-block to every admitted entry; state the §3.4 guard once.

### 6. Persist

Save the map per §5.1 to the out-path (or the default). If a prior map exists for this practice, this is a re-run — follow §3.6 (create/load `_sweep.md`; emit a versioned map; stale-flag in the index; author the new ✓ column empty).

### 7. Assess

Emit §5.5's telemetry and the self-assessment verdict. Check the LAYER 1/2 catalog (§4) before calling PROCEED.
